# docs/11 — THE PROSE CHART (v1, 2026-07-20)

The generation-side sibling of the natal chart: attack the mode BEFORE generation instead of scrubbing after. Mechanism (why the natal chart worked, generalized): (1) combinatorial entropy — a rolled seed space keeps every voice off the mode; (2) a deep interpretive literature — every axis is a knowledge system the model has absorbed thoroughly, so it derives rather than decorates; (3) indirection — never ask for the quality, specify the constraint and let the quality arrive as an inference. One chart per narrator per work. Rolled with real dice, then FITTED: the fit pass reconciles rolls into one plausible biography (occupation feeds metaphor domain; region+era feed lexicon; contradictions resolved by inventing the life that explains them). The chart is enforced at write time (it IS the style section of the author prompt) and measured after (every axis maps to a miner instrument — docs/10 enforcement).

## CHART LAB-01 (rolled 2026-07-20: 2,4,6,3,3,1,1,0,8,1,3,2,3,2,1,1)

THE FIT: male, ~50. Texas Gulf Coast (Port Arthur register). Formed in the 1990s. Thirty years a short-order cook — diner flat-top, refinery-shift cafes. One year of college, quit, doesn't bring it up. Birder — the quiet kind; the upper Gulf flyway is his church (High Island in April, spoonbills on the intracoastal). VOICE SPEC: 90% Anglo-Saxon word stock; Latinate vocabulary appears ONLY when institutions speak through the page (the bank's letter, the county's notice, hospice paperwork) and reads as a register break on purpose. Understatement by negation is his temperature (litotes: "it wasn't much of a funeral"). Compression by omission (ellipsis: dropped subjects, dropped verbs — Told him no. Told him twice.). No quotation marks; dialogue runs bare in the line. Parentheses carry his asides (the things he says to himself and not to you). Full profanity, load-bearing not decorative. Short choppy paragraphs. Distant first person: he reports himself like a man giving a statement, does not perform his insides. Metaphor: at most one per scene, and the vehicle must come off the flat-top or out of the birds. Sentences: mid-length mean with spiky variance — every so often one runs long like a shift that won't end, or stops at two words.

## THE EXPERIMENT (LAB protocol, first run 2026-07-20)

Same premise, two drafts by the same generator: CONTROL = the default register, unguarded ("write it well"); CHART = under LAB-01. Premise: a man returns to his dying hometown to empty his father's house before the sale. Measure both with the miner instruments (docs/10 enforcement: slop-word density, phrase hits, construction regexes, burstiness, geometry, said-ratio) and the detector rings when standing. Success criterion: the chart draft moves materially toward the human side on the instruments WITHOUT losing craft on a human read. Files: stories/prosechart-lab/.

RESULTS (run 1, 2026-07-20, SlopScore v0.1): the chart draft won EVERY instrumented axis — em-dashes 10→0, participial tails 3→1, -ly adverbs 5.0→1.5/1k, sentence burstiness 0.66→1.06, paragraph raggedness 0.30→0.76, said-only attribution held. The control (unguarded default) also produced four tells the instruments can't yet see: an aphorism button ("You do not empty a house. The house empties you"), personification ("the town clearing its throat"), crafted-phrase reach ("the quiet arithmetic of a man"), and a poetically tidy closer — these name the NEXT INSTRUMENTS to build (aphorism-button detector, personification-of-abstractions detector, closer-tidiness check). CAVEATS: one author generated both while knowing the experiment (demand characteristics); ~600-word samples; no external detector ring yet (Open Pangram pending). The author's quality read is the deciding gate (Goodhart guard: better prose, not better scores).

RUN 2 (2026-07-20, drama-first): the author diagnosed run 1's root — NO DRAMA IN THE PREMISE ("we can only fix the language when the underlying concept is fixed") and supplied premises with walls (squatters in the house / graffiti against the father). Run 2 = his squatter premise, drama-carded BEFORE writing (want: broom-clean in 9 days; wall: Darla + kids + the father's cash-ledger arrangement; clock: the auction; turn: arrives owner, leaves stranger; argument: papered vs lived possession — the author's recurring possession engine, same as Kabza). Scores: 0 slop-words, 0 phrases, 0 em-dashes, burstiness 1.26, said 19/fancy 0 — cleanest yet, and the prose stopped performing because the SITUATION carried. File: chart_LAB01_run2_squatters.md. Author's interest verdict pending.

VERDICT COMPLETED (author, 2026-07-20): both samples rejected as "human slop" — uninteresting even if 100% human-written. DOCTRINE CORRECTION: passing AI detection is a FLOOR, not a target; the bar is the author's interest; the workshop-epiphany register is a HUMAN genre the models learned from humans, and it fails regardless of author species. CONFESSION on record: the experiment's premise ("man empties dead father's house") was the mode's own favorite subject, and no story was designed — the locked drawer was a mystery-box with nothing planned inside it, withholding as a substitute for having something to withhold. STANDING RULE: no premise-less prose experiments; every lab sample sits on a concept engine + drama spine first (see [[simple-prose-complex-concepts]] — the concept layer precedes the prose chart).

THE DISTINCTION RETIRED (2026-07-20, run 2's opening line): I drew a "legal ellipsis (drops grammar) vs illegal rationing (drops information)" distinction — and then opened run 2 with "The lawyer said vacant. Said it twice on the phone" — the author caught it instantly as 100% AI ("we literally had a conversation about this"). My own "legal" example ("Told him no. Told him twice.") is the identical cadence. THE RULE, no nuance: the reader sees only the SURFACE, so the surface is banned — any short subjectless verb-headed sentence echoing the previous sentence's verb (forward: "Said X. Said it twice." / reverse: "Knocked. ...and I knocked."), and info-in-installments chains ("a bird. On the wire. A scissor-tail."). ENFORCED: SlopScore's echo-fragment detector (forward + reverse) — proved on my own text: 4 hits I hadn't seen, 3 rewritten, 1 triage-reviewed as plain-verb repetition at distance and accepted (the instrument over-flags by design; the human adjudicates). Non-echo telegraphic sequence remains chart-legal ("Went back to the truck. Sat there.").
