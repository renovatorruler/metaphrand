# docs/12 — THE METAPHOR STACK (v1, 2026-07-20)

The founding architecture, returned and formalized for prose. Origin: the repo's first commits (the metaphor-DAG story engine; the janitor story encoded as a DAG; the DAG rendered to Fountain) and the project's name itself — Jaynes' METAPHRAND = the thing a metaphor exists to express; the METAPHIER = the concrete vehicle expressing it. The author's law: in story, everything is a metaphor — "I rotated the knob twice" carries a metaphrand whether the writer chose it or not. Slop, precisely defined: surface text with no metaphrand — sentences that exist for rhythm, texture, or probability alone.

## THE LAW

Prose is never generated from a premise. It is generated DOWN the stack, each layer a reviewable artifact, the surface being the last and least free transform. This mirrors the screenplay discipline (beats → plot → scene cards → pages) which the engine already enforces for scripts and had failed to apply to prose.

## LAYER 0 — THE WHOLE (added 2026-07-20 after the pilot's failure; nothing below it may run without it)

A scene cannot be written in isolation: it is a FUNCTION of the whole — the story's world, its act position, its aftermath, and its plants. The canonical illustration (the author's poker example): a man suspects a poker buddy is sleeping with his wife and will discover which one tonight. The scene is computed from five inputs before any writing question exists: (1) the WORLD — fighter pilots vs bank clerks changes the room's language and what a slip sounds like; (2) the ACT POSITION — a doorway-of-no-return scene must close a door forever; (3) the AFTERMATH — if he will coldly plan murder afterward, the scene ends in control, rage turning inward, not explosion; (4) the PLANT — the wife's tantric spirituality established innocently chapters earlier, so the friend's slip can wear its vocabulary (only her lover could know); (5) the SUSPICION'S CAUSE — why this friend. Then the activity carries the meaning: the friend has bluffed him all night as he has all year, and when the husband finally SEES, he starts winning the pots. The cards become the marriage. "All these things are going through a writer's mind — not just predicting the next token."

THEREFORE the full descent for a novel: LAYER 0 = concept with mass ([[simple-prose-complex-concepts]]) → spine through the drama gates (docs/08) → act map with doorways → chapter cards (each knowing its position, aftermath, plants-in and payoffs-out) → and only then, per chapter, layers 1–4 below, with layer 1's meanings INHERITED from above, never invented in place. The 2026-07-20 pilot (squatter meaning sheet) is PARKED as an orphan-scene artifact: correct machinery, no story above it — kept as the record of the failure that forced layer 0.

## THE LAYERS

1. THE MEANING SHEET — the chapter's metaphrands: the finite list of things this chapter exists to say. Meanings, not events ("the paper's claim about the house is false"; "the father had a whole life the son wasn't in"; "neither party is wrong and the clock doesn't care").
2. STRUCTURE — the drama card (want / wall / clock / turn, docs/08); each metaphrand assigned a position in the scene's motion.
3. THE METAPHIER PASS — the new organ: for each metaphrand, CHOOSE the concrete vehicle before any sentence exists. "Paper's claim false" → the purple bike with training wheels. "Father's unknown life" → his coffee brand in a stranger's kitchen; his nickname in a child's mouth. "The informal ledger" → the spiral notebook with shift-sheet checkmarks. Authorship happens HERE; the sentence is delivery. Vehicles obey the character/author vehicle-domain constraints (docs/11: the cook's kitchen and birds; Dale's medicine and work).
4. THE SURFACE — the AUTHOR persona (docs/11 hierarchy: author → narrator → character) renders the metaphier list into prose under their dogmas. Composition rule: the metaphor stack decides what each line is FOR; the author decides how a line like that gets said. (Dale's furniture dogma = this system as craft superstition: no metaphier without a metaphrand.)

## THE GATE IT CREATES

The traceability check: every sentence in a draft must trace to a meaning-sheet entry (directly, or as connective tissue serving one). A sentence with no papers is filler BY DEFINITION — flaggable without pattern-matching its surface, immune to the arms race, and equally lethal to machine slop and human slop. Implement as a judge pass: annotate each sentence with its metaphrand-id or FILLER; filler density is the score. (Kin of [[clear-pane-law]]: intrigue lives in events/meaning, never in sentence construction — this stack is the generative side of that law.)

## APPROVAL FLOW (per the script-first law)

Layers 1–3 are presented BEFORE prose exists — the author reviews what the chapter says and through which objects, exactly as he approves scene cards before pages. Layer 4 runs only on approved sheets. Evidence from the lab (stories/prosechart-lab/): run 2's strong beats were untracked instances of this stack (bike vs "vacant"); its slop was sentences with no metaphrand.
