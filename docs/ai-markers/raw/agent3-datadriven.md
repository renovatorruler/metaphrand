## SECTION 5 — eqbench.com Slop Score metric + sam-paech/slop-score engine (updated 1,648-word list, regex detector suite)

**Pages/repos:** https://eqbench.com/slop-score.html ; https://github.com/sam-paech/slop-score ; leaderboards at https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html and https://eqbench.com/creative_writing_longform.html

**Slop Score definition (verbatim weights):** weighted composite — **60% Slop Words** (frequency of individual words unnaturally common in LLM output, matched vs. wordfreq human baseline), **25% Not-x-but-y Patterns** (contrast constructions), **15% Slop Trigrams** (matched vs. human baseline corpus of essays and creative writing). **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136 (v4; PDF text extracted); code/data: https://github.com/jenna-russell/storyscope
**Scale:** 10,272 human stories (Books3 anthologies) mirrored by 5 LLMs (Gemini 3 Flash, Kimi K2.5, DeepSeek V3.2, Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT 5.4) = 61,608 stories, ~4,753 words each, 304 narrative features per story.
**Fiction-market numbers cited:** Hachette pulled the novel *Shy Girl* after being flagged ~78% AI-generated (March 2026, first commercially published novel canceled over AI allegations); **~20% of a sample of 14,000 self-published Amazon novels flagged by Pangram as largely AI-generated, +41% year-over-year**; fine-tuning to mimic human style drops creative-writing AI detection from 97% to 3% (Chakrabarty et al.).
**Findings:** narrative features alone (no style) reach 93.2% macro-F1 human-vs-AI detection and 68.4% six-way attribution; AI stories over-explain themes, favor tidy single-track plots, moralize explicitly; human stories have more morally ambiguous protagonist choices, flashbacks/nonlinear time, higher rarity in narrative space (0.71 vs 0.49 percentile). Per-model fingerprints: Claude = flat event escalation + epilogues; GPT = over-indexes on dream sequences; Gemini = external character description. It cites em-dashes, "delve", "tapestry" as the reliable surface tells its style-free method deliberately excludes.
