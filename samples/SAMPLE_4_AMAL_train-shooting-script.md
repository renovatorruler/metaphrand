# अमल — "THE TRAIN" — SHOOTING SCRIPT (v2, screenplay format)

Scene-numbered, camera direction inline, shot markers [in brackets] keyed to the shot list (v1 doc). Companion documents: shot list + breakdown (2026-07-20_TRAIN_SHOOTING_SCRIPT_v1.md). Pages lock on approval; later additions become A-shots.

1 INT. RAILWAY COMPARTMENT — DAY 1

[1-01] WIDE — the length of a crowded 1990s general compartment. Blue walls, barred windows, ceiling fans turning slow in the heat. In the near bay: a WOMAN by the window, a baby wrapped to the crown in a faded green cloth against her shoulder, one hand flat and still on its back. Facing her, SURESH (40s, tired) and his wife KAMLA, who has been talking since the platform. His younger brother RAJESH (20s) sprawls on the side berth across the aisle.

[1-02] TWO-SHOT — Kamla and Suresh.

KAMLA: मैंने कहा था ना सुबह वाली पकड़ लेते। अब बैठो इस लू में। और वो अचार वाला डब्बा तुमने ऊपर रखा कि नीचे? मुझे तो याद ही नहीं आ रहा।

SURESH: (looking out, half there) रखा है कहीं।

KAMLA: और जीजाजी को फ़ोन कर देना उतरते ही, वरना वो फिर मुँह फुलाए बैठे रहेंगे शादी भर।

Suresh nods at nothing.

[1-03] CLOSE ON THE WOMAN — face turned to the glass. The platform slides away in the window; she does not move with it. The flat hand on the bundle, still.

[1-04] INSERT — the green cloth. Two flies land near the crown. The hand does not move.

[1-05] BACK ON KAMLA — flapping a hand at the air near her face.

KAMLA: उई, ये मक्खियाँ कहाँ से आ गईं इतनी। (to the woman, friendly) बहन, तुम्हारे ही ऊपर भिनभिना रही हैं। कुछ मीठा रखा है क्या साथ में?

The woman shakes her head. She says nothing.

KAMLA: कितने महीने का है? लड़का है ना?

A small nod from the woman.

KAMLA: इतनी गरमी में इत्ता लपेट के रखा है बेचारे को, घुटन नहीं होगी उसको? ज़रा खोलो ऊपर से।

[1-06] CLOSE ON THE WOMAN.

WOMAN: सो रहा है। ठीक है वो।

Kamla shrugs and turns back to her husband.

KAMLA: हाँ तो मैं कह रही थी, जीजी का घर स्टेशन से दस मिनट है, पर रिक्शे वाले दिन में लूटते हैं...

[1-07] CLOSE ON SURESH — not listening anymore. His eyes move to the woman. The flat hand. The cloth drawn up over the child's head.

[1-08] SURESH'S POV — SLOW PUSH toward the bundle: the covered face, the flat hand. No rise and fall of breath. (The only camera move in the compartment until the last scene.)

[1-09] ON THE WOMAN — she feels his eyes. A glance at him, then she turns a few degrees toward the window, drawing the bundle in, making herself smaller.

[1-10] WIDE — Suresh stands and stretches, slow, angling for a look down at the baby's face. The woman rotates the bundle away from him, against her other shoulder. He sits back down.

[1-11] TIGHT ON SURESH AND RAJESH — Suresh leans across the aisle, low.

SURESH: (low) राजेश। इधर आ ज़रा।

Rajesh leans in.

SURESH: (low) उस औरत को देख। बच्चे को। इतनी गरमी है और उसने मुँह तक ढक रखा है। और एक बात बता — गाड़ी चली तब से, वो बच्चा एक बार हिला तेरे को?

[1-12] ON RAJESH — a grin, eyes already closing again.

RAJESH: सो रहा होगा भैया। छोटे बच्चे दिन भर सोते हैं। और ठंड भी जल्दी लग जाती है इनको, इसीलिए ढका होगा। तुम भी ना।

2 EXT. SMALL STATION — DAY 2

[2-01] WIDE — the train at a low platform. Vendors. Suresh comes back along it with a paper packet of snacks.

[2-02] AT THE BARRED WINDOW — Suresh holds the packet up to Kamla inside.

SURESH: ये लो। पानी भी चाहिए?

KAMLA: अरे इतना सब। राजेश को भी देना थोड़ा। नमकीन लाया कि मीठा?

[2-03] CLOSE ON SURESH AT THE BARS — as Kamla takes the packet his eyes go past her — FOCUS SHIFTS to the woman and the bundle deep in the frame.

SURESH: सब ठीक है ना बहन? बच्चा कैसा है? ... ज़रा एक बार चेहरा दिखा दो।

[2-04] INTERIOR REVERSE — CLOSE ON THE WOMAN. She draws the green cloth closed and turns the bundle in.

WOMAN: ठीक है वो। सो रहा है।

[2-05] WIDE — the guard's whistle. Suresh climbs back up, one last look along the train.

3 INT. RAILWAY COMPARTMENT — DAY 3

[3-01] ON SURESH — sitting, but turned, looking down the carriage. He can't let it go.

SURESH: मैं कह रहा हूँ कुछ गड़बड़ है। दो बार बोला चेहरा दिखा दो, नहीं दिखाया। मेरा दिल नहीं मान रहा।

[3-02] ON RAJESH.

RAJESH: भैया छोड़ो। अकेली औरत है, छोटे बच्चे के साथ जा रही है, और तुम घूर-घूर के सवाल कर रहे हो। कोई तमाशा खड़ा कर देगा।

[3-03] KAMLA LEANS IN — she has caught the tail of it.

KAMLA: क्या हुआ? तुम दोनों किसके बारे में खुसुर-पुसुर कर रहे हो?

RAJESH: भैया को लग रहा है उस औरत के बच्चे में कुछ गड़बड़ है।

[3-04] TIGHT THREE-SHOT — the huddle. Kamla drops her voice to a hush, the chatter the same, only quieter, leaning between them.

KAMLA: मुझे भी ना ठीक नहीं लगा था। मैंने खोलने को बोला तो खोला ही नहीं उसने। और बच्चा एक बार भी नहीं रोया, एक आवाज़ नहीं। और वो मक्खियाँ... राम राम।

[3-05] ON RAJESH — the grin gone.

RAJESH: तुम दोनों वहम कर रहे हो। ... पर हाँ, मक्खियाँ कुछ ज़्यादा ही हैं।

[3-06] ON SURESH.

SURESH: अगले स्टेशन पे मैं सिपाही को बता दूँगा। चेक कर लें, बस।

[3-07] ACROSS THE AISLE — CLOSE ON THE WOMAN, very still. Not looking at them. Listening to the shape of it — three heads bent together, a voice that says सिपाही. HOLD as her hand presses flat on the bundle.

4 INT. RAILWAY COMPARTMENT — DAY (NEXT STATION) 4

[4-01] WIDE — the train slows and stops. Suresh and Rajesh step down.

[4-02] CLOSE ON THE WOMAN at the glass — watching them go.

[4-03] HER POV — THROUGH THE WINDOW BARS: down the platform, Suresh stands with a CONSTABLE, talking, his hand pointing back at the carriage.

[4-04] ON THE WOMAN — she gets up, gathers the bundle close, moves — the FRAME HOLDS a beat on her steel trunk left under the seat.

[4-05] AISLE — she pushes quickly through standees, through the vestibule, into the next bogie.

[4-06] The CONSTABLE and a second one come up into the compartment behind Suresh. Kamla half rises, pointing down the car.

KAMLA: उधर गई, उस तरफ़ वाले डिब्बे में।

5 INT. RAILWAY COMPARTMENT — NEXT BOGIE — CONTINUOUS 5

[5-01] HANDHELD from here — WIDE, the crowded aisle. The constables reach the woman. She turns, the bundle high against her.

CONSTABLE: बहन जी, ज़रा रुकिए। दो मिनट बात करनी है।

[5-02] ON THE WOMAN — loud, for the whole carriage to hear.

WOMAN: क्या है? मैंने क्या किया? वो आदमी मेरे पीछे पड़ा है तब से। औरत अकेली देखी तो... मैं इसीलिए सीट छोड़ के आई।

[5-03] WIDE — the aisle court: passengers turning. Her finger goes past the constables, at Suresh, who has come up behind them.

WOMAN: गंदी नज़र से देख रहा था मुझे। इसकी घरवाली बैठी है वहीं, पूछ लो। मैं हट के बैठी फिर भी पीछे आ गया।

[5-04] CLOSE ON SURESH — the constable's glance lands on him, reassessing. His face goes hot.

SURESH: मैडम, बस बच्चा एक बार दिखा दो। बस एक बार।

WOMAN: बच्चा सो रहा है मेरा।

[5-05] ON THE CONSTABLE — easy, coaxing.

CONSTABLE: अरे जगाएँगे नहीं बहन जी। बस एक नज़र, और आप जाओ अपने रास्ते।

[5-06] STATIC — the handheld stops; the carriage holds its breath. CLOSE ON the woman, cornered. Slowly she folds the green cloth down from the top. A baby's face shows in the gap, eyes closed, still. It could be a child asleep.

[5-07] ON THE CONSTABLE — the breath goes out of him, shoulders drop.

CONSTABLE: सो ही तो रहा है। (turning on Suresh, irritated) क्यों बेकार में परेशान कर रहे हो भाई साहब? चलो।

The constables move to go.

[5-08] CLOSE ON SURESH — he does not move.

SURESH: साहब, रुको। ठीक से देखो ज़रा। पूरा खोल के।

[5-09] The constable — sharp now, a hand out to move him along.

CONSTABLE: बस करो अब! बहन जी अकेली जा रही हैं, और तुम सुबह से पीछे पड़े हो। चलो हटो यहाँ से।

[5-10] SURESH'S EYES — the woman. The bundle. The flies.

[5-11] HANDHELD — HE STEPS IN and takes hold of the child and pulls it out of her arms.

WOMAN: (screaming) नहीं! मत छू उसको! छोड़! दे दे मुझे!

[5-12] STATIC INSERT — the green cloth comes away in his hands. The face that looked asleep does not change. Below it the small body has been opened at the belly and stitched and split again at one end, and from the split, packed and taped, the opium spills out onto the berth.

    [5-12A — ALTERNATE, same slot: the spill hitting the berth and the frozen faces above it; the body held low, out of frame.]

[5-13] WIDE — for one second nobody in the carriage moves.

[5-14] HANDHELD — the woman throws herself at him, clawing for the body; the constables catch her and hold her, and she is screaming, a sound with no words left in it, reaching past all of them for what they have taken.

WOMAN: मेरा बच्चा! दे दो! मेरा बच्चा है वो!

[5-15] SLOW PULL-AWAY down the aisle — her screaming fills the carriage and goes on filling it as we go.

CUT TO BLACK.
