# MOON KING — plotline v2 (Boomer's Last Stand / rage-and-relief caper)

Supersedes v1. Lighthearted. Fixed spine + Sky King homage kept. Engine tropes kept (MutualKill softened to bloodless per the comedy mandate).

## GENRE & ENGINE
Lighthearted heist-satire. THE EMOTIONAL ENGINE = RAGE then RELIEF: the whole middle keeps enraging Americans (escalating regime outrages) until a coordinated, multi-thread "fuck you" delivers catharsis. The cookout is one payoff among several that land at once, Ocean's Eleven style — the audience knows the boomers are moon-bound, but most of the counter-op is happening behind their backs and pays off simultaneously.

## FIXED SPINE (unchanged)
1. Socialist president elected. 2. Administration announces it will remove the moon flags. 3. Americans hold the most illegal July 4 cookout on the moon, defending the flag.

## THE THREE BOOMERS (Boomer's Last Stand; sneak aboard; character-economy distinct)
- CY BURKE — retired Apollo-era pad/propulsion engineer. The planner and the heart. Sky King PERSONAL GRIEF (light touch): his kid brother burned in a pad fire in '67 and the program buried it; Cy's the one who still knows every hatch. Grief stays private, never the movie's weight.
- COL. RAE "TANK" DILLARD — 80s, a real former astronaut, the only one of them who's flown. The nerve and the credibility. Bone-dry. The obsolete pro (PoorMan'sSubstitute, inverted — he's the discount astronaut who turns out to be the only real one).
- MICKEY DOSS — ex-ironworker, runs a bar, no space cred, all mouth and guts. The comic engine, R-rated. The one who says what America's screaming at the TV.

## THE ELON LAYER (Elon-bait; bigger role, not more screen time)
- THE FOUNDER (Musk-analog) — the private space company's owner. Off to the side, memeing, "playing the fool," but he is the SECRET ARCHITECT of the entire counter-op. His bigger role = he's the mastermind of the relief; revealed late.
- SWAN GILLIS (Shivon-Zilis analog) — his CEO and the mother of his kid. She runs the company, signs the regime's contract, takes all the public heat and does every deed ON HIS BEHALF while he plays 4D chess in the background. Our inside operator.

## THE REGIME (the rage source; real villains, not strawmen)
- PRESIDENT (socialist, apology-and-reset platform).
- ADMINISTRATOR CORWIN — the flag-removal czar. True believer, smart, ruthless; thinks the flags are an obscenity. The face of every outrage.

## THE RAGE LADDER (escalating outrages; each a rung; cut against the caper's comedy)
1. New president cancels July 4 as a holiday; renames it Global Reconciliation Day.
2. Announces the six moon flags come down — "retiring symbols of conquest."
3. LOYALTY TEST (EatThisKitten): the astronaut corps must publicly denounce the flag on camera to keep their jobs. A beloved old astronaut refuses and is ruined on air.
4. The regime strong-arms the one American success story — the Founder's company — into flying the removal, threatening nationalization if they refuse. (Govt boot on the private win.)
5. GEOGRAPHY PROPAGANDA (ArtisticLicenseGeography): the regime publishes "corrected" moon maps erasing Tranquility Base, teaches that the landings were "contested." Erasing the history itself.
6. THE OH-CRAP: leak reveals the real plan — not quiet retrieval but a LIVE GLOBAL BROADCAST of the flags being destroyed, a spectacle of the new order. Peak rage.

## THE CAPER (present-tense; Prequel AVERTED — no origin flashbacks)
The three boomers, written off as senile relics, are exactly who nobody suspects. Swan quietly threads them aboard the removal mission as "ceremonial consultants"/deadweight (the Founder's plan). Comedy of three old men vs modern spaceflight, Tank's real skills, Cy's hatch knowledge, Mickey's mouth. GASLIGHTING (subverted): the regime keeps telling the public these are dangerous cranks acting alone whom nobody supports — and it will collapse.

## THE MULTI-THREAD "FUCK YOU" (Ocean's Eleven climax; RELIEF; all at once)
July 4. Everything lands in the same few minutes:
1. VISIBLE THREAD: the boomers reach Tranquility, block the removal, plant themselves, and fire up the most illegal July 4 cookout on the moon — grills, chairs, the flag standing.
2. THE SWITCH: the company fed the regime false telemetry; the regime's own "destruction broadcast" cameras were rerouted. Instead of flags burning, the whole planet is watching the cookout. (The Founder's con.)
3. THE FOUNDER'S REVEAL: the removal contract had a poison pill; the regime can't touch the company and just paid, on the record, to fly patriots to defend the flag. He engineered all of it. (Musk-analog's bigger role pays off.)
4. THE POLITICAL DOUBLE-TAKEDOWN (MutualKill, bloodless): cornered on live TV, Corwin and the President's chief of staff turn on each other and both go down in the same hour — the regime's two heads take each other out.
5. THE EARTH ANSWERS: the regime swore nobody supported them; instead millions light illegal grills and fly flags coast to coast (gaslighting subverted; Sky King collective register = optimism/relief).
6. THE SONG: "Won't Get Fooled Again" (The Who) drops as it all connects — "meet the new boss." Boomer rock, real song, straight.

## THE SKY KING PERSONAL BEAT (grief stays personal)
Inside the collective triumph, one quiet private grace: Cy sets a plate at the moon cookout for the brother the program buried in '67 — his personal grief, never spoken aloud, never bleeding into the collective optimism. Flags never blank.

## METAPHORICAL FLIP (light, never interpreted)
Everyone reads taking the flag down as growing up / moving forward. Shown not told, it flips: it's the most childish, backward thing on offer — kicking over the one sandcastle everybody helped build. The regime thinks it's the adult in the room; it's the toddler.
