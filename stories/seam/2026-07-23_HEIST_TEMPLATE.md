# THE HEIST TEMPLATE (research doc, 2026-07-23) — for the Fort Knox movie

## The two families
HEIST family beats an OBJECT (Ocean's Eleven, Rififi, The Italian Job): infiltrate, take, escape. CON family beats a PERSON via his own greed (The Sting, Hustle). Leverage = the hybrid. OUR FILM = fusion: a heist target (Fort Knox) executed by con means (the fake evacuation) — you don't crack the vault, you play the institution.

## The 12-phase composite template
1. THE HOOK — crew status quo + wound (Danny at parole; one-last-job).
2. THE IMPOSSIBILITY ARIA — the target established as unbeatable, lovingly (the Bellagio vault tour) + THE GRAVEYARD beat (Reuben's montage of prior failed attempts). Audience must believe it cannot be done.
3. THE DESERVING MARK — the moral license (Benedict is cruel; Hustle: "you can't cheat an honest man"). Audience permission to root for crime.
4. RECRUITMENT BY ROLE — Leverage taxonomy: mastermind / grifter / hacker / hitter / thief. Each intro = a mini-demo (competence porn, Rogers' coinage). Each specialist's FLAW = a complication on layaway.
5. THE BRIEFING — the plan taught with model + objections; CONTRACT: always omits >=1 step from the audience.
6. PREP + ACQUISITIONS — the pinch, the replica, the van; everything shown pays or deliberately misfires.
7. THE MARK MOVES (midpoint law, Leverage room) — the plan MUST break mid-job; the audience watches to see them IMPROVISE, not to see a plan work.
8. THE EXECUTION — time-locked, cross-cut, near-wordless.
9. THE APPARENT BUST — looks blown; some blown-ness is secretly the plan (the fake SWAT).
10. THE REVEAL UNSPOOL — flashback grammar showing withheld steps; FAIR-PLAY law: each reveal recontextualizes something already seen and misread (the fake vault floor, the switched bags).
11. THE MARK SELF-DESTRUCTS — Benedict chooses money over Tess on tape; the crew never defeats the mark, his appetite does.
12. THE FOUNTAIN — wordless fellowship coda, quiet dispersal, small personal-loop tag.

## TV weekly variant (Leverage / Hustle)
Client intake → "let's go steal a ___" → mark punches back at midpoint → flashback reveal → mark self-destructs → client tag (4-act TV rhythm).

## The long-con stage names (Hustle's classic grift stages) — MAPS ONTO OUR EVACUATION CON
Foundation (research the evacuation protocol) → Approach (disinformation: Kentucky about to fall) → Build-up (the paper chain, the signatures) → Convincer (the system's own logic confirms the threat) → Blow-off (the trucks roll) → the Fix (the vanishing).

## Laws under the template
- Competence porn balanced by the improvisation law (plans exist to break; recovery is the show).
- Concealment contract: withhold >=1 step; reveals must be fair-play.
- The deserving mark: license the crime morally (OUR problem: the "mark" is the Union treasury — license via the war's brutality, "we rob the till, not lives," soldiers-not-thieves, and the in-world Taiwan precedent citation).
- Roles = capabilities AND liabilities.
- Double clock: external deadline + internal one.
- Antagonist = the mark's security/investigator, not cops-as-heroes.
- Endings are quiet (the fountain law).

## Ocean's Eleven compact beat sheet (the primary reference)
Parole hook → Rusty pitch ("the house always wins... unless") → Reuben bankroll + the graveyard montage → crew assembly with demos → surveillance + briefing with model + objections → personal-motive reveal (Tess; "tell me this is not about her") → acquisitions (the pinch) → execution night cross-cut (blackout, Yen in the cart, Danny caught on purpose, fake SWAT) → flashback reveal (fake vault footage, bag switch) → Benedict's taped choice → fountain → tag (loop closed: parole + Tess pickup). Save the Cat classes it GOLDEN FLEECE (team + road + prize).

## THE TAIWAN PRECEDENT (verified 2026-07-23; the real-world spine for our con)
Night of Dec 1-2, 1948: soldiers seal the Shanghai Bund; porters with shoulder-poles carry crates from the Bank of China to a waiting customs/naval vessel (Hai Xing), ~90 tons / ~2M of 4.6M treasury taels, sailed to Keelung; witnessed by a British journalist from the Peace Hotel. ~4 batches, 200+ tons total, by sea AND air over ~12 months, ordered through central bank governor Yu Hung-chun on Chiang's authority — WHILE Chiang was formally stepping back from the presidency (sanction ambiguity!). The gold underwrote the New Taiwan Dollar and the breakaway state's survival. STORY-GRADE PATTERNS: mundane cover vessel; night porters; sealed street; staged multiple shipments; the witness who sees; whose-orders fog; treasury-as-survival. In-world use: a character CITES the Nationalists' gold at the briefing (our world is downstream of real history — precedent as argument).

## THE KEY MAPPING (the movie's structural gift)
Phase 11 for us: the crew never beats the Union. Once Knox's emptiness is KNOWN, THE DOLLAR ITSELF is the mark's appetite springing the trap — the Union destroyed by its own system's faith collapsing, as Benedict is destroyed by his own taped choice. The crew tells the truth loudly and lets the mark do the rest. (Also: phase 2 impossibility aria = Knox's legend, "the vault nobody ever tried"; phase 5's omitted step = the vanishing method; phase 7 mark-moves = the evacuation order issuing EARLY; phase 12 fountain = the diner coda with the men history never names.)
