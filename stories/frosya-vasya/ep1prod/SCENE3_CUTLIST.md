## THE LENGTH QUESTION

At 48s this runs twice Scene 1 and Scene 2. Nothing here is padding — it is seven real lines
and the emotional pivot of the episode — but half of it is faces talking in one room.

Handling it three ways inside the cut above:
1. **Two reaction cutaways** (shots 4 and 10) break the two 8-second speeches, so no single
   face holds for more than ~5s.
2. **Clips top and tail** the scene, so it opens and closes on movement.
3. **The held beat** (shot 8) is deliberate silence before Мама answers — it buys the moment
   its weight instead of padding.

If it still plays long, the cheapest trim is Мама's last line: «Кувшин в погребе. Бегемот
вернётся с крыши голодный, так что не тяни.» could drop to just «Кувшин в погребе» and save
~4s — but that loses Бегемот, and the hunger clock that pushes Вася. **Recommendation: shoot
it at 48s and judge it on screen.** Cutting is easy later; re-generating is not.
