# The state that decorates you demands annual proof you exist
kind: fact
date: standing practice (banked 2026-07-16)
source: Government of India pension rules — the annual life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan): every pensioner must furnish yearly proof of being alive for the pension to continue; digital submission via Aadhaar biometrics since 2014
snag: a decorated, paralyzed hero can be required, once a year, to prove to the office that he is still alive

Indian pensioners must submit a life certificate every year — historically by appearing in person before a bank or pension officer — or the pension stops. The ceremony inverts the medal: the same state that garlands a man for his service makes the continuation of his bread conditional on annual evidence of his own existence. For a paralyzed veteran, the ritual means being wheeled in so a clerk can confirm the body. Bureaucratic texture: honor as a decoration, existence as paperwork, and the burden of proof on the hero.
