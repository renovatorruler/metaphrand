# Richard Russell — the Sky King flight
kind: incident
date: 2018-08-10
source: Sea-Tac ATC recordings (public, widely archived); NTSB/FBI statements; contemporaneous reporting
snag: a man committing the most desperate act of his life stayed funny, gentle, and polite for all 73 minutes of it

A 29-year-old Horizon Air ground-service agent — baggage, tow tractors, never
a pilot — took an empty Bombardier Q400 from Sea-Tac and flew it for about 73
minutes. He performed a barrel roll a professional wasn't sure the plane could
survive. On the radio he apologized to the controller for making his night
harder, worried aloud about disappointing the people who loved him, asked
whether Alaska Airlines would give him a job if he landed it, called himself
"just a broken guy, got a few screws loose I guess. Never really knew it,
until now." He admired the Olympics at sunset mid-crisis. He never hurt
anyone but himself. The controller talked to him like a friend to the end.
