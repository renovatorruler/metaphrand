# The machine that renames you after something famous
kind: fact
date: 2026-07-15 (observed in this project)
source: firsthand — ElevenLabs v3 renders of the Brehon walkthrough
snag: a speech model pronounces a rare name right the first few times, then drifts to the famous neighbor — Brehon becomes Brecon

With no lexicon entry for a rare word, the voice model pronounces it by
spelling statistics, per occurrence — so early takes guessed right and later
takes resampled toward "Brecon" (the Welsh Beacons), especially in the
collocation "___ law." Identity decays toward celebrity under repetition.
The fix on the record: a pronunciation map recorded in the artifact, never a
silent respell.
