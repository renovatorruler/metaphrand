A senior jailer at Tihar whose corruption is unglamorous plumbing — money and favors metabolizing through him as institutional maintenance, never scored as villainy. Anti-eloquent: blunt, profane, zero pretension. Protects the decent newbie while insulting him; teaches looking-away as survival, and means it as kindness. The charm is that he never performs virtue and never lies to himself about what the place is — honesty about the dishonesty.
