# The Star Trek Into Darkness capitalization war
kind: incident
date: 2012-12-01 to 2013-01-31
source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia_Star_Trek_Into_Darkness_debate ; https://xkcd.com/1167/ ; dailydot.com coverage
snag: 40,000 words by grown adults over one capital letter, and nobody could lose, so nobody could stop

Wikipedia's style rules lowercase short prepositions — unless they open a
subtitle. The studio deliberately left the colon out of "Star Trek Into
Darkness," making the question undecidable: is "Into" a preposition or a
subtitle's first word? Editors fought from December 1, 2012 to January 31,
2013; the dispute passed 40,000 words (a short novel). xkcd mocked it while
it was still running. A month after capital-I won, Google was still serving
the lowercase version. Nobody was paid to settle it; nobody lost a thing by
refusing to quit.
