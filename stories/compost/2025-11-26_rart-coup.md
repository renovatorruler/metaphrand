# The r/Art moderator implosion
kind: incident
date: late November (per Dexerto reporting)
source: https://www.dexerto.com/entertainment/r-art-drama-explained-reddit-mod-sparks-chaos-after-banning-artist-3287798/ ; https://tribune.com.pk/story/2579599/
snag: a one-minute moderation action with no reasons on the record ended with every moderator removed — the only appeal court was the mob

A moderator banned artist Hayden Clay from one of the internet's biggest art
forums over a prints/self-promotion rule. The exchange escalated in minutes
("power hungry"); Clay was banned; users protested; a moderator locked the
subreddit and removed the other mods; the platform then removed the
moderators entirely, the remaining ones resigned, a new team reversed the
ban and other "unfair bans," and the CEO announced platform-wide limits on
how many major subreddits one person can moderate. No process existed at any
step — only force, then counter-force.
