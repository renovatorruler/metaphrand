[slowly] In December of 2012, somebody made the Wikipedia page for the next Star Trek film and typed the title: Star Trek Into Darkness. And they ran into a problem. Wikipedia's style rules say a small word like into stays lowercase, unless it opens a subtitle. The studio, on purpose, had left the colon out of the title. [pause] Capital I, or small i? Grown adults fought over that single letter for two months. The argument ran past forty thousand words, the length of a short novel. [flatly] Nobody was paid to settle it, and nobody lost a thing by refusing to quit. So nobody quit.
