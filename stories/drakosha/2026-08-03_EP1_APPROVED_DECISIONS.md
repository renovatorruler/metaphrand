## 1. Episode-level story decisions

### Frosya’s want

- The giants’ toy in the opening is objectively a **small remote-controlled car**, which can fit beneath the dresser.
- From beneath the dresser, Frosya sees only the moving car and the giant child’s feet; the remote remains out of sight. She therefore believes the car moves by itself.
- Frosya wants a **cool red car with headlights that moves by itself**. She does not know or say that the giants’ toy is remote-controlled.
- When her car is eventually realized later in the series, it should be properly sized for Frosya to sit in and drive.
- Her motivation is **not** dissatisfaction with old, repaired, found, or hand-me-down things.
- She genuinely loves the handmade/repaired gifts her parents give her.
- Remove repeated emphasis on `new`, `unused`, and `mine` as her defining motivation.
- Her dialogue should flow naturally rather than use a staccato list of short fragments.

### Episode literacy set

- The initial authorized letter pool is fixed by the viewers’ real knowledge: **А, О, М, С, К, Т, Л, Б**.
- Do not redesign the pool to solve a plot problem.
- Keep the episode words and core progression: **СОК, САЛАТ, МАК, МАМА, КОТ, ОСА, БАК, САМОКАТ, МОТОК**.
- Keep **ОСА**. Vasya lays out and names the letters `О-С-А`, then pronounces the complete familiar word naturally as `оса́`, with unstressed О sounding like А. Nobody explains or comments on this in the pilot.
- The first angry **ОСА** and the later calm, useful **ОСА** are assembled separately from different physical copies of the same letters.

### Dramatic spine

- Begin with Frosya and Vasya cooperating to return the sock.
- Let them share the spinning-top carousel happily.
- The scooter conflict grows from an ordinary failure to take turns, not from Frosya declaring that everything must be hers.
- **ОСА** is first used as a weapon and then as a tool.
- The children create the problem together, admit `Это мы`, and solve it together.
- The candle rescue remains the episode’s proof that the two gifts work best in partnership.

## 2. Magic canon for this episode

### Frosya: writing creates ЧТО

- Drawing is not required for magic. **A complete written word activates the pencil.**
- A picture alone has no magical force.
- Frosya’s domain is **ЧТО**: tangible things, substances, food, and plants—not people, creatures, events, wishes, or abstract conditions.
- The beginner pencil is forgiving. One correctly spelled, unmodified noun creates one ordinary, usable instance:
  - a countable noun produces one object;
  - food or liquid produces one serving;
  - the simplest necessary container is supplied automatically;
  - the result appears at a useful домовёнок scale.
- Therefore `СОК` produces **one** small glass/thimble serving, not two servings and not a puddle; `САЛАТ` produces one bowl; `МАК` produces one already-cut flower; `САМОКАТ` produces one usable scooter.
- The written word glows; the object materializes immediately above or beside it and settles onto the surface. Nothing sprouts out of the house itself.
- A created object becomes completely real and persistent. Erasing the word does not remove it. It can be eaten, wilt, spoil, break, wear out, or require cleanup.
- The magical pencil shortens with use.
- The pencil may understand **rough immediate placement**. This permits `БАК` to appear inverted directly above Vasya-cat for the comic drop. It does not infer unspoken descriptive qualities such as color, electric power, exact size, or toy status.

### Spelling failures

- An incomplete word does nothing.
- A nonexistent word does nothing; there is no silent autocorrection.
- If the apparent “mistake” forms a different valid word, the magic obeys that valid word rather than the child’s intention.
- No malformed or frightening hybrid object is produced merely as punishment for a spelling error.
- The same valid-word/nonword principle applies to Vasya.

### Vasya: reading transforms КТО

- Vasya’s domain is **КТО**: people, animals, creatures, and character-like beings.
- The source of the word does not matter. Tiles, a sign, a label, a word Frosya wrote, or an ordinary printed book can all work.
- Vasya must genuinely read the legible word himself; merely repeating a prompted answer does not count.
- Every letter in the word must be authorized by Mom.
- The word must name a **КТО**, not a **ЧТО**.
- Vasya must deliberately say `ВЖУХ` to transform.
- Vasya normally constructs familiar words from loose tiles in the pilot. This is supported word-building with a restricted familiar set, not implausible spontaneous spelling of unknown words.
- Only Vasya can end his transformation, by deliberately saying `ВЖУХ` again.
- The second `ВЖУХ` returns him to ordinary Vasya. Transformations do not stack and do not return him to a preceding creature form.
- Moving, scattering, erasing, or abandoning the source word does not affect his current form.
- Frosya and Mom cannot change him back for him.

### Transformation scale

- A specific-person word such as `МАМА` copies that known person’s actual size, clothes, and proportions. Vasya-mama is the same size as the real Mom.
- Generic creatures use a contextual story-serving scale selected at activation.
- The form must remain recognizable, fulfill the action, and neither become unreadably small nor destroy the set.
- The selected scale remains stable until Vasya changes back. Merely moving into another room does not resize him.
- The same noun may produce a different contextual scale in a later activation or a different environment.
- A noun with scale inherent in its meaning, such as `ВЕЛИКАН`, must still honor that meaning within safe environmental limits.
- Every form retains Vasya’s voice and enormous shaggy eyebrows.

For this episode:

- Vasya-cat is a Vasya-scale kitten: nose-to-rump length approximately Vasya’s standing height, shoulder roughly at Vasya’s knee, with a readable face and a long blocking tail.
- Vasya-wasp must be large enough to read his face, eyebrows, wings, and stripes. Relative to Frosya’s supplied character design, it is closer to her small hand than to something smaller than her fingertip.
- The tank can cover the curled cat and can still contain boy-Vasya crouched with his knees drawn up after he changes back.

### Mom’s magical restriction

- Baba Yaga gives the children the raw gifts. The alphabet chest does **not** come from Baba Yaga.
- Mom is Baba Yaga’s daughter and has quiet, exact, regulatory magic of her own.
- Mom cannot erase Baba’s gifts, but as the household’s `хозяйка` she can bind both gifts to the letter types she authorizes.
- The binding is shown visually, not treated as a mere parental claim:
  1. Mom retrieves the alphabet chest from a concealed niche.
  2. She lays Frosya’s pencil across the lid and asks Vasya to put his hand beside it.
  3. She puts her hands over theirs and states the rule.
  4. The chest clicks; the pencil and Vasya’s magic answer with the same brief glow/sound.
  5. She opens the chest and issues the known letters.
- Approved rule wording:

  > **МАМА:** Какие буквы я вам выдам, только из тех волшебные слова и получатся.

- The chest is not displayed openly beforehand. It is kept in a concealed masonry niche secured by a magical latch that responds only to Mom.
- Do not add a key, a solvable combination lock, or a puzzle-lock mechanism in the pilot.

### Letter release and physical tiles

- Mom first authorizes the eight letters Vasya already knows.
- Mom gives a new letter **before** the children study it. They learn it through repeated use in words and consequences. Once Vasya has mastered it, she may release the next one.
- Approved explanation:

  > **МАМА:** Эти восемь букв ты уже знаешь. А дальше я буду давать вам по одной новой. Выучишь её — получите следующую.

- The children use ordinary loose wooden letter tiles, with several physical copies of each authorized letter.
- The limitation is the available **letter types**, not scarcity of copies.
- Mom may present one representative of each available type while the working set/pouch contains repeated copies. Do not dramatize inventory counts.
- The tiles do not stamp, duplicate magically, split magnetically, or act as consumable fuel.
- Vasya carries the working tiles in a drawstring pouch attached at his hip to his oversized shoelace belt.
- The pouch transforms with Vasya and returns with him when he becomes a boy again.
- Loose tiles used for a word remain where he placed them until someone later collects them.

## 3. Baba Yaga and the stove route

- It is not a monumental traditional masonry `русская печь`.
- In English, the appliance may be called a **stove** or **wood-burning kitchen range**.
- In Russian screenplay geography and dialogue, use **плита / за плитой / труба** consistently with the author-supplied current title-song lyric.
- The appliance’s color was never selected. Do not call it orange merely because an earlier speech-to-text sentence was misread.

### Arrival and departure

- Keep a compact exterior glimpse of Baba Yaga approaching in **ступа, with пест and помело**.
- Treat all three objects as one immediately recognizable silhouette. Do not explain their individual folklore functions in dialogue.
- The arrival is a brief visual transition, approximately 8–10 seconds, not an extended sky journey.
- Baba Yaga, the ступа, пест, помело, and everything she carries spin and shrink together above the chimney, corkscrew down the pipe, and emerge in miniature through the rear cleanout route.
- The miniature ensemble passes from the cleanout hatch/ledge through the concealed plaster opening into the family space.
- The pipe transformation explains why Baba and her carried gifts—including the pencil and birthday candles—are correctly miniaturized for the домовята.
- The departure must use the same mechanism in reverse. Nothing is left waiting on the roof.
- The chicken-legged hut does not accompany Baba in this episode.
- Approved simple departure line:

  > **БАБУШКА-ЯГА:** Пора мне. До леса ещё лететь да лететь.

### Baba Yaga’s physical design

- She has two legs.
- One lower leg may be subtly bone-like or covered with a pale, knobby boot.
- It gives a distinctive small `цок` and perhaps a slightly stiff step.
- Do not give her an exaggerated comic limp or turn the feature into a disability joke.

## 4. Approved house geography

### The family home

- The house is an older remodeled brick house. Exterior and load-bearing walls may be masonry; selected later interior partitions may be timber-framed, covered with lath and plaster, and hollow at домовёнок scale.
- The family home occupies a **remodeled former fireplace recess behind the cast-iron плита**.
- The home is at human-floorboard level. Its wooden floorboards continue from the giant room into the concealed recess.
- The stove hides the access point; the family enters through a concealed crack/opening behind it.
- The family home, underfloor routes, and selected wall passages are physical hidden architecture, not a magical larger-on-the-inside void.

### Travel networks

- The pilot’s primary route is:

  `HOME → warped-floorboard ramp → UNDERFLOOR ROAD → vent beneath porch → OUTSIDE`

- One warped floorboard slopes down from the home into the underfloor travel network.
- The underfloor roads run between the giant floor joists.
- Their rideable surface is a smooth constructed plank road, `дощатая дорожка / настил / мостки`, not a human `длинная половица`.
- The normal outside route is a foundation ventilation opening beneath the porch.
- Selected wall passages exist inside particular remodeled lightweight partitions. Their exits are fixed and can occur behind furniture, baseboards, pipes, cornices, or stable plaster cracks.
- Not every wall is hollow; studs, fills, and repairs block many bays.
- Baba’s chimney route is exclusive to Baba. The family does not use it.
- Do not establish a general system of true underground tunnels in the pilot.

### Episode movement

- Frosya creates the scooter in the main home.
- She rides down the warped-board ramp and along the underfloor plank road.
- The cat-and-tank conflict occurs on the underfloor route.
- Vasya-wasp chases Frosya back up into the main room.
- Frosya crashes into the spool table in the main room, and the candle falls there.
- Give genuine location changes clear scene headings in the rewrite.
- The existing locations provide enough visual variety. Do not add the yard, tall grass, porch adventure, or extra rooms merely to increase the scene count.

### Candle opening and pocket

- The opening is in the wooden floor of the family’s main room and leads into a deep, sealed pocket that cannot be reached from the normal underfloor route.
- The opening is large enough for the **miniature candle** to tip and fall through and for readable Vasya-wasp to pass through carrying the thread.
- Because the wasp must be visually recognizable, the opening is not a hairline slit and is not smaller than Frosya’s fingertip.
- Frosya can insert her fingers and possibly part of her hand/arm. She fails because the candle is too far below and her head/shoulders cannot pass through.
- The obstacle is depth plus body size, not an absurd claim that Dad’s fingers might fit.
- The exact floor defect may be described as a small knot opening or similar natural flaw, but the animation must obey the character scale above.

### Daily rhythm

- The домовята are not nocturnal by rule.
- They follow an ordinary family day—awake during the day, asleep at night—while choosing safe routes and moments to avoid the giants.
- This does not need spoken exposition.

## 6. Future-series ideas — approved direction, not Episode 1 material

These are development paths the author liked or accepted. They must **not** be imported into the pilot unless a later instruction asks for them.

### Frosya’s growth

- The magical pencil visibly wears down over time.
- Baba Yaga may eventually replace it with a pen.
- The pen is more powerful but less forgiving. It understands quantities, adjectives, agreement, and longer noun phrases and interprets imprecision more literally.
- The educational progression is: letters → single nouns → quantities → adjective agreement → descriptive phrases.
- Examples discussed include `КРАСНАЯ ИГРУШЕЧНАЯ МАШИНА`, `ЭЛЕКТРИЧЕСКАЯ МАШИНА`, and `ДВА СТАКАНА СОКА`.
- A vessel-free spill can be saved as a later joke for the less-forgiving pen; it does not happen with the beginner pencil in this episode.

### Vasya’s growth

- Vasya’s parallel growth is primarily internal: reading fluency, comprehension, vocabulary, and self-control.
- Later adjective+noun and longer phrases may let him control appearance, species, and size.
- The possibility of partial transformations was discussed as a much-later development, not committed pilot canon.
