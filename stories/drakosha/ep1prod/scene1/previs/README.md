## Locked direction from review

- Preserve the generous opening of the bottom drawer from `s1_dresser_room_anchor_v1.png`.
- Preserve the very low, floor-level camera and monumental dresser perspective from v1.
- Preserve a lived-in child-room feeling, including curtains and a recognizable toy box.
- The house is an older remodeled home, not a village hut. The set may contain a cast-iron stove elsewhere while still having ordinary child-room furnishings.
- Use restrained age cues: worn floorboards, slightly irregular plaster, softened wood, and a faded woven rug.
- Screen the dresser's right-side passage from the open room with a shallow plastered wall return. Do not turn the entrance into a mousehole or small doorway.

## Non-negotiable continuity

- Do not reduce the drawer opening merely to solve scale or staging.
- Do not raise the camera.
- Neither the children nor the giant child's toy car goes beneath the dresser. The children watch from the narrow right-side passage; the car remains on the open room floor and can only nose into the passage entrance.
- Scene 1 Frosya has not received Baba Yaga's magic pencil. No pencil may appear behind her ear or elsewhere on her body. Use `../references/frosya_pregift_lowpoly.png`, not the general post-gift character sheet.
- Do not show the cast-iron stove in this room anchor unless a later shot specifically requires it.
- Do not add rendered letters or other text; lettering is reserved for VFX.
