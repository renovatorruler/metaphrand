## Global continuity

- Low-poly 3D children's animation, landscape 16:9.
- Frosya is 3.5 inches tall; Vasya is 3.15 inches tall. The 4-inch baseboard is the local scale ruler.
- Frosya has no magic pencil in Scene 1.
- The children and toy car never travel under the dresser.
- The dresser stands normally close to the wall. The narrow passage is a cinematic set view, not a five-inch furniture gap shown in a master shot.
- The sock is about 9 inches long, flexible, compressible, and light.
- No rendered letters, captions, signage, watermarks, or logos.
- No spoken dialogue in this visual pass.
- Prefer short internal cuts; do not attempt long continuous physical action.

## Setups

### S1A — room, rear corner, and sock approach

Begin with the approved low floor-level view of the dresser and open bottom drawer. Cut inside the narrow side passage, looking toward the dresser's rear. Frosya and Vasya come around the dresser's back-right wooden corner and drag the long striped sock toward the camera. Frosya leads; Vasya follows with the other end. They remain beside the dresser, never beneath it.

### S1B — safety check and hook preparation

At the room-facing end of the passage, Frosya peeks around the plaster return as in the retained reference, confirms that the room is safe, and signals Vasya forward. Protected by the drawer front, Vasya removes his long shoelace belt. Use a close insert of the old blunted fishing hook beside the lace, then cut to the hook already tied. He tests the knot with one tug.

### S1C — climb, hoist, and sock payoff

Vasya throws the hook onto the real upper edge of the open drawer's right side panel. Show the climb in separate cuts: starting, halfway, hands reaching the edge, then seated on the rim. He lowers the hook; Frosya slips it under the sock cuff. Show the sock rising in three cuts. Its heel catches, Vasya gives one final pull, and the sock flops into the drawer on top of him. After a comic pause, he emerges, lowers the hook and lace, hangs from the edge, and drops safely beside Frosya.

### S1D — reset and approaching motor

Vasya returns the hook, wraps the shoelace around his waist again, and dusts himself off. Frosya checks the room and gives a matter-of-fact shrug. They hear a toy motor. At the passage mouth, Frosya peeks above Vasya while both remain hidden between the dresser and wall.

### S1E — the car and the children's wonder

From the passage mouth, a cool red toy car with working headlights drives by itself across the open room floor. Only the feet of a giant child are visible behind it; no remote control is shown. The car does not pass beneath the dresser. Cut to the headlight beam crossing Vasya's eyes and enormous eyebrows. Cut to Frosya following the car with delighted fascination.

### S1F — near discovery and escape

The red car turns and noses into the narrow passage entrance but is too wide to enter. Its headlights illuminate the children. A giant hand descends to retrieve it; one finger presses beside the bumper and comes close to Frosya's bare toes. Frosya signals an immediate retreat. Cut behind the dresser's rear corner: Vasya runs out first, Frosya follows, and the hand cannot reach around the corner. Frosya glances back once at the two red headlights between the giant fingers before disappearing.
