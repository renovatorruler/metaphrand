# «Фрося и Вася» — Episode 1, Scene 1

> **SUPERSEDED FOR PRODUCTION.** This 13-slot proposal was reduced after review. The current 10-slot compact cut is maintained in [`CURRENT.md`](./CURRENT.md) and [`scene1.production.v1.json`](./scene1.production.v1.json). Preserve this file only as development history.

## Simplified AI-first shooting proposal

**Status:** proposal only. This does not replace the current spec or shooting script.

**Working runtime:** approximately 45 seconds. Final shot lengths follow the recorded dialogue.

**Production target:** three short motion clips; all other coverage uses approved stills, lip-sync, editorial cuts, sound, and simple compositing. No generated letters.

---

## Editorial spine

1. Frosya and Vasya cooperate to return a lost sock.
2. Their exchange establishes the rule of the house: lost things are put back.
3. They discover the red car that appears to move by itself.
4. A giant nearly discovers them, forcing them to retreat; Frosya looks back at the car.

## Image system

- **Striped sock:** their ordinary responsibility.
- **Hook and shoelace:** their resourcefulness; the mechanism is communicated through montage, not continuous choreography.
- **Red headlights:** first wonder, then danger, and finally Frosya's lingering desire.

## Proposed cut

### S1-01 · 2 sec · LOCKED STILL WITH SLOW PUSH

Low, wide view of the giants' room. The dresser dominates the frame. Its bottom drawer is open, and the narrow protected passage is visible between its right side and the plaster wall return. No characters are visible.

**Sound:** a large clock ticks; the floor creaks; faint panting comes from the passage.

> <small>Низкий общий план комнаты великанов. В кадре господствует комод. Нижний ящик приоткрыт; между правым боком комода и штукатурным выступом виден узкий защищённый проход. Детей пока не видно.</small>

### S1-02 · 4 sec · MOTION CLIP 1: ONE ACTION

Inside the side passage, Frosya and Vasya come around the dresser's rear corner and drag the long striped sock toward camera. Frosya leads; Vasya follows. They move in one direction and perform no additional business.

**VASYA:** *(panting)* А почему великаны всё время носки теряют?

> <small>**VASYA:** *(panting)* Why do the giants keep losing their socks?</small>

### S1-03 · 4.5 sec · DIALOGUE STILL + LIP-SYNC

They have stopped beside the sock near the front end of the protected passage. Frosya catches her breath, checks the room, and looks back at Vasya.

**FROSYA:** *(businesslike)* Потому что не считают. У меня всё посчитано. Доставай крючок.

> <small>**FROSYA:** *(businesslike)* Because they don't keep count. I keep track of everything. Get the hook.</small>

### S1-04 · 5 sec · FOUR-STILL MONTAGE

Four brief, locked images connected by lace sounds and Vasya's exertion:

1. Vasya's hand removes the blunted hook from the wall cache.
2. The hook is already seated on the real top edge of the drawer's right side panel; the shoelace hangs down.
3. Vasya is already sitting securely on the drawer's side rim, looking down.
4. Below him, Frosya has already slipped the lowered hook beneath the sock cuff.

Do not show the knot being tied, the hook being thrown, or the climb. The montage makes the method understandable without requiring the animation model to execute it.

**Sound:** a metal tick; lace sliding against wood; two short grunts of effort.

> <small>Четыре коротких зафиксированных положения: Вася берёт крючок из тайника; крючок уже держится на верхнем краю правой стенки ящика; Вася уже сидит на краю; Фрося уже завела спущенный крючок под резинку носка. Завязывание узла, бросок и подъём не показываются.</small>

### S1-05 · 3.5 sec · EDITED PAYOFF, NO CONTINUOUS CLOTH PHYSICS

Three quick cuts:

1. The sock is already halfway up the drawer side.
2. Almost all of it is inside, but the heel is caught on the rim; Vasya gives one final tug.
3. Cut directly to the drawer interior: Vasya is now hidden beneath the sock. The landing is sold by a soft flop and a small movement under the fabric.

**FROSYA:** *(quietly, from below)* Вась?

**VASYA:** *(muffled, deadpan)* Носок на месте.

> <small>**FROSYA:** *(quietly, from below)* Vasya?</small>

> <small>**VASYA:** *(muffled, deadpan)* The sock's back where it belongs.</small>

### S1-06 · 5 sec · TWO DIALOGUE STILLS

**A.** Cut ahead. Vasya is safely back on the floor with his shoelace belt around his waist again. He dusts himself off. The descent, hook return, and belt tying happen between cuts.

**VASYA:** *(puzzled)* А почему мы кладём на место?

> <small>**VASYA:** *(puzzled)* Why do we put things back?</small>

**B.** At the passage entrance, Frosya looks into the room and shrugs.

**FROSYA:** *(simply)* Так дом устроен.

> <small>**FROSYA:** *(simply)* That's how a house works.</small>

### S1-07 · 1.5 sec · REACTION STILL

An electric motor approaches. Frosya peeks around the plaster return; Vasya appears just below her. They remain hidden inside the passage.

**Sound:** electric whirring, little wheels, distant laughter.

> <small>Приближается звук моторчика. Фрося выглядывает из-за штукатурного выступа; Вася показывается ниже неё. Оба остаются внутри прохода.</small>

### S1-08 · 3.5 sec · MOTION CLIP 2: ONE MOVING OBJECT

From floor level, the cool red toy car drives once across the open room with its headlights on. Only the giant child's lower legs are visible behind it. The children are not included in this shot, and the remote is never shown.

> <small>С уровня пола красная игрушечная машинка один раз проезжает по открытой комнате с включёнными фарами. Позади видны только ноги ребёнка-великана. Детей в этом кадре нет; пульт не показывается.</small>

### S1-09 · 2.5 sec · VASYA DIALOGUE STILL + LIP-SYNC

Close on Vasya inside the dark passage. A headlight sweep is added as a simple lighting effect across his eyes and enormous eyebrows.

**VASYA:** *(spellbound)* Ух ты! Она сама едет!

> <small>**VASYA:** *(spellbound)* Wow! It moves all by itself!</small>

### S1-10 · 5.5 sec · FROSYA DIALOGUE STILL + LIP-SYNC

Close on Frosya, watching the car offscreen. Reflected red light and moving highlights are added in compositing; the car does not need to appear behind her.

**FROSYA:** *(whispering, delighted)* Смотри, какая классная, у неё даже фары по-настоящему светятся! Вот бы мне такую…

> <small>**FROSYA:** *(whispering, delighted)* Look how cool it is, the headlights actually light up and everything! I wish I had one like that…</small>

### S1-11 · 3 sec · TWO-STILL DANGER MONTAGE

**A.** The car is already stopped with its bumper against the passage entrance. Its headlights shine into the darkness. The children are not visible.

**B.** Cut closer. A giant shadow covers the car; a giant hand is already closing around it. Do not show the hand, car, and children interacting in the same generated image.

**GIANT CHILD'S VOICE:** *(from the room)* Ой, застряла!

> <small>**GIANT CHILD'S VOICE:** *(from the room)* Oh! It's stuck!</small>

### S1-12 · 1.3 sec · FROSYA DIALOGUE STILL + LIP-SYNC

The sudden shadow crosses Frosya's face. Her delight vanishes; she turns to Vasya.

**FROSYA:** *(sharp whisper)* Назад!

> <small>**FROSYA:** *(sharp whisper)* Back!</small>

### S1-13 · 3.2 sec · MOTION CLIP 3 + FINAL STILL

**A.** From behind the dresser's rear corner, Vasya and Frosya run toward safety and disappear around the corner. They only run; there is no hand in the shot.

**B.** A very brief final still before Frosya disappears: she looks back toward the two distant red headlights at the far end of the passage.

**Sound:** hurried feet; the motor whirs; the ticking clock recedes.

> <small>Из-за заднего угла комода видно, как Вася и Фрося бегут к укрытию и скрываются за углом. В последнем коротком кадре Фрося успевает оглянуться на две далёкие красные фары.</small>

## What this version removes

- tying the knot on screen;
- throwing and testing the hook;
- every stage of Vasya's climb;
- continuous flexible-cloth hauling;
- Vasya's complete descent;
- returning the hook and retying the belt on screen;
- a giant finger passing beside Frosya's toes;
- the car, giant hand, and both children interacting in one generated shot;
- a second background pass of the car behind Frosya.

The audience still understands every story beat through the before-and-after states, dialogue, and sound.

## Sound-off read

Two tiny siblings return an enormous sock. Their hook-and-lace method is understood through a quick montage. The sock lands on the boy, but the job is done. They discover a glowing red car and are captivated. The car reaches their hiding place; a giant shadow falls. The siblings retreat, but Frosya cannot resist one final look at the headlights.
