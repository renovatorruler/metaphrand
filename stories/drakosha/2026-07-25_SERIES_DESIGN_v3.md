# «Скажи — и стань!» — Russian letter series, design v3 (species, world, and becomings locked)

Status: v3 supersedes v2 (same day). Locked by dad on 2026-07-25: the heroes are ДОМОВЯТА drawn as чудики; the world is the HOMESTEAD; every episode carries a full becoming; Vesper's rolled-Р limitation is confirmed real and is honored, never cured. Working title stays «Скажи — и стань!» until the breakfast-table vote (ballot at the bottom).

## THE CORE FUSION (why this show is one idea, not three)

Vesper loves transformation. Vesper struggles with the suppress-then-explode cycle. Vesper is learning to read. The show makes these ONE mechanic: in this world, magic answers only words READ ALOUD off writing. Find the written word, decode it right, say it clear — and become the thing, or make it real. Reading IS the power. Clear speech IS the power. So every letter learned = more written words he can unlock = more forms he can choose = more power over his own storm. Literacy is emotional regulation is transformation. Never preached; it is simply the physics of the world.

And in this world the chant carries a second meaning for free: a домовёнок who says the word HOLDS HIS SHAPE. «Скажи — и стань» — say it, and become; say it, and STAY yourself (see РЫК below).

## THE CREATURES — ДОМОВЯТА DRAWN AS ЧУДИКИ

The heroes are домовята — little house-spirits, the keepers of the homestead. Folklore never settled what a domovoi looks like: nobody has ever properly seen one — a rustle behind the stove, a glimpse, a ball of something in the dark. The show makes that canon: NO TWO ДОМОВЯТА LOOK ALIKE. Each one is a wobbly, mismatched, scribble-edged little whatsit in the full Kakė Makė register — Vesper's shape is his own, Furia's is her own, mama has her own silhouette. The design freedom of invented monsters lives inside the folklore anchor.

ESTABLISH-FIRST RULE (hard law): the kids watching have never heard of домовята, лешие, or водяные — and the show assumes NOTHING. Episode 1 teaches the world from zero, in-world: we live behind the stove; we keep the house; no human has ever seen us properly — that is why we all look different. Every later spirit is introduced the same way, as a brand-new character with a clear job, never as a reference. The folklore is the PARENTS' recognition layer, not the children's homework.

THREE AUDIENCE LAYERS (the dual-audience design, now symmetric): the pre-reader gets the plot; the reading child gets Furia's hidden written layer; the parents get the folklore layer (mom recognizing the Домовёнок Кузя lineage and the old spirit-names). Each layer is a bonus, never a prerequisite.

## РЫК — THE WILD FORM (redesigned for the hand-drawn world)

In a world of scribble-creatures, the wild form is not a separate monster. РЫК is Vesper's OWN OUTLINE COMING UNDONE. When the feelings go unspoken — held in, held in, held in — his lines go wild: the crayon scrawls past his edges, the strokes storm, he becomes a whirl of his own unraveling drawing. Saying the word is literally what holds his shape together. The valley whispers it: «Когда Веспер молчит-молчит-молчит — приходит РЫК.» РЫК is not a villain; he is what unspoken feelings turn into. Across the season Vesper learns to send words ahead of the storm.

(This resolves the old shown-vs-whispered question: РЫК is inherently VISIBLE — it is Vesper's own drawing scribbling itself apart, shown softly and kindly. Mom's veto standing.)

## THE WORLD — THE HOMESTEAD (home base, not a cage)

The world is one big old wooden house and everything the homestead holds: stove country and the warm dark behind it, the under-floor roads, the windowsill frontier, the yard, the banya, the garden, the birch wood at the fence line, the open field, the pond. The hearth is the center, and the further from it you go, the wilder things get — a built-in GEOGRAPHY OF DISTANCE. Early missions stay near the stove; as Vesper's word-pool grows, the missions range farther out, so the season's growth is visible in geography as well as vocabulary. The finale storm happens at the farthest, most roofless point.

COUSIN-SPIRITS AS GUESTS: folklore gives every place its keeper — the дворовой (yard), банник (bathhouse), овинник (barn), полевой (field), леший (forest), водяной (pond). Available as guest characters when the geography opens to their ground, introduced establish-first like everything else. Used sparingly; the core cast stays small.

## THE MECHANIC (the world's physics, exactly)

The magic has two gates, and both are the curriculum.

Gate one, DECODE: the word must be read correctly off real writing in the world — a sign, a label, a note, a word Furia writes in the air. Misread it and the WRONG thing appears. This is where the comedy lives, and every gag is a phonics lesson in disguise: he wants САМ (myself) and reads СОМ — a giant catfish flops into the kitchen. The wasps' nest ОСЫ read as УСЫ puts mustaches on everyone. УРА shouted as УХА rains fish soup on the picnic. (Season 1 pairs are built only from taught letters; richer pairs like МИШКА/МЫШКА, КИТ/КОТ, НОС/СОН wait for their letters in season 2.)

Gate two, DELIVER: the word must be said clearly. A whined, mumbled, or swallowed word produces the droopy comic version of the thing — a wilted slide, a boat with a sag in the middle. The world never punishes; it just visibly gives you exactly as much as you gave it. Say it again like you mean it and you get the real one.

«ПЕРЕМОТКА!» — the do-over ritual. When a word comes out wrong or whined, anyone may call "Rewind!", make the little rewind gesture, and take the line again fresh. No shame, no teasing; the do-over is a rule of the world, not a concession. This is deliberately a ritual the family can import home word-for-word.

THE GRIEF GUARD (hard rule): the droopy-voice comedy applies only to whine-as-habit. Real tears, real hurt, real fear get comfort first, always, from everyone, and the magic waits. The show must never teach that crying is a malfunction.

## THE TWO HEROES (neither is ever a side character)

ВЕСПЕР is the hero, first-person — the therapy is his seat, not a mascot's. Everyday name Веся; when he transforms he announces his power-name: ВЖУХ! He is the only домовёнок who can BECOME, but only what he can read, and he is just learning letters. HARD RULE — DISPLACED TRIGGERS: his on-screen frustrations are never reenactments of real home incidents. The feelings are his; the situations are the show's (a stuck jar, a race lost to a squirrel, a tower that falls). He must recognize the fire, never the scene of the crime.

CONFIRMED LIMITATION (dad's field test, 2026-07-25): Vesper cannot roll the Russian Р. This is canon and load-bearing: episode 7 is built on it, the finale is built on it, and the show HONORS it — no magic ever fixes his tongue. Р comes last for many little домовята; it comes when it comes.

ФУРИЯ is the Scribe. She already reads and writes (like the real Furia). What she WRITES becomes real — she draws a word in the air with her claw-pen and it appears. In leader mode she announces: «Я — ФУРИЯ». Everyday name for the title vote: Фуся or Фрося (Фифи is flagged off the ballot — to Russian ears it lands as a poodle's name). The reader-writer and the becomer: she can write the word he cannot read yet; he can become the thing she cannot become. Neither completes a mission alone — the cooperation is structural, not polite.

Furia's own arc, so she is never decoration: the leader's lesson. Her brother reads slowly and her speed can silence him; she learns to make room — proudest when she helped someone else be first. And the READER'S SECRET LAYER built just for her: every episode hides written words in the world (signs, notes, jam-jar labels) that only a reading child can catch, including one secret word per episode that predicts the ending. The pre-reader gets the plot; the reader gets prophecy.

МАМА-ДОМОВУШКА is the mentor (the real mom teaches Russian; in-world the mother spirit holds the letter lessons). Warm, unhurried. When words are used properly, she ALWAYS responds — the show models that words WORK: speak early, get heard. This is the assertiveness payoff loop, and gently, a model for the grown-ups too.

## THE RECURRING FORCE — БУЯН-ВЕТЕР, THE ANTI-VOICE

The Rowdy Wind: the enemy of words in both channels. Буян cannot speak — only howl («У-у-у!») — and he SCATTERS SPOKEN WORDS (snatches the sound right out of the air mid-sentence, so a crucial word arrives at the listener in pieces) and he SCATTERS WRITTEN LETTERS (blows the sign apart, so СЛОН arrives as Н-О-Л-С and must be reassembled before it can be read). Every power the kids gain, he has the anti-power. He is not evil — he is lonely and wordless, the hero's dark mirror at full scale. And the opposition is now elemental: the hearth-keepers inside, the wind outside, rattling the windows, wanting in. He owns everything with no roof on it.

## THE CLUTCH STRUCTURE (how every episode's save works)

Mid-episode, Vesper's struggle mirrors real life: the word will not come, the whine rises, the storm rises. Then the save-the-day moment arrives — and clarity comes BECAUSE he shifts focus to who needs him. The little one is stuck; somebody is scared; Furia's hands are full. He stops hearing himself and starts seeing them, and the word comes out clean. Other-focus dissolves the whine. This is the show's deepest move and it is NEVER stated aloud — no character ever says "when you think of others, the words come." It just always happens that way.

## THE ESCALATION LAW (why season-long tension holds)

His clarity grows — and the garble grows faster. Words get longer and phonetically harder up the Zhukova ladder; Буян gets bolder in both channels; the emotional load climbs; the stakes climb; the missions range farther from the hearth. AT LEAST TWO real on-screen failures per season — episodes where the word genuinely does not come in time and the cost is real (small, kind, but real). Episode 7 is failure one by design; the second lives in episode 10. Mastery that never fails is not mastery; it is decoration.

## PEDAGOGY SPINE (real Russian primer order — Zhukova's БУКВАРЬ, the standard home bukvar; mom will recognize it, and mom's veto is standing)

Season 1 = the first ten letters: А У О М С Х Р Ш Ы Л. Cumulative by design: each episode's magic words use ONLY letters already taught. The word-pool grows: АУ → МАМА → САМ → УРА → ШУМ → МЫ → СЛОМАЛ. Vesper's readable forms grow with it — progress is visible as power. The day's letter is ALWAYS the missing key to the climax word: the one letter he could not read yesterday is the one the ending turns on today.

## SEASON 1 LADDER (letter × word(s) × feeling-skill × THE BECOMING × mission sketch)

Every episode has at least one full ВЖУХ becoming, and the becomings grow up across the season: letters → shapes → creatures → and finally a becoming that serves someone else.

1. А — «А-А-А!» — NOTICE THE STORM + the roar-release: when it rises, roar it into the sky, not at people. Voice out, fists never. THE BECOMING: at first he can only become the letters themselves — the А-shaped slide that rescues a toy from behind the stove. Tiny power, honestly tiny. (Also the establish-the-world episode: who домовята are, from zero.)
2. У — «АУ!» — ASKING FOR HELP IS BRAVE. A little one wanders to the birch fence line; АУ! read off the carved trail-mark is how you are found. THE BECOMING: the У-shaped horn that carries his call across the wood.
3. О — «О!» — WONDER: big feelings are not only anger. The round things collect (the moon, a bubble, a berry) and the sound of awe. THE BECOMING: the О-hoop/wheel that rolls the runaway pancake home.
4. М — «МАМА» «АМ» — THE COMFORT WORD. His first full decoded word — and Furia helps him WRITE it as a surprise card FOR МАМА. Made by their mother, his first read word being МАМА as a gift back to her — the meta-payoff is the point. THE BECOMING: «АМ» — the great friendly CHOMP that eats the problem.
5. С — «САМ!» — MYSELF: autonomy as the good channel of assertiveness. He wants to do it himself; the world makes room. THE BECOMING: the misread catfish returns — he reads СОМ ON PURPOSE this time, because a catfish is exactly what the flooded kitchen needs. The mistake becomes the tool. (ОСА complicates the kitchen.)
6. Х — «ХА-ХА» «УХО» — LAUGHTER AND TEASING: laughed WITH vs laughed AT (a real trigger); the tools: «Мне не смешно. Стоп.» — and УХО, the listening ear, for the other side. THE BECOMING: МУХА — a fly, tiny enough for the keyhole.
7. Р — «Р-Р-Р» «УРА!» — THE HONEST FAILURE (failure one of two). The growl-letter — the storm's own letter — is the one his tongue cannot do. The becoming FAILS ON SCREEN: the Р won't roll, the word won't take. The day is saved with an OLD word — proof that what you have already learned does not abandon you — and mama names the truth without fixing it: Р comes last for many little домовята; it comes when it comes. (The УРА/УХА soup gag rides along.) Plants the finale.
8. Ш — «Ш-Ш-Ш» «ШУМ» — THE CALM BREATH. Too much noise, too many guests, overwhelm — and Ш itself is the tool. THE BECOMING: ШАР — the long quiet sh-sh-sh breath literally inflates him into the balloon that rises above the ШУМ. The letter IS the technique.
9. Ы — «МЫ» — WE: you are not alone with big feelings. The team word unlocked. THE BECOMING: СЫР for the picnic (with the ОСЫ/УСЫ mustache gag en route). Plants the finale's МЫ.
10. Л — «СЛОМАЛ» «МЫЛО» — THE REPAIR EPISODE (the season's heart; failure two): the storm slips out and something a friend loves gets broken. The walk back: say what happened («Я сломал»), help fix it, wash up (МЫЛО), the friendship survives the storm. THE BECOMING: СМОЛА — he becomes the resin-glue that mends what he broke. The repair made literal. Anger does not make him bad; repair is what strong домовята do.

## THE FINALE (the storm at maximum, at the most roofless point)

Буян at his loudest and loneliest, out in the open field — both channels of garble at full strength, letters flying, sounds shredded. The climax word is the season's longest and hardest, and Vesper's letters are NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT: he physically cannot deliver it alone — the rolled Р sits in the middle of it. He and Furia say it TOGETHER — she rolls the Р he cannot, he carries the rest (МЫ made literal, not stated). The word lands, the wind gets his first word — «МЫ» — and the howl that could only spin alone stops spinning alone. Vesper's own mastery arrives quietly in the tag, offhand, no fanfare: he reads something small without noticing he did it. His Р is NOT fixed — that is the point. Finale-word candidate from taught letters: «ХОРОШО» (Х-О-Р-О-Ш-О — the growl-letter right in the middle, and it is what you say when the storm ends); final choice with mom, constrained to taught letters.

## EPISODE TEMPLATE (~9–11 min, mission rhythm)

Cold open whimsy (60–90s, Kakė Makė register: a kid-scale absurdity) → the mission lands → today's letter discovered IN the world (its shape hiding in the homestead) → мама-домовушка's short diegetic letter beat (sound, shape, air-writing; call-and-response) → first attempt fails: a misread gag or a whine-droop, the storm rises (shown kindly; «Перемотка!» available) → mid-episode mirror struggle → the clutch: someone needs him, the word comes clean → «Скажи — и стань!» becoming (ВЖУХ!) → mission solved with Furia's written word + Vesper's becoming → tag: Furia's secret word pays off + a goodnight whimsy button.

Chant: «Скажи — и стань!». Feelings mantra (tiny, used sparingly): «Заметь. Назови. Скажи.» (Notice it. Name it. Say it.)

## STYLE (the Kakė Makė mandate)

Hand-drawn 2D whimsy: wobbly ink lines, crayon and watercolor texture, collage bits, homely details drawn with love (socks, jam jars, ladders), kid-logic physics. The creatures are scribble-edged and mismatched by canon. Cozy domestic-fantastic scale: missions are the size of a lost boot, a ruined pancake tower, a twisted bridge. Deliberately NOT the Hindi show's 3D papercraft — two shows, two crafts, no doubled effort.

## DIFFERENTIATION FROM THE HINDI SHOW (so the tracks never blur)

- Creatures: papercraft dragons vs scribble-drawn house-spirits.
- Mechanic: restore the stolen letter (कुकु) vs unlock magic by READING ALOUD (this).
- Threat: an eraser-thief who redeems by MAKING his first letter vs a wordless wind who redeems by SAYING his first word — owning vs belonging.
- Hero: mascot hero (Kuku) + kids as team vs VESPER HIMSELF as the transformer — the therapy is first-person.
- Pedagogy: letter-of-the-day + shape-becomes-solution vs the cumulative Zhukova word-building ladder + decode/deliver gates.
- Emotional core: kindness/नीति strategy vs self-regulation + assertiveness.
- Look: 3D papercraft valley vs Kakė Makė hand-drawn homestead.

## THE ASSERTIVENESS CURRICULUM (the quiet spine; never a lecture on screen)

The cycle to break: suppress → suppress → explode. The show's answer, in order, across the season: (1) the storm is REAL and honored — it gets its own letter, its own roar-space; never "don't be angry." (2) Voice beats fists — the roar-to-sky, the growl-letter. (3) Words beat the storm — small words said early («Я злюсь», «Стоп», «САМ», «Помоги») get results on screen every single time, and saying the word is literally what holds your shape. (4) Calm is a skill with a letter of its own (Ш). (5) A clean do-over is always available and costs nothing («Перемотка!»). (6) Repair, not shame, after the slip (СЛОМАЛ). (7) You are not alone (МЫ). Adults in-show always reward the early small word — the world proves that speaking early works better than exploding late.
