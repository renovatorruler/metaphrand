# «Скажи — и стань!» — Russian letter series, design v4 (built on his real map)

Status: v4 supersedes v3. The author of this show is the kids' MOTHER — she teaches them Russian herself; every call marked "author's" is hers, made directly. v4 folds in the placement-test results (both rounds, 2026-07-26), the author's three new laws (easy wins, vocabulary, male speech model), and the Бобуквы pairing with her physical book set. The season now teaches HIS actual frontier, not a textbook's page one.

AGE CALIBRATION (author's correction): Vesper is almost five and a half. The reading curriculum sits at his real decode level, but the STORY pitches at 5.5–6: multi-step plots, real suspense, dramatic irony, jokes with a twist — never toddler beats. Benchmark plot complexity against shows for 5–7 (Octonauts-grade missions). If a mission would bore him, the mission is wrong, not the letter.

## THE REAL MAP (placement tests, 2026-07-26)

KNOWS the Russian sound (8): А С К Т М О Л Б. Combined with his confirmed blending ability, this is a working word-pool from day one: КОТ, СОМ, МОСТ, СТОЛ, СОК, ТАМ, КТО, МАМА, САМОКАТ are decodable today.

FALSE FRIENDS (gives the English/Spanish sound): У Р Н Х — his English-reading eye sees Y, P, H, X. Plus В, which wobbles toward B. These letters are not unknown; they LIE to him.

UNKNOWN: Ы (guessed as English P — pattern-hunting with nothing to grab), Ш (seen, forgotten). Round 2 (partial): Д and П both mistaken for Л; Ф known 100% because it is Furia's initial; the rest untested-or-unknown and treated as unknown.

THREE INTERFERENCE TYPES the show must handle differently: (1) Latin false friends — the letter with an overseas twin who looks identical and speaks differently; (2) Cyrillic shape-family confusion — Л/Д/П are all two-legged gate shapes and Л (known) swallows the other two; lookalikes are NEVER taught in adjacent episodes, and each gets its own strongly distinct character; (3) plain unknowns — clean mnemonic episodes.

THE Ф PROOF: nobody drilled him on Ф. It stuck because it is his sister's letter. Letters stick through belonging — this is the show's mnemonic engine, demonstrated in the wild before we built it.

## THE LAWS (author's, all hard)

VOCABULARY LAW: both kids have weak Russian vocabulary — neither knows what a СОМ is. This is therefore a VOCABULARY show delivered through reading. Never assume any Russian word is known, however basic. Every load-bearing word is taught by the story itself — and the mechanic is the teacher: read the word, the thing appears. The magic is a living picture-dictionary. Misread gags teach two words for one laugh (ОСЫ and УСЫ in the same scene). Unknown words are material, not obstacles — but never load-bearing before they are taught.

MALE SPEECH MODEL LAW: Vesper self-refers in the feminine («Я сделала») because every Russian voice in his life is female. Вася in-show is the missing Russian BOY: his dialogue is a deliberate, relentless stream of correct masculine forms — «Я сделал», «Я сам», «Я прочитал» — never a lesson, just the water he swims in. The «Перемотка!» ritual quietly absorbs an occasional gender-form do-over: someone calls rewind, the line comes again in the right form, no shame, no lecture. Падежи: season 1 gives ambient case-rich natural dialogue only, no teaching. PARKED for a later season: Буян blows the ENDINGS off words — a падежи mechanic already living inside our villain.

LETTER-OWNER LAW: every letter gets an owner where possible — a person or creature the kids love. Canon so far: В = Вася's own letter (ВЖУХ); Ф = Фурия's (season 2, arrives as a celebration — he already owns it); М = мама's (known, and no wonder); П = папа's (ПАПА, and the ворота he keeps mending — П IS the gate shape); Б = Буян's; Р = РЫК's (the storm's own letter, the one his tongue cannot do); Д = ДОМ, the house itself, in a show about house-spirits.

EASY-WIN LAW: known letters may still headline episodes — an easy win is an easy win — with one retirement: А, too rudimentary. In this season the win-rhythm is carried by episode 1 (a pure victory lap over all eight known letters) and by the Бобуквы pairing: every episode unlocks the next numbered book(s) as an immediate, private win at home.

NATIVE-RUSSIAN LAW: every word the show teaches or hands the kids as a tool must be native Russian — no anglicisms, even naturalized ones («стоп» banned; the boundary-word is «Хватит!», siblings «Не надо!», «Перестань!»).

## THE CORE FUSION (why this show is one idea, not four)

Vesper loves transformation. Vesper struggles with the suppress-then-explode cycle. Vesper decodes letters but the words themselves are foreign to him. The show makes these ONE mechanic: magic answers only words READ ALOUD off writing. Find the written word, decode it right, say it clear — and become the thing, or make it real. Reading is the power; clear speech is the power; and every word unlocked is a word OWNED — the thing appears, so the meaning lands with the laugh. Literacy is vocabulary is emotional regulation is transformation. Never preached; it is the physics of the world.

And the chant carries its second meaning: a домовёнок who says the word HOLDS HIS SHAPE. «Скажи — и стань» — say it, and become; say it, and STAY yourself.

## THE CREATURES — ДОМОВЯТА DRAWN AS ЧУДИКИ

The heroes are домовята — little house-spirits, keepers of the homestead. Folklore never settled what a domovoi looks like, so the show makes it canon: NO TWO ДОМОВЯТА LOOK ALIKE. Each is a wobbly, mismatched, scribble-edged little whatsit in the full Kakė Makė register.

ESTABLISH-FIRST RULE (hard law): the kids have never heard of домовята, лешие, or водяные — the show assumes NOTHING. Episode 1 teaches the world from zero, in-world: we live behind the stove; we keep the house; no human has ever seen us properly — that is why we all look different. Every later spirit is introduced the same way. The folklore is the PARENTS' recognition layer, never the children's homework.

THREE AUDIENCE LAYERS: the pre-reader gets the plot; the reading child gets Furia's hidden written layer; the parents get the folklore layer (the Домовёнок Кузя lineage, the old spirit-names). Each layer is a bonus, never a prerequisite.

## РЫК — THE WILD FORM

РЫК is Vesper's OWN OUTLINE COMING UNDONE. When feelings go unspoken — held in, held in, held in — his lines go wild: the crayon scrawls past his edges and he becomes a whirl of his own unraveling drawing. Saying the word is literally what holds his shape together. «Когда Веспер молчит-молчит-молчит — приходит РЫК.» РЫК is not a villain; he is what unspoken feelings turn into. Shown softly and kindly (author's veto standing). And Р — the letter his tongue cannot roll — is РЫК's own initial: the storm's letter is the hard one, and the show honors that rather than hiding it.

## THE WORLD — THE HOMESTEAD

One big old wooden house and everything the homestead holds: stove country and the warm dark behind it, the under-floor roads, the windowsill frontier, the yard, the banya, the garden, the birch wood at the fence line, the open field, the pond. The hearth is the center; the further out, the wilder — a geography of distance that makes the season's growth visible in space as well as vocabulary. The finale storm happens at the farthest, most roofless point. Cousin-spirits (дворовой, банник, леший, водяной) are available as establish-first guests when the geography opens; the core cast stays small.

## THE MECHANIC (the world's physics, exactly)

TWO MODES, AND THE SWITCH IS ВЖУХ (author-prompted codification, 2026-07-26): (1) a written word READ ALOUD becomes REAL in the world — the summoning mode, the living picture-dictionary (abstract words like САМ or МЫ have nothing to summon and safely fizzle, which makes them the "safe practice words"); (2) read aloud PLUS your power-cry — «ВЖУХ!» — aims the magic at yourself: you BECOME the word. Only Вася has a power-name, which is exactly why he is the only домовёнок who can become. Фрося's Scribe power is the mirror: what she WRITES becomes real. «Скажи — и стань!» is the family motto describing the whole magic — never a trigger. HELD IN RESERVE (do not spend early): a misread WITH ВЖУХ = becoming the wrong creature — a future episode engine (МИШКА/МЫШКА, season 2).

Gate one, DECODE: the word must be read correctly off real writing — a sign, a label, a note, a word Furia writes in the air. Misread it and the WRONG thing appears; every gag is a phonics-plus-vocabulary lesson in disguise (САМ read as СОМ floods the kitchen with catfish — and now both kids know what a сом is, forever, because it was funny).

Gate two, DELIVER: the word must be said clearly. A whined, mumbled, or swallowed word produces the droopy comic version of the thing. The world never punishes; it visibly gives exactly as much as it was given. Say it like you mean it and get the real one.

«ПЕРЕМОТКА!» — the do-over ritual. When a word comes out wrong, whined, or in the wrong form, anyone may call "Rewind!", make the little gesture, and take the line again fresh. No shame; a rule of the world. Deliberately importable to the family table word-for-word — including for «Я сделала» → «Я сделал».

THE GRIEF GUARD (hard rule): droopy-voice comedy applies only to whine-as-habit. Real tears, real hurt, real fear get comfort first, always, and the magic waits. The show never teaches that crying is a malfunction.

FALSE-FRIEND BEAT TYPE: for У Н Р Х В, the episode's engine is unmasking — the letter has an overseas twin who looks identical and speaks differently, and the plot turns on which one you are facing. The fix always anchors in a native sound he already owns (У is the sound inside his own «АУ!»; Р is the growl). This lands hardest for the one child in the audience whose English eye is being lied to — which is the point.

## THE TWO HEROES

ВЕСПЕР is the hero, first-person. Everyday name Вася; power-name announced at transformation: ВЖУХ! He can BECOME, but only what he can read. HARD RULE — DISPLACED TRIGGERS: his on-screen frustrations are never reenactments of real home incidents. The feelings are his; the situations are the show's. CONFIRMED LIMITATION (author's field test): he cannot roll the Russian Р — canon, load-bearing, honored, never magically cured. And per the male-speech-model law, his dialogue is the correct masculine Russian the household cannot otherwise model for him.

ФУРИЯ is the Scribe. What she WRITES becomes real. Leader mode: «Я — ФУРИЯ». Her arc: the leader's lesson — her speed can silence her slower brother; she learns to make room. Her READER'S SECRET LAYER: every episode hides written words only a reading child can catch, including one secret word that predicts the ending. The pre-reader gets the plot; the reader gets prophecy.

МАМА-ДОМОВУШКА is the mentor (the author teaches the kids Russian in real life; in-world the mother spirit holds the letter lessons). Warm, unhurried. When words are used properly, she ALWAYS responds — words WORK: speak early, get heard. She is NEVER shown alone: РУСЯ and МУСЯ, the two littlest домовята (about one and a half; the real Castor and Leda, renamed as a matched nursery pair — with them the whole family rhymes: Фрося, Вася, Руся, Муся; Руся the boy, Муся the girl), are permanently attached — one on the hip, one on the back, nursing, dozing, grabbing. They are pre-verbal texture, not letter-prodigies (that bit belongs to the Hindi track — differentiation holds): their whole comedy is silent, relentless clinging.

ПАПА-ДОМОВОЙ is always THERE (the real father is constantly present — he simply lives in the other language: he teaches the kids Hindi and reads only a little Russian). In-show: big, broad, sawdust in his fur, from a far-off house where they speak differently — he speaks Russian rarely and carefully, "like carrying a full cup," simple short phrases with a foreign lilt. He is the homestead's hands: gates, roofs, planks. He understands far more than he says. The show must NEVER read as a fatherless family; he appears warm and wordless-ish, and his few words land heavy. THE ПАПА EPISODE is Ep7 (П — his letter, see the ladder): Вася reads Russian FOR папа — the kid gets to be the expert, autonomy in service of someone he loves.

БЕГЕМОТ is the humans' cat — huge, coal-black, canon-named after the family's real cat (Biggie Mott → Бегемот). For the kids the name is a joke (a cat called Hippo); for the parents it is the deepest literary wink the show will ever make (Bulgakov's black cat — «Мастер и Маргарита», with Krylov's «А Васька слушает да ест» hovering nearby as a bonus echo). The old pact holds: cat and домовята keep the same house. Ally, negotiator, mercenary — his price is молоко and ear-scratches, always за два уха.

## THE RECURRING FORCE — БУЯН-ВЕТЕР, THE ANTI-VOICE

Буян cannot speak — only howl — and he is the enemy of words in both channels: he SCATTERS SPOKEN WORDS (snatches sound out of the air mid-sentence) and SCATTERS WRITTEN LETTERS (blows the sign apart; СЛОН arrives as Н-О-Л-С and must be reassembled before it can be read). Not evil — lonely and wordless, the hero's dark mirror at full scale. The hearth-keepers inside; the wind outside, rattling the windows, wanting in. He owns everything with no roof on it.

## THE CLUTCH STRUCTURE

Mid-episode, the struggle mirrors real life: the word will not come, the whine rises, the storm rises. The save arrives because he shifts focus to who needs him — the little one is stuck, somebody is scared, Furia's hands are full — and the word comes out clean. Other-focus dissolves the whine. NEVER stated aloud; it just always happens that way.

## THE ESCALATION LAW

His clarity grows — and the garble grows faster: harder words, bolder Буян, heavier feelings, missions farther from the hearth. TWO real on-screen failures this season, by design: episode 4 (Ы — the alien letter, a small real cost) and episode 9 (Р — the big one). Mastery that never fails is decoration.

## PEDAGOGY — THE БОБУКВЫ PAIRING (author owns the physical set)

The author's Бобуквы book order (numbered, verbatim): А О Б В М Н Л Ы Т Д К У С П З Р Й Э Х Г Ж Е И Я Ё Ц Ф Ш Ю Щ Ч Ь Ъ.

His eight known letters are books 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 — and his ENTIRE tested frontier sits inside books 4–16. So season 1 walks the books' own order through his frontier: В(4) → Н(6) → Ы(8) → Д(10) → У(12) → П(14) → З(15) → Р(16). THE CONSOLIDATION LOOP: after every episode, the next numbered book(s) become readable — the show plants the mnemonic, the book gives the practice, and his known-letter books (1–13's odd shelf) are his anytime victory laps. His documented weakness is retention; this loop is aimed at exactly that.

Every episode's magic words use ONLY letters he owns at that point (the eight + everything taught so far). The day's letter is ALWAYS the missing key to the climax word.

## SEASON 1 LADDER (10 episodes: letter × unlocked words × feeling-skill × THE BECOMING × mission sketch)

1. THE STARTING KIT (no new letter) — establish the world from zero AND bank his eight letters as powers he arrives with: the show mirrors reality, he is already armed. First becoming: КОТ — a real creature on day one. The САМ/СОМ misread gag runs here (both legal, both hilarious, both words taught — the catfish in the kitchen). Feeling-skill: notice the storm; roar it into the sky, not at people. Unlocks books 1–3 + the known shelf.
2. В (book 4) — THE CLAIM-YOUR-LETTER EPISODE. В is Вася's own initial and his wobbliest letter — the one his English eye bends toward B. He unmasks the twin and claims his letter: ВЖУХ is written with it. Words: ВОЛК, СОВА, ВАТА, СЛОВО. THE BECOMING: ВОЛК — the wolf, earned by reading his own letter right. Feeling-skill: my name, my letter, my voice — САМ energy pointed at identity.
3. Н (book 6) — false-friend rehab (looks like H, says n). Words: СЛОН, НОС, СОН, ОКНО, НОТА. The first minimal-pair gag lands: НОС/СОН — read it backwards and the nose becomes a nap. THE BECOMING: СЛОН — an elephant in the under-floor roads is a plumbing-scale crisis and a giant win. Feeling-skill: asking for help is brave.
4. Ы (book 8) — REAL FAILURE #1. The alien letter: no English cousin, nothing to pattern-match (he guessed it as P — the show gives Ы an unforgettable character instead of a guess). Words: МЫ, СЫН, БЫК, МЫЛО. The word does not come in time; something small and real is lost; kindness follows; the letter sticks BECAUSE it cost something. THE BECOMING: БЫК — the bull, once the letter finally lands. Plants МЫ for the finale. Feeling-skill: losing without exploding.
5. Д (book 10) — SHAPE-FAMILY UNTANGLE #1 (he reads Д as Л). Д gets the strongest character in the show: it is the house's own letter — ДОМ — in a show about house-spirits. Words: ДОМ, ДЫМ, ВОДА, ДЫРА is near (needs Р — waits). THE BECOMING: ДОМ — he becomes shelter, the roof over the little ones when the rain hits the yard. Feeling-skill: strong is what protects.
6. У (book 12) — false-friend rehab (looks like Y, says oo), anchored in a sound he has owned forever: his own «АУ!». Words: УТКА, СТУК, КУСТ, УКУС, ЛУНА (legal at last). THE BECOMING: УТКА — the duck that ferries the little ones across the pond. The СТУК episode texture: something knocking at the fence line that nobody can see — suspense, resolved warm. Feeling-skill: call out early; АУ is how you are found.
7. П (book 14) — THE ПАПА EPISODE + shape-family untangle #2. П is the gate — ворота — the shape you walk through, nothing like Л's tent or Д's house, and it is папа's letter: he has been mending the ворота since episode 1 (planted in the pilot). The mission: the gate job goes wrong and the fix hides in WRITING — the lumber is labeled (ДУБ, СОСНА, ДОСКА — all Вася-legal by now) and папа's Russian reading is small, so ВАСЯ READS FOR ПАПА. The kid is the expert; the dad receives. Words: ПАПА, СУП, ЛАПА, ПОЛ, ДУБ, СОСНА. THE BECOMING: the gate itself — П — holding the gap while the real gate is mended. Feeling-skill: autonomy in service — «Я сам прочитал. Для папы.» (the masculine forms load-bearing and modeled).
8. З (book 15) — clean new letter. Words: ЗУБ, КОЗА, ЗАМОК, ЗВУК — the word for sound itself, in a show about saying. THE BECOMING: the ЗАМОК — castle-lock double meaning played for the geography's farthest indoor adventure yet. Feeling-skill: some doors need the right word, not a harder push.
9. Р (book 16) — REAL FAILURE #2, THE HONEST ONE. The growl-letter, РЫК's own initial — false friend to his eye (P) AND impossible for his tongue. The becoming fails ON SCREEN: the Р will not roll, the word will not take. The day is saved with an OLD word — what you have already learned does not abandon you — and mama names the truth without fixing it: Р comes last for many little домовята; it comes when it comes. Words orbiting the episode: РАК, НОРА, УРА, РОТ, ТРАКТОР (fully legal — and the season's biggest becoming lives here as the post-failure gift: the tractor, seven letters, every one of them his). Plants the finale.
10. FINALE — THE STORM AT THE MOST ROOFLESS POINT. Буян at his loudest and loneliest in the open field, both channels of garble at full strength. The climax word contains the rolled Р he cannot make — he and Furia say it TOGETHER, she rolls the Р, he carries the rest. МЫ made literal, never stated. The wind gets his first word — «МЫ» — and stops spinning alone. Vesper's mastery arrives quietly in the tag: he reads something small without noticing. His Р is NOT fixed — that is the point. FINALE WORD (author's call): «БРАТ» recommended — a word the kids live, the Р dead center, and the team makes the wind family; alternates from the season's sixteen letters: «ДОБРО», «ПОДАРОК». (Note: «ХОРОШО» retired — Х and Ш are season 2 letters now.)

SEASON 2 SEED (books 17+): Й Э Х Г Ж Е И Я Ё Ц Ф Ш Ю Щ Ч Ь Ъ — И unlocks the great minimal-pair comedy (МИШКА/МЫШКА, КИТ/КОТ); Ш and Х arrive with their false-friend/forgotten histories; Ф arrives as Фурия's celebration episode (the one letter he never needed teaching); Е/Ё/Э and Я close the Latin-mirror traps.

## EPISODE TEMPLATE (~9–11 min, mission rhythm)

Cold open whimsy (60–90s, kid-scale absurdity) → the mission lands → today's letter discovered IN the world (its shape hiding in the homestead) → мама-домовушка's short diegetic letter beat (sound, shape, air-writing; call-and-response) → first attempt fails: a misread gag, a whine-droop, or the letter's lie unmasked too late; the storm rises (shown kindly; «Перемотка!» available) → mid-episode mirror struggle → the clutch: someone needs him, the word comes clean → «Скажи — и стань!» becoming (ВЖУХ!) → mission solved with Furia's written word + Vesper's becoming → tag: Furia's secret word pays off + the next Бобуквы book handed over in-world or in the closing card + a goodnight whimsy button.

Chant: «Скажи — и стань!». Feelings mantra (tiny, sparing): «Заметь. Назови. Скажи.»

## STYLE (the Kakė Makė mandate)

Hand-drawn 2D whimsy: wobbly ink lines, crayon and watercolor texture, collage bits, homely details drawn with love, kid-logic physics. Scribble-edged mismatched creatures by canon. Cozy domestic-fantastic scale. Deliberately NOT the Hindi show's 3D papercraft.

## DIFFERENTIATION FROM THE HINDI SHOW

- Creatures: papercraft dragons vs scribble-drawn house-spirits.
- Mechanic: restore the stolen letter vs unlock magic by READING ALOUD.
- Threat: an eraser-thief redeemed by MAKING his first letter vs a wordless wind redeemed by SAYING his first word.
- Hero: mascot + team vs VESPER first-person.
- Pedagogy: letter-of-the-day + shape-becomes-solution vs his real frontier + Бобуквы pairing + decode/deliver gates + vocabulary-through-appearance.
- Emotional core: kindness/नीति strategy vs self-regulation + assertiveness + the missing male speech model.
- Look: 3D papercraft valley vs Kakė Makė hand-drawn homestead.

## THE ASSERTIVENESS CURRICULUM (the quiet spine; never a lecture)

(1) The storm is REAL and honored — its own letter, its own roar-space; never "don't be angry." (2) Voice beats fists. (3) Words beat the storm — small words said early («Я злюсь», «Хватит», «САМ», «Помоги») get results on screen every single time, and saying the word literally holds your shape. (4) Calm is a skill (the Ш breath waits in season 2; season 1 uses the roar-to-sky and the long exhale unnamed). (5) A clean do-over always available, costs nothing («Перемотка!»). (6) Repair, not shame, after the slip. (7) You are not alone (МЫ). Adults always reward the early small word.
