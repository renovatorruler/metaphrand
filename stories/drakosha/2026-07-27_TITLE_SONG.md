# «Фрося и Вася» — заглавная песня (v3 — про них, а не про урок)

Form: series title track, ~1:15 full version, sung by Фрося and Вася trading lines.

TWO LAWS THIS VERSION FIXES (author's notes, 2026-07-27):

NOT PREACHY. v2 chanted the show's emotional curriculum out loud («Заметь. Назови. Скажи.») and promised the child he could become whatever he wanted. That is a lesson set to music. Cut entirely. A title song's job is to make you love the characters, not to teach you anything; the teaching happens inside the episodes, in the world's mechanics, where it belongs.

THE SHOW IS CALLED «ФРОСЯ И ВАСЯ», NOT «СКАЖИ — И СТАНЬ». So the hook is their NAMES, the verses are their PERSONALITIES, and the bridge is them squabbling. «Скажи — и стань!» stays the in-show chant; it is not what the theme is about.

EVERGREEN LAW (kept from v2): plays before every episode for years, so no named letters, no named magic words, no counts that grow, no plot, no Буян, no РЫК.

## SUNO — STYLE PROMPT (paste into Style of Music)

Joyful Russian children's television theme song, bright and cozy, 100 BPM, major key, 2/4 skipping rhythm. Two young voices trading lines back and forth — a quick bossy girl and a slower dreamy boy — with a small children's chorus chanting the two names in the hook and shouting the word VZHUKH with delight. One short comic argument section where the two voices interrupt each other and then agree. Homemade acoustic band as if played on things found around a house: button accordion, plucked balalaika, toy piano, glockenspiel, wooden spoons, thimble taps, a jar of buttons shaken as a shaker, hand claps, brushed snare, upright bass, penny whistle. Gentle Slavic folk flavour, nursery-rhyme melody, extremely singable, big name-chanting sing-along chorus. Starts with an intimate hush, almost whispered, then bursts into full band. Clean warm analog recording, close vocals, playful and mischievous, storybook charm.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (not for the song page)

- The hook is the show's name, chanted. Children chant names — it is how they claim a show as theirs.
- The two character verses do the work the lesson used to do, sideways: Фрося writes everything and has the answer in her pocket; Вася is slow, and what he summons is often not what anyone ordered — but it comes anyway. The misread gag is stated as a permanent feature of him rather than a plot point, which is both evergreen and funny.
- THE BRIDGE IS THE WHOLE SERIES IN SIX LINES: «Я первая!» versus «А я сам!» — the two hungers, planted in the theme song so both catchphrases land before every single episode, ending in «Ладно. Вместе?» «Вместе!» That is the sibling arc in miniature, played for laughs and never explained.
- Nothing in this song instructs the listener. The only imperative is «Тише», which is an invitation, not a lesson.
- The whispered «Тише… тише… кто там? — Мы!» opening is the show's signature sound: it makes the child go quiet and lean in, and it establishes creatures nobody has ever properly seen without a word of exposition.
- The instrument list is a design decision: the score is played on the same found household objects the characters live among, so the music belongs to the world.
- If generating several takes, keep the one whose children's voices sound like real children rather than processed ones, and whose argument section actually sounds like two siblings interrupting each other.
- BANNED from this song forever: named letters, named magic words, counts, Буян, РЫК, plot beats, and any line that tells the listener what to do or who to be.
