# «Фрося и Вася» — заглавная песня «Скажи — и стань!» (v2 — evergreen)

Form: series title track, ~1:15 full version. Sung BY Фрося and Вася AT the listener — the song wants something from the child (hush, come closer, now say it with us), per the lyrics-must-act law.

EVERGREEN LAW (v2 fix, author's note): the title track plays before EVERY episode for years, so it must contain no volatile facts — no named letters, no named magic words, no counts that grow, nothing that reveals what happens in any episode. It states only what is permanently true: who they are, where they live, what the magic is, and the invitation. v1 named specific letters and words and was rewritten.

Vocabulary deliberately tiny and repeatable. Вася's lines carry correct masculine forms. Native Russian only, no anglicisms.

## SUNO — STYLE PROMPT (paste into Style of Music)

Joyful Russian children's television theme song, bright and cozy, 100 BPM, major key, 2/4 skipping rhythm. Warm female lead voice with a small children's chorus answering her, the kids shouting the word VZHUKH with delight. Homemade acoustic band as if played on things found around a house: button accordion, plucked balalaika, toy piano, glockenspiel, wooden spoons, thimble taps, a jar of buttons shaken as a shaker, hand claps, brushed snare, upright bass, penny whistle. Gentle Slavic folk flavour, nursery-rhyme melody, extremely singable, big sing-along chorus with a call-and-response section where children echo each line. Starts with an intimate hush, almost whispered, then bursts into full band. Clean warm analog recording, close vocals, cozy and intimate, storybook charm.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (not for the song page)

- The hook teaches the show's physics without spending a fact: «Буква к букве — будет слово, слово вслух — и вот оно!» states the reading mechanic in abstract, so it fits any letter of any season. «Скажи — и стань!» is the in-show chant, and ВЖУХ is Вася's permanent power-cry — both are series-level, never episode-level.
- «а молчание — темно» in the second chorus is the only place the emotional spine surfaces, and it stays a feeling rather than a plot point: unspoken things are dark, spoken things make the house bright. It quietly prefigures both РЫК and Буян without naming or revealing either.
- The bridge is the series mantra — «Заметь. Назови. Скажи.» — with «И — СТАНЬ!» added as the payoff. It is evergreen by construction, it drills the emotional curriculum in four words, and it is built for children to shout back in call-and-response.
- The whispered «Тише… тише… слышишь?» opening and closing is the show's signature sound: it makes the child go quiet and lean in before the episode starts, and it establishes creatures nobody has ever properly seen without spending a line on exposition.
- The instrument list is a design decision, not decoration: the score is played on the same found household objects the characters live among, so the music belongs to the world.
- If generating several takes, keep the one whose children's chorus sounds like real children rather than processed ones — this show's warmth depends on it.
- BANNED from this song forever, per the evergreen law: named letters, named magic words, letter counts, character names beyond the family itself, Буян, РЫК, and any plot beat.
