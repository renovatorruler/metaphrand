# «Фрося и Вася» — Table-read language and voice policy

Recorded from the user’s direction on 2026-08-12. This file is operational authority for future table reads.

## Mandatory language question

Before preparing or rendering every new table read, ask the user:

> Should this table read be in Russian or in English?

Do not infer the answer from:

- the language of the previous table read;
- a bilingual screenplay;
- which translation appears first;
- voices used in an earlier experiment.

No paid voice generation begins until the language is confirmed.

## Voice-language requirements

### English table read

- Use native, natural English voices in the requested English variety.
- For this show, default to the approved native-US English cast only after the user confirms English.
- The narrator must also sound like a native US English speaker unless the user requests another accent.
- A voice being marketed as “multilingual” is not evidence that its English performance is appropriate.

### Russian table read

- Use natural native-Russian voices approved for the roles.
- Use a native-Russian narrator approved by the user.
- Do not assume that a person’s name, accent in another language, or provider metadata proves native Russian ability.

## Anna Zub status

- **Rejected for English narration:** her English is heavily accented and does not work for the show’s English table reads.
- **Not approved for Russian narration:** the repository does not establish that she is Russian or that her Russian performance is suitable.

## Scope of “agreed-upon voices”

An approved voice is approved for a particular role **and language**. Approval in one language does not automatically transfer to another language. A new character with no approved voice must be identified before rendering; do not silently cast it as permanent series canon.
