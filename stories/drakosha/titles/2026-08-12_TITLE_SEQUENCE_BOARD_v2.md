# «ФРОСЯ И ВАСЯ» — открывающая заставка, v2

Revised for the «День рождения» pilot. 52 seconds, timed to `TITLE_SONG_52s.mp3`.

## What changed from v1 (the 35-panel board)

**Cut: the sock haul and all dialogue.** The sock drag is now the opening scene of the episode itself — showing it in the titles would spend the same reveal twice and start the show on a repeat. All whispered lines go with it: the titles have no voices, per the evergreen rule.

**Cut: the eyes in the dark.** Two pairs of glowing eyes opening in a crawlspace is horror grammar and gives a small child a jolt the rest of the sequence doesn't earn. It also split the payoff — creatures revealed, then a title. Now there is one answer to the sequence's question.

**New ending: the letters.** Down among the lost things, the camera finds scattered wooden letter tiles. Some slide away into the dark on their own. The ones left arrange themselves into the title and ignite, in the show's own spell grammar.

**Kept: everything that still works.** The house, the floor-level camera, the ball, the thread, the drawer, the human passing with a tiny shadow behind him, the steam, the pencil yanked into the dark, the drop through the floorboards, the world of lost things. Two of these have grown meaning: the pencil is now Яга's pencil, and the underfloor space is now the road.

## Production notes

- **No faces, no eyes, no voices.** The only living presence is evidence: things that move, things that are taken.
- **Panel 13 uses the existing tile and ignition plates** — the title is made of the same objects the children spell with, so the titles and the spells share one visual language.
- **Panel 14's ignition is the show's established grammar**, running left to right the way reading does.
- Technique per shot: 3, 4, 6, 9, 10, 12 want generated motion (something moves on its own); 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 11 are camera moves and can be 2.5D parallax off a still; 13 and 14 are compositing.
