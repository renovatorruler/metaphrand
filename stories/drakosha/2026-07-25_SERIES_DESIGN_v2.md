# «Скажи — и стань!» — Russian letter series, design v2 (the converged design)

Status: v2 supersedes the v1 strawman (2026-07-22). v1 was material to react against; this document is the shape we converged on in conversation, written up for dad's review and then mom's. Working title stays «Скажи — и стань!» until the breakfast-table vote (ballot at the bottom).

## THE CORE FUSION (why this show is one idea, not three)

Vesper loves transformation. Vesper struggles with the suppress-then-explode cycle. Vesper is learning to read. The show makes these ONE mechanic: in this world, magic answers only words READ ALOUD off writing. Find the written word, decode it right, say it clear — and become the thing, or make it real. Reading IS the power. Clear speech IS the power. So every letter learned = more written words he can unlock = more forms he can choose = more power over his own fire. Literacy is emotional regulation is transformation. Never preached; it is simply the physics of the world.

## THE MECHANIC (the world's physics, exactly)

The magic has two gates, and both are the curriculum.

Gate one, DECODE: the word must be read correctly off real writing in the world — a sign, a label, a note, a word Furia writes in the air. Misread it and the WRONG thing appears. This is where the comedy lives, and every gag is a phonics lesson in disguise: he wants САМ (myself) and reads СОМ — a giant catfish flops into the kitchen. The wasps' nest ОСЫ read as УСЫ puts mustaches on everyone. УРА shouted as УХА rains fish soup on the picnic. (Season 1 pairs are built only from taught letters; richer pairs like МИШКА/МЫШКА, КИТ/КОТ, НОС/СОН wait for their letters in season 2.)

Gate two, DELIVER: the word must be said clearly. A whined, mumbled, or swallowed word produces the droopy comic version of the thing — a wilted slide, a boat with a sag in the middle. The world never punishes; it just visibly gives you exactly as much as you gave it. Say it again like you mean it and you get the real one.

«ПЕРЕМОТКА!» — the do-over ritual. When a word comes out wrong or whined, anyone may call "Rewind!", make the little rewind gesture, and take the line again fresh. No shame, no teasing; the do-over is a rule of the world, not a concession. This is deliberately a ritual the family can import home word-for-word.

THE GRIEF GUARD (hard rule): the droopy-voice comedy applies only to whine-as-habit. Real tears, real hurt, real fear get comfort first, always, from everyone, and the magic waits. The show must never teach that crying is a malfunction.

## THE TWO HEROES (neither is ever a side character)

ВЕСПЕР is the hero, first-person — the therapy is his seat, not a mascot's. Everyday name Веся; when he transforms he announces his power-name: ВЖУХ! He is the only dragon who can BECOME, but only what he can read, and he is just learning letters. HARD RULE — DISPLACED TRIGGERS: his on-screen frustrations are never reenactments of real home incidents. The feelings are his; the situations are the show's (a stuck jar, a race lost to a squirrel, a tower that falls). He must recognize the fire, never the scene of the crime.

ФУРИЯ is the Scribe. She already reads and writes (like the real Furia). What she WRITES becomes real — she draws a word in the air with her claw-pen and it appears. In leader mode she announces: «Я — ФУРИЯ». Everyday name for the title vote: Фуся or Фрося (Фифи is flagged off the ballot — to Russian ears it lands as a poodle's name). The reader-writer and the becomer: she can write the word he cannot read yet; he can become the thing she cannot become. Neither completes a mission alone — the cooperation is structural, not polite.

Furia's own arc, so she is never decoration: the leader's lesson. Her brother reads slowly and her speed can silence him; she learns to make room — proudest when she helped someone else be first. And the READER'S SECRET LAYER built just for her: every episode hides written words in the world (signs, notes, jam-jar labels) that only a reading child can catch, including one secret word per episode that predicts the ending. The pre-reader gets the plot; the reader gets prophecy.

МАМА-ДРАКОНИЦА is the mentor (the real mom teaches Russian; in-world the mother dragon holds the letter lessons). Warm, unhurried. When words are used properly, she ALWAYS responds — the show models that words WORK: speak early, get heard. This is the assertiveness payoff loop, and gently, a model for the grown-ups too.

## THE RECURRING FORCE — БУЯН-ВЕТЕР, THE ANTI-VOICE

The Rowdy Wind, upgraded from v1's random mischief into the mechanic's true opponent. Буян cannot speak — only howl («У-у-у!») — and he is the enemy of words in both channels: he SCATTERS SPOKEN WORDS (snatches the sound right out of the air mid-sentence, so a crucial word arrives at the listener in pieces) and he SCATTERS WRITTEN LETTERS (blows the sign apart, so СЛОН arrives as Н-О-Л-С and must be reassembled before it can be read). Every power the kids gain, he has the anti-power. He is not evil — he is lonely and wordless, transformation with no words at all, the hero's dark mirror at full scale.

## THE CLUTCH STRUCTURE (how every episode's save works)

Mid-episode, Vesper's struggle mirrors real life: the word will not come, the whine rises, the fire rises. Then the save-the-day moment arrives — and clarity comes BECAUSE he shifts focus to who needs him. The little one is stuck; somebody is scared; Furia's hands are full. He stops hearing himself and starts seeing them, and the word comes out clean. Other-focus dissolves the whine. This is the show's deepest move and it is NEVER stated aloud — no character ever says "when you think of others, the words come." It just always happens that way.

## THE ESCALATION LAW (why season-long tension holds)

His clarity grows — and the garble grows faster. Words get longer and phonetically harder up the Zhukova ladder; Буян gets bolder in both channels; the emotional load climbs; the stakes climb. AT LEAST TWO real on-screen failures per season — episodes where the word genuinely does not come in time and the cost is real (small, kind, but real). Mastery that never fails is not mastery; it is decoration.

## PEDAGOGY SPINE (real Russian primer order — Zhukova's БУКВАРЬ, the standard home bukvar; mom will recognize it, and mom's veto is standing)

Season 1 = the first ten letters: А У О М С Х Р Ш Ы Л. Cumulative by design: each episode's magic words use ONLY letters already taught. The word-pool grows: АУ → МАМА → САМ → УРА → ШУМ → МЫ → СЛОМАЛ. Vesper's readable forms grow with it — progress is visible as power. The day's letter is ALWAYS the missing key to the climax word: the one letter he could not read yesterday is the one the ending turns on today.

## SEASON 1 LADDER (letter × word(s) × feeling-skill × mission sketch)

1. А — «А-А-А!» — NOTICE THE VOLCANO + the roar-release: when the fire rises, roar it into the sky, not at people. Voice out, fists never. Mission: his first tiny reading — say А off mama's air-writing, become the А-shaped mountain-slide, rescue a toy.
2. У — «АУ!» — ASKING FOR HELP IS BRAVE. The lost-call episode: someone is lost in the birch wood; АУ! read off the carved trail-mark is how you are found. Fear, and that calling out is not weakness.
3. О — «О!» — WONDER: big feelings are not only anger. The О episode collects the round things (the moon, a bubble, a berry) and the sound of awe.
4. М — «МАМА» «АМ» — THE COMFORT WORD. His first full decoded word — and Furia helps him WRITE it as a surprise card FOR МАМА. Made by their mother, his first read word being МАМА as a gift back to her — the meta-payoff is the point. АМ = the nom-nom feeding comedy.
5. С — «САМ!» — MYSELF: autonomy as the good channel of assertiveness. He wants to do it himself; the world makes room. Mission: make СУП himself. The САМ/СОМ misread delivers the catfish; ОСА (wasp) complicates the kitchen.
6. Х — «ХА-ХА» «УХО» — LAUGHTER AND TEASING: laughed WITH vs laughed AT (a real trigger); the tools: «Мне не смешно. Стоп.» — and УХО, the listening ear, for the other side.
7. Р — «Р-Р-Р» «УРА!» — LOSING WITHOUT EXPLODING. He loses the race, the growl rises, the tools engage, and the episode ends with an УРА that is real (with the УРА/УХА soup gag on the way). Р is the growl-letter — the fire gets its own letter, honored, not shamed. NOTE: this episode carries the open rolled-Р slot below.
8. Ш — «Ш-Ш-Ш» «ШУМ» — THE CALM BREATH. Too much noise, too many dragons, overwhelm — and Ш itself is the tool: the long quiet sh-sh-sh breath. The letter IS the technique.
9. Ы — «МЫ» — WE: you are not alone with big feelings. The team word unlocked (СЫР for the picnic, ОСЫ/УСЫ for the mustache gag). Plants the finale.
10. Л — «СЛОМАЛ» «МЫЛО» — THE REPAIR EPISODE (the season's heart, one of the two real failures): the fire slips out and something a friend loves gets broken. The walk back: say what happened («Я сломал»), help fix it, wash up (МЫЛО), the friendship survives the fire. Anger does not make him bad; repair is what strong dragons do.

## THE FINALE (the storm at maximum)

Буян at his loudest and loneliest — both channels of garble at full strength, letters flying, sounds shredded. The climax word is the season's longest and hardest, and Vesper's letters are NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT: he physically cannot deliver it alone. He and Furia say it TOGETHER — she supplies exactly what he cannot (МЫ made literal, not stated). The word lands, the wind gets its first word — «МЫ» — and the howl that could only spin alone stops spinning alone. Vesper's own mastery arrives quietly in the tag, offhand, no fanfare: he reads something small without noticing he did it. Finale-word candidate from taught letters: «ХОРОШО» (Х-О-Р-О-Ш-О — six letters, the growl-letter in the middle of it, and it is what you say when the storm ends); final choice with mom, constrained to taught letters.

## EPISODE TEMPLATE (~9–11 min, mission rhythm)

Cold open whimsy (60–90s, Kakė Makė register: a kid-scale absurdity) → the mission lands → today's letter discovered IN the world (its shape hiding in things) → mama-dragon's short diegetic letter beat (sound, shape, air-writing; call-and-response) → first attempt fails: a misread gag or a whine-droop, the fire rises (shown kindly; «Перемотка!» available) → mid-episode mirror struggle → the clutch: someone needs him, the word comes clean → «Скажи — и стань!» transformation (ВЖУХ!) → mission solved with Furia's written word + Vesper's becoming → tag: Furia's secret word pays off + a goodnight whimsy button.

Chant: «Скажи — и стань!». Feelings mantra (tiny, used sparingly): «Заметь. Назови. Скажи.» (Notice it. Name it. Say it.)

## STYLE (the Kakė Makė mandate)

Hand-drawn 2D whimsy: wobbly ink lines, crayon and watercolor texture, collage bits, homely details drawn with love (socks, jam jars, ladders), kid-logic physics. Cozy domestic-fantastic scale: missions are the size of a lost boot, a ruined pancake tower, a twisted bridge. Deliberately NOT the Hindi show's 3D papercraft — two shows, two crafts, no doubled effort.

## DIFFERENTIATION FROM THE HINDI SHOW (so the tracks never blur)

- Mechanic: restore the stolen letter (कुकु) vs unlock magic by READING ALOUD (this).
- Threat: an eraser-thief who redeems by MAKING his first letter vs a wordless wind who redeems by SAYING his first word — owning vs belonging.
- Hero: mascot hero (Kuku) + kids as team vs VESPER HIMSELF as the transformer — the therapy is first-person.
- Pedagogy: letter-of-the-day + shape-becomes-solution vs the cumulative Zhukova word-building ladder + decode/deliver gates.
- Emotional core: kindness/नीति strategy vs self-regulation + assertiveness.
- Look: 3D papercraft vs Kakė Makė hand-drawn.

## THE ASSERTIVENESS CURRICULUM (the quiet spine; never a lecture on screen)

The cycle to break: suppress → suppress → explode. The show's answer, in order, across the season: (1) the fire is REAL and honored — it gets its own letter, its own roar-space; never "don't be angry." (2) Voice beats fists — the roar-to-sky, the growl-letter. (3) Words beat the roar — small words said early («Я злюсь», «Стоп», «САМ», «Помоги») get results on screen every single time. (4) Calm is a skill with a letter of its own (Ш). (5) A clean do-over is always available and costs nothing («Перемотка!»). (6) Repair, not shame, after the slip (СЛОМАЛ). (7) You are not alone (МЫ). Adults in-show always reward the early small word — the world proves that speaking early works better than exploding late.

## OPEN SLOT — VESPER'S TRUE LIMITATION

The finale needs him to genuinely lack something only Furia supplies. Leading candidate: the rolled Р (his hardest sound sitting in the middle of «ХОРОШО» would make the together-saying literal and earned). DEFERRED pending dad's field test — he speaks four languages and may roll Р fine. If Р falls through, the limitation slot stays structural (a sound, a length, a speed — something real from the field test), and episode 7 is where it gets honored.

## THE BREAKFAST-TABLE BALLOT (open for mom and the family)

1. TITLE VOTE: «Фуся и Веся» / «Фрося и Веся» / «Фрося и Веспик» / «Вжух и Фрося» — or hers to name. («Скажи — и стань!» stays the in-show chant regardless.)
2. Mama-dragon literally voiced by / modeled on mom (recommended: yes).
3. РЫК, the wild form (what unspoken feelings turn into): kept as a whispered name only, or shown softly as a scribbly silhouette (recommended: shown softly — kids need to SEE the wild form to name it). Either way it obeys the displaced-triggers rule.
4. Episode length target: 9 vs 11 minutes.
5. Furia's secret-word layer: single-episode payoffs only, or a season-long cipher — ten secret words assembling into the finale's sentence (recommended: the cipher; exact words with mom, constrained to taught letters).
6. The finale word: «ХОРОШО» recommended; mom's call.
7. The rolled-Р field test result, when dad has run it.
