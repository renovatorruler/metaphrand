SCENE CONTEXT
A soot-black hatch bursts open in a hidden passage; a tiny mortar comes through, a plaster seam opens, and an old woman with her mortar, pestle, broom, and bundles enters the tiny home before the wall closes behind her.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None. All content described.

LOCATION MAP
A short soot-blackened passage runs between the stove's metal cleanout hatch and a hidden seam in the home's plaster wall. Beyond the seam: the warm main room of the tiny home.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
Shot A opens inside the dark passage facing the closed metal cleanout hatch at its far end. Soot dust hangs in the air.

PHYSICS
Soot behaves as fine black dust: it billows, hangs, and settles on surfaces. The hatch has metal weight and slams with rebound. The mortar glides at walking pace with its bundles swinging slightly. The plaster seam opens and closes as a heavy hidden mechanism, scraping softly.

LIGHTING
The passage is near-dark, lit by thin spill through the hatch; the room beyond the seam glows warm. Strong contrast between the sooty dark and the hearth light. No flat lighting.

AUDIO
The hollow BOOM of the hatch; soot hiss; the metallic clap of the hatch closing; the soft plaster scrape as the seam opens and closes. No dialogue. No music, no subtitles.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
The mortar fits the passage with room to spare. No fire, no sparks — soot and air only. The seam is fully closed and ordinary-looking in the final second.
