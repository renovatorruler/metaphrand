SCENE CONTEXT
The old woman commands her mortar; wind spins her whole rig into a tight spiral that shrinks it tiny, and the spiral drops into the chimney.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
None. All content described.

LOCATION MAP
The sky directly above the human house's brick chimney. Morning light from screen-right.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
Shot A opens close on BABA YAGA in the hovering mortar above the chimney: tiny wiry old woman, headscarf, ember eyes; she plants her feet wider, grips the rim with both hands.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE. Three shots. HARD CUTS only.
Shot A, 0:00 to 0:02: close on her bracing in the mortar; she calls out in Russian, commanding and cheerful: «Ну, родимые, закрутились!»
HARD CUT.
Shot B, 0:02 to 0:06: distant view of the roof: wind wraps the mortar, the woman, pestle, broom, and bundles into a dense spinning spiral of air and leaves that hides them completely; the spiral tightens and shrinks until a tiny flying silhouette emerges from its center, no bigger than a beetle against the sky.
HARD CUT.
Shot C, 0:06 to 0:08: from above the chimney looking down: the tiny spiral drops cleanly into the dark chimney opening and vanishes. The roof stands empty and quiet.

OPTICS
Shot A: 29° short telephoto portrait, her face and grip sharp, sky dissolved behind.
Shots B and C: 47° standard normal, deep focus, stable straight lines.
No lens drift inside any shot.

CAMERA
Static locked frames. The spiral does its work inside a motionless frame.

PHYSICS
The spiral is wind made visible: it accelerates rotation, pulls in stray leaves, and shrinks with a vortex's tightening spin. The drop into the chimney is a clean gravity fall. Nothing explodes, nothing flashes; it is wind craft, not magic sparkle.

LIGHTING
Bright morning sun from screen-right; the spiral catches light on its edges. No flat lighting.

AUDIO
Her single commanding Russian line in shot A; then wind winding upward in pitch through shot B; a soft whoosh and sudden quiet in shot C. No music, no subtitles.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
Only the scripted line is spoken. No sparkles, no glowing magic effects — wind and air only. The roof is empty in the final second.
