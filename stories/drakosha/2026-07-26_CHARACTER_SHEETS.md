# «Фрося и Вася» — character sheets for illustration (v2 — yarn identity)

Author's physical canon baked in: none of the kids are neat — Фрося's hair is permanently unkempt; Вася gets off easier only because his hair is short; all the kids are skinny EXCEPT Муся, who is the one round baby.

## THE MATERIAL IDENTITY — READ THIS FIRST (v2, 2026-07-26)

The v1 sheets had no answer to "what are they MADE of," so generators defaulted to folklore and produced either Домовёнок Кузя clones or dirty, grabby, faintly scary gremlin-children. The fix is a material, exactly as the Hindi show is anchored by papercraft:

**THEY ARE MADE OF YARN.** Knitted and felted little creatures — the house's own lost wool, thread, and scraps come alive. Soft round felt bodies, limbs of thick knitted cord, hands like little mittens, button eyes, a nose that is one embroidered stitch, a mouth of stitched thread. They look like beloved handmade toys that a grandmother knitted and someone's love brought to life — clean, soft, huggable.

Why this works: felt cannot have fangs, claws, or grime, so "creepy" becomes impossible; nobody's Кузя is a knitted toy, so the reference collapses; messy hair becomes loose YARN LOOPS (adorable, not unwashed); skinny becomes thread-thin knitted limbs; and the lore improves — spirits who keep a house being made of that house's lost wool is more folk-true than a generic hairy gnome. Bonus: РЫК becomes literal — when feelings go unspoken, a thread pulls loose and Вася starts to UNRAVEL; saying the word knits him back together.

### BANNED WORDS in image prompts (they summon the failures)

Never write: домовой, домовёнок, house spirit, brownie, goblin, gnome, elf, imp, creature-of-folklore; fur, furry, shaggy, unkempt, matted, wild hair, dirty, grubby, ragged, feral, mischievous-looking; scribble-edged, sketchy, scratchy.

Write instead: knitted toy character, felted wool doll, soft handmade plush, yarn loops, embroidered stitch, button eyes, mitten hands, cozy, clean, soft.

## REFERENCE BOARD (gather these before drawing)

THE CULTURAL ANCESTOR — «ВАРЕЖКА», Союзмультфильм 1967, dir. Roman Kachanov, production design by Leonid Shvartsman (the designer of Cheburashka), animation including Yuri Norstein; first prize at Annecy 1967. A lonely girl's knitted red mitten becomes a living puppy. This is the single most useful reference we have: a knitted thing brought to life by love, made by the greatest Soviet animation team, and beloved by exactly the generation of Russian parents who will watch this show. It gives the yarn identity a native lineage — the same recognition-bonus Кузя would have given, without the clone problem. https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Варежка_(мультфильм)

THE UNRAVELLING PROOF — YARNY, from the game «Unravel» (Coldwood/EA, 2016). A small creature made entirely of red yarn who UNRAVELS as he moves away from what he loves, growing visibly thinner and weaker; players read the symbolism instantly. This validates РЫК: a yarn character coming undone is legible to anyone, needs no explanation, and is sad rather than frightening. Study his construction (wire-and-yarn doll, eyes threaded in, no mouth or nose). https://unravel.fandom.com/wiki/Yarny and the designer's account: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/how-i-unravel-s-i-memorable-protagonist-yarny-was-woven-together

KNITTED CHARACTERS CARRYING A WHOLE SERIES — THE CLANGERS (BBC, 1969, Smallfilms; characters designed by Peter Firmin and knitted by Joan Firmin). Proof that knitted creatures sustain a series and read as warm, not creepy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clangers

WORN-AND-LOVED CLOTH — BAGPUSS (Smallfilms, 1974), the saggy old cloth cat, loose at the seams and adored. The exact emotional register we want for a house of handmade beings.

THE 2D TRANSLATION — KIRBY'S EPIC YARN (Nintendo, 2010): a whole world rendered as fabric, felt, and thread in flat 2D, with characters unravelling and re-knitting. The best study for how to keep yarn readable when it is DRAWN rather than photographed.

FABRIC EXPRESSIVENESS — SACKBOY / LittleBigPlanet: how much emotion button eyes and stitched features can carry.

SHAPE LANGUAGE — amigurumi pattern books (Japan Amigurumi Association's «Complete Guide to Crochet Dolls and Animals», Miya's doll books): the actual craft — round heads, cord limbs, mitten hands. Use for proportions.

THE ILLUSTRATION REGISTER (locked) — Lina Žutautė's «Kakė Makė» books. https://english.lithuanianculture.lt/lithuanian-culture-guide/illustrators/2015/07/13/lina-zutaute/

## THE SHARED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every illustration prompt)

Hand-drawn 2D children's book illustration, warm and cozy, in the Kakė Makė register: soft hand-inked outlines, watercolor and crayon texture with the strokes visible, occasional paper-collage bits, warm hearth palette (honey, ochre, rust, plum, deep brown) in candle-warm light. The characters are tiny KNITTED AND FELTED TOY CREATURES the size of a matchbox — soft round felt bodies, knitted-cord arms and legs, mitten hands, button eyes, stitched thread mouths, loose yarn loops for hair. Every one is knitted to a different pattern, so no two look alike. They live behind a stove in a house of lost things: spool tables, matchbox beds, a Christmas-bulb lamp. Clean, soft, huggable, sweet-natured. NOT 3D, NOT clean vector lines, NOT Pixar/DreamWorks, NOT anime, no plastic smoothness — drawn by a warm human hand, slightly crooked on purpose.

Scale anchor: each one stands about as tall as a matchbox. The house cat is a mountain. A milk jug is a tower.

## ВАСЯ — the hero (boy, ~5)

Shape language: a small round felt head on a thin knitted body — everything expressive is in the head and eyes, the body is skinny knitted cord. Knitted in warm rust-orange wool with ochre patches darned on.

Hair: a SHORT fuzzy crop of clipped yarn ends, cropped close — the one kid whose hair behaves, purely because there's so little of it. Two short loops still stick up.

Face: big round button eyes, the biggest in the family; embroidered eyebrows that fly all over (astonishment is his resting state); a stitched grin with one stitch missing, like a lost tooth.

Signature details: a little cloth pouch on a string holding his eight carved matchstick letter-chips. His transformation pose: feet planted, chest out, «ВЖУХ!»

WHEN FRUSTRATED (РЫК): a thread pulls loose at his cuff and he begins to UNRAVEL — a loop of yarn springing free, his outline going wobbly and thready, his button eyes still visible and worried inside the tangle. Always soft, never scary; it is a knitted toy coming undone, never a beast.

Avoid: chubby body (he's skinny), neat parted hair, cool/collected expressions — he runs hot and open. No fur, no grime.

## ФРОСЯ — the Scribe (girl, ~7)

Shape language: taller and thinner than Вася, knitted in a tighter, neater stitch — a slim felt body with a small pointed chin, everything about her construction precise. Plum and berry-red wool with ink-blue darning.

Hair: THE GLORIOUS EXCEPTION — a huge cloud of loose YARN LOOPS in every direction, a soft tangled pom-pom nest with her claw-pen (a whittled pencil-stub) stuck in it and a stray button caught in the loops. Clean, soft, enormous, and never once brushed. The joke of her design: the tidiest mind in the house under the wildest yarn.

Face: bright button eyes set close and sharp — she reads everything twice as fast as anyone; embroidered brows that narrow with focus when she air-writes.

Signature details: the rolled chore list tucked in a stitched belt; glowing letters hanging where she writes them (thin sparkling golden-white strokes, wobbly like sparkler trails); her mitten hand shooting straight up («Я первая!»).

Avoid: neat braids, bows, brushed or flat hair; making her rounder than Вася — taller, just as thin. No fur, no grime, nothing "wild-child."

## МАМА-ДОМОВУШКА — the mentor

Shape language: one big soft rounded knitted silhouette — a warm woollen dumpling of a mother, the softest-looking thing in the show. Design her, Руся, and Муся as a SINGLE composite shape: the babies are permanently attached (Руся on the hip-arm, Муся on the back) and she is never drawn without them.

Hair: honey-brown yarn gathered under a patchwork kerchief made from a candy-wrapper scrap.

Face: unhurried half-lidded button eyes; the calmest face in the show; a stitched smile that arrives slowly.

Colors: hearth browns and honey golds, a patchwork apron of lost fabric scraps, everything visibly hand-sewn.

Signature details: letter-chips in her apron pocket; one arm always cradling, the other always mid-task (pouring, folding, rescuing the chore list from Руся's mouth).

Avoid: exhaustion, frazzle, sternness — she is tired-proof and never raises her voice; do not draw her slim, she is the round warm center of the family. No fur.

## ПАПА-ДОМОВОЙ — the hands of the homestead

Shape language: big, broad and square — the largest of them by a head and shoulders, knitted in thick chunky stitch like a friendly wardrobe of a sweater. A few wood shavings caught in the wool from his work.

Hair: chunky bark-brown yarn, a bit of it standing up; big embroidered eyebrows that do half his talking.

Face: a wide stitched grin as the default — he is a joker, always mid-tease or mid-game. When he speaks the local language he squares up with comic confidence (he gets words wrong and enjoys it thoroughly).

Colors: bark-brown chunky wool with moss-green patches darned on.

Signature details: a tool belt of found things — a thimble bucket, a matchstick plank over one shoulder, a bent-pin hook; huge soft mitten hands (one can cover Вася's whole head, and often does). Frequently drawn mid-play with kids hanging off his arms.

Avoid: sternness, stoicism, distance — he is the involved, joking, always-there dad; never draw him leaving or apart from the family. No fur, no grime — the shavings are from work, and there are only a few.

## РУСЯ — the littlest boy (~1.5)

Shape language: the smallest thin one — knitted-cord noodle limbs and a determined round felt head. Silent and relentless: always mid-grab (the chore list, mama's ear, whatever is nearest).

Colors: pale ochre-yellow wool.

Signature details: permanently attached to mama's hip/arm; something in his mitten hand on its way to his mouth; wide unblinking button eyes of total concentration.

Avoid: chubbiness (that's Муся's), talking poses — he is pre-verbal. Nothing grabby-menacing: he is a baby, and the comedy is his solemn determination.

## МУСЯ — the littlest girl (~1.5)

Shape language: THE ONE ROUND CHILD — a stuffed woollen dumpling of a baby, all cheeks, the only plump one in the family. Rides mama's back like a small warm backpack.

Colors: lavender-pink wool.

Signature details: blows delighted bubbles, points with one round mitten, burbles back at fish; often asleep mid-scene with her cheek squashed into mama's shoulder.

Avoid: making her skinny — the round is canon; she and Руся must read instantly as a matched-but-opposite pair (thin/round).

## БЕГЕМОТ — the humans' cat

Shape language: a mountain of coal-black brush-ink — to the little knitted ones he is landscape, not animal. IMPORTANT CONTRAST: he is a REAL cat, painted with real weight and glossy fur — the softness of the yarn family reads best against something genuinely animal. Huge, glossy black, drawn as a soft massive blob with elegant long whiskers and a tail like a calligraphy stroke.

Face: enormous yellow-moon eyes glowing out of the black; permanently deadpan; conveys everything with slow blinks and the angle of his ears.

Signature details: for size, always compose him filling a doorway or looming over the tiny spirits; the invincible innocent look after mischief; one ear tilted when negotiating (his price: молоко and ear-scratches, за два уха).

Avoid: cute-kitten proportions, visible fangs/menace — he is vast and mercantile, never actually frightening.

## СОМ — the catfish (recurring pond guest)

Shape language: a boat — to the little knitted ones he is literally boat-sized. Like Бегемот, he is a REAL animal, watercolored with real weight. Long glossy grey-green body, magnificent drooping усы (whiskers), a huge round perpetually astonished mouth.

Signature details: round bewildered eyes; happy bubble-rings on the pond surface (буль); in the cellar scene he flops with tragic dignity.

Avoid: teeth, slime, anything gross — he is dignified and absurd.

## РЫК — Вася's wild form (a state, not a character)

What it is: Вася literally UNRAVELLING. A thread pulls loose and his knitting comes undone — loops springing free, a whirl of loose orange yarn where his outline used to be, his two button eyes still visible inside the tangle, worried and lost. Saying the word knits him back together, loop by loop.

Rules: always soft, always kind, never a monster — no teeth, no claws, no darkness, no shadow-form. A beloved toy coming apart is sad and fixable, which is exactly the feeling we want; the button eyes inside are the anchor that keeps him HIM.

## БУЯН-ВЕТЕР — the wind (future; heard in Ep1, seen later)

What he is: the Rowdy Wind — no solid body ever. He is visible only as what he moves: spirals of leaves, scattered letters, dust ribbons, rattling shutters. At his most present, a long ghostly ribbon-shape in the gust with two lonely eyes, there and gone.

Signature: the long thin howl «У-у-у» from beyond the field; letters torn off signs spiraling in his wake. Yarn-world bonus: he is the one force that can TUG A LOOSE THREAD — the wind that unravels, the natural enemy of knitted people.

Avoid: a face with a mouth (he cannot speak — that is his tragedy), storm-god muscle, menace — he is lonely, not evil.

## THE HUMANS (the giants)

Never shown properly — the Borrowers rule: feet, shins, shadows, a hand reaching into frame, a yawn from upstairs. Their world is furniture-scale geography for the knitted ones.
