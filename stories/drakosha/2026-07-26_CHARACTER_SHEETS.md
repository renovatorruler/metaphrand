# «Фрося и Вася» — character sheets for illustration (v1)

Author's physical canon baked in: none of the kids are neat — Фрося's hair is permanently unkempt; Вася gets off easier only because his hair is short; all the kids are skinny EXCEPT Муся, who is the one round baby.

## THE SHARED STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every illustration prompt)

Hand-drawn 2D children's book illustration in the Kakė Makė register: wobbly ink outlines that visibly wobble, crayon and watercolor texture with the strokes showing, occasional paper-collage bits, warm hearth palette (honey, ochre, rust, plum, deep browns) with candle-warm light. The characters are домовята — little house-spirits the size of a matchbox, scribble-edged, each one shaped differently from every other. Cozy domestic-fantastic scale: furniture made of lost things (spool tables, matchbox beds, a Christmas-bulb lamp). NOT 3D, NOT clean vector lines, NOT Pixar/DreamWorks, NOT anime, no plastic smoothness — it must look drawn by a warm human hand, slightly crooked on purpose.

Scale anchor: a домовёнок stands about as tall as a matchbox. The house cat is a mountain. A milk jug is a tower.

## ВАСЯ — the hero (boy, ~5)

Shape language: round. A small round-headed sprout of a spirit with skinny arms and legs — everything on him is circles and things sticking out in different directions. Noticeably skinny; his roundness is in the head and eyes, not the body.

Hair/fur: SHORT fuzzy crop — the one kid whose hair behaves, purely because there's so little of it. A couple of tufts still stick out.

Face: big wide curious eyes (the biggest eyes in the family), eyebrows that fly all over — astonishment is his resting state. Quick grin with a missing-tooth gap.

Color: warm rust-orange with ochre patches.

Signature details: a little cloth pouch on a string holding his eight wooden letter-chips (matchstick wood, hand-carved). When he's frustrated, his outline frays — pencil scribbles escape past his edges (draw the fray, keep it soft, never scary). His transformation cry pose: feet planted, chest out, «ВЖУХ!»

Avoid: chubby cheeks/body (he's skinny), neat gelled-looking hair, cool/collected expressions — he runs hot and open.

## ФРОСЯ — the Scribe (girl, ~7)

Shape language: sharp and angular — a taller, skinny spirit made of quick triangles and precise little points. Everything she OWNS is neat: the chore list rolled just so, her claw-pen sharpened.

Hair: THE GLORIOUS EXCEPTION TO HER NEATNESS — a huge permanently unkempt dark tangle, a magpie's nest of hair with her claw-pen (a whittled pencil-stub) stuck somewhere in it, plus maybe a stray button or crumb the nest has collected. The joke of her design: the tidiest mind in the house under the messiest hair. The hair is never, ever brushed.

Face: sharp bright eyes that read everything twice as fast as anyone; a small proud chin; when she air-writes, her eyes narrow with focus.

Color: plum/berry-red with ink-blue shadows.

Signature details: the rolled chore list in her belt; glowing letters hanging in the air where she writes (thin sparkling golden-white strokes, slightly wobbly like sparkler trails); her hand shooting up («Я первая!»).

Avoid: neat braids, bows, brushed hair of any kind; making her bigger/rounder than Вася — she is taller but just as skinny.

## МАМА-ДОМОВУШКА — the mentor

Shape language: one big soft rounded silhouette — a warm haystack of a spirit. Design her, Руся, and Муся as a SINGLE composite shape: the babies are permanently attached (Руся on the hip-arm, Муся on the back) and she is never drawn without them.

Hair/fur: soft honey-brown, wrapped in a patchwork kerchief made of a candy-wrapper scrap.

Face: unhurried, half-lidded kind eyes; the calmest face in the show; a smile that arrives slowly.

Color: hearth browns and honey golds, patchwork apron of lost fabric scraps.

Signature details: wooden letter-chips in her apron pocket; one arm always cradling, the other always doing something else at the same time (pouring, folding, rescuing the chore list from Руся's mouth).

Avoid: exhaustion, frazzle, sternness — she is tired-proof and never raises her voice; do not draw her slim — she is the round warm center of the family.

## ПАПА-ДОМОВОЙ — the hands of the homestead

Shape language: big, broad, square-ish — the largest домовёнок by a head and shoulders, built like a friendly wardrobe. Sawdust permanently in his fur.

Hair/fur: shaggy bark-brown, wood shavings caught in it; big expressive eyebrows that do half his talking.

Face: a playful grin as the default — he is a joker, always mid-tease or mid-game; laugh lines. When he speaks the local language he squares up with comic confidence (he gets words wrong and enjoys it).

Color: bark-brown with moss-green patches.

Signature details: a tool belt of found things — a thimble bucket, a matchstick plank over one shoulder, a bent-pin hook; huge warm hands (one hand can cover Вася's whole head, and often does). Frequently drawn mid-play: kids hanging off his arms.

Avoid: sternness, stoicism, distance — he is the involved, joking, always-there dad; never draw him leaving or apart from the family group.

## РУСЯ — the littlest boy (~1.5)

Shape language: a skinny little sprout — the smallest thin one, all noodle limbs and a determined round head. Silent and relentless: always mid-grab (the chore list, mama's ear, whatever is nearest).

Color: pale ochre-yellow.

Signature details: permanently attached to mama's hip/arm; something in his mouth or on the way to it; wide unblinking eyes of total concentration.

Avoid: chubbiness (that's Муся's), talking poses — he is pre-verbal.

## МУСЯ — the littlest girl (~1.5)

Shape language: THE ONE ROUND CHILD — a happy dumpling baby, all cheeks, the only plump kid in the family. Rides mama's back like a small warm backpack.

Color: lavender-pink.

Signature details: blows delighted bubbles, points at things with one fat little finger, burbles back at fish; often asleep mid-scene with her cheek squashed against mama's shoulder.

Avoid: making her skinny — the cheeks are canon; she and Руся must read instantly as a matched-but-opposite pair (thin/round).

## БЕГЕМОТ — the humans' cat

Shape language: a mountain of coal-black brush-ink — to the домовята he is landscape, not animal. Huge, glossy black, drawn as a soft massive blob with elegant long whiskers and a tail like a calligraphy stroke.

Face: enormous yellow-moon eyes glowing out of the black; permanently deadpan; conveys everything with slow blinks and the angle of his ears.

Signature details: for size, always compose him filling a doorway or looming over the tiny spirits; the invincible innocent look after mischief; one ear tilted when negotiating (his price: молоко and ear-scratches, за два уха).

Avoid: cute-kitten proportions, visible fangs/menace — he is vast and mercantile, never actually frightening.

## СОМ — the catfish (recurring pond guest)

Shape language: a boat — to the домовята he is literally boat-sized. Long glossy grey-green body, magnificent drooping усы (whiskers), a huge round perpetually astonished mouth.

Signature details: round bewildered eyes; happy bubble-rings on the pond surface (буль); in the cellar scene he flops with tragic dignity.

Avoid: teeth, slime, anything gross — he is dignified and absurd.

## РЫК — Вася's wild form (a state, not a character)

What it is: Вася's OWN outline coming undone. His lines fray, the crayon scrawls past his edges, and he becomes a soft storm of his own strokes — a scribble-whirl with his two big eyes still visible inside it, worried and lost.

Rules: always soft, always kind, never a monster — no teeth, no claws, no darkness; it is a drawing unraveling, not a beast emerging. The eyes inside are the anchor: it is still him in there.

## БУЯН-ВЕТЕР — the wind (future; heard in Ep1, seen later)

What he is: the Rowdy Wind — no solid body ever. He is visible only as what he moves: spirals of leaves, scattered letters, dust ribbons, rattling shutters. At his most present, a long ghostly ribbon-shape in the gust with two lonely eyes, there and gone.

Signature: the long thin howl «У-у-у» from beyond the field; letters torn off signs spiraling in his wake.

Avoid: a face with a mouth (he cannot speak — that is his tragedy), storm-god muscle, menace — he is lonely, not evil.

## THE HUMANS (the giants)

Never shown properly — the Borrowers rule: feet, shins, shadows, a hand reaching into frame, a yawn from upstairs. Their world is furniture-scale geography for the spirits.
