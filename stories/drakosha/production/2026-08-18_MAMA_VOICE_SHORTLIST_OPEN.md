## The author's note on each voice

> **Kuragina doesn't enunciate enough. Ilinca over-enunciates.**

That is why there are two takes of each rather than one of each: the two complaints pull on different dials.

- **On Kuragina, `stability` is the dial.** Low stability is what softens her consonants. 21 and 22 walk it up, and 22 is the one at risk of buying clarity at the cost of the performance, because `style` had to come down to make room.
- **On Ilinca, `similarity_boost` is the dial.** The over-precision is her source manner, so dropping similarity loosens her off it; the speed increase stops her dwelling on each consonant. Below about 0.55 she starts drifting off her own timbre, so 24 is the far edge.

This direction is approved in shape — the author's remaining objection is to the voices, not to the reading. Earlier attempts are superseded and are on disk only as history: `-v2` was pointed rather than sly and made her sound depressed on «Подарок хороший», and the very first set had no performance tags at all, which is why every candidate sounded identically flat and the audition was nearly wasted.
