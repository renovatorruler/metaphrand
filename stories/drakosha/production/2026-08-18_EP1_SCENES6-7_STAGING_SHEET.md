# Scenes 6–7 staging — the pencil, the rule, the chest

Rebuilt 2026-08-18 after the first draft was rejected. Two things were wrong with it: it buried Мама's new line, and it spent generation time on people walking across a room.

---

## THE LAW THAT SHOULD HAVE GOVERNED THE FIRST DRAFT

**Only shoot what moves the story.** If a shot can be lifted out and the story still stands, it should never have been generated. See `STAGING_MATRIX_DOCTRINE.md`.

The first draft failed it by staging the family rising from the table and walking to Бабушка-Яга — thirty seconds of people relocating, which raised the fair question of why she had crossed the room at all. She hasn't. Nobody has. **The script already supplies the cut that does this work: SH066 is a tight close-up on her POCKET.** Go into a detail, and when you come out of it the geography has changed and nobody needs to see how.

Бабушка-Яга stands at the end of s5job3 — already shot, and all the "she is up" the audience needs. Вася "coming over" at SH072 is him **arriving into frame**, never crossing a room.

**Audited against the law, the shot list below loses one thing and keeps everything else.** Вася's arrival is folded into the beckon: she crooks her finger, we cut, he is there. Every other shot carries a gift, a condition, a rule, a question or a refusal — all of them move it.

## МАМА'S NEW LINE — the spine of both scenes

A **script change made 2026-08-18**, and the reason scene 7 exists at all:

> **МАМА:** *(ровно)* Подарок хороший. Только Вася читать ещё не умеет, а Фрося пишет как слышит. Но теперь научатся. У меня тоже подарок есть.

**The last two sentences turn her from objector into giver** — Бабушка-Яга gave the wish, Мама gives the means to use it, and the two women are parallel rather than opposed. It also gives her a reason to cross to the wall: she is going to fetch something, not walking off. «Научатся» is plural — she means both children. «Потому что» was dropped; Мама is flat and unadorned, and two short sentences let the causal link land without her explaining it.

**Every word of the first half is load-bearing.** «Ещё» makes it a stage rather than a deficiency — he cannot read YET, which is a mother saying he will. «Пишет как слышит» is precisely what a child who knows all her letters but cannot spell does: she writes it the way it sounds. It describes rather than accuses, which matters because she is saying it in front of both children, and it forecasts the plot exactly — Фрося will write МАШЫНА and get something that is not a car.

Two earlier drafts were rejected and the reasons are worth keeping. «Фрося писать не умеет» is false: she writes constantly through the episode. «Фрося ни слова правильно не напишет» is a verdict passed on a child in front of her, and Мама does not disparage her children to win an argument she has already lost. She is naming what has to happen next, which is why she goes for the chest.

## THE FOUR CELLS

Per `STAGING_MATRIX_DOCTRINE.md`: every character in frame needs POSITION, HANDS, EYELINE and FACE in every shot. A blank is a defect that has not surfaced yet. Each job's prompt is generated from that matrix and may contain nothing it does not.

Constants across the scene:

- **HANDS, Бабушка-Яга** — empty; the pencil only between SH066 and SH067; then empty again. **Never the broom**: her bound sheet holds one and it must be excluded in every shot.
- **HANDS, Фрося** — empty until SH067, then the pencil, held against her chest from SH071.
- **HANDS, Вася** — empty throughout. He receives a breath, not an object.
- **EYELINE** — at SH066 the pocket takes every eye in the room; at SH073 the breath and the light do the same. Attention is an event: every character's eyeline cell points at it.
- **FACE, Вася at SH074** — his beat is that he is blown on and *nothing happens*. Confusion, not delight.
- **FACE, Фрося at SH079** — wary, not thrilled. She asks what the secret is because she suspects a catch.

## THE SCRIPT IS STALE AT SH068 — AND WHY THAT MATTERS

SH068 reads «Карандаш лежит поперёк неё и почти достаёт до локтя» — the pencil lying across her palm and almost reaching her elbow. That is **pre-miniaturisation canon and is wrong.** Бабушка-Яга miniaturises everything she brings; the pencil arrives sized for the person receiving it. It sits in Фрося's palm, she writes with it one-handed, and it goes behind her ear. `D-FRO-PENCIL-01_approved.png` shows exactly that — a short stubby faceted pencil, not a beam.

The registry carried the same stale version in `propScale` and has been corrected. **The shooting script has not been**, and it is the document the gate reads and the one I copy from — so this will resurface until the line is amended at source. Worth fixing in `2026-08-04_EP1_den-rozhdeniya_SHOOTING_numbered_bilingual.md` rather than remembering.

The general law, which explains the ступа fitting through the hatch as well: **whatever Бабушка-Яга brings is sized for whoever receives it.**
