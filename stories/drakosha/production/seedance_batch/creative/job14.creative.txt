SCENE CONTEXT
The tiny girl finds a red core in her gift tin, hugs her mother, and draws one bright red line on a scrap of paper.

LOCATION MAP
Beside the plank-on-spools table in the hall inside the old bricked-up fireplace: giant boulder-brick walls, the riveted iron plate closing the old fireplace mouth as the far wall, a colored garland of bulbs overhead, plank floor. Мама with both babies faces Фрося; the open round striped tin between them, its five faceted hexagonal colored cores — red, orange, yellow, green, blue — lying FLAT side by side in one straight row on a cream woven cloth liner, all their points facing the same way, held down by two stitched cream cloth bands crossing the row; a torn scrap of pale paper lies on the table.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
Shot A opens with Фрося holding the open round tin between herself and Мама, her finger already reaching toward the red core, which lies at one end of the flat row.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE. Three shots. HARD CUTS only.
Shot A, 0:00 to 0:04: by the table, both in frame: Фрося touches the red core with one fingertip and says in Russian, delighted: «Цветны́е! И кра́сный есть!» Мама answers in Russian, warm: «Для всех твои́х рису́нков.»
HARD CUT.
Shot B, 0:04 to 0:08: Фрося sets the tin down on the table in front of her, then wraps her arms around Мама — around the babies too, a full family hug — and says in Russian: «Спаси́бо, мам! Я всё-всё нарису́ю!»
HARD CUT.
Shot C, 0:08 to 0:12: close on the paper scrap on the table: the red core, held in the fingers of Фрося, draws one bright confident red line across it. She lifts the core, admires the color for a beat, lays it back flat in its place in the row under the cloth band, and sets the striped lid on.

OPTICS
Shots A and B: 47° standard normal at child height.
Shot C: 29° detail lens on the paper, the red line razor-sharp, background softly dissolved.
No lens drift inside any shot.

CAMERA
Static locked frames only.

PHYSICS
The core drags on the paper with real pigment friction, leaving a slightly waxy bright line; the lid seats on the tin with a soft metal tap; the hug compresses cloth and squeezes one small protest-squeak from a baby.

LIGHTING
Warm bulb light pooling on the table and paper. No flat front light.

AUDIO
The three Russian lines exactly as scripted, hers delighted, Мама warm; the dry sound of the core on paper in shot C; stove crackle underneath. No music, no subtitles.

SHOT RULES
Only the scripted lines are spoken. The red line is drawn once, one stroke, no writing, no letters. The tin is a round shallow metal tin with a rolled rim, bare worn metal, its lid painted in parallel diagonal red and orange stripes chipped back to metal at the edges, and its five cores lie FLAT in one row under stitched cloth bands — never standing, never fanned. The core returns to the tin and the lid goes on in the final second.
Dialogue lock: in shot A only the lips of Фрося move for her line, then only the lips of Мама for her answer — strictly in sequence. In shot B only the lips of Фрося move. No offscreen voices.
