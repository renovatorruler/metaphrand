SCENE CONTEXT
The mother opens a hidden panel in the back wall, lifts out a chest, and sets it down in front of two children who have never seen it before.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Nobody speaks in this job.

WHERE WE ARE
The back corner of the hall, past the rope railing — the kitchen end. The stove and the shelf of thimble cups are to the left, the couch with its floral rug behind, and a plain wooden panel with an iron ring is set into the boulder-blocks at the right. A woven play mat lies on the plank floor in front of it. Nobody is shown walking here.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what a face does. Describe the muscles, not the mood.
Мама: her mouth is closed and level and her brows are low and steady. She does not look at the wall while her palm is on it — she is watching the children, and by the end of Shot B one corner of her mouth has come up a few millimetres and nothing else about her has changed.
Фрося: her brows go up and her eyes widen as the seam lights, then her eyes narrow again and her mouth flattens as she works out what she is looking at. By the end she has stopped blinking.
Вася: his mouth falls open at the light and stays open the whole way through; his eyebrows climb and do not come down.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Only Мама, Фрося and Вася. Бабушка-Яга, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job — not in the background, not at the frame edge, not out of focus.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Мама: in Shot A her right palm is flat on the wooden panel, fingers spread, and stays there until the latch knocks. In Shot B both her hands take the chest by its sides, lift it out of the niche and set it down on the planks at the near edge of the play mat. She carries nothing else at any point.
Фрося: both hands wrapped around her pencil and held flat against her chest, and they tighten on it as the panel opens. She reaches for nothing.
Вася: both hands hang loose and empty at his sides, then both come up and grip the front of his own shirt. He touches nothing.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, three shots, HARD CUTS only, no fades.

NOBODY SPEAKS in this job and no lips move in any shot.
