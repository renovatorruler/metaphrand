SCENE CONTEXT
A flying wooden mortar carrying an old woman crosses the sky above a human house and its shadow sweeps over the chimney. She is at FULL HUMAN SIZE here — she does not shrink until the spiral, later.

LOCATION MAP
Exterior, morning. A human-scale house roof with a brick chimney, seen from a distance. Open sky. The mortar enters from screen-left, flying toward the chimney.

Shot A opens on the roof and chimney in the distance with the flying mortar already visible entering from screen-left — a plain carved wooden mortar big enough for a grown woman to stand in, tapering to a stepped round foot, bare unpainted faceted wood, the old woman standing braced inside it, her birch broom held up in one hand and her rag помело in the other, a white curl of wind already turning around the mortar's body.
