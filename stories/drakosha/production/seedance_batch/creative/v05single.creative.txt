THE WORD TAKES HIM. From the start image: Вася sits on the floorboards facing camera, legs crossed, both hands on his knees, grinning wide. Four wooden blocks lie in a row directly in front of him, ALL FOUR ALREADY GLOWING warm gold and throwing their light onto his face and hands. More blocks lie scattered unlit to both sides. His woven pouch lies on its side at the right of frame.

CAMERA
The shot begins on the start image's framing. It holds there through everything Вася does, through the light, and through her arrival on the floor. From the moment she comes up onto her feet the camera does whatever it must to keep her whole and in view — she is a great deal taller standing than the boy was sitting, and the shot follows her rather than losing her.

DIALOGUE
**ВАСЯ:** *(быстро, довольный собой; потом тихо и неуверенно)* «МА… МА. МА́-МА! ВЖУХ?»

Only the last part is spoken here — he throws the word away quickly and then tries the other one, small and uncertain. The sounding-out belongs to the shot before this. Only Вася's lips move, and only while he is still Вася. After the light, NOBODY SPEAKS: Вася-мама does not say a word in this shot and her lips do not move. No offscreen voices.

SOUND
The four blocks hum faintly all through, a low warm tone that rises as the light does. Nothing else — no music, no voices.

WHAT HAPPENS
He is delighted with himself as it opens, and says the word QUICKLY and lightly — the hard part is behind him.

Then he stops, looks down at the glowing row and up again, and tries the other word: SMALL AND UNCERTAIN, half a question, expecting nothing.

AND NOTHING HAPPENS. He waits, the grin loosening at the edges.

THEN THE LIGHT ANSWERS. The glow in the four blocks swells, climbs off the floor and up over him, spreading OUT OF THE BLOCKS until it floods the frame and he cannot be seen inside it. Then it SINKS BACK DOWN into them as fast as it rose, and the room is there again exactly as it was.

AND SOMEBODY ELSE IS THERE. Where a small boy sat cross-legged, Вася-мама is now CROUCHED ON ALL FOURS in exactly that spot — a full-grown woman down on her hands and knees on the floorboards, palms flat on the boards just behind the four blocks, her head low and thrust forward over them. Вася is gone entirely.

SHE IS BADLY STARTLED, and holds it for a beat, dead still, down on the floor.

AND THEN SHE COMES UP — fast, all at once, jumping straight from her hands and knees onto her feet, the way somebody moves when they have just realised where they are. She ends standing where she crouched, upright and full height, a grown woman at Мама's height, a head taller than Фрося, standing on the spot where a small boy had been sitting.

A RIM OF WARM LIGHT TRACES HER WHOLE CROUCHED OUTLINE — over the kerchief, along her back, down each arm to the hands on the boards — so her entire silhouette is edged in gold. It is a rim AROUND her, not light falling on her face or her clothes. The four blocks still burn gold beneath her.

AND AS SHE COMES UP THE MAGIC LETS GO ALL AT ONCE. The rim around her and the light in the four blocks GO OUT TOGETHER — one steady fade, both at the same time, not one after the other and not in a snap — until the blocks are plain dark wood like the scattered ones and she is edged by nothing, lit only by the room's own warm lamplight. Everything is ordinary again except who is standing there.

THEN THE CAMERA MOVES IN ON HER FACE, closing steadily until her head fills the frame. She stands quite still and lets it arrive.

AND WHEN IT IS CLOSE, ONLY HER EYES MOVE. They slide all the way to one side, hold, and slide all the way back, hold — checking the room without daring to turn. HER HEAD DOES NOT TURN AND NOTHING ELSE ON HER FACE MOVES: not the brows, not the mouth, not the chin. Only the eyes.

THEN THE CAMERA MOVES BACK OUT, until she can be seen from the knees up with the room around her.

SHE LOOKS DOWN AT HER OWN HANDS — chin dropping, both hands coming up in front of her chest, turning them over, palms up then palms down.

AND THEN SHE GRINS: her head comes up and her face opens all at once into a wide gap-toothed grin with all the teeth showing. The shot ends on it.

REACTIONS
Вася starts on the wide open grin of the start image, chin up. Through the quick word the grin stays. Then his mouth closes and pushes forward and his brows lift as he tries the second word, eyes going up and sideways. In the beat that follows nothing moves except the grin loosening — corners easing down, brows staying up. As the light swells his eyes widen and his mouth opens, and that is the last of him.

Вася-мама arrives STARTLED and holds it for a beat — eyes round and huge with white showing all the way round, the shaggy eyebrows high under the kerchief, the mouth a small open O, the whole body frozen down on all fours. A grown woman crouched on a floor like a small child, with a small child's alarm on her face.

The alarm survives the jump and the light going out — nothing on her face changes as she comes up or as the glow drains. In the close, only the EYES move, sliding hard to one side and hard back, the brows staying high and the mouth staying open. Then, as she looks at her hands, the brows pull down and together and the mouth closes — the first sign of somebody working something out. And on the last beat it turns over into delight: the eyebrows shoot up again, the eyes go bright rather than frightened, and the mouth pulls back into a broad gap-toothed grin with all the teeth showing. Alarm, then working it out, then delight. Neither of them looks into the camera.

HANDS — Вася: both hands rest palm-down and open on his knees exactly as the start image has them, and they stay resting there through the word, the question and the beat, until the light covers him. Вася-мама: when she appears both her palms are flat on the floorboards in front of her, fingers spread, taking her weight on all fours. As she comes up they push off the boards and she ends standing with them hanging loose and open at her sides. They stay hanging there through the move in, through the eyes, and through the move back out. Only then do both hands come up in front of her chest, palms turning up and then over, fingers spread, and they stay held up in front of her to the end. They stay there while her eyes move. Then both come up in front of her, palms turning up and then over, fingers spread, as she looks down at them, and they stay held out in front of her to the end. Nothing is held in either hand at any point and neither of them touches a block.

PROPS
The four blocks stay exactly where they lie for the whole shot — before the light, under it and after it — and never move, slide, change places or vanish. Only their brightness changes: lit, blazing, still lit as she arrives, then fading to plain dark wood. The scattered blocks stay put and stay unlit. The pouch lies on its side at the right and stays there, untouched.

GLYPHS
NOT READABLE. The blocks are seen at a grazing angle, foreshortened almost to nothing, and for most of the shot glow brightly enough that their faces read as light rather than writing. Faded, they are plain dark wood at the same angle. Nothing here asks the audience to read them — the word was read in the shot before. No letter is legible, none is redrawn, and no block turns its face to camera.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind Вася, and behind Вася-мама after him — they occupy the same spot on the floor — runs the rope railing — turned posts with round red finials, rope slung between them — standing forward of the block wall with the gap visible behind it. Off to the left, further back, the shelf of metal canisters and the small stool with the bowl. String lights across the top left. The room is the SAME ROOM before the light and after: nothing added, nothing moved.

HOLD
Nobody enters or leaves the frame on foot — the only arrival happens inside the light. The blocks stay put. The pouch stays put. The room behind is identical in the first frame and the last.
