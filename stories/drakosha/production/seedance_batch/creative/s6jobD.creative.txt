SCENE CONTEXT
The grandmother tells them their gifts share one secret, and we turn to see it land on both children at once.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the line below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The open plank floor of the hall, clear of the table. Nobody changes position. The table and the rest of the family are behind the camera and never appear.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what their faces do. Nobody holds one expression.
Бабушка-Яга: the sly warmth has come back after the seriousness of the rule, and it widens as she says it — she is enjoying knowing something they do not.
Фрося: delight going out of her and suspicion arriving — by the end of the shot she is frowning.
Вася: still pleased and a beat behind, then he checks Фрося's face and copies it, the way he does everything.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Бабушка-Яга: BOTH HER HANDS ARE EMPTY AND STAY EMPTY. They are folded together low at her waist, one over the other, and they do not come up. She is carrying nothing, she is leaning on nothing, and nothing is propped against her.
Фрося: both hands wrapped around the pencil, held flat against her chest under her chin.
Вася: both hands hang loose and empty at his sides.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Мама, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job. Shot A: Бабушка-Яга alone. Shot B: Фрося and Вася, with the back of Бабушка-Яга's shoulder and kerchief as a large soft shape in the near corner — her shoulder only, never her face.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, two shots, ONE HARD CUT, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:05.5 — CLOSE ON БАБУШКА-ЯГА, hall soft behind her, gold tooth showing. She looks off to SCREEN RIGHT where the two children are. Slyly, pleased with herself:
БАБУШКА-ЯГА: «Пода́рки у вас ра́зные, а секре́т у них оди́н на двои́х.»
HARD CUT.

Shot B, 0:05.5–0:08 — ON ФРОСЯ AND ВАСЯ TOGETHER, shot OVER БАБУШКА-ЯГА'S SHOULDER from where she stands. The two of them side by side, Фрося holding the pencil against her chest. Neither speaks. We watch the word "секрет" land: Фрося's pleasure goes out and her brows come down; Вася looks at Фрося first, sees her face change, and only then loses his own.

OPTICS
29° short telephoto throughout, close framing by lens reach not proximity. Shot B carries an oversized soft shoulder in the near corner. Rectilinear, no fisheye, no vignette, no iris.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind БАБУШКА-ЯГА: the hall running away, soft and out of focus — never red brick.
Behind ФРОСЯ AND ВАСЯ: the hall running away from them, soft and out of focus — never red brick.

SOUND
Low room tone. Her one line, then two and a half seconds carrying only the room. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.

SHOT RULES
Only Бабушка-Яга speaks. Фрося and Вася say nothing at all in this job, and in Shot B no lips move.
Бабушка-Яга is at her small домовой size and has NO broom in any frame.
Вася holds NOTHING. Фрося holds only the pencil.
The walls are ENORMOUS pale sandstone boulder-blocks, each bigger than a character's whole body — never small or normal-scale masonry.
NO fire, NO chest, NO table, NO food.
