САЛАТ — the second word, written, spelled and SEALED in one take. Фрося sits frame-right, writing on the paper against her knee, exactly as the start image has her. The left half of the frame is the open floor and the soft far room, and it stays clear — the letters will live there.

DIALOGUE
**ФРОСЯ:** *(деловито)* «С. А. Л. А. Т. Сала́т!» «И то́чка.»
Two lines, with a clear pause between them: the spelling and the word first; then, after she has looked at what she made, the seal.

CAMERA
Locked off at the start image's framing. It does not move.

WHAT HAPPENS
She is already writing when the shot opens. She works the pencil across the page in small sure strokes — faster and more confident than the first word — and sounds out each letter as she makes it, a beat between letters, her mouth shaping each one clearly. On the whole word her head lifts a little and her eyes go to the open space to her left, where the word hangs. She holds there a beat, pleased, pencil raised.

THE SEAL. Her eyes come back down to the page. She says the second line, and on its stressed syllable her fist brings the pencil down in one deliberate stroke and presses the point into the page. Only after that contact, a bloom of warm golden light swells up from where the point meets the paper, wraps her fist, lights her face from below, and grows until it fills the frame. The clip ends inside the brightness. The paper is tilted toward her and its surface is never seen.

REACTIONS
Фрося starts with the small satisfied smile of the start image, eyes down on the page, brows level. As she spells, the brows draw in a fraction with each letter and the mouth works through the sounds — businesslike, not straining. On the whole word the brows lift, the smile widens, and her eyes come up and off to the left, bright. For the seal her eyes drop to the page, the smile presses thin for one deliberate instant on the press, and then as the light rises the brows release and the eyes widen, catching the glow. She never looks into the camera.

HANDS — Фрося: her right hand grips the pencil in a child's grip and makes small writing strokes against the page through the spelling, lifts clear on the word, then drives the point down in one stroke for the seal and holds the pressure as the light comes; her left hand holds the paper steady against her knee and stays planted there.

PROPS
The pencil: a short faceted honey-wood stub with one sharpened dark point at the writing end and a blunt, flat, bare wooden top above her fist. The paper: a plain sheet held against her knee, tilted away from camera.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind Фрося is the back wall, the kitchen end of the hall, far out of focus: the stove glow, the sock chairs, the string lights as soft discs. The left half of the frame is bare floorboards running back into the soft room, and nothing enters it.

GLYPHS
NOT READABLE. The page is never visible and nothing legible appears anywhere in the frame; the letters the audience sees are the author's artwork, composited into the left half afterwards on her voice.

HOLD
She does not stand or turn, and does not look into the camera. The camera does not move and focus does not shift. The pencil keeps its one point. The left half of the frame stays empty until the bloom overtakes it. The bloom comes from the pencil's contact with the page and from nowhere else.
