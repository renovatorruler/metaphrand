SCENE CONTEXT
Straight on. Фрося asks whether the car counts, the grandmother names the condition, then calls the boy over and gives him his.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the lines below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The open plank floor of the hall, clear of the table. Nobody is shown travelling: Вася ARRIVES INTO FRAME on a cut and is never seen crossing a room. The table and the rest of the family are behind the camera and never appear.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what her face does. Nobody holds one expression.
Фрося: disbelief turning to demand — she is not asking politely, she wants it confirmed; then when the condition lands her face tightens.
Бабушка-Яга: enjoying herself, and the sly warmth widens as she names the price.
Вася: pure appetite as he arrives, then puzzled — he expected an object and is getting a breath.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Бабушка-Яга: BOTH HER HANDS ARE EMPTY AND STAY EMPTY. In Shot B they are folded together low at her waist, one over the other, the way an old woman stands when she is enjoying herself. In Shot C her right hand comes up LARGE into the near foreground, palm toward her own face, and one finger crooks twice; her left hand stays folded at her waist. In Shot D both hands are planted on her own knees as she bends to blow. She is carrying nothing, she is leaning on nothing, and nothing is propped against her.
Фрося: both hands wrapped around the pencil and held flat against her chest, knuckles under her chin, the way a child holds something she has decided is hers. They do not move.
Вася: both hands hang loose and empty at his sides. He is given a breath, not an object, and he touches nothing.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Мама, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job. Shot A: Фрося alone. Shot B: Бабушка-Яга alone. Shot C: Бабушка-Яга, with Вася entering the bottom edge. Shot D: Бабушка-Яга and Вася only.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, four shots, HARD CUTS only, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:02.1 — CLOSE ON ФРОСЯ, the pencil now held in both hands against her chest. Not a polite question — she wants it confirmed:
ФРОСЯ: «И маши́ну мо́жно!»
HARD CUT.

Shot B, 0:02.1–0:06.3 — CLOSE ON БАБУШКА-ЯГА, hall soft behind her, gold tooth showing. Slyly, taking her time, because this is the price and she knows it:
БАБУШКА-ЯГА: «Суме́ешь и́мя написа́ть — бу́дет тебе́ и маши́на.»
HARD CUT.

Shot C, 0:06.3–0:08.6 — MEDIUM-CLOSE ON БАБУШКА-ЯГА, chest up. Her hand comes up LARGE in the near foreground and one finger crooks, twice. Her face is behind it, in focus. ВАСЯ ARRIVES at the bottom left edge of frame — only his head enters, and he is never seen walking:
БАБУШКА-ЯГА: «А ты, кавале́р, иди́ сюда́.»
HARD CUT.

Shot D, 0:08.6–0:13 — TIGHT. Her face bent low from the upper left and the TOP OF ВАСЯ'S HEAD filling the lower right — two heads and nothing else in frame. Her lips are pursed and her eyes are down on his crown. She blows. A warm ripple of golden light runs through his hair from front to back, and it fades out completely. In the last two seconds he lifts his head and we see his face for the first time: eyebrows up, waiting for something to happen to him. Nobody speaks and no lips move.

OPTICS
29° short telephoto throughout, close framing by lens reach not proximity. Shot D is the tightest in the job. Rectilinear, no fisheye, no vignette, no iris.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind ФРОСЯ and behind БАБУШКА-ЯГА: the hall running away, soft and out of focus — never red brick.
Shot D has no readable background at all, only warm blur.

SOUND
Low room tone. The two spoken lines, then a short warm ВЖУХ on the breath. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.

SHOT RULES
Фрося speaks once and Бабушка-Яга twice. Вася says nothing at all in this job.
Бабушка-Яга is at her small домовой size and has NO broom in any frame.
Each character's lips move ONLY for her own line in her own shot. In Shot D no lips move at all.
Вася is given NOTHING to hold — his gift is a breath, not an object.
The walls are ENORMOUS pale sandstone boulder-blocks, each bigger than a character's whole body — never small or normal-scale masonry.
NO fire, NO chest, NO table, NO food.
