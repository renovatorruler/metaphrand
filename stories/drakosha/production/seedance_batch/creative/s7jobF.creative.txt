SCENE CONTEXT
The girl says she already knows all her letters, and her mother answers her with the only fact that matters.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the lines below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The back corner of the hall, the open chest on the planks between the mother and the two children. Nobody changes position.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what a face does. Describe the muscles, not the mood.
Фрося: she starts with her eyes down and her mouth flat, then her head comes up sharply and her brows go up with it — this is a protest, not a question. By the end of her mother's answer her mouth has closed and flattened again and her eyes have dropped.
Мама: she starts level — mouth closed, brows low — and she does not hurry or blink much. On the second half of the line one corner of her mouth comes up a few millimetres and stops there, and that is the whole change.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Only Фрося and Мама. Вася, Бабушка-Яга, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Фрося: both hands are planted flat on the play mat either side of herself, and she pushes up on them as her head comes up. She holds nothing.
Мама: both hands rest on her own knees and do not move at all while she answers. She holds nothing and points at nothing.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, two shots, ONE HARD CUT, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:03.5 — CLOSE ON ФРОСЯ, low, at her own height. Her head comes up sharply toward her mother. This is a complaint:
ФРОСЯ: «Но я́-то все бу́квы зна́ю!»
HARD CUT.

Shot B, 0:03.5–0:10 — ON МАМА from the children's height. She holds Фрося's look and does not raise her voice at all. She agrees with her, and then she does not:
МАМА: «Зна́ешь. А брат твой пока́ зна́ет во́семь.»

OPTICS
29° short telephoto throughout, close framing by lens reach not proximity, razor focus on the eyes. Two locked positions. Rectilinear, no fisheye, no vignette, no iris.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind ФРОСЯ in Shot A: the room running away from her toward the table end — strung coloured bulbs overhead and the rope railing of the ramp at the frame edge, soft. She has her back to the room and is facing the back wall.
Behind МАМА in Shot B: the BACK WALL — the open niche behind her shoulder, the kitchen end soft beyond.

SOUND
Low room tone under everything. The two lines. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.

SHOT RULES
Each speaks once, in her own shot, and lips move only for her own line.
The letters are CYRILLIC — no Latin letters anywhere in frame.
NO glow, NO magic and NO light effects anywhere in this job.
