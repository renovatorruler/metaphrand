SCENE CONTEXT
The boy gets his own pouch of letters and says so, his sister turns the whole thing into a demand, and across the corner the grandmother enjoys it.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the lines below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The back corner of the hall. The children are on the play mat by the open chest. Бабушка-Яга is sitting some way off to the left in one of the knitted sock armchairs at the little round tea table, where she has been the whole scene. Nobody walks anywhere.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what a face does. Describe the muscles, not the mood.
Вася: cheeks up and eyes creased, the plainest happiness in the episode — he has been given a thing that is his, and his face does not complicate it at all.
Фрося: she starts with her brows down and her mouth flat, still sulking about the eight; then her eyes snap sideways to Вася and her whole face changes — brows up, mouth open, leaning at him. By the end she is not sulking at all, she is organising him.
Бабушка-Яга: she starts watching them with her mouth closed, and it goes to a laugh she is keeping quiet — her shoulders move first, then her eyes crease almost shut. She does not say a word.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Shot A: Вася, plus МАМА'S HANDS ONLY in the first two seconds — her face is never in frame. Shot B: Фрося alone. Shot C: Бабушка-Яга alone. Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Мама: in the first two seconds of Shot A her hands knot the pouch onto Вася's shoelace belt, pull the knot tight and let go. They leave frame and she is not seen again.
Вася: both hands come up and wrap around the pouch, hugging it against his own side. The pouch stays tied to the shoelace belt at his hip and the long loose end of the lace hangs down.
Фрося: her right hand grips her pencil and her left hand is planted on the mat; on her line the right hand comes up and ONE FINGER points straight at Вася and stays pointing.
Бабушка-Яга: her right hand is closed around the shaft of her broom, its head planted on the floorboards beside the armchair, and she is leaning a little of her weight on it as she sits. Her left hand rests flat on her own knee.
