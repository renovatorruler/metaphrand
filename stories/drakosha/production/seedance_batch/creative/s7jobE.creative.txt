SCENE CONTEXT
The mother tells them the letters come one at a time, and the girl looks past the eight in front of her at the hundreds still shut in the chest.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the line below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The back corner of the hall, the open chest on the planks between the mother and the two children, the row of eight tiles on the floor in front of them. Nobody changes position.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what a face does. Describe the muscles, not the mood.
Мама: she nods once, small and definite, at the start, and her face does not soften after it. Her mouth stays level and her brows stay low. There is nothing sour in it — she is a woman who has already worked out how this goes.
Вася: his cheeks are up and he is grinning through the whole first half. This is praise and he takes it plainly, and it does not leave his face.
Фрося: she starts looking down at the row she can already read, and her eyes travel past it and INTO the open chest, and stay there. Her brows come down and her mouth flattens as she takes in how many are in there. By the end of the shot she is not looking at her mother at all.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Only Мама, Фрося and Вася. Бабушка-Яга, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Мама: both hands rest on her own knees and stay there for the whole job. She holds nothing and points at nothing.
Фрося: both hands folded in her lap at the start; by the end her right hand has come up and gripped her own left wrist. She touches no tile.
Вася: both hands planted flat on the plank floor either side of the row of tiles, holding his weight as he leans over them.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, two shots, ONE HARD CUT, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:07 — ON МАМА from the children's height, the open chest low in the near foreground with the tiles inside catching the light. She nods once to Вася, then goes on:
МАМА: «Э́ти во́семь букв ты уже́ зна́ешь. А да́льше я бу́ду дава́ть вам по одно́й но́вой.»
HARD CUT.

Shot B, 0:07–0:14 — ON ФРОСЯ AND ВАСЯ TOGETHER, sitting on the play mat in front of the open chest, the row of eight tiles on the planks between them and camera. Вася is looking down at the row he can read. Фрося is looking past it, INTO the chest, at everything still in there. Neither of them speaks and neither one's lips move. МАМА'S VOICE CONTINUES OFFSCREEN and she is NOT in frame:
МАМА (OFFSCREEN): «Вы́учишь её — полу́чите сле́дующую.»

OPTICS
29° short telephoto throughout, close framing by lens reach not proximity, razor focus on the eyes. Two locked positions, no camera move during a spoken line. Rectilinear, no fisheye, no vignette, no iris.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind МАМА in Shot A: the BACK WALL — the open niche behind her shoulder, the kitchen end with its stove and shelf of cups soft beyond.
Behind ФРОСЯ AND ВАСЯ in Shot B: the room running away from them toward the table end — strung coloured bulbs overhead and the rope railing of the ramp at the frame edge, soft. They have their backs to the room and are facing the back wall.

SOUND
Low room tone under everything. Her line running unbroken across both shots. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.

SHOT RULES
Only Мама speaks. Фрося and Вася say nothing at all in this job and their lips never move. In Shot B Мама is not in frame and no lips move anywhere.
The chest stands OPEN in both shots and is visibly still nearly full of tiles.
The letters are CYRILLIC — no Latin letters anywhere in frame.
The ball of green wool stays BEHIND the children and out of the space between them and the chest.
NO glow, NO magic and NO light effects anywhere in this job.
