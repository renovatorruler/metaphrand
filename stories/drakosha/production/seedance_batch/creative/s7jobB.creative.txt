SCENE CONTEXT
The mother has the two children put their hands on the chest, and binds both their gifts to it.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the line below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The back corner of the hall, the chest standing on the planks at the edge of the play mat between the mother and the two children. Nobody changes position.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what a face does. Describe the muscles, not the mood.
Мама: she starts with her brows low and her mouth closed and level, and it does not change when the glow comes — she is not looking at it. Her eyes go to the children's faces at the start of the shot and stay there to the end.
Фрося: her brows come down and her jaw sets as she is told what to do; she does it slowly. At the glow her eyes go wide and her mouth opens.
Вася: his eyebrows start up and his mouth opens wider as it goes on. He watches Фрося's hands rather than his mother's and copies what she does a beat late, every time; by the end he has forgotten to close his mouth at all.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Only Мама, Фрося and Вася. Бабушка-Яга, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear anywhere in this job.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Мама: both hands rest on her own knees while she speaks. Then her left hand comes down flat over Фрося's fingers and her right hand comes down flat over Вася's palm, and both stay there, pressing lightly.
Фрося: her right hand lays the pencil down flat on the left of the closed lid and two fingers stay resting on it; her left hand is planted on the mat beside her, holding her weight.
Вася: his right palm comes down flat on the right of the lid, fingers spread; his left hand is planted on the mat beside him.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, three shots, HARD CUTS only, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:05 — ON МАМА from the children's height, the closed chest low in the near foreground. She is looking down at both of them. Level, and leaving no room for argument:
МАМА: «Фро́ся, положи́ каранда́ш на кры́шку. Ва́ся, ру́ку ря́дом.»
HARD CUT.

Shot B, 0:05–0:09 — STRAIGHT DOWN ONTO THE LID. Фрося's hand lays the pencil flat on the left and keeps two fingers on it. Вася's palm comes down flat on the right. No dialogue.
HARD CUT.

Shot C, 0:09–0:13 — STILL LOOKING DOWN ONTO THE LID, all three pairs of hands in frame and nothing else. МАМА's hands come down over the children's. The hasp latch snaps shut by itself. The SAME warm glow runs once along the pencil and once across the back of Вася's hand, at the same moment and in the same colour, and goes out. Nobody speaks.

OPTICS
Shot A is 29° short telephoto at the children's height. Shots B and C look straight down at the lid with the same lens reach, hands filling the frame. Rectilinear, no fisheye, no vignette, no iris.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind МАМА in Shot A: the BACK WALL — the open niche behind her shoulder, the kitchen end soft beyond it.
Behind ФРОСЯ AND ВАСЯ: they are not seen against a wall in this job; Shots B and C look straight down and show only the chest lid, hands and a little of the play mat. This framing shows no wall, and that is deliberate.

SOUND
Low room tone. Her line, the small knock of the pencil on the lid, then the snap of the hasp and one short shared resonance under the glow. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.

SHOT RULES
Only Мама speaks, and only in Shot A. Фрося and Вася say nothing at all in this job and their lips never move.
The glow appears ONCE, in Shot C, on the pencil and on Вася's hand together. Nothing glows before that moment.
