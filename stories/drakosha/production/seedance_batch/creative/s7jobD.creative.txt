SCENE CONTEXT
The boy reads the eight letters aloud, one at a time, and each one answers him.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the line below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
The back corner of the hall, the row of eight letter tiles lying on the planks in front of the play mat. Nobody changes position.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what a face does. Describe the muscles, not the mood.
Вася: he starts with his brows drawn together and his lips pressed shut, working at it. As the tiles begin answering him his brows climb and his mouth opens wider on each letter, and by the last one his cheeks are up and his eyes are creased — he is grinning with his whole face. This is the one thing in the episode he is better at than his sister, and his face finds that out during the shot.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Вася is the ONLY character in this job. Мама, Фрося, Бабушка-Яга, Папа, Руся and Муся do not appear — not in the background, not at the frame edge, not out of focus, not as a hand.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Вася: his right index finger rests on the plank floor just below the first tile and slides along the row, stopping under each one as he names it and moving on. His left hand is planted flat on the mat beside him, holding his weight as he leans over the row. He picks nothing up and touches no tile.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, two shots, ONE HARD CUT, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:05.5 — STRAIGHT DOWN ONTO THE ROW OF EIGHT TILES on the plank floor, filling the frame. Small square pale-wood tiles, each carved with one Cyrillic letter, reading left to right: А О М С К Т Л Б. ВАСЯ'S FINGER slides along beneath them. As each letter is spoken, THAT TILE and only that tile lights up warm from inside for about half a second and goes out again, in strict left-to-right order. His face is not in frame; his voice is offscreen:

Low room tone. His eight letters, spaced, with a small warm chime under each tile as it lights. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.
