SCENE CONTEXT
Continuous from the table. Бабушка-Яга takes the pencil out of her pocket and puts it into Фрося's hand.

ALL DIALOGUE IS IN RUSSIAN. Speak exactly the line below, in Russian, and nothing else. No English anywhere.

WHERE WE ARE
On the open plank floor of the hall, clear of the table. Nobody is shown travelling: the first shot is tight on a pocket and carries no room, and when we widen we are simply here. The dinner table and the rest of the family are behind the camera and are never seen.

REACTIONS — WHAT THE FACES DO
A reference sheet fixes who a character IS, not what her face does. Nobody holds one expression.
Бабушка-Яга: unhurried and pleased with herself, and it deepens as she watches it land.
Фрося: waiting, then her face opens — mouth falling open, eyes widening, brows going up — and by the end she has gone completely still and is not blinking.

HANDS — WHAT EVERY PAIR IS DOING
Бабушка-Яга: her right hand is inside her skirt pocket in Shot A and comes out with the pencil pinched between finger and thumb. In Shot B that hand lowers the pencil into the girl's palm and her left hand comes in under it. In Shot D both her hands are closed around Фрося's fist, fingers laced over the back of it. HER HANDS ARE FULL OF THIS CHILD'S HAND FROM SHOT B ONWARD and there is nothing else in either of them at any point.
Фрося: in Shot A she is not in frame. In Shot B her left palm is open and flat, held out waist-high, and her right hand hangs empty at her side. In Shot C both hands are down out of frame. In Shot D her right hand has closed into a fist around the pencil and her left hand is underneath it, supporting it.

WHO IS IN EACH SHOT — AND NOBODY ELSE
Only Бабушка-Яга and Фрося exist in this job. Мама, Папа, Вася, Руся and Муся do not appear, not in the background, not at the frame edge, not out of focus.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, four shots, HARD CUTS only, no fades.

Shot A, 0:00–0:02.5 — TIGHT ON HER POCKET. The frame holds only her skirt and shawl from chest to hip: a patch pocket in the cloth, her hand inside it, rummaging. NO face, NO floor, NO room, NO other person. Her hand comes out holding a short stubby wooden pencil. No dialogue.
HARD CUT.

Shot B, 0:02.5–0:06 — CLOSE ON HANDS ONLY, no faces. Фрося's open palm below, Бабушка-Яга's hand above lowering the pencil into it and laying it down flat. The pencil is SIZED FOR THE GIRL'S HAND — it lies across her palm with room to spare, small enough to tuck behind an ear. She speaks over this, and her line runs on across the next two shots without stopping:
БАБУШКА-ЯГА: «Каку́ю штуко́вину на бе́лом све́те душа́ пожела́ет, напи́шешь и́мя, поста́вишь то́чку, и бу́дет по-тво́ему.»
HARD CUT.

Shot C, 0:06–0:09 — CLOSE ON ФРОСЯ, the hall soft behind her. She is looking down at her own hand. Her mouth falls open, her eyes widen, her brows lift. Бабушка-Яга's voice continues OFFSCREEN over her — Фрося's own lips do not move at all.
HARD CUT.

Shot D, 0:09–0:12 — CLOSE ON HANDS, the SAME framing as Shot B so the cut reads as the same hands a moment later. Фрося's hand has closed around the pencil; Бабушка-Яга brings both her hands over the girl's fist and closes them round it. ON THE LAST FOUR WORDS OF THE LINE a warm golden glow comes up between their hands and light escapes between the fingers. Nothing glows before this moment.

OPTICS
Tight throughout — 29° short telephoto, close framing by lens reach. Shots B and D are hands filling the frame with the background dissolved. Rectilinear, no fisheye, no vignette, no iris.

BACKGROUNDS
Behind ФРОСЯ: the hall running away from her, soft and out of focus — never red brick.
Shots A, B and D have no readable background at all: cloth in A, dissolved warmth in B and D.

SOUND
Low room tone carried over from the table and thinning away. Cloth and the small knock of the pencil. Then her line. No music, no subtitles, no narrator.

SHOT RULES
Only Бабушка-Яга speaks. Фрося says nothing at all in this job.
Бабушка-Яга is at her small домовой size and has NO broom in any frame — both her hands are busy.
Фрося's hands are EMPTY until Shot B. She is not holding a pencil, or anything else, in Shot A. She receives the pencil in this job and has nothing before it.
Her lips move ONLY in Shot B. In Shots A, C and D no lips move at all.
The walls are ENORMOUS pale sandstone boulder-blocks, each bigger than a character's whole body — never small or normal-scale masonry.
NO fire, NO chest, NO table, NO food.
