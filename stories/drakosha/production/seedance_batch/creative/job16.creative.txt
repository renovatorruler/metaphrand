SCENE CONTEXT
The old woman commands her mortar; wind spins her whole rig into a tight spiral that shrinks it tiny, and the spiral drops into the chimney.

LOCATION MAP
The sky directly above the human house's brick chimney. Morning light from screen-right.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
Shot A opens close on Яга in the hovering mortar above the chimney, still at full human size; she plants her feet wider and grips the rim with both hands.

FORMAT MODE
CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE. Three shots. HARD CUTS only.
Shot A, 0:00 to 0:02: close on her bracing in the mortar, full human size; she calls out in Russian, commanding and cheerful: «Ну, роди́мые, закрути́лись!»
HARD CUT.
Shot B, 0:02 to 0:06: distant view of the roof: the white curl already turning around her winds up hard and becomes a proper little tornado — a tight spinning funnel of air and leaves that swallows the mortar, the woman and her broom completely. The funnel narrows and shrinks as it turns, until what is left is small enough to fit down a chimney.
HARD CUT.
Shot C, 0:06 to 0:08: from above the chimney looking down: the little tornado leans over the chimney mouth and pours down into it, tail last, and is gone. The roof stands empty and quiet.

OPTICS
Shot A: 29° short telephoto portrait, her face and grip sharp, sky dissolved behind. Her gold tooth shows when she calls out.
Shots B and C: 47° standard normal, deep focus, stable straight lines.
No lens drift inside any shot.

CAMERA
Static locked frames. The spiral does its work inside a motionless frame.

PHYSICS
The tornado is wind made visible: it accelerates its rotation, pulls in stray leaves, and narrows as it spins, exactly as a real funnel tightens. Going down the chimney it behaves like a funnel finding a hole — it leans, narrows to fit, and pours in. Nothing explodes, nothing flashes; it is wind craft, not magic sparkle.

LIGHTING
Bright morning sun from screen-right; the spiral catches light on its edges. No flat lighting.

AUDIO
Her single commanding Russian line in shot A; then wind winding upward in pitch through shot B; a soft whoosh and sudden quiet in shot C. No music, no subtitles.

SHOT RULES
Only the scripted line is spoken. No sparkles, no glowing magic effects — wind and air only. The roof is empty in the final second. SCALE LOCK: she enters this shot at her flying size and the shrink happens ONLY inside the tornado in shot B, hidden by it. She is never seen at two sizes in one frame, and she is never seen inside the chimney.
Dialogue lock: only the lips of Яга move, and only during her command in shot A. No offscreen voices.
