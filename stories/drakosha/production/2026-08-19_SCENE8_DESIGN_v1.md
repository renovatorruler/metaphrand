# Scene 8 — design v1 (cards as black boxes)

SH102–SH140, lines 52–66. Four escalating uses of the magic: СОК works, САЛАТ backfires as comedy, МАК lands as feeling, МАМА is the catastrophe. Designed 2026-08-19 with the transformation/lettering system treated as sealed cards.

## Card interfaces (fixed, internals arrive later)

**WRITE(word)** — IN: cut from any room shot of Фрося starting to write, paper unseen. INTERNALS: OTS plate → sound-out (letters light on her voice) → whole word lights → «Точка.» → tap → dot rises → flash. OUT: flash-cut to the room WITH THE OBJECT ALREADY THERE (f27→f28 pattern). Duration = her recorded line + ~3s tail.

**READ-ASSEMBLE(word)** — Вася's version. IN: cut from room. INTERNALS: transformation space, chips fall/settle → he names letters, chips light on his voice → whole word → «ВЖУХ!». OUT: for objects, as WRITE; for self-transformation, chains into TRANSFORM.

**TRANSFORM(target)** — IN: from READ-ASSEMBLE's ВЖУХ or in-room ВЖУХ. INTERNALS: INTRO (arms up, ribbons start) → DISSOLVE (reused from cat animation) → REVEAL(target card). OUT: flash-cut to room, transformed Вася in place. REVERSE = flash only (no card), cutting back to normal Вася — keeps the second «Вжух!» snappy.
