# How long each spelling beat actually is (2026-08-20)

Measured off the approved recordings with word-level timestamps, not estimated. The shot length for a letter beat is set by the voice, and it differs per word.

## СОК — `line52_FROSYA`, 7.52s total

Full line: «Так, пробуем. Что можно написать? С-О-К. Сок!»

| | from | to | length |
|---|---|---|---|
| «Так, пробуем. Что можно написать?» | 0.00 | 3.10 | 3.10 |
| **С** | 3.41 | 3.96 | 0.55 |
| **О** | 3.96 | 4.94 | 0.98 |
| **К** | 4.94 | 5.84 | 0.90 |
| *(pause)* | 5.84 | 6.18 | 0.34 |
| **«Сок!»** | 6.18 | 7.28 | 1.10 |

**The spelling beat is 3.87 seconds** — first `С` to the end of «Сок!». With a quarter-second of air before the first letter and a beat after the word, the shot wants **about 4.4 seconds**, so a **5-second take** holds it comfortably and a 4-second take is tight.

Note the cadence is not even: 0.55, then 0.98, then 0.90. She slows down as she goes. Lighting the tiles on a regular pulse would fight her.

The question part of the line — the first 3.1 seconds — belongs to the earlier shots, not to this one.

### Two things the recordings say that the script does not

1. **САЛАТ is never spelled.** The shooting script has «С-А-Л-А-Т. САЛАТ!»; the recording is «Салат. Салат.» Either the beat is a plain double-take of the word — which is fine, and is the escalation, since she has stopped needing to sound it out — or it needs re-recording. It cannot be cut as a letter beat from what we have.
2. **Вася reads in syllables, not letters** — «Ма-ма», not «М-А-М-А». That is correct for a child at his stage and it means his tiles light **two at a time**, in pairs, not one by one. Any card built for him has to light `МА` then `МА`.
