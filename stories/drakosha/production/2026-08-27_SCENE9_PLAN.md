## Transformations — how the whole system works, and what it means for scene 10

### The two gifts are opposites, and that is the engine

Яга's rule, SP075: *«Слово вслух прочтёшь да "ВЖУХ!" крикнешь — кого оно называет, в того и обернёшься. А второй раз вжухнешь — снова Васей станешь.»* Read the word aloud, shout ВЖУХ, become what it names; shout it again to come back.

And SP076: *«Подарки у вас разные, а секрет у них один на двоих.»*

So **Фрося writes and a thing appears. Вася reads aloud and becomes it.** Writing makes; reading transforms. In a show about learning to read that is the lesson wearing a plot, and it is also why the two children never stop fighting: her power produces objects she controls, his power is himself and can only ever be aimed at somebody.

Scene 10 is that difference in one argument. He turns *into* an obstacle. She *makes* a thing and drops it on him.

### The wasp appears twice, and that decides the question

SP158 is a wasp attacking his sister. SP190 is the same wasp doing the bravest thing he does in the episode — taking the end of a thread through a gap to help.

**The script distinguishes them by sound, not by appearance.** SP158: *«резкое злое "з-з-з-з"»* — sharp, angry buzzing. SP190: *«мягкий ровный ВЖУХ; спокойное "з-з-з"»* — a soft even ВЖУХ and calm buzzing. Even the transformation itself is written as sounding different. The ВЖУХ has a whole register already: shouted, muffled under the tank at SP156, soft at SP169 and SP199.

So the author's own script has already answered it. **The form is constant; the mood is carried by sound and behaviour.** That is one locked card per form, and it is the design the script assumes.

### Why the card can be locked without flattening the performance

**The brows are the identity anchor and they are already written in.** SP146 gives the cat «огромными лохматыми бровями»; SP158 gives the wasp «своими огромными бровями». They survive every transformation, and they are how the audience knows the animal is still Вася.

**The brows are also his emotional instrument.** Every emotion the gate declares for him is differentiated by what his brows do — down and in for anger, up for shock, drawn together for sulk. The same feature that fixes his identity carries his mood, so the card locks the silhouette and the shot supplies the feeling.

**Reuse is immediate.** ОСА pays for its card twice inside EP1. EP2 adds ВОЛ, СОВА and ВОЛК; one card per form scales to that, three cards per form does not.

**Mood variants would duplicate the shot.** A cowering-cat card and an angry-cat card would be two references doing what one emotion declaration already does — and the gate refuses any shot that fails to name its emotion and match the anatomy.

The real risk is a locked card inviting a frozen face, which is what happened to Вася-мама in V09. The fix there was not more references. It was continuous-action language and moving the settle into the last half second, and it is the same fix here.
