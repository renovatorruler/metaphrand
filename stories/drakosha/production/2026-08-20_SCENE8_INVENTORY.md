## WHAT WE NEED

**1 · The «Точка» recording.** The only missing audio in the scene. Three short takes in Фрося's voice. No credits.

**2 · Мама's voice.** Still undecided between takes 21/22/23/24. It gates line59 and line65 here, and re-dubbing every Мама line in scenes 1-6.

**3 · Вася's chips.** There is no `alphabet/vasya/` at all. His МАМА tiles have no artwork, and the same is true for every later word he reads. This is the largest missing asset in the scene.

**4 · A master for the room shots.** We have room plates but no master of the birthday work zone with the tea table and the party behind it — the layout the room map fixes. Every SH102-122 shot needs to start from that geography. The cheapest source is the last frame of a scene 7 clip, which already has the right room, the right people and the right light.

**5 · A corrected Точка shot.** The one generated is the wrong shot — it shows the paper and the mark being made, and it was built on an upscaled crop. What the design calls for is a closer framing where we do NOT see the paper and she simply says it.
