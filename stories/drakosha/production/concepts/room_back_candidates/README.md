# SET-HOME-ROOM-01 — back-of-the-fireplace concepts

The family lives INSIDE the bricked-up fireplace. The hall is the fireplace's own cavity, and the giant sandstone boulder-blocks are its masonry seen at their size. There is no fireplace standing in their room — the room is the fireplace.

So the two ends of the hall are named for the fireplace, which cannot move, and never for the furniture, which can:

- **FRONT** — the riveted iron plate sealing the hearth mouth against the human room, with the small cleanout hatch set into it. Bound in the registry as `@ROOM_FRONT`, `@ROOM_FRONT_LOW`, `@ROOM_FRONT_HATCH` (author plates `SET-HOME-ROOM-01_author_master_v4_hatch_cradle-clear`, `_author_low_angle`, `_author_hatch_angle`).
- **BACK** — the brick rear of the fireplace. Bound as `@ROOM_BACK` (author plate `SET-HOME-ROOM-02_author_far_wall_v2`).

These files were previously called "reverse" views. That word is banned in this project: the reverse of the front is the back and the reverse of the back is the front, so no statement written with it can be checked. `Drakosha_SeedanceBatch.res` carries the same rule in a comment on `propToken`.

Everything in this directory was generated with ChatGPT native image generation on 2026-08-16 from `SET-HOME-ROOM-01_author_master_v4_hatch_cradle-clear.png`. They are review candidates only. They are not bound production references and do not supersede the approved author plates unless the author approves them and they are formally promoted into the registry.

## Current review state

`SET-HOME-ROOM-01_back_combined_matched-floor_candidate_v10.png` is the current review candidate. Versions 1–9 are retained as iteration history, not competing finals. The side-by-side image compares the approved front plate with v9 and predates the final floor adjustment in v10.

The v10 design decisions are:

- one large back-of-room view containing both the kitchen/dinette and the children's play/sitting area;
- the camera is already beyond the underfloor ramp, so no ramp or staircase is visible in this plate;
- the actual underfloor route remains one broken-off wooden floorboard used as a ramp, never stairs;
- furniture is made from visibly repurposed human objects, including adult-scale sock chairs and a human matchbox used as the children's toy box;
- the near sock chairs must read larger than the more distant couch;
- a wall niche/hatch is large enough for Mama to open it and pull out the secret letter chest;
- broad, aged floorboards match the approved front plate: pronounced grooves, staggered butt joints, restrained texture, and a visible knot;
- the visual treatment remains clean low-poly rather than photorealistic.

## Scale and continuity guardrails

- Frosya is approximately 3–4 inches tall; Vasya and the family scale from the established character registry.
- Ordinary human objects must read as enormous at domovoy scale. Do not resize a matchbox into a domovoy-made miniature.
- The family's home is inside the bricked-up fireplace cavity. The iron front plate and the brick back wall are fixed geography.
- The front author plate remains `stories/drakosha/ep1prod/scene1/references/SET-HOME-ROOM-01_author_master_v4_hatch_cradle-clear.png`.
