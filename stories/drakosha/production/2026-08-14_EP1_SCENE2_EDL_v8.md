## Known and accepted

Our reads run longer than the picture in three places, so the lip sync drifts within a line: Папа's birthday line is 5.2s against 3.4s of mouth, Фрося's «Цветны́е!» 2.45s against 2.10s, her «Спаси́бо, мам!» 3.35s against 2.65s. Мама's two lines are shorter than their pictures and sit comfortably. Fixing this means either re-rendering the long lines tighter or adjusting the picture; it is a single pass across all seven, not a patch.

Muting the original across E and F took their sound effects with it. The tin lid coming free and the core drawing on paper both need replacing in the finishing pass.

From 31s the bed is silent between lines. A room-tone loop under the full 56 seconds would settle it, and can be lifted from the quiet part of any of these clips.

## What the typed plan is still missing

`Drakosha_Ep1Plan.res` maps A, B, E and F to lines 11 through 17 already, but it has no segment for the ride at all — the assembled episode cuts from the reveal straight to Мама's present. It also cannot express any of the trims above. Folding both in requires `visual` to carry an in-point and the clip-reuse guard to key on clip plus range rather than clip alone, since A legitimately backs two segments here.
