# EP1 defect register — what has to be fixed

Audited 2026-08-11 against v16. Every item verified by opening the frame, not from memory. Ordered by severity: A breaks the story, B breaks continuity, C is a missing beat, D is polish.

## A · Story-breaking — the magic doesn't read

**A1 · No transformation is ever shown.** Вася becomes мама, a cat, and a wasp three times, and the audience never sees it happen. We cut from the casting frame straight to the result. The show's entire mechanic — say the word, become the thing — has no picture. *Fix: a transformation beat per change (boy dissolving into light into cat), ideally motion, at minimum a mid-transform still. 3 beats.*

**A2 · Materializations don't materialize either.** Фрося writes a word and the object simply exists in the next frame. The flash, the arrival, the moment of appearance — absent. *Fix: an appearance beat for СОК, САЛАТ, МАК, САМОКАТ, МОТОК, БАК. Can be one reusable "flash + object forming" treatment. 6 beats.*

**A3 · f28 shows juice while the line is about salad.** One frame is doing double duty for two different spells: Фрося offers the juice (correct for «Сока хочешь?») and the same frame plays under «С-А-Л-А-Т. САЛА́Т!». The salad bowl sits on the floor unremarked. *Fix: split into two frames, one per object.*

**A4 · The cat isn't blocking anything, and he's in the wrong place.** f35 puts him on the home's floorboards — railing and iron door visible behind — lying beside Фрося with no scooter in frame. The script has him blocking the underfloor road with his body and tail while she tries to pass. The whole obstacle beat is missing. *Fix: regenerate on the road, body across the planks, scooter braking. The motion clip m_cat has the right idea but also reads as the hall.*

**A5 · f40 shows the thread before it's created, with English writing on the floor.** The ball of yarn is already in frame while Фрося is still saying she needs thread, and the scrawl on the boards reads as Latin letters, not «МОТОК». *Fix: regenerate as "before" (empty floor, she's explaining) and let the appearance beat from A2 deliver the ball.*

## B · Continuity errors

**B1 · Underfloor scenes are staged in the hall.** f35 and f36 (the tank) both show home flooring and the railing. The road is a plank corridor under the giants' floor with brick foundation walls — a different place. *Fix: regenerate both on the road set.*

**B2 · Babies are inconsistent.** In f31 they're held by their heads like dolls; elsewhere they read as generic infants. Руся (thin, topknot) and Муся (round) are distinct characters. *Fix: reroll f31; check every frame carrying them.*

**B3 · No scooter in the cat confrontation.** She rode it down there; it vanishes for the argument and returns later.

**B4 · The юла is never seen spinning with the children on it in stills.** Only the clip covers it, and the clip is in scene 2 while the stills around it show it stopped.

## C · Missing beats the script calls for

**C1 · No title sequence.** The theme song exists; the episode starts cold.
**C2 · No end card.** SP226–227 specifies the hand scattering А О М С К Т Л Б which form the episode's nine words — the literal teaching summary. Absent.
**C3 · Яга's arrival through the door is unseen** — we have the soot burst but not her stepping through into the room.
**C4 · Яга's departure is unseen** — SP215–216: she climbs into the mortar, the door, the chimney, the whirlwind over the roof.
**C5 · Руся and Муся racing to Яга (SP047)** — one of the warmest beats, missing.
**C6 · The candle's fall (SP166)** — we see the crash and then the hole; the candle rolling to the edge and dropping is not shown.
**C7 · Вася-as-wasp squeezing into the hole and tying the knot (SP190–193)** — the rescue's actual work happens off screen.
**C8 · Scene 1 uses none of the existing dresser-gap footage** (clips C07–C14) though it was generated for exactly these beats.

## D · Polish

**D1 · Word cards only appear on casting frames.** The result frames carry no reinforcement of the word just taught.
**D2 · Мама holds a giant matchstick in several frames** — set dressing leaking into her hands.
**D3 · Pencil-behind-ear appears in some pre-gift frames** (packet sheet bleed).
**D4 · The plank ramp reads inconsistently** — sometimes stairs, sometimes plank, depending on frame.
**D5 · No reaction shots anywhere.** Every frame carries dialogue; nobody ever just listens.

## Rough cost

Regenerations at ~40cr per still: A3, A4, A5, B1×2, B2 ≈ 7 frames ≈ 280cr. New beats (A1 3, A2 6, C3–C7 5) ≈ 14 frames ≈ 560cr, or as motion clips ≈ 36–54cr each. Title/end card ≈ 2 frames + assembly. **Current balance: 398.** So the full list exceeds what we have; it needs prioritising or a top-up.

## Suggested order

1. A1 and A2 — the magic itself, the reason the show exists.
2. A3, A4, A5, B1 — the frames that are actively wrong.
3. C2 end card — cheap, high teaching value.
4. C1 title sequence — we have the theme.
5. Everything else.
