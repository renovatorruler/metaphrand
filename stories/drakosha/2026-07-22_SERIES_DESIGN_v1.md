# «Скажи — и стань!» — Russian letter series, arc design v1
(Working title options: «Скажи — и стань!» / «Супер-Дракоши» / «Драко-Азбука» — mom picks.)

## THE CORE FUSION (why this show is one idea, not three)
Vesper loves transformation. Vesper struggles with the suppress-then-explode cycle. The show makes these ONE mechanic: in this world, a dragon TRANSFORMS BY SAYING. Say the word — become the thing («Скажи — и стань!»). A dragon who transforms WITHOUT words — who lets the feeling do it — turns into his WILD FORM: big, blurry, scribbly, breaks things, scares friends. The same fire, unshaped. So every letter learned = more words he can say = more forms he can choose = more power over his own fire. Literacy IS emotional regulation IS transformation. Never preached; it's the physics of the world.

The wild form has a name the valley whispers: РЫК (the Roar). «Когда Веспер молчит-молчит-молчит — приходит РЫК.» (When Vesper keeps it in and keeps it in — the Roar comes.) РЫК is not a villain; he is what unspoken feelings turn into. Across the season Vesper learns to send words ahead of the fire.

## THE TWO HEROES (neither is a side character)
- ВЕСПЕР — the TRANSFORMER. The only dragon who can become. But he can only become what he can SAY, and he's just learning letters. His arc = the emotional curriculum below.
- ФУРИЯ — the SCRIBE. She already reads and writes (like the real Furia). Her power: what she WRITES becomes real — she draws a letter/word in the air with her claw-pen and it appears as an object. The reader-writer and the becomer: she can write the word he can't read yet; he can become the thing she can't become. Neither completes a mission alone — the cooperation is structural, not polite.
- Furia's own arc (so she's never decoration): the leader's lesson — her brother transforms slowly, speaks slowly, and her speed can silence him; she learns to make room (mirrors her Hindi-bible richness: proudest when she helped someone ELSE be first). And a READER'S SECRET LAYER built just for her: every episode hides written words in the world (signs, notes, labels) that only a reading child can catch — including one secret word per episode that predicts the ending. The pre-reader gets the plot; the reader gets prophecy.
- МАМА-ДРАКОНИЦА — the mentor (the real mom teaches Russian; in-world the mother dragon holds the letter lessons). Warm, unhurried. When words are used properly, SHE ALWAYS RESPONDS — the show models that words WORK: speak early, get heard. (This is the assertiveness payoff loop, and gently, a model for the grown-ups too.)

## THE RECURRING FORCE (differentiated from the Hindi show's letter-thief)
БУЯН-ВЕТЕР — the Rowdy Wind. A whirlwind who cannot speak — only howl («У-у-у!») — and cannot hold any shape. He is transformation with NO words at all: he spins through the valley changing things at random (the bridge into a pretzel, the laundry into a flock). Not evil — lonely and wordless, the hero's dark mirror at full scale. Missions are usually "fix what Буян twisted" (deliberate change repairs chaotic change). SEASON FINALE: the team teaches Буян his first word — and it is «МЫ» (WE), from the letters the season taught. The wind that could only howl says "we," and stops spinning alone. (Deliberately NOT the Hindi Mitasur beat: his was making his first LETTER — owning; Буян's is saying his first WORD — belonging.)

## PEDAGOGY SPINE (real Russian primer order — Zhukova's БУКВАРЬ, the standard home bukvar; mom will recognize it)
Season 1 = the first ten letters: А У О М С Х Р Ш Ы Л. Cumulative by design: each episode's words use ONLY letters already taught. The word-pool grows: АУ → МАМА → САМ → УРА → ШУМ → МЫ → СЛОМАЛ. Vesper's "sayable forms" grow with it — progress is VISIBLE as power.

## SEASON 1 LADDER (letter × word(s) × feeling-skill × mission sketch)
1. А — «А-А-А!» — NOTICE THE VOLCANO + the roar-release: when the fire rises, roar it INTO THE SKY, not at people. First tool: voice out, fists never. Mission: his first tiny transformation (say А, become the А-shaped mountain-slide to rescue a toy).
2. У — «АУ!» — ASKING FOR HELP IS BRAVE. The lost-call episode: someone's lost in the birch wood; АУ! is how you're found. (Feeling: fear, and that calling out isn't weakness.)
3. О — «О!» — WONDER: big feelings aren't only anger. The О episode collects the round things (the moon, a bubble, a berry) and the sound of awe.
4. М — «МАМА» «АМ» — THE COMFORT WORD. His first full spelled word — and Furia helps him write it as a surprise card FOR МАМА. (Made by their mother, his first word being МАМА as a gift to her — the meta-payoff is the point.) АМ = the nom-nom feeding comedy.
5. С — «САМ!» — MYSELF: autonomy as the GOOD channel of assertiveness. He wants to do it himself; the world makes room; doing-it-myself is honored, not punished. Mission: make СУП himself; an ОСА (wasp) complicates.
6. Х — «ХА-ХА» «УХО» — LAUGHTER AND TEASING: being laughed WITH vs laughed AT (a real trigger); the tools: say «Мне не смешно. Стоп.» — and УХО, the listening ear, for the other side.
7. Р — «Р-Р-Р» «УРА!» — LOSING WITHOUT EXPLODING. He loses the race, the growl rises, tools engage (roar-to-sky, then words), and the episode ends with an УРА that's real. Р is also the growl-letter — the fire gets its own letter, honored, not shamed.
8. Ш — «Ш-Ш-Ш» «ШУМ» — THE CALM BREATH. Too much noise, too many dragons, overwhelm — and Ш itself is the tool: the long quiet sh-sh-sh breath that shrinks the wild form. The letter IS the technique.
9. Ы — «МЫ» — WE: you're not alone with big feelings. The team word unlocked (and СЫР for the picnic mission, УСЫ for the comedy). Plants the finale.
10. Л — «СЛОМАЛ» «МЫЛО» — THE REPAIR EPISODE (the season's heart, handled gently): the wild form slips out and something a friend loves gets broken. The walk back: say what happened («Я сломал»), help fix it, wash up (МЫЛО), the friendship survives the fire. Anger doesn't make him bad; repair is what strong dragons do. THEN the finale beat: Буян-Ветер at his loneliest — and the team gives the wind «МЫ».

## EPISODE TEMPLATE (~9-11 min, Super Kitties mission rhythm)
Cold open whimsy (60-90s, Kakė Makė register: a kid-scale absurdity) → the mission lands → today's letter discovered IN the world (its shape hiding in things) → mama-dragon's short diegetic letter beat (sound, shape, air-writing; call-and-response) → first attempt fails / the fire rises (the wild-form temptation, shown kindly) → feeling-tool + letter combine → «Скажи — и стань!» transformation → mission solved WITH Furia's written word + Vesper's becoming → tag: Furia's secret written word pays off + a goodnight whimsy button.
Chant: «Скажи — и стань!» Feelings mantra (tiny, used sparingly): «Заметь. Назови. Скажи.» (Notice it. Name it. Say it.)

## STYLE (the Kakė Makė mandate)
Hand-drawn 2D whimsy: wobbly ink lines, crayon and watercolor texture, collage bits, homely details drawn with love (socks, jam jars, ladders), kid-logic physics. Cozy domestic-fantastic scale: missions are the size of a lost boot, a ruined pancake tower, a twisted bridge. Deliberately NOT the Hindi show's 3D papercraft — two shows, two crafts, no doubled effort.

## DIFFERENTIATION FROM THE HINDI SHOW (so the tracks never blur)
- Mechanic: restore the stolen letter (कुकु) vs unlock transformation by saying (this).
- Threat: an eraser-thief who redeems by MAKING vs a wordless wind who redeems by BELONGING.
- Hero: mascot hero (Kuku) + kids as team vs VESPER HIMSELF as the transformer (the therapy is first-person).
- Pedagogy: letter-of-the-day + टोपी hat-rule vs cumulative Zhukova word-building ladder.
- Emotional core: kindness/निति strategy vs self-regulation + assertiveness.
- Look: 3D papercraft vs Kakė Makė hand-drawn.

## THE ASSERTIVENESS CURRICULUM (the quiet spine; never a lecture on screen)
The cycle to break: suppress → suppress → explode (sometimes at mom/dad). The show's answer, in order, across the season: (1) the fire is REAL and honored — it gets its own letter, its own roar-space; never "don't be angry." (2) Voice beats fists — the roar-to-sky, the growl-letter. (3) Words beat the roar — name it early, small words early («Я злюсь», «Стоп», «САМ», «Помоги») get RESULTS on screen every time. (4) Calm is a skill with a letter of its own (Ш). (5) Repair, not shame, after the slip (СЛОМАЛ episode). (6) You're not alone (МЫ). Adults in-show always reward the early small word — the world proves that speaking early works better than exploding late.
