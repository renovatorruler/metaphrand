## VESPER'S REAL LETTER MAP (tested 2026-07-26)

METHOD: one letter per card, ask for the SOUND not the name («Какой звук?»), played as a game, stop at the first tiredness. Warm-up letters first.

BLENDING: confirmed ALREADY PRESENT before the test (tried Бобуквы books previously — he struggled to remember letters but blended fine when given the sounds). So the season's real frontier is letter-sound MEMORY, not blending — which is exactly what story mnemonics are for.

ROUND 1 (the 10 season letters + 5 probes) — RUN IN FULL:

- KNOWS the Russian sound (8): А С К Т М О Л Б
- FALSE FRIENDS — gives the English/Spanish sound (4): У Р Н Х (his English-reading eye sees Y, P, H, X)
- CONFUSES: В (wobbles toward B)
- DOES NOT KNOW (2): Ы (guessed as English P — pattern-hunting with nothing to grab), Ш (has seen it, does not remember the sound)

ROUND 2 (the remaining 16) — ONLY HALF-RUN, and worth completing:

- Д and П were BOTH mistaken for Л → a third interference type: Cyrillic shape-family confusion (Л/Д/П are all two-legged gate shapes and Л, which he owns, swallows the other two). Never teach lookalikes in adjacent episodes; give each a strongly distinct character (Д = ДОМ, the house's own letter; П = ворота, the gate).
- Ф: KNOWN 100%, unprompted, because it is his sister's initial. Nobody drilled it. THIS IS THE PROOF OF THE SHOW'S WHOLE MNEMONIC ENGINE — letters stick through belonging.
- Everything else in round 2 is untested and treated as unknown.

CONFIRMED LIMITATION (separate field test): he CANNOT roll the Russian Р. Canon, load-bearing, honored, never magically cured.

THE THREE INTERFERENCE TYPES, each needing a different kind of episode: (1) Latin false friends — the letter with an overseas twin that looks identical and speaks differently; (2) Cyrillic shape-family confusion; (3) plain unknowns — clean mnemonic episodes.

## THE ART REFERENCE BOARD (durable links)

For the record: the show's look was LOCKED as painted Kakė Makė-register children in patchwork sewn from the house's own scraps (see the character sheets). These references belong to the earlier knitted-material exploration and to the world's folklore lineage; several remain useful for texture, warmth, and the unravelling imagery.

- «ВАРЕЖКА», Союзмультфильм 1967 — dir. Roman Kachanov, production design Leonid Shvartsman (Cheburashka's designer), animation incl. Yuri Norstein; first prize Annecy 1967. A lonely girl's knitted red mitten becomes a living puppy. The cultural ancestor, and the reason Муся sleeps in a mitten. https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Варежка_(мультфильм)
- YARNY, from «Unravel» (Coldwood/EA, 2016) — a creature of red yarn who UNRAVELS as he moves away from what he loves; the proof that a coming-apart character reads as sad rather than frightening. https://unravel.fandom.com/wiki/Yarny · designer's account: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/how-i-unravel-s-i-memorable-protagonist-yarny-was-woven-together
- THE CLANGERS (BBC, 1969, Smallfilms; designed by Peter Firmin, knitted by Joan Firmin) — knitted creatures carrying a whole beloved series. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clangers · photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clangers_2016_V%26A_Museum_of_Childhood.jpg
- BAGPUSS (Smallfilms, 1974) — the saggy, loose-at-the-seams, adored cloth cat; the worn-and-loved emotional register. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bagpuss.jpg
- KIRBY'S EPIC YARN (Nintendo, 2010) — the best study for keeping fabric and thread readable when DRAWN in flat 2D rather than photographed.
- AMIGURUMI craft references for proportion (round heads, cord limbs, mitten hands): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amigurumi_graduate.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Knitted_rag_doll.jpg
- THE ILLUSTRATION REGISTER (locked): Lina Žutautė's «Kakė Makė» books. https://english.lithuanianculture.lt/lithuanian-culture-guide/illustrators/2015/07/13/lina-zutaute/
