# «Фрося и Вася» — the magic redesign + Episode 1 «Самокат» (design for approval)

Author's notes being answered (2026-07-30): Фрося's power was never clear; Вася had two different ways of doing magic; the wooden letter-chips had no job; the air-writing was one more unexplained thing; nothing fitted together; the older sister needs an EQUIVALENT AND COMPLEMENTARY power so the two of them are needed to solve anything; the educational layer was weak; no explaining-who-we-are dialogue in the first five seconds; Episode 1 is FROSYA'S BIRTHDAY.

## PART 1 — THE MAGIC, REBUILT (one power each, and neither works alone)

### ФРОСЯ WRITES.
She can write any word — with her pencil stub, on anything the house offers: a dust film, a windowpane, a paper scrap, a floorboard. What she writes GLOWS faintly. And that is all it does. Written words are silent and inert; she can fill a whole floorboard with words and nothing will happen. She is the only one in the family who can write.

### ВАСЯ READS ALOUD — AND BECOMES THE WORD.
When Вася reads a written word aloud, HE TURNS INTO IT. «ВЖУХ!» is the sound of it happening, his own cry, not a separate spell. He cannot write a single letter. And he can only read words built from letters he actually knows — today that is eight.

### THAT IS THE WHOLE SYSTEM. Two powers, one each.
Writing without reading is silent. Reading needs something written. Neither child can do anything alone, and it is not a rule anybody has to explain — it is just how the world works. It is also true about literacy, which is why it will keep paying off.

CUT ENTIRELY: the second Вася mechanic (reading a word to make the thing APPEAR beside him). One boy, one power. Everything the old summoning did, becoming does better, because it puts him inside the problem instead of next to it.

### THE RULES THAT MAKE IT DRAMA

- ONE WORD AT A TIME. While he is a word, he is that word.
- HE STAYS UNTIL THE WORD IS BROKEN. Wipe the writing, or scatter the letters, and he comes back. So Фрося holds the off-switch — and if the letters get scattered by someone else at the wrong moment, he is stuck.
- HE CAN ONLY READ WHAT HIS LETTERS ALLOW. This is the engine of the whole series: the word that would save the day contains a letter he does not have yet, and the episode is about getting it.
- MISREAD, AND YOU BECOME THE WRONG THING. Reading САМ as СОМ turns him into a catfish flopping on the floor — funnier and more dangerous than a fish merely appearing, and the vocabulary lands harder because he is wearing it.
- ФРОСЯ CANNOT BECOME ANYTHING. She can make anything in the world happen, and never gets to BE any of it. She writes the adventure and watches her little brother have it. This is her wound, it explains «Я первая!» without a word of explanation, and it is the sister-arc for the whole series.

### THE WOODEN LETTER-CHIPS FINALLY HAVE A JOB
Вася carries eight small wooden tiles, one letter each, in the pouch on his shoulder. They are his ALPHABET MADE VISIBLE — everything he owns. Two jobs:

1. INVENTORY. He can count them, spread them out, check whether a word is within reach. When the season teaches him a letter, HE GETS A NEW CHIP — progress you can see and hold, and the child watching gets the same count.
2. HE CAN LAY THEM OUT TO BUILD A WORD HIMSELF. He cannot write, but he can arrange. A word he builds from his own chips, he can read alone — no sister required. Today that is almost nothing, because eight letters is almost nothing. As the season goes on it becomes more, which is exactly how it feels to learn to read, and exactly what quietly costs Фрося her job as the one who holds the pencil.

### WHAT THIS FIXES EDUCATIONALLY
Every episode now runs on real decoding: a word appears on screen, Вася sounds it out letter by letter, and the child at home decodes with him at his exact level, because his level is the show's level. Wrong reading produces a visible wrong thing. The alphabet he owns is a physical, growing, countable pile. And the two children need each other for reasons that are literally what reading and writing are.

## PART 2 — EPISODE 1: «САМОКАТ» (Фрося's birthday)

### THE ENGINE
It is Фрося's birthday. Вася wants to give her a present. He is five, he owns eight wooden letters, and everything he could possibly give her would have to be WRITTEN FIRST — by her. He cannot surprise the only person who can make his surprise happen. That is the wall, and it is a real one.

The answer, which he finds himself: he builds a word out of his own eight chips, reads it alone for the first time in his life, and BECOMES her present. САМОКАТ — a kick-scooter. С, А, М, О, К, А, Т: every letter is one he already owns. And hidden inside САМОКАТ is САМ — "by myself." He did it himself, and the word says so. The reading child will find that; nobody on screen will ever mention it.

### WHY IT IS HER PRESENT AND NOT JUST A TRICK
What Фрося wants, always, is to be first and to go fast, and what she never gets is to be the one having the adventure. He turns himself into the thing that lets her have it. On her birthday she is not holding the pencil, she is riding — and the thing carrying her is her little brother.

### THE SHAPE (beats, not yet scenes)

1. COLD OPEN — the sock haul, kept. Two tiny people drag an enormous striped sock through the under-floor dark and post it back where it belongs. No one explains who they are or what домовята are. When Вася asks why the giants keep losing things, Фрося's answer is a shrug and a house-fact: people lose, we find, that is how the house works. Mystery intact. And in the middle of it she mentions, for the fortieth time today, that it is her birthday.
2. HOME — behind the stove. Мама with Руся on her arm and Муся on her back. Папа mending something with a joke in his mouth. Фрося writes the day's list, because she writes everything. Small demonstration of both powers without a syllable of explanation: she writes a word, he reads it, he briefly becomes it, everyone carries on as if this is Tuesday.
3. THE PROBLEM — everyone has something for Фрося. Вася has nothing. He asks мама what to give. She does not lecture: give her the one thing nobody else can.
4. THE IMPOSSIBLE SURPRISE — he tries to get Фрося to write words without telling her why. She is a fast, suspicious, birthday-shaped older sister, and it goes badly. Attempts fail; one produces something absurd; she gets annoyed.
5. THE HURT — it lands where it really lands. On her own birthday she is still the one holding the pencil for her brother. She says something sharp and true and goes off by herself. He is left with the thing he cannot do.
6. THE UNRAVELLING — the loose thread pulls at his cuff (РЫК, shown, never named), and he takes it outside and roars it into the sky instead of at anyone. Then quiet.
7. THE IDEA — he tips out his chips and counts them. Eight. He starts arranging them in the dust, testing what eight letters can be. This is the education beat and it is played as suspense, not as a lesson.
8. БЕГЕМОТ TAKES A LETTER — the giant black cat bats a chip away and sits on it. Вася has to negotiate with a mountain: milk, and scratching behind two ears. The cat drives a hard bargain and keeps his word.
9. THE BUILD — slowly, letter by letter, out loud, with the word on screen: С… А… М… О… К… А… Т. He nearly gives up. The clutch: he stops thinking about whether he can do it and thinks about her face, and the word comes clean.
10. «ВЖУХ!» — for the first time, he reads a word nobody wrote for him, and becomes it.
11. THE RIDE — Фрося, on her birthday, first and fastest, flying down the floorboard with her hair coming apart behind her. Nobody says what it means.
12. BUTTON — the chips scatter, he flops back into himself, dizzy and pleased. Family. Папа. And far away past the field, the wind howls once — lonely, unexplained, gone.

### WHAT THIS EPISODE ESTABLISHES WITHOUT EXPOSITION
Who the family is, that nobody has ever seen them, that she writes and he becomes, that his letters are few and countable, that the cat is a power to be bargained with, that his feelings can start to come apart and there is a way to handle it, and that something is out there in the wind. All of it carried by events. Nobody introduces themselves.
