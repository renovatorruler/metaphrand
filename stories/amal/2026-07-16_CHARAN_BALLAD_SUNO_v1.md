# चारण की बल्लाड — the Jhujhar ballad (Suno seed, v1)

*The cold-open saka song, written for Suno generation — and designed to double as Ratan's song at the finale reprise (same lyrics, same voice via a saved Persona; "this time he sings for Ratan"). Every line reads true twice: the 1305 Jhujhar on the surface, Ratan underneath. The warriors drinking अमल before the ride plants the title in minute one. The final refrain — the stone's name fades, the song does not — is the glory engine's thesis and the paper-vs-ballad ending, seeded in the first scene.*

## Style field (Suno "Style of Music")

Rajasthani folk war ballad, bhopa epic-singing tradition, solo ravanhatta bowed strings with sparse dholak, weathered elderly male voice, gravelly hoarse and cracked with age, quavering trembling sustained notes, slow mournful tempo, raw unpolished single-take field recording, stone courtyard natural reverb, vintage archival mono, traditional bardic storytelling

## Exclude styles
