# अमल — THE TRAIN (cold open) — SHOOTING SCRIPT v1 (2026-07-20)

*Source: train-scene.md (director's cut, June) converted as-is; the Ratan-in-the-compartment question is OPEN and not staged here. Process: beats → coverage → numbered shots → assets → audio-first manifest. 720p approved by the author. Once shot numbers are used downstream, they never renumber — additions become A-shots (e.g., 5-12A).*

## THE CAMERA GRAMMAR (the ordinary-surface law as camera behavior)

Scenes 1–3: locked-off, patient, ordinary frames — the camera is just another passenger; the horror only ever enters as texture (flies, a too-still hand) inside ordinary compositions. ONE slow push in the whole first half (1-08, Suresh's POV) — the camera gets curious exactly when Suresh does, never before. Handheld begins only at 5-01, when the pursuit starts. The two reveal shots (5-06, 5-12) are STATIC — the camera refuses to flinch or editorialize. One designed pull-away closes the scene (5-15), per the script's "as we pull away." Heat texture throughout: slow fans, shimmer, sweat, flies.

## BEATS (coverage targets)

B1 ordinary world (Kamla's chatter) · B2 the plant (woman, bundle, flat hand) · B3 flies inside small talk; her four-word walls · B4 Suresh notices · B5 Rajesh dismisses · B6 station probe through bars; refusal #2 · B7 the huddle; Kamla flips; the सिपाही plan · B8 the woman HEARS (her stillness) · B9 flight — she leaves the trunk · B10 cornered in the next bogie · B11 the weaponized accusation (the reversal) · B12 the compelled peek; FALSE RELIEF · B13 Suresh alone against everyone · B14 THE GRAB and the horror · B15 the scream; pull away; black.
