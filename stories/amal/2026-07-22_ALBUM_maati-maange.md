# AMAL — concept album «माटी माँगे» (The Earth Demands)

Origin: the झूझार song (the Bhat's invocation, sung via Suno, came out excellent). Goal: a concept album of songs in that exact register.

## THE FRAME (how one song becomes an album)
The bhopa/phad tradition (Pabuji ki phad): one blind bard sings a hero's whole epic across a night on a ravanhatta, unrolling a painted scroll. That IS a concept album — one bard, one instrument, one saga, in movements. The album = the AMAL saga sung as a bhopa epic. झूझार opens the scroll.

## THEME + TITLE
«माटी माँगे» — the earth still demands (from the song's last whisper «माटी अब भी माँगे»). Blood, courage vs cowardice, kartavya, a soil owed justice it never gets. Elegiac, unresolved.

## ROSTER (each track ACTS — address/command/question/vow; lyrics-are-drama law)
1. उठ रे जुझार — INVOCATION (exists). Summons the warrior-blood; commands the coward's veins.
2. THE FATHER — bard to Ratan: his father did the dishonest thing that had to be done, and was loved for it (anti-Harishchandra).
3. THE PRICED GIRL — lament to the sold girl; «किण रो मोल?» whose price.
4. RANA — the fear; the bard's warning about the feared man.
5. THE COWARD — indictment of Ratan: honesty without courage is only cowardice.
6. KARTAVYA — the dharma argument sung; where Harishchandra failed the higher duty.
7. THE SAKA — last stand, death in Mahakal's city, «मौत सूँ कुण डरे»; a vow.
8. माटी अब भी माँगे — CLOSE; the refrain as finale, unresolved fade.

## PRODUCTION TEMPLATE (sonic unity + controlled variation)
LOCKED core (every track): lone ravanhatta + low tambura drone; raw outdoor field-recording of a blind bhopa; weathered, wavering, untrained, distant-mic; evening wind + crickets; no drums; mournful; unresolved fade.
MASTER STYLE PROMPT (reuse verbatim as base): "Rajasthani-Malwa folk elegy, sparse cinematic instrumental, lone mournful ravanhatta over low tambura drone, vocal is a raw outdoor field recording — old blind village bard singing in open air, weathered wavering untrained voice cracked with age, distant from the microphone, faint evening wind and crickets, bhopa epic-singer style, documentary authenticity, no drums, mournful, unresolved fading ending"
MASTER EXCLUDE: "studio vocals, polished vocals, professional classical singer, trained voice, pitch-perfect vocals, autotune, close-mic vocals, clean vocal production, Bollywood, film orchestra, pop, EDM, drums, female vocals, youthful voice, rap"
VARY per track (ONE lever, so kin not clones): add a single folk color (morchang pulse / kamaicha or sarangi shadow / algoza flute); shift tempo (dirge for the saka, a hair faster for the indictment); drop voice lower for death, crack higher for grief.
LYRIC DISCIPLINE: Marwari-Malwi dialect; the सुण रे/उठ रे grammar; run the Mamet gate (who wants what from whom) before "done"; no inventory/descriptive lyrics.
