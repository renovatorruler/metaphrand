# अलबेलो (ALBELO) — PITCH v1 (2026-07-17; the author's commission — awaiting his read)

*Commissioned on: the folk song about "albelo Hindustan" sung by Thar Hindus; Pakistani Hindus as a minority whose mere existence affronts the majority; a sincere Muslim who would not kill them but cannot stomach idolaters IN HIS COUNTRY and aims to convert them; the author's real first conversation with a Pakistani Muslim ("the idol won't come save you" / "your sky-god won't come down either" — and the recognition that an INDIAN Muslim would never ask the question, because proximity breeds understanding). THE SOD, stated here once and NEVER on the page: the film is a commentary on the majority-minority question in India (how does a religious majority live with an ethno-religious minority that negates its beliefs); every grievance is written so the nouns swap cleanly; no character ever looks at the mirror.*

## Entropy work (modes named → swerves)

MODE: hate-village misery drama → SWERVE: Mithi, Tharparkar — Pakistan's famous harmony town (half Hindu; Muslims forgo public cow slaughter; Hindus serve Muharram sherbet); the harmony is real AND lazy/commercial/syncretic — it runs on nobody taking theology seriously. MODE: mullah/landlord villain → SWERVE: the "villain" is the town's one SINCERE man, and likable. MODE: abduction-plot engine → SWERVE: the abduction/conversion economy is offstage weather until the climax; the engine is a conversion project colliding with a secret exit. MODE: India as promised land OR the cheap India-disappoints inversion → SWERVE: India is never reached, never shown; it stays a country made of longing (the dream is never tested — also protects the Sod).

## The two men (want vs want; the secret powers every scene)

QARI YOUSUF — the town's Quran teacher, its one sincere man. Empty evening dars while the pir's shrine overflows; the town's easy Islam embarrasses him almost as much as the idols. Not cruel; despises the girl-snatchers (a forced kalima is a lie told in God's name — worse than shirk). But it eats him that idolaters live IN HIS COUNTRY — elsewhere is Allah's business; here their lamps burn opposite his mosque. Human want: to be HEEDED — one true conversion of the heart to prove his unheeded life right. Target: the one man worth winning.

VERSI MAL — the Hindu compounder (half-doctor, no degree), beloved, funny, theologically unbothered; has dosed the whole town's children including the Qari's. SECRET: the family has filed for the pilgrimage visa (the train hasn't run since 2019; the door is a visa and a bus to Wagah); the buffalo is quietly sold; you tell NO ONE (announcing calls in debts, sharpens the conversion men, marks the door). So the Qari arrives nightly with tea and arguments to save the soul of a man who cannot say "I am leaving" — Versi parries, jokes, stalls: the long farewell he isn't allowed to name.

THE TRAP (the author's insight as architecture): to convert them honestly he must UNDERSTAND them; understanding is the death of the easy contempt. Distance made the question possible ("वो मूर्ति उठ के तुम्हें बचाने आएगी?"); proximity kills it. His Karachi cousin (has Hindu colleagues) laughs at the question outright. The man who takes conversion seriously is exactly the man proximity converts to understanding.

## The counter-want inside the Hindu house

MEERAN, 16, born in Mithi, has never seen India, doesn't want to go — her Hindustan is her father's song; her home is her lane. "मैं तुम्हारे अलबेले हिन्दुस्तान की कौन हूँ? यहाँ की तो कोई हूँ।" The exit is also a tearing. And leaving quietly abandons the bonded Bheel families who can never leave — the minority has its own class line (Versi carries it). No saints.

## Key scenes / mechanics

- THE IDOL CONVERSATION (restaged from the author's life, scene 2, comic register): "वो मूर्ति उठ के तुम्हें बचाने आएगी?" / "और तुम्हारा ऊपरवाला उतर के आएगा? कम से कम मेरा तो सामने बैठा है — नाराज़ होता है तो लड्डू से मान भी जाता है।"
- THE CORRECTION BY BEHAVIOR (never lectured; midpoint mirror): the night Meeran's fever breaks, the Qari watches Versi SCOLD the murti — the way a man scolds his mother's photograph — and understands: nobody thinks the stone is God; the stone is family, an address, a doorway.
- THE SONG: the traditional refrain (अलबेलो हिन्दुस्तान) as the HELD thing — sung indoors, at their own weddings, never outside. Address-grammar (Hindustan as adorned bridegroom; a bidaai in reverse). One song, two meanings: the father hears "going to the beloved"; the daughter hears "the bride leaving home forever." Our verses under the lyrics-are-drama law.
- DOORWAY OF NO RETURN (ordinary-surface law): a SCHOOL FUNCTION. Meeran sings the beautiful song from home; a phone films it; by morning the harmony town has a treason problem. The outside machinery wakes: seminary men arrive to "resolve" it (a dastarbandi + public kalima as the proposed happy ending); the visa agent evaporates with the exit fund.
- THE BIRD-DOG REVEAL (all-is-lost): the Qari discovers his nightly visits MARKED the door — the hard men greet him as a colleague: "आप तो पहले से काम कर रहे थे उन पर। मुकम्मल हम कर देंगे।" His sincerity was the reconnaissance. Fund gone, door marked, the town's laughing man gone silent.
- CLIMAX (antagonist in the room; temptation at full scale): they hand the Qari his life's dream — preside over the conversion. He sees his own face armed and REFUSES ON HIS OWN THEOLOGY'S GROUND: a kalima at knifepoint is a lie manufactured in God's name; he will not certify it. Not tolerance discovered — his sincerity's true requirement discovered. Cost: standing, safety, students. The family goes out through the fair-night crowd to the Wagah bus (his silence or his help — author's call).
- ENDING (the custodian): the murti cannot cross the border. The man who wanted every idol out of his country becomes the secret keeper of one — wrapped in cloth in his niche, not worshipped, KEPT — because he alone knows what it is: not their god; their grandmother. Last sound: the Qari at dusk, humming the refrain he can't get out of his head. The only completed conversion is the song's — it crossed the border into him. Unstated: he is now the thing he couldn't tolerate — a man hiding a sacred thing from a majority that would destroy him for it.
