# कब्ज़ा (KABZA) — CONCEPT v1 (2026-07-20; premise LOCKED by the author: the haunting as a contract; details awaiting his read)

*Track: the audiobook-novel pivot — Hindi, voice-first, NO sound effects, multi-voice ElevenLabs, R-rated. Register: horror-comedy-satire — the horror genuinely frightens, the comedy comes from inside the fear, the satire target bleeds. Model machinery: Dungeon Crawler Carl (system-voice orientation, explicit rules doing orientation + dread, two-voice banter core, serialized arcs). Market gap: Indian horror audio is sanitized and earnest; filthy-funny-scary Hindi audio for adults does not exist. The R rating is the moat.*

## The title

कब्ज़ा — one word meaning illegal occupation of property AND spirit possession. The premise lives in the pun. (Note: a 2023 Kannada film shares the romanized title; titles are not protectable; revisit if it bothers the author.)

## The man

A Delhi property lawyer, mid-40s, brilliant and foul-mouthed (Tis Hazari register). HUMAN STAKES (the law): his divorce ate his own flat — the possession lawyer lives in a rented barsati; his whole life is other people's property; he wants one thing that is HIS. The firm dangles partnership on the file nobody survives. NOT a redemption arc: he does not learn to respect tradition; he learns to litigate it. Name/city: OPEN (author).

## The haunting is a contract; the horror is breach

The haveli's dead hold TERMS from an old settlement no living person has read properly: occupancy rights to a wing, a lamp owed nightly, transfer void without consent of ALL heirs — and the dead are heirs. Every previous buyer/broker/hard-case died of a SPECIFIC clause; reconstructing which clause killed which corpse = the case structure (episode = clause + death + negotiation round). The SYSTEM VOICE is native: the deed's terms, recited — the document as the voice of God. Rules give constant orientation ([[curiosity-not-confusion]] as form); dread = waiting for a term to bite.

## The second voice (the two-hander core) — room's recommendation: THE MUNSHI

The mode (rejected): the thakur ghost in white. The swerve: the thakurs MOVED ON — owners escape comfortably, even death. What remains is the MUNSHI — the clerk who drafted the deed 150 years ago, still keeping ledgers for a family that abandoned him in death as in life, bound to the document by a clause he himself mis-drafted: the one error of a meticulous life. Urdu-precise formality against Delhi filth — the register collision is the comedy engine; two document-men across a century and a half; the first living reader who has ever read his work properly is the man sent to destroy it. Not hero/sidekick: opposing counsel who become, appallingly, colleagues. DREAD GUARD: the munshi is courteous the way a contract is courteous; he is not the danger's master, he is its CLERK — the danger itself stays unnegotiable and real (flinch audit: people die on the page; the banter is predator-adjacent, never cuddly).

## The Sod (never stated on any page)

In India the dead never vacate: inheritance IS haunting, every family property fight is a séance, the past holds the paper and the living pay rent on it. Surface satire targets (stated, bleeding): developers heritage-washing corpse-houses into resorts; the family that wants the money but not the lamp-lighting; the courts — a suit attached to the haveli adjourned since 1911, all original parties dead, the case itself the district's longest-lived ghost.

## Register sample (the agreed R)

Lawyer, first night, to the dark: "आप मर चुके हो, मुंशी जी। डेथ सर्टिफिकेट दिखाऊँ? ...नहीं बना? तो क़ानूनन आप ज़िंदा हो — और ज़िंदा आदमी पे ट्रेसपासिंग लगती है, भोसड़ी के।" From the dark, unhurried: "तमीज़ से, वकील साहब। इस दस्तावेज़ में तहज़ीब की भी एक शर्त है।" The comedy runs both directions: legal logic applied to the supernatural; supernatural terms applied to a man who bills by the hour.

## Season and series shape

Season 1 = the one haveli (floor-one style): clear the title or become a clause. The client: a resort developer. Big loop: the settlement — and whether winning is survivable. Series engine: he becomes the profession's only haunted-property specialist (the firm's encumbered-assets desk) — one property per season.
