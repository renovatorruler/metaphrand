# «बस मोर्चा बदला है» — a soft homage through रण and छोड़

**STATUS: SUPERSEDED CONCEPT DRAFT.** Its repeated explanation of retreat was judged too on-the-nose. It has been replaced by [«आज डेरा उस पार»](2026-08-02_SONG_aaj-dera-us-paar.md), which lets the orderly movement carry the meaning. Preserve this file as craft history; do not treat it as the active render draft.

The warrior has not fled. He has ordered a controlled retreat and chosen another ground. The lyric does not explain or repeatedly announce the Krishna connection. The name appears only once, in the final refrain: «रणछोड़ चला वो» can be heard both as the name and as the act of leaving the battlefield. Earlier verses use «युद्ध», «मैदान», and «मोर्चा» so that «रणछोड़» retains its force. The distant gates opening in the sea remain a quiet allusion rather than an explanation.

The Bhagavad Gita 2.23 verse is a spoken coda after the sung composition and all instrumentation have ended. It must not be sung, chanted, underscored, or answered musically.
