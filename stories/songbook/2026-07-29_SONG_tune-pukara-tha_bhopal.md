# «तूने पुकारा था» — Bhopal, the water/name song (v2, AI-writing-cleaned)

STATUS: author lukewarm («it came out really well, but it does not feel like it's about Bhopal» → 1984 added → then «nah» to the whole water frame) — SUPERSEDED in direction by «दस्तक» (the protection song). Kept as material; the conceit (the city's name as a daily unknowing summons of the dead lake) remains strong and reusable.

Conceit: Bhopal = Bhoj's pal (the dam). The great lake Hoshang Shah drained addresses the city at night: you call me every day — your city's name IS my name — and you don't hear yourself. The sea comes back with every rain to listen for one person who says the name and stops short. December 1984 enters as one movement: the night the air turned heavy and flowed like water, every door was shut, and it came from inside; morning gave the name its second meaning. The killer of the lake is refused a name («मुझे नाम याद नहीं») — oblivion as the deepest cut, and the political safety.
