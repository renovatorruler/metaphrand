# «उस साल कठे हो?» — the उपालंभ to the raincloud (DELIVERY v2, 2026-08-19)

**Why this song exists:** one failed monsoon makes a migrant. The rain skipped a year; the crop failed; the loan came due; the field went into the moneylender's ledger and the man went to the city to service the debt. Now, on scaffolding, he sees the season's first cloud — loaded, glistening, keeping its appointment for everyone else — and holds it to account. Same form as «कर्क की तांती»: उपालंभ, a petitioner with an unpaid account against the very power he must still beg from. There the god who binds snakes had let one loose; here the cloud that keeps the yearly appointment skipped his.

**The mappings that argue (no cultural decoration — nothing appears unless it works):**
- **The tanker.** The cloud is a loaded water-tanker on the highway; he flags it like one. Trucker grammar throughout: routes, tolls, overloading, unloading.
- **बाटी → चूरमा.** A बाटी is wheat baked hard, sealed, alone — him, in the city. चूरमा is the same बाटी broken open by hand, ghee and gur worked in — him, at home. In the city breaking is shattering; at home breaking is how you turn sweet. The chain rain→wheat→आटा→बाटी→चूरमा broke at its first link the year the rain failed.
- **पोहा.** The city's food: eaten standing at a थड़ी among strangers, no one's hands in it but a stranger's. Loneliness stated as a meal, never as a feeling.
- **गारा / आटा.** He kneads mortar all day; she kneads flour. Same gesture, opposite meals.
- **The ledger.** The moneylender's book is the song's spine: the cloud's default is still standing against his name; the turn forgives it.

Place-names carry direction, never badges: Ujjain is a road and a drum; Malwa is not named — the destination is "my village", "the fields".

## Gloss (author's convenience, not for Suno)

V1: Stop, cloud — loaded full, where are you going? I stand on the roof tying rebar; you pass over my head. V2: Every year you come on time, you soak everyone's fields — so tell me, where were you *that* year, when my seed lay dry? V3: One year you didn't come, brother; the field went into the moneylender's ledger. I came to the city to pay the interest — and now you rain on the city. टेक: Don't unload here — unload on my fields; here water comes from a tap. V4: Still — listen, I'll give you the road. Don't take the bypass, take the Ujjain road, hear the evening drum as you pass. V5: No toll post for you, no RTO stops you, you roll overloaded and nobody writes you a challan. V6: Here I eat poha standing at a stall among strangers, served on a scrap of paper; no hand of mine touches it. V7: All day I knead mortar; at home she'll be kneading flour — one hand raises a wall, one hand makes bread. V8: I've become a बाटी in this city — hard outside, alone inside. Break here and I scatter; break there and I'm चूरमा. V9: Listen — the seed is in the field, sown with borrowed money; mine is the house with the नीम — rain your heart out there. V10 (turn): My account is ruined, it won't be mended now. You — settle *your* account with my village. That's all. Final: Take my whole share of the monsoon; don't unload here — unload on my fields.
