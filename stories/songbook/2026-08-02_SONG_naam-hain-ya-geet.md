# «नाम हैं या गीत?» — the bard runs out of page

**STATUS: ACTIVE DRAFT v2 — COMPLETE SUNO PACKET; AUTHOR VERDICT AND NATIVE-EAR PASS PENDING.**

## Why this replaces «रघुवीर की तान»

The previous draft protected every name so carefully that it became an orderly list. This version gives the bard a real problem. He is writing the family's genealogy; the names begin to sound like a song, and then one page proves too small. The joke is on the bard's page, never on a family member.

The song does not manufacture a meaning for any name. «कुबेर» carries no reference to money, wealth, abundance, or compensation. «रणछोड़» and «रणजीत» remain intact proper names rather than a retreat-versus-victory comparison. The family order supplied by the author is preserved exactly, and there is no Gita passage or death imagery.

## Dramatic design

- **Singer:** one dry, weathered genealogical भाट, performing alone under a tree.
- **Listener:** the family gathered just beyond the microphone.
- **Want:** fit the complete male line onto one genealogy page without losing a name.
- **Resistance:** the page runs out while every name must remain whole.
- **Turn:** the family refuses abbreviation and tells him to add paper instead.
- **Earned line:** «पोथी छोटी निकली… रघुवीर का वंश नहीं.»

## PERSONA / TAKE STRATEGY

- Make the first two takes without a saved Persona. The dry timing and intelligible dialogue matter more than continuity on this diagnostic pass.
- Then test the saved «उठ रे जुझार» Malwa Bard Persona once. Keep it only if it can sound amused and conversational; reject it if it pulls the lyric toward वीर-रस, grief, or ceremony.
- Generate at least three seeds. Choose the take in which «नाम हैं या गीत?» sounds accidentally discovered, not the take with the cleanest vocal.
- Reject any take that decorates «कुबेर», semantically splits «रणछोड़» or «रणजीत», makes «विक्रांत» the climax, or turns the quoted family answer into a shouted chorus.
- The final line must finish the song. Do not let Suno improvise a refrain, moral, Gita passage, or spoken addendum after «नहीं».

## Native-ear and spelling gates

- Current assumed family spellings: रघुवीर, रणछोड़, कुबेर, रणजीत, कपिल, अखिल, प्रशांत, हितेश, विक्रांत.
- Before a final render is locked, confirm the family's own spellings of Raghveer/Raghuvir, Ranchor/Ranchhod, and Ranjit/Ranjeet; family usage outranks dictionary convention.
- «वंशावली», «गुनगुनाए», and «पन्ना कम पड़ गया» are deliberately plain Hindi. Do not make them artificially regional merely to advertise Malwa.
