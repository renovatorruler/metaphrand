# «कर्क की तांती» — the photographs (design for approval)

Sixty-three photographs across six life beats plus the return, at ~3 seconds each, filling the eleven couple-slots. **Format law:** these are photographs, not frames — era-correct print stock and borders, and roughly one in six is held in a hand (a thumb at the edge, a corner bent, the album page behind it). The rest sit full-bleed but still read as prints. Ken Burns pushes and drifts are added free in ffmpeg; no generated motion.

Status: ✅ = exists and approved · ○ = to generate. Sixteen already exist; **47 to generate ≈ 94 credits**.
