## The three timelines

**A-tape — the singer. One place, one hour, no time travel.** मांगू भोपा (the approved star frame: ~58, saffron turban, silver cuffs, red silk kurta, ochre shawl) performs on the hospital helipad through one storm-dusk. His only clock is the sky, and it moves in three states matched to the song's three segments: (1) dry burning gold, the storm a wall far off — (2) the storm crossing the city, wind rising, the तांती on the IV stand whipping — (3) first rain reaching the helipad, hitting him exactly when the vow lands in the lyric. The दीया stays lit through the rain. Final whisper: he is drenched and still, bow lowered, eyes on the lens.

**B-tape — the couple, running backward.** Opens the night before this year's भादवा (the corridor frame we approved: wheelchair, reports bag, hospital band + faded तांती on his wrist). From there the film runs in reverse chronology through five भादवा fairs across ~50 years. Going back: the illness strips away almost immediately (the कर्क is recent — only the newest era carries it), he stands straighter, they get younger, the grade gets warmer, and the note gets sweeter — the middle of the journey is pure marriage. The reverse run ends at their FIRST fair, where the first thread was tied. Then the one jump forward: the present-day fair, granted.

**The fair timeline — the same fair aging backward.** The देवरा and the thread-tying ritual are the invariant — every era contains the same gesture, a thread tied at the same shrine, which is the lyric's theology made visible («हर साल पूरो होवे — हर साल फेर बंधे»). Everything around the ritual ages:

- **भादवा 2026 (the ending, the granted fair):** first monsoon rain on the crowd; giant steel ferris wheel LED-lit; phone flashlights held up; he ties the fresh thread WITH HIS OWN HAND beside the hospital band; the vow. Digital-clean grade washed warm by rain and string lights.
- **भादवा ~2018 (his last fully-healthy fair):** smartphones everywhere, selfies mid-crowd, drone-of-lights feel, plastic toy stalls; the couple in their 60s laughing at the selfie-takers; he ties the thread without looking, habit of decades.
- **भादवा ~2005:** point-and-shoot cameras and cassette-stall speakers, STD-booth-yellow paint (no readable text), auto-rickshaws parked in rows; the couple ~50 — the era of her scooter, kids just left home; he buys her glass bangles, she pretends it's unnecessary, wears them immediately.
- **भादवा ~1990:** film-camera families posing stiffly, hand-painted cinema-style fair hoardings (imagery only), a smaller iron wheel hand-cranked, kerosene lamps at stalls; the couple mid-30s with small kids on shoulders; 16mm grain, faded Kodachrome color.
- **भादवा ~1976 (the first fair, the origin):** bullock carts and camels at the fairground edge, the wheel is wooden, all light is fire and pressure-lantern; the couple newlywed — she leads him to the देवरा through the crowd (SHE brought HIM to Tejaji first — her competence begins here), the FIRST thread goes on his wrist; warm faded-postcard grade, the sweetest note in the film.

**The couple's between-fair life fragments** (slotted between fair eras, same reverse direction, options 2+3 textures): the waiting-room joke that makes her laugh against her will; tea from one thermos on a hospital bench; the two dance steps with the IV stand as partner, her scolding smile; the competence montage cut brisk like sport — forms, clerks argued down, buses caught, him lifted — «ठोकर एक नी खाई»; the scooter years, her driving, him pillion holding the vegetables; the kids' era chaos. Deadpan, dry, never saccharine. The single sanctioned tearjerk: the new thread at the 2026 fair.

## Mapping to the song's three segments

- **Segment 1 (arrival → naming the कर्क):** present tense. Corridor, ward, fair-eve; the A-tape sky dry gold. The reverse run BEGINS at the segment's final instrumental bar — the first step back (2018 fair) lands there.
- **Segment 2 (the quarrel → needles → the लीलण verse):** the deep reverse run — 2005, 1990 — with the competence montage under the quarrel verses and the tenderest marriage beats under the लीलण verse (the verse is HIS description of HER: it plays over her driving, her carrying, her never stumbling, across eras).
- **Segment 3 (the turn → the ask → the vow → whisper):** 1976, the first thread, held long and warm — then the cut forward to भादवा 2026 as the ask is granted: rain breaks over the fair AND over मांगू in the same cut rhythm; he ties the thread; «अगले भादवे फेर आऊँला» lands on his mouth saying it (the one B-tape lip-sync moment in the video); the renewal line over the wheel turning in the rain. Final whisper: the देवरा at night, rain dripping, the fresh thread among hundreds of older ones on the same post — then मांगू, drenched, silent. [Silence] = black.

## Sync honesty rules (A-tape)

The track has no drums: the dholak player NEVER plays — he waits with a palm resting flat on the skin for the entire video, a held tension that pays off never (the god's percussion never arrives). The manjira/second-string silhouettes stay atmospheric in wides; no close-up shows an instrument articulating notes that don't exist in the audio. The algoza is the god's instrument and is NEVER on the helipad: during the track's two distant algoza phrases, cut to an unseen player far away — a silhouette on a distant rooftop (segment 1) and a boy at the 2026 fair's edge (segment 3) — the god answering from wherever he likes.

## Consistency mechanics (production)

The couple must read as the same two people across five eras plus present — this is the project's hardest generation problem. Locks: her nose stud, bun, and green-family saris in every era; his build, brow and the dry half-smile in every era; the wrist ritual staged identically every time (same shrine geometry, same hands framing). Each era gets its own reference still approved BEFORE any coverage of that era is generated; era stills are anchored from the approved present-day couple frame via omni_reference so the faces carry.

## PRODUCTION LAWS v2 — from the previz post-mortem (author, 2026-08-12)

1. ELEMENTS FIRST, ALWAYS. No person is ever generated from a text description again. Three soul-id elements get trained (मांगू, the husband, the wife); every scene prompt names its people BY ELEMENT. People-shots render on Higgsfield Soul 2.0 (text2image_soul_v2), not nano_banana_pro — nano only for unpopulated scenery.
2. MALWA LOCK IN EVERY PROMPT with people: North Indian, Malwa region — no exceptions, including crowds and clips.
3. EDITING GRAMMAR: sung sections belong to the SINGER performing; instrumental sections belong to the COUPLE's photo timeline. The singer must appear frequently — he owns the vocals.
4. LYRIC PRE-ROLL: a B-tape image answering a lyric arrives 2–3 seconds BEFORE its line (the लीलण image leads the लीलण verse).
5. THE KEYFRAME: one recurring composition — the couple tying the तांती in front of the SAME temple, identical staging, once per era. It is the film's chronological spine; every era sequence contains it.
6. CUT: the flute-boy imagery (all of it) and the dholak-player-on-helipad conceit. The algoza plays unseen; no waiting-drummer image.
7. STAGING LOGIC CHECK before any crowd/device shot: cameras point AT subjects, posers face the lens that photographs them, era devices used the way they were actually used.
8. CAMERA/THEME CONSISTENCY: one defined grammar per tape (A-tape: locked low hero angles on the singer; B-tape: era-correct photo/film looks per the era table) — no random jumps between styles within a tape.
