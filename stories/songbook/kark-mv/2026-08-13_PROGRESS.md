## The film's design (treatment RATIFIED — `2026-08-12_TREATMENT_kark-ki-taanti_MV.md`)

Two tapes. **A-tape:** the singer मांगू भोपा performs on the helipad rooftop of a city hospital through one storm-dusk — he never time-travels; his only clock is the sky (dry gold → storm crossing → rain at the vow). **B-tape:** the couple's fifty years, told CHRONOLOGICALLY (v2 inversion — the reverse-chronology v1 was tested in previz and rejected): their photos/scenes run 1976 → present while the song grieves from line one; the disease appears only in the big instrumental; on the vow line the film returns to the happy memories. Editing grammar: sung sections belong to the singer on camera; instrumentals belong to the couple's timeline. B-tape wordless except ONE lip-synced moment — he mouths «अगले भादवे फेर आऊँला» at the 2026 मेला. The singer's algoza answers come from far away (never on the helipad); the dholak player NEVER plays (track has no drums). Five भादवा fairs: 2026 (village मेला, rain, the granted ending), 2018, 2005, 1990, 1976 (the origin: SHE leads HIM to the shrine; first thread). The recurring keyframe — the couple tying the तांती at the SAME shrine, identical staging, once per era — is the film's spine. Parked idea (revisit later): a photo taken at every era's fair with era-correct cameras.

## A-TAPE MOTION LAW (settled 2026-08-13, after ~47 credits of failures)

Seedance 2.5 CANNOT render the ravanhatta being bowed. Three approaches tested and failed: (1) explicit bowing prompt → he plucks; (2) forced start-frame + end-frame with the bow drawn out → he plucks while holding the bow; (3) real bowing footage as --video reference → the model ignored the start image and reproduced the reference footage wholesale (also a rights problem; that source clip and its outputs were trashed). Do not attempt a visible bow stroke again without the author explicitly authorising a test on a different video model (kling/veo).

THE DESIGN-AROUND (author-approved): a bhopa bows BETWEEN sung lines, not during them — so the A-tape is built on shots where the bow is STILL and he is singing. Motion in those shots = wind in the safa tail and wrist threads, storm churning, rain, chest/head movement on held notes — all of which seedance does well (proved on the first motion test). Where bowing must read: only very wide shots (arm too small for detail to matter) or a 1–2s whip-cut of a single stroke. Never a sustained visible bowing close-up.

## AGE-BAND METHOD (settled 2026-08-16, after 10 credits of failures)

Anchored age-down SATURATES: each pass changes less, and no amount of prompting moves a face more than ~10 years from its anchor. Fresh recast gets the age right but loses identity. **THE FIX — composite two references:** one image carrying the quality you want (a fresh cast at the target age), one carrying the identity you need (the approved canonical), with the prompt assigning explicitly — take skin/age/softness from the first, take face shape/eye placement/nose/mouth from the second. Third time on this project that multi-reference beat description (ravanhatta grip, keyframe staging, now age).
