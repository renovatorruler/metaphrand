## Places I'd question — your call on each

**Cue 11, "but they are the great physicians'."** The possessive apostrophe at the end is doing a lot of work and reads as a typo on screen. Alternatives: *"but they belong to the great physicians"* or *"but they are the doctors' marks."*

**Cue 15, "Lilan".** A viewer who doesn't know the Tejaji story won't know this is his mare's name — the line risks landing as a stray word. Options: leave it (the next line explains it by implication), or *"Your Lilan was a mare — mine is this woman."*

**Cue 12, "the needle's fang sets in".** Faithful to the Hindi image, slightly odd in English. Alternative: *"Every day the needle's fang bites in…"*

**Cue 20/21, "So let him reach Bhadva."** Bhadva is unglossed. If you want it legible to non-Hindi viewers: *"So let him reach the Bhadva fair"* on first use only.

**Cue 2, "where is the sting?"** Now that bite marks is fixed, "sting" may want to match — *"But where is the bite?"* keeps the two lines consistent.

**Names.** Teja appears untranslated throughout, which is right. No change proposed.
