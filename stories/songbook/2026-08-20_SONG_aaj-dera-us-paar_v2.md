# «आज डेरा उस पार» — LOCKED TEXT (2026-08-20, expanded 2026-08-24)

**WHAT THE SONG IS (author, 2026-08-24).** It is a death song. «डेरा उस पार» is the crossing; «इतना था फ़रमान» is the summons and its limit; the earth holding the noise behind him is the mourning; the Gita verse at the end is what people actually say plainly over a body once the music has stopped. **This is never acknowledged in the text.** No line may name death, dying, the end, the soul, heaven, God's call, or any farewell. The song only shows a camp being moved. Any line that states the metaphor is banned exactly as the fleeing lines are — naming it would collapse the whole song into an explanation.

**STATUS: LOCKED.** Three standing rulings:

1. **«रण छोड़ चला वो» is load-bearing and PERMANENT** — it is the only place the name रणछोड़ lives, and it must never be removed. «कौन कहे वो भागा है» and every other formulation that questions, denies, or even defends against the idea of fleeing is BANNED — raising the accusation to refute it still lets the accusation into the room. The song never argues; the departure simply happens with dignity.
2. **No sea, no ocean, no seafaring (author, 2026-08-24).** These are desert people; the far bank is across the dunes, not across water. «समंदर» and «जल में जागे द्वार» were a द्वारका allusion and are removed. **«उस पार» stays** — crossing over is the metaphor's own word (वैतरणी included); it was only the seafaring that was foreign.
3. **The death reading above** — implied, never stated.

The Gita 2.23 coda stays at the very end, spoken only.

## What the two new verses are doing (never render this section)

**The orders (verse 3).** His last words to his own commanders are administrative: the flagpole has cracked, bind it. His final concern is that the निशान stays upright — which is exactly what the closing refrain says outlives him. «इस बार» carries the whole weight: there will not be a next time he can do it himself. No one in the song notices; the listener does.

**The laugh (verse 5).** They fill the waterskin for the journey and he laughs at the load — will there be no water on the other side? It is the only moment anyone says out loud what is happening, and it is survivable only because he says it as a joke. Then **चुप रह गई कतार** — the same कतार that turned at his hand in the opening verse now has nothing to say. Nobody weeps and nobody answers; the silence is the entire reveal, and no word for death appears anywhere in the song.
