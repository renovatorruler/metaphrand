# «दस्तक» — the protection song (Bhopal, v1)

The Bhopal cycle, song 2. (Song 1, «तूने पुकारा था» — the water/name song — cleaned of AI-writing 2026-07-29, status: author lukewarm, superseded by this direction.)

Design: the whole song happens in the time between a knock and the opening of a door. Someone stands with a hand on the latch; at the door, a voice says "I've come to protect you." The singer stalls the listener with three true stories — the queen, the paper, the factory — in each of which the protection was REAL and DELIVERED, which is exactly why the city keeps buying it. December is the turn: every door was shut that night, and it came from inside. The demand at the end is not "refuse" (the singer concedes we never refuse — we are frightened people); it is one inherited question to ask before the latch turns. The knock recurs as the only percussion; the song ends on the question, and one last knock, unanswered.

Register: Bhopali Hindi — Urdu-inflected (हिफ़ाज़त, दस्तक, दहलीज़, हवेली, मुहर), courteous, half-spoken. NOT Malvi; not film-Hindi.

Instances stay unnamed (no Kamlapati, no Company, no Union Carbide, no gas) — a Bhopali recognizes each; an outsider still follows every event. The one near-name is the station line, which names her only by what the city did with her.

## Lyrics (paste as-is)

```
[Intro: lone ravanhatta over a low tambura drone, a soft harmonium breath underneath; one dry knock on wood]

[Verse: low, urgent, at the listener's shoulder]
रात है। दरवाज़े पर दस्तक है।
कोई कहता है — डरो मत,
मैं तुम्हारी हिफ़ाज़त करने आया हूँ।
ठहर। खोलने से पहले मेरी बात सुन ले।

[Verse: the queen]
एक रानी थी। झील उसके सिरहाने थी।
दुश्मन उसकी दहलीज़ पर था, और फ़ौज उसके पास नहीं थी।
उसने एक सिपाही रखा और कहा — मेरी हिफ़ाज़त करो।
सिपाही ने हिफ़ाज़त की। सच्ची की।
फिर रानी गुज़र गई।
अब उस झील के ऊपर सिपाही की हवेली है,
और रानी — रानी अब स्टेशन का नाम है।

[A second knock, a little harder — EXTEND SEAM 1]

[Verse: the paper]
फिर एक और सदी, और फिर वही डर।
इस बार दुश्मन घोड़ों पर आता था।
शहर ने एक काग़ज़ पर मुहर लगाई —
लिखा था: उनकी फ़ौज हमारी हिफ़ाज़त करेगी।
फ़ौज ने हिफ़ाज़त की। सच्ची की।
लुटेरे फिर कभी नहीं आए।
बस अब तख़्त पर बैठने से पहले
उनसे पूछना पड़ता था।

[A third knock, impatient — EXTEND SEAM 2]

[Verse: the factory]
फिर हमारा ज़माना आया। हम पढ़े-लिखे हो गए थे।
अब डर फ़सल का था — कीड़ा खेत खा जाता था।
इस बार हिफ़ाज़त ख़ुद चलकर आई।
कहा — दवा बनाएँगे, कीड़ा मरेगा, पेट भरेगा।
इस बार दरवाज़ा हमने ख़ुद खोला। ख़ुशी से।
ज़मीन हमने दी, और शुक्र भी हमने मनाया।

[Verse: December — the drone thins, no swell, almost spoken]
फिर दिसंबर की वो रात आई।
उस रात शहर का हर दरवाज़ा बंद था।
हर खिड़की बंद थी।
...वो अंदर से आई थी।

[Silence for a breath. Then the knocking resumes, steady, under the voice — EXTEND SEAM 3]

[Verse: the inheritance]
अब तू खड़ा है, और दस्तक हो रही है।
मैं तुझे रोकूँगा नहीं।
हम डरे हुए लोग हैं, बेटा —
डरा हुआ आदमी दरवाज़ा खोल ही देता है।
बस खोलने से पहले, उससे एक बात पूछ लेना।

[Final line, almost spoken, very quiet]
रात को सोएगा कहाँ?

[Outro: the drone fades. One last soft knock. Silence.]
```

## Gloss (reference only)

It is night. There is a knock at the door. Someone says: don't be afraid, I've come to protect you. Wait — before you open, hear me out. — There was a queen; the lake lay at her pillow. The enemy was at her threshold and she had no army. She hired a soldier and said: protect me. And he protected her. Truly. Then the queen passed on. Now the soldier's mansion stands over that lake — and the queen? The queen is the name of a railway station now. — Then another century, and the same fear again. This time the enemy came on horses. The city put its seal on a paper that said: their army will protect us. And the army protected. Truly. The raiders never came again. Only — before anyone sat on the throne, they had to be asked. — Then our own age came. We were educated now. Now the fear was for the crop — the worm was eating the fields. This time protection came walking on its own. It said: we'll make the medicine, the worm will die, bellies will fill. This time we opened the door ourselves. Gladly. We gave the land, and we gave the thanks too. — Then that December night came. That night every door in the city was shut. Every window was shut. ...It came from inside. — Now you stand there, and the knocking is going on. I won't stop you. We are frightened people, son — a frightened man always opens the door in the end. Just ask him one thing before you do. — Where will you sleep at night?

## Craft notes

- «सच्ची की» recurs once per historical instance by design: the protection was always genuine — that is the mechanism, not a flaw in it. The city is never stupid; it is afraid, and the fear is always justified.
- December carries no gas, no company, no casualties: only the fact that shut doors did not matter, and four words — वो अंदर से आई थी.
- The station line names the queen only by her erasure; the 2021 renaming makes it literal.
- The final question is the niti: a guard who sleeps outside is a guard; the one who sleeps inside owns the house tomorrow. The song never says this — the three stories already did.
