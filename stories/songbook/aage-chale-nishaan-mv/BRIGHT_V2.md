# Bright historical-cinematic v2

`frames_v2_bright/` is the preferred delivery. It retains every approved frame ID and story action from v1 while moving the visual language toward polished Indian historical-epic cinema.

## Treatment

- Performance world: luminous royal-cobalt blue hour, warm amber key on Mangu, cool shadow fill, a restrained gold rim, saturated saffron and vermilion, polished silver, and a fully readable Phad.
- Story world: radiant late golden hour, saffron-apricot cloud detail, bright silver-blue water, warm directional sunlight, rich madder and indigo fabrics, and clear faces, horses, earth, and crossing action.
- Mythological influence comes through scale, color, light, dignity, and iconic composition—not deities, halos, magical portals, divine beams, palaces, or fantasy effects.
- The look stays photorealistic and period-grounded rather than becoming a glossy television serial, illustration, or CGI tableau.
