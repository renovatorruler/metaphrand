## Creative lock

- The performer is the established Mangu Bhopa from the *Kark ki Tanti* work: weathered face, large upturned moustache, open salt-and-pepper beard, saffron Rajasthani safa with turra and pech, red silk kurta, white dhoti trousers, and heavy silver jewelry.
- Mangu holds a round pale-hide **dafli** with small colored pom-poms. He never holds a ravanhatta or any bowed instrument.
- He performs before one real Phad textile stretched between two rough poles in an open field. The Phad remains a physical painted cloth, never a magical portal.
- The interlaced story follows an approximately seventy-year-old commander on one ash-grey horse. He remains last in the column while looking toward the river and the far bank.
- The army moves consistently from screen-left to screen-right. It has one plain faded madder-red standard and two pack animals. There is no battle, triumphal spectacle, supernatural imagery, palace, or fort.
