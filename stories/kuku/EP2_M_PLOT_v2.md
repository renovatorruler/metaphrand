# कुकु और मिटासुर — Episode 2: म — Plot v2 (ADVENTURE MODE, 2026-07-21)
### "जिस दिन म ग़ायब हुआ" — "The Day म Went Missing"

**Mode:** letter-hero adventure (see `SERIES_ADVENTURE_MODE.md`). Supersedes EP2_M_PLOT_v1
(gentle tier). Same म words, same emotional core (lost chick + माँ, the दादी-is-पापा's-माँ
reveal) — now wrapped in a villain/mission/team story at Miniforce/Super-Kitties tier.

**Letter:** म · **Words at stake:** माँ · मेला · मिठाई · मोर · मछली · मदद · माया.
**Villain:** मिटासुर (the Eraser). **Sod (never on page):** everyone is someone's child; and
the one who steals what others make is really just someone who was never taught he could make
his own.

## Beat sheet (8-beat adventure engine)

**1. Ordinary open (~1 min).** A postcard arrives — from **माशा**! She's at a huge मेला in her
city. The kids erupt: our मेला is TODAY! The valley bustles — Dadi's **मिठाई** stall, the
**मछली** pond, a **मोर** family parading, chicks trailing their माँ. Kuku, Furia, Vesper, Kalu
tumble in. Establish म is everywhere and everyone's happy.

**2. THE THEFT (the hook).** High on a hill, **मिटासुर** — a roly-poly purple goblin with
sponge-hands — watches the मेला, sulking: he has no मेला, no friends, nothing of his own. So he
does what he always does: he **rubs out a letter.** He erases **म** from the whole valley. In one
sweep: the **मिठाई** goes flavourless (िठाई), the **मोर** can't fan its feathers, and worst — a
tiny **मोर का बच्चा** cries out for its "…" but the word **माँ** is GONE; mother and chick can no
longer even name each other. The मेला buckles. मिटासुर scoops the glowing stolen म into his sack
and waddles off, cackling.

**3. आज का अक्षर (motivated reveal).** Chaos. Dadi gathers the team on her rock: "म चोरी हो गया।
उसे वापस लाने के लिए, हमें म सीखना होगा!" She reveals the golden **म** shape (a spare, from her
own name — «'माया' में म है!»). **Vesper** drifts: "that's M!" → «हिंदी में, वैस्पर!» → Dadi:
English M **टोपी नहीं पहनता**; म पहनता है (the hat rule, generalized past Furia's Ф). Sound,
call-and-response «बोलो मेरे साथ — म!», corner-bug parks. Now they know their weapon.

**4. THE HUNT (team set-piece).** «अक्षर वीर, तैयार?!» **Kalu** catches मिटासुर's chalky scent.
**Furia** races «मैं पहले!» through the un-spelled मेला (dodging comic chaos — a flavourless
मिठाई cart, a moping मोर). मिटासुर scampers, tossing erased-letter banana-peels. **Vesper** —
everyone shouting, he staring up — is the only one who SEES where मिटासुर hid the म: inside a big
clay **मटका** by the stream. They corner him. But मिटासुर hugs the sack: «नहीं! ये मेरा है! मेरे
पास अपना कुछ नहीं है!» (No! It's mine! I have nothing of my own!) — his wound, out loud.

**5. THE POWER-UP.** Across the stream the chick teeters at the water's edge, crying for the माँ
it can't name; the मोरनी searches the far bank. No time to argue with मिटासुर. Kuku, scared but
steady: «अक्षर वीर, तैयार!» → the team locks in → «साँस… टोपी… अक्षर!» → hatless ball → **टोपी
SNAPS → म!** A blazing golden **म**.

**6. THE FIX + FOIL.** The forged **म**'s loop unfolds into a **cradle-boat** on the stream — the
letter that holds like a mother's arms — and carries the chick across to its माँ (reunion nuzzle).
At the same instant the restored म **floods back through the valley**: मिठाई sweet again, the मोर
FANS its feathers in a burst of color, माँ and बच्चा everywhere can say each other's names. मिटासुर's
stolen sack empties — foiled, comic, deflating like a balloon.

**7. HEART (the turn + the reveal).** मिटासुर sulks in the mud: he only ever stole because he
could never make his own. Kuku — who once couldn't make fire — kneels: «तुम भी बना सकते हो। हम
सिखाएँगे।» (You can make them too. We'll teach you.) They hand him a **मिठाई** and pull him into
the मेला. Then Kuku, watching the peacock family: «दादी, क्या आपकी भी माँ थीं?» And the reveal —
**दादी माया, पापा की माँ हैं.** Papa arrives to fetch them, kisses Dadi's head: «हाँ, ये मेरी माँ
हैं।» Everyone is someone's child.

**8. RECAP + goodnight.** Dadi to camera counts the म-words they SAVED: «म से — माँ! मेला! मिठाई!
मोर! मछली! मदद! माया!» टोपी rule: «हिंदी का हर अक्षर टोपी पहनता है — English M नहीं!» Furia writes
म in her किताब «मैं पहले!». Pan: **Vesper asleep in Dadi's empty मिठाई basket**, Kalu curled beside
it, मिटासुर snoring nearby with a मिठाई in his fist. «शुभ रात्रि, वैस्पर।»

## Why this hits the mature tier (checklist vs the reference shows)
- **Villain with agency + pathos** (Miniforce/Super-Kitties): मिटासुर steals, has a wound, gets a turn.
- **Real stakes:** the whole valley's म in peril, not one puppy.
- **Team mission + set-piece:** each hero's gift load-bearing in the hunt.
- **Transformation/power-up as heroism**, not just a quirk.
- **Emotional core preserved:** the chick + माँ, the दादी reveal.
- **Pedagogy intact & STRONGER:** the letter is the weapon; "what breaks without म?" = phonemic
  awareness as gameplay; motivated reveal; explicit recap.
