Camera tracks at child height. Small red-pink Furia eagerly runs several short steps across level grass, opens her small wings, makes one very low brief hop, then lands safely on both feet and steadies herself. Her four small friends and winged silver Dadi watch quietly in the background. Furia remains a small child dragon. No sustained flight, gliding, fire, bracelet transformation, body growth, disappearing friend or slapstick fall.
