## FIX 1 — Why only Kuku's letter can hold the gate

**Broken:** order 106 — *"इसे हमेशा के लिए केवल एक जीवित अक्षर सँभाल सकता है।"* "Living
letter" is unexplained jargon; the rule exists only because the plot needs Kuku, and it shows.

**Canon that fixes it (CHARACTER_BIBLE, the show's core magic rule):** *a completed
Kuku-forged structure cannot be unmade — creation and persistence are his power.* Eight
episodes old. The script simply never used it.

**The apparent contradiction, resolved by lines we already recorded:** the eagle broke Kuku's
letter-bridge (order 68). How, if Kuku's work can't break? Because the eagle carries **Rishi's
own boon** — *"जो उड़े — वो मुझसे कभी जीत न पाए"* (order 111). The boon outranks the letter.
That is exactly why Rishi says *"यह मुसीबत मेरे दिए वर से आई है। मैं सँभालूँगा"* (order 112).
The script already carries its own answer — the new line just states the rule so the boon
exception lands as meaningful instead of unnoticed.

**Line changes (re-record 1, replacing order 106):**
- 106a RISHI: «यही गुरुकुल का रास्ता है। मेरी छड़ी इसे थोड़ी देर थाम लेगी — पर पत्थर एक बार
  टूटा है, फिर टूटेगा। छड़ियाँ झुक जाती हैं।»
- 106b RISHI: «कुकु की साँस से बना अक्षर कभी नहीं टूटता। द्वार की खाली जगह उसे ही चाहिए —
  हमेशा के लिए।»

A child can repeat the syllogism: stone breaks again → staffs bend → what Kuku makes never
breaks → so the gate needs Kuku's letter. "Why gate-sized" follows free: the letter fills the
hole, so it must be the hole's size (the S59 picture already shows notch and ब as the same
shape).

**Picture:** no new beats needed — S3 (notch), S59 (letter/notch match) already carry it.

## FIX 2 — Why a broken gate pulls things: the DRAFT

**Broken:** the gate "wakes" (order 105) and then menacingly attracts clouds with no
mechanism. The three orbits (order 202) are an arbitrary countdown.

**Fix — a mechanism every child knows:** the gate is the door between the valley and the
gurukul above the clouds. With its piece fallen out it is **stuck half-open, and an open door
makes a draft**. The draft pulls air, clouds, and anything riding them, up through the
doorway. The bell rings in the draft. Rishi's staff is a doorstop, not a spell. And the draft
**spirals** things up like water around a drain — each loop closer to the door. Three loops,
then through. The countdown becomes physics a five-year-old can see.

Vesper already seeded it, recorded: *"हवा आज सीधी नहीं चल रही है। कोई उसे ऊपर खींच रहा है"*
(order 88, survives untouched and now pays off).

**Line changes (re-record 2):**
- 105 RISHI (was: "...यह टूटा द्वार जाग गया"): «कल चील ने तुम्हारा अक्षर-पुल तोड़ा। उसकी गूँज
  मेरे गुरुकुल तक पहुँची — और द्वार का घिसा हुआ हिस्सा गिर पड़ा। अब द्वार अधखुला अटका है, और
  अधखुला द्वार हवा खींचता है — जैसे खुला दरवाज़ा।»
- 202 LEDA (was: "बादल तीन चक्कर में द्वार तक पहुँच जाएगा। पहला चक्कर शुरू हो गया है।"):
  «बादल पानी की तरह घूमता हुआ ऊपर जा रहा है — हर चक्कर द्वार के और पास। तीन चक्कर में पहुँच
  जाएगा। पहला शुरू हो चुका है।»

**Picture:** already carried — S18 (`ST_gate_pull`) shows cloud streamers flowing up into the
gap; S3 shows the notch and swinging bell. Optional 2s insert of the notch "breathing" cloud
wisps through itself if scene 1 wants it, not required.

## FIX 3 — Why we care about the goat

**Broken:** the goat is a stakes prop nobody has met, standing on a cloud for no reason. The
rescue of a stranger's goat is not a story.

**Seeds that exist, recorded:** Dadi's *"कालू, बकरी के बच्चे को खेलने दो। तुम उसकी घंटी पहचान
गए हो"* (order 94), and beat S02-B04 (S13) — currently staged as a geography diagram.

**Fix — spend the same seconds on a bond, and give the cloud a reason:**
- Restage S13 (6s): the goat kid **copies the children's practice hops** beside Kalu, tumbles
  over its own legs, the five laugh mid-drill. One added line for warmth:
  - NEW (CASTOR, ~2s): «देखो! बकरी का बच्चा भी अभ्यास कर रहा है!»
- Establish the low cloud as **the goat's napping spot** — it grazes so low it touches the
  meadow; the goat curls up on it like a cushion. One added line while the kids rest:
  - NEW (VESPER or KUKU, ~3s): «बकरी का बच्चा फिर अपने बादल पर सो गया।»
- Then scene 6: when the wrong-rhythm flight widens the crack and the draft surges, the draft
  takes **that** cloud — its favourite cushion — with the goat asleep on it. Order 199
  (*"बकरी का बच्चा जमीन से उठ गया है"*) survives and now lands as *their friend is on that
  cloud*, not "a goat is flying."

Orders 133, 182, 205, 218–266 (the whole rescue chain) survive untouched and inherit the
stakes.

**Picture:** restage S13 (goat copying hops, one still); one new still of the goat napping on
the low cloud (also becomes the scene-6 lift's setup frame). The existing goat-lift shots
(S73/S75) already match.
