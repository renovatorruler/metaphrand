कुकु और अक्षर — Episode 2: «म से माँ»
(Devanagari dialogue with English glosses; English sluglines & action. Papercraft dragons, ages 4–7.)

FADE IN:

EXT. अक्षर घाटी (LETTER VALLEY) — MORNING

Cut-paper hills in soft morning light. A stream winks. The whole valley is dressed for a fair — paper bunting, little stalls. This is the मेला, and it is today.

At the center, DADI MAYA — an elderly grey dragon grandmother, spectacles on her snout, a knitted paper shawl over her folded wings — arranges her मिठाई stall. The sweets glow like little jewels.

KUKU, a small round moss-green baby dragon, tumbles in. Behind him come FYURIA (bright pink-red, a head taller, paws already on her hips) and her little brother VESPER (soft blue, half asleep on his feet). A tiny black puppy, KALU, bounces after them. Last, the toddler CHEEKU (tiny, yellow), waddling with a paper spoon.

A paper postcard flutters down into Kuku's paws — a picture of a huge, faraway fair.

KUKU
(reading, delighted)
माशा का ख़त आया है! वो बड़े शहर के मेले में गई है!
(Masha's postcard has come! She has gone to the big city fair!)

FYURIA
और हमारा मेला आज है!
(And our fair is today!)

She throws her arms wide. A peacock family struts past — a मोर, a मोरनी, and three fuzzy chicks trailing their mother in a line. A मछली pond sparkles. On every stall and banner, the letter म.

Behind sleepy Vesper, CHEEKU lifts a paper party-popper and squeezes it.

POP!

Vesper's dreamy eyes snap open. His mouth opens, and —

VESPER
AAAAAAAA—!!!

— he SHRIEKS. A colossal, ear-splitting sound. Huge papercraft sound-rings blast outward. Everyone claps their paws over their ears. The hills ripple. The peacock chicks scatter, cheeping.

Then, just like that, Vesper closes his mouth. Yawns. Blinks slowly, calm again.

FYURIA
(ears ringing)
वैस्पर!

Vesper only smiles, sleepy, as if nothing happened.

DADI
(chuckling, to camera)
हमारा वैस्पर सबसे शांत भी है — और सबसे ज़ोरदार भी।
(Our Vesper is both the quietest — and the loudest.)

EXT. HILL ABOVE THE VALLEY — CONTINUOUS

High above, someone watches the happy मेला and does not smile.

मिटासुर — a roly-poly, dusty grey-purple goblin, soft and smudgy at the edges, with big googly eyes and two fat pink-purple sponge-hands. A burlap sack hangs off one shoulder, faint glowing letters poking out. He sulks.

मिटासुर
(quiet, to himself)
सबके पास मेला है। सबके पास दोस्त हैं। और मेरे पास? मेरे पास अपना कुछ भी नहीं है।
(Everyone has a fair. Everyone has friends. And me? I have nothing of my own.)

He looks down at the biggest, brightest thing in the valley — the letter म, glowing on every banner. His sponge-hands twitch.

मिटासुर (CONT'D)
मैं मिटासुर हूँ। जो चीज़ मैं बना नहीं सकता, उसे मैं मिटा सकता हूँ!
(I am Mitasur. What I cannot make, I can erase!)

He sweeps a sponge-hand across the air —

WHIFF.

— and the म is GONE, erased from the whole valley at once.

EXT. अक्षर घाटी — CONTINUOUS

The world buckles. On Dadi's stall, मिठाई becomes िठाई, the letters half there. Kuku bites a sweet. No taste. He blinks.

The मोर spreads his tail to fan it — and can't. The feathers hang flat.

By the stream, the smallest chick turns to call for its mother. Its beak opens. Nothing comes — the word is gone. It cheeps, lost. Across the pond the मोरनी lifts her head, searching, and cannot call back. Mother and child, and neither can name the other.

Up on the hill, मिटासुर scoops the glowing म into his sack and waddles off.

EXT. DADI'S ROCK — DAY

The team gathers on Dadi's flat rock. Around them the मेला sags, grey and quiet.

DADI
बच्चों, हमारा म चोरी हो गया है। उसे वापस लाने के लिए हमें पहले म को पहचानना होगा।
(Children, our म has been stolen. To bring it back, we must first learn to recognize म.)

She breathes. A golden म blooms above the rock, glowing warm.

DADI (CONT'D)
देखो, मेरे नाम 'माया' में भी म है। म से माया!
(Look, there is a म in my own name, 'Maya.' म is for Maya!)

Vesper gazes up at it, drifting.

VESPER
(soft, in English)
that's... M.

FYURIA
हिंदी में, वैस्पर!
(In Hindi, Vesper!)

VESPER
(reaching, finding it)
...म।

DADI
(to camera, warm)
जब भी तुम माँ बोलते हो, मेला बोलते हो, या मिठाई खाते हो — सबसे पहले म आता है। बोलो मेरे साथ — म!
(Whenever you say माँ, say मेला, or eat मिठाई — म comes first. Say it with me — म!)

KUKU / FYURIA / VESPER
म!

Kalu barks. Cheeku claps.

DADI (CONT'D)
अब जाओ, बच्चों — अपना अक्षर वापस ले आओ।
(Now go, children — bring your letter back.)

EXT. THE UN-SPELLED मेला — DAY

KUKU
(planting his little feet)
अक्षर वीर — तैयार?!
(Letter Heroes — ready?!)

FYURIA / VESPER
तैयार!

Kalu drops his nose, sniffs, and yips — a scent, chalky and smudgy. He bolts. Fyuria tears after him.

FYURIA
पहले मैं!
(Me first!)

They chase through the faded fair, past the flat-tailed मोर and the tasteless मिठाई cart. Ahead, मिटासुर scampers, flinging slippery erased-letter peels behind him. Fyuria skids. Kuku hops the peels.

But Vesper has stopped. He is looking — really looking, the way only he does — and he sees it: down by the stream, a fat clay मटका, a thread of golden light leaking from its mouth.

VESPER
(soft)
वो देखो... म उस मटके में है।
(Look there... the म is in that pot.)

Nobody hears him. Fyuria is shouting, Kalu is barking, the chase is loud.

VESPER (CONT'D)
(a little louder, still gentle)
म मटके में है...
(The म is in the pot...)

Still nothing. The म is slipping further away.

Vesper takes a breath. Plants his feet.

VESPER (CONT'D)
AAAAAAAA—!!!

THE SHRIEK. The whole valley freezes mid-step. Fyuria, Kuku, Kalu — even मिटासुर — clap their paws over their ears. Sound-rings roll across the paper hills. Total, ringing silence.

Into the silence, Vesper lowers his paw and points, calm.

VESPER (CONT'D)
म मटके में है।
(The म is in the pot.)

Every head turns to the clay मटका by the stream. There it is.

They rush it. मिटासुर dives and throws his sponge-arms around the sack.

मिटासुर
नहीं! ये मेरा है! मेरे पास अपना कुछ भी नहीं है!
(No! This is mine! I have nothing of my own!)

The team stops short. That was not a villain's laugh. That was the truth, and it is sad.

EXT. STREAMSIDE — CONTINUOUS

A cry cuts across the water. The chick has wandered to the very edge of the stream, cheeping for the माँ it cannot name, one wobbly step from the current. Across the pond the मोरनी flaps, frantic, unable to call it back.

FYURIA
(alarm)
मदद करो! वो चूज़ा पानी में गिर जाएगा!
(Help! That chick is going to fall in the water!)

No time for the sack, no time for मिटासुर. Kuku turns to the chick. His little paws shake — but he steadies.

KUKU
अक्षर वीर — तैयार!
(Letter Heroes — ready!)

The team locks in around him, paw to paw.

FYURIA / VESPER
कुकु, तुम कर सकते हो!
(Kuku, you can do it!)

Kuku draws a huge breath — and breathes out a river of golden light. It gathers over the water, curls, and takes shape —

— म!

A blazing golden म hangs over the stream.

And the instant it is MADE, मिटासुर's sack bursts open. The eraser cannot hold against a thing freshly created. Every letter he ever stole pours out and streams back over the hills, home.

EXT. STREAM — CONTINUOUS

The new म turns in the air. Its lower loop unfolds, and unfolds again, into a little boat curved like two arms — a cradle on the water.

It slides under the chick just as it topples, catches it, and carries it gently across to its mother. The मोरनी folds her wings around it. A nuzzle. Safe.

Behind them the valley wakes all at once. The मिठाई glows sweet again. The मोर throws his tail wide in a hundred colors. Everywhere — every mother, every little one — they can say each other's names again. म is home.

मिटासुर sits in the mud, the empty sack in his lap, deflated like a balloon. Alone.

EXT. STREAMSIDE — CONTINUOUS

Fyuria bounces, flush with the win.

FYURIA
हम जीत गए! चलो, वापस मेले में चलते हैं!
(We won! Come on, let's go back to the fair!)

But Kuku does not move. He is looking at the small, beaten goblin in the mud.

KUKU
रुको। अगर हम उसे ऐसे ही छोड़कर चले गए, तो वो कल फिर से चुराएगा।
(Wait. If we just leave him like this, he will steal again tomorrow.)

Fyuria stops. She had not thought of that.

Kuku crosses and kneels in front of मिटासुर — the little dragon who once could not make his own fire.

KUKU (CONT'D)
तुम चुराते हो क्योंकि तुम बना नहीं सकते। पर तुम बनाना सीख सकते हो — मैं तुम्हें सिखाऊँगा।
(You steal because you cannot make. But you can learn to make — I will teach you.)

मिटासुर
(small)
मैं? नहीं... मेरे हाथ तो सिर्फ़ मिटाना जानते हैं।
(Me? No... my hands only know how to erase.)

KUKU
आँखें बंद करो और मेरे साथ लंबी साँस लो।
(Close your eyes and take a long breath with me.)

Kuku breathes with him, and guides him.

KUKU (CONT'D)
अब धीरे से फूँक मारो।
(Now blow out, gently.)

मिटासुर's fat sponge-hands tremble. He breathes out — and a tiny, wobbly little म flickers into the air. Lopsided, and completely his own.

His googly eyes go huge. He cups it in his sponge-hands as if it might break.

मिटासुर (CONT'D)
(a whisper of wonder)
मैंने... मैंने ख़ुद बनाया!
(I... I made it myself!)

The team cheers. Fyuria puts a मिठाई in his other hand and pulls him up.

FYURIA
चलो, अब सब मेले में चलते हैं!
(Come on, now let's all go to the fair!)

They tug the stunned, glowing goblin into the bright मेला.

EXT. THE मेला — EVENING

A warm shadow falls across the stalls. PAPA — broad, dark-green, a little tired, a tiny paper radio on his chest strap — arrives. He walks straight to Dadi Maya, leans down, and kisses the top of her grey head.

Kuku stares — first at Papa, then at Dadi, then back.

KUKU
पापा, आपने दादी को प्यार किया... क्या दादी आपकी माँ हैं?
(Papa, you kissed Dadi... is Dadi your mother?)

PAPA
(smiling)
हाँ बेटा, दादी मेरी माँ हैं। माँ तो सबकी होती है — बड़ों की भी।
(Yes, son, Dadi is my mother. Everyone has a mother — even the grown-ups.)

Kuku's eyes go wide. Dadi — his Dadi — is Papa's माँ. Everyone, even the grown-ups, even the old ones, is somebody's child.

EXT. DADI'S HOUSE — EVENING

The little oil lamp glows. The मेला winds down, warm and gold.

Dadi turns to camera, counting on her claws.

DADI
आज हमने अपना म वापस पाया। बोलो, म से हमें क्या-क्या मिला?
(Today we got our म back. Tell me, what did म give us?)
म से माँ! मेला! मिठाई! मोर! मछली! मदद! और — माया!
(म is for mother! Fair! Sweets! Peacock! Fish! Help! And — Maya!)

She settles, and her voice drops into something older and wiser.

DADI (CONT'D)
और एक बात, बच्चों। तुमने सिर्फ़ अपना अक्षर वापस नहीं लिया — तुमने चोरी की वजह भी छीन ली।
(And one more thing, children. You didn't just take your letter back — you also took away the reason to steal.)
दुश्मन को दोस्त बना लो, तो वो दोबारा कभी नहीं चुराता।
(Turn an enemy into a friend, and he never steals again.)

Nearby, Fyuria opens her किताब and carefully writes the day's म.

FYURIA
(grinning)
पहले मैं!
(Me first!)

The camera drifts. Curled up fast asleep in Dadi's now-empty मिठाई basket is VESPER, out cold, Kalu snuggled against him. And right beside them, snoring softly, is मिटासुर — his lopsided little म cradled in one sponge-hand, a मिठाई in the other.

DADI (O.S.)
(soft)
शुभ रात्रि, वैस्पर।
(Goodnight, Vesper.)

The lamp dims.

FADE OUT.

THE END.
