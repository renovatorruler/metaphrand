कुकु और अक्षर — Episode 2: «म से माँ»
(Devanagari dialogue with English glosses; English sluglines & action. Papercraft dragons, ages 4–7.)

FADE IN:

EXT. अक्षर घाटी (LETTER VALLEY) — MORNING

Cut-paper hills in soft morning light. The whole valley is dressed for a fair — paper bunting, little stalls, and on every banner a glowing letter म. DADI MAYA arranges her मिठाई stall. KUKU tumbles in with FYURIA, sleepy VESPER, puppy KALU, and toddler CHEEKU.

A paper postcard flutters down into Kuku's paws.

KUKU
(reading, delighted)
माशा का ख़त आया है! वो बड़े शहर के मेले में गई है!
(Masha's postcard has come! She has gone to the big city fair!)

FYURIA
और हमारा मेला आज है!
(And our fair is today!)

DADI
(warm, to camera)
और देखो, बच्चों — आज हमारे मेले का अक्षर हर झंडे पर चमक रहा है। ये है म! म से मेला!
(And look, children — today our fair's letter is shining on every banner. This is म! म is for मेला!)

A peacock family struts past — a मोर, a मोरनी, and three fuzzy chicks. A मछली pond sparkles.

Behind sleepy Vesper, CHEEKU lifts a paper party-popper and squeezes it.

POP!

Vesper's dreamy eyes snap open. His mouth opens, and —

VESPER
AAAAAAAA—!!!

— he SHRIEKS. Sound-rings blast outward. Everyone claps their paws over their ears. Then Vesper closes his mouth, yawns, calm again.

FYURIA
(ears ringing)
चीकू! तुमने वैस्पर को डरा दिया!
(Cheeku! You scared Vesper!)

Cheeku giggles. Vesper only smiles, sleepy.

DADI
(chuckling, to camera)
हमारा वैस्पर सबसे शांत भी है — और सबसे ज़ोरदार भी।
(Our Vesper is both the quietest — and the loudest.)

EXT. HILL ABOVE THE VALLEY — CONTINUOUS

High above, मिटासुर — a roly-poly grey-purple goblin with googly eyes, fat pink sponge-hands, a burlap sack on his shoulder — watches the happy मेला and sulks.

मिटासुर
(quiet, to himself)
सबके पास मेला है। सबके पास दोस्त हैं। और मेरे पास? मेरे पास अपना कुछ भी नहीं है।
(Everyone has a fair. Everyone has friends. And me? I have nothing of my own.)

He looks down at the glowing म on every banner. His sponge-hands twitch.

मिटासुर (CONT'D)
मैं मिटासुर हूँ! जो चीज़ मैं बना नहीं सकता, उसे मैं मिटा सकता हूँ।
(I am Mitasur! What I cannot make, I can erase.)

मिटासुर (CONT'D)
(gleeful, deciding)
आज मैं इस मेले का सबसे चमकता अक्षर मिटाऊँगा — म! आ जाओ, म — मेरे थैले में!
(Today I will erase this fair's brightest letter — म! Come, म — into my sack!)

He sweeps a sponge-hand across the air — WHIFF — and golden light streams up off the whole valley into his sack.

मिटासुर (CONT'D)
(hugging the glowing sack)
अब ये म मेरा है! सिर्फ़ मेरा!
(Now this म is mine! Only mine!)

He waddles off over the hill.

EXT. अक्षर घाटी — CONTINUOUS

Below, the fair's colors dim to grey. Kuku bites a sweet — and blinks.

KUKU
दादी, इस मिठाई का स्वाद कहाँ चला गया? ये तो बिल्कुल फीकी है!
(Dadi, where did this sweet's taste go? It's completely flavorless!)

DADI
(gasping at her stall sign)
अरे! मेरे बोर्ड को देखो! 'मिठाई' से म उड़ गया है — अब सिर्फ़ 'ठाई' बचा है!
(Oh no! Look at my sign! The म has flown off 'मिठाई' — only 'ठाई' is left!)

VESPER
(soft, pointing)
मोर भी उदास है... उसकी पूँछ खुल ही नहीं रही।
(The peacock is sad too... his tail won't open at all.)

By the stream, the smallest chick opens its beak — and no sound comes.

FYURIA
और वो नन्हा चूज़ा देखो! वो अपनी माँ को पुकार नहीं पा रहा — 'माँ' बोलने के लिए भी तो म चाहिए!
(And look at that little chick! It can't call its mother — you need म even to say 'माँ'!)

DADI
(grave, gathering them)
बच्चों, ये किसी ने हमारा म चुरा लिया है। मेरे साथ चलो।
(Children, someone has stolen our म. Come with me.)

EXT. DADI'S ROCK — DAY

The team gathers on Dadi's flat rock. Around them the मेला sags, grey and quiet.

DADI
म को वापस लाने के लिए हमें पहले म को पहचानना होगा।
(To bring म back, we must first learn to recognize म.)

She breathes. A golden म blooms above the rock, glowing warm.

DADI (CONT'D)
देखो — ये है म। मेरे नाम 'माया' में भी म है। म से माया!
(Look — this is म. There is a म in my own name, 'Maya,' too. म is for Maya!)

Vesper gazes up at it, drifting.

VESPER
(soft, in English)
that's... M.

FYURIA
हिंदी में, वैस्पर!
(In Hindi, Vesper!)

VESPER
(reaching, finding it)
...म।

DADI
(to camera, warm)
जब भी तुम माँ बोलते हो, मेला बोलते हो, या मिठाई खाते हो — सबसे पहले म आता है। बोलो मेरे साथ — म!
(Whenever you say माँ, say मेला, or eat मिठाई — म comes first. Say it with me — म!)

KUKU / FYURIA / VESPER
म!

Kalu barks. Cheeku claps.

DADI (CONT'D)
अब जाओ, बच्चों — अपना अक्षर वापस ले आओ।
(Now go, children — bring your letter back.)

EXT. THE UN-SPELLED मेला — DAY

KUKU
(planting his little feet)
अक्षर वीर — तैयार?!
(Letter Heroes — ready?!)

FYURIA / VESPER
तैयार!

KUKU
कालू, सूँघो! चोर ने यहीं से म चुराया है — उसका पता ढूँढो!
(Kalu, sniff! The thief stole म from right here — find his trail!)

Kalu drops his nose, sniffs along a chalky smudge-trail, and yips — he BOLTS.

FYURIA
कालू को चोर का पता मिल गया है! वो देखो — वो रहा चोर! पकड़ो उसे!
(Kalu has found the thief's trail! Look — there's the thief! Catch him!)

She tears after him.

FYURIA (CONT'D)
पहले मैं!
(Me first!)

They chase through the faded fair. Ahead, मिटासुर scampers, flinging slippery peels behind him.

मिटासुर
(running, gleeful)
भागो, भागो! तुम मुझे कभी नहीं पकड़ पाओगे!
(Run, run! You will never catch me!)

But Vesper has stopped. He is looking — really looking — down toward the stream: a fat clay मटका, golden light leaking from its mouth.

VESPER
(soft)
वो देखो... म उस मटके में है।
(Look there... the म is in that pot.)

Nobody hears him. Fyuria is shouting, Kalu is barking, the chase is loud.

VESPER (CONT'D)
(a little louder, still gentle)
म मटके में है...
(The म is in the pot...)

Still nothing. Vesper takes a breath. Plants his feet.

VESPER (CONT'D)
AAAAAAAA—!!!

THE SHRIEK. The whole valley freezes mid-step. Everyone — even मिटासुर — claps their paws over their ears. Ringing silence.

Into the silence, Vesper points, calm.

VESPER (CONT'D)
म मटके में है।
(The म is in the pot.)

Every head turns. There it is — the glowing मटका. They rush it. मिटासुर dives and throws his sponge-arms around the sack.

मिटासुर
नहीं! ये मेरा है! मेरे पास अपना कुछ भी नहीं है!
(No! This is mine! I have nothing of my own!)

The team stops short. That was not a villain's laugh. That was the truth, and it is sad.

EXT. STREAMSIDE — CONTINUOUS

A cry cuts across the water — the chick has wandered to the stream's edge, one wobbly step from the current.

FYURIA
(alarm)
मदद करो! वो चूज़ा पानी में गिर जाएगा!
(Help! That chick is going to fall in the water!)

Kuku turns to the chick. His little paws shake — but he steadies.

KUKU
अक्षर वीर — तैयार! मैं अभी म बनाता हूँ!
(Letter Heroes — ready! I will make a म right now!)

The team locks in around him, paw to paw.

FYURIA / VESPER
कुकु, तुम कर सकते हो!
(Kuku, you can do it!)

Kuku draws a huge breath — and breathes out a river of golden light. It gathers over the water, curls, and takes shape — म! A blazing golden म hangs over the stream.

And the instant it is made, मिटासुर's sack BURSTS open. Letters pour out and stream home over the hills.

VESPER
(clear, amazed — the seer)
देखो! मिटासुर का थैला फट गया! जो अक्षर ख़ुद बनाया जाता है, उसे कोई मिटा नहीं सकता!
(Look! Mitasur's sack has burst! A letter you make yourself, no one can erase!)

The new म turns in the air, unfolds into a little boat curved like two arms, and slides under the chick just as it topples.

FYURIA
देखो, देखो! म एक नाव बन गया! वो चूज़े को उसकी माँ के पास ले जा रहा है!
(Look, look! The म has become a boat! It's carrying the chick to its mother!)

The मोरनी folds her wings around her chick. Safe. Behind them, color floods back across the valley — the मिठाई glows, the मोर fans his tail wide.

KUKU
(joy)
रंग वापस आ रहे हैं! मिठाई फिर मीठी है, मोर फिर नाच रहा है — हमारा मेला वापस आ गया!
(The colors are coming back! The sweets are sweet again, the peacock is dancing again — our fair is back!)

मिटासुर sits in the mud, the empty sack in his lap, deflated. Alone.

EXT. STREAMSIDE — CONTINUOUS

FYURIA
हम जीत गए! चलो, वापस मेले में चलते हैं!
(We won! Come on, let's go back to the fair!)

But Kuku does not move. He is looking at the small, beaten goblin in the mud.

KUKU
रुको। अगर हम उसे ऐसे ही छोड़कर चले गए, तो वो कल फिर से चुराएगा।
(Wait. If we just leave him like this, he will steal again tomorrow.)

Fyuria stops. She had not thought of that.

Kuku crosses and kneels in front of मिटासुर.

KUKU (CONT'D)
तुम चुराते हो क्योंकि तुम बना नहीं सकते। पर तुम बनाना सीख सकते हो — मैं तुम्हें सिखाऊँगा।
(You steal because you cannot make. But you can learn to make — I will teach you.)

मिटासुर
(small)
मैं? नहीं... मेरे हाथ तो सिर्फ़ मिटाना जानते हैं।
(Me? No... my hands only know how to erase.)

KUKU
आँखें बंद करो और मेरे साथ लंबी साँस लो।
(Close your eyes and take a long breath with me.)

Kuku breathes with him.

KUKU (CONT'D)
अब धीरे से फूँक मारो।
(Now blow out, gently.)

मिटासुर's sponge-hands tremble. He breathes out — and a tiny, wobbly little म flickers into the air. Lopsided, and completely his own.

KUKU (CONT'D)
(delighted)
देखो! तुमने म बनाया! थोड़ा टेढ़ा है — पर ये तुम्हारा अपना है!
(Look! You made a म! A little crooked — but it is your very own!)

मिटासुर cups it in his sponge-hands as if it might break.

मिटासुर
(a whisper of wonder)
मैंने... मैंने ख़ुद बनाया!
(I... I made it myself!)

The team cheers. Fyuria puts a मिठाई in his other hand and pulls him up.

FYURIA
चलो, अब सब मेले में चलते हैं!
(Come on, now let's all go to the fair!)

EXT. THE मेला — EVENING

Warm lights. PAPA — broad, dark-green, a little tired — arrives, walks straight to Dadi Maya, leans down, and kisses the top of her grey head.

Kuku stares — first at Papa, then at Dadi, then back.

KUKU
पापा, आपने दादी को प्यार किया... क्या दादी आपकी माँ हैं?
(Papa, you kissed Dadi... is Dadi your mother?)

PAPA
(smiling)
हाँ बेटा, दादी मेरी माँ हैं। माँ तो सबकी होती है — बड़ों की भी।
(Yes, son, Dadi is my mother. Everyone has a mother — even the grown-ups.)

Kuku's eyes go wide. Everyone, even the grown-ups, is somebody's child.

EXT. DADI'S HOUSE — EVENING

The little oil lamp glows. The मेला winds down, warm and gold. Dadi turns to camera, counting on her claws.

DADI
आज हमने अपना म वापस पाया। बोलो, म से हमें क्या-क्या मिला?
(Today we got our म back. Tell me, what did म give us?)
म से माँ! मेला! मिठाई! मोर! मछली! मदद! और — माया!
(म is for mother! Fair! Sweets! Peacock! Fish! Help! And — Maya!)

She settles, and her voice drops into something older and wiser.

DADI (CONT'D)
और एक बात, बच्चों। तुमने सिर्फ़ अपना अक्षर वापस नहीं लिया — तुमने चोरी की वजह भी छीन ली।
(And one more thing, children. You didn't just take your letter back — you also took away the reason to steal.)
दुश्मन को दोस्त बना लो, तो वो दोबारा कभी नहीं चुराता।
(Turn an enemy into a friend, and he never steals again.)

Nearby, Fyuria opens her किताब and carefully writes the day's म.

FYURIA
(grinning)
पहले मैं!
(Me first!)

The camera drifts. Asleep in Dadi's empty मिठाई basket: VESPER, with Kalu snuggled against him. Beside them, snoring softly, मिटासुर — his lopsided little म cradled in one sponge-hand.

DADI (O.S.)
(soft)
और देखो — हमारा नया दोस्त भी अपने पहले म के साथ सो गया।
(And look — our new friend has fallen asleep with his first म.)

DADI (O.S.) (CONT'D)
शुभ रात्रि, वैस्पर। शुभ रात्रि, बच्चों।
(Goodnight, Vesper. Goodnight, children.)

The lamp dims.

FADE OUT.

THE END.
