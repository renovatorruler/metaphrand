# कुकु और अक्षर (Kuku aur Akshar) — Character Design Bible

## ⚖️ SERIES LAWS — READ FIRST, THESE OVERRIDE EVERYTHING BELOW (updated 2026-07-24)

Any draft that violates these gets sent back. They exist because each was a real
author decision on a real episode.

1. **RADIO-PLAY RULE.** The episode must be fully followable with eyes closed. Every
   story beat is carried in dialogue (a character says what is happening); action
   lines only decorate. Sound cues in (कोष्ठक) are welcome and encouraged.
2. **EXPRESSION TAG ON EVERY LINE.** Every single dialogue line carries a Hindi
   performance parenthetical — (हँसकर), (फुसफुसाकर), (हाँफते हुए)… — no bare lines.
   These map to ElevenLabs v3 voice tags at recording.
3. **PURE HINDI SCRIPT.** Hindi sluglines, Hindi action, Hindi parentheticals. No
   English glosses. The ONLY permitted English is वैस्पर's in-character drift
   (e.g. "that's... R."), which फ्यूरिया corrects: «हिंदी में, वैस्पर!» — at most
   once per episode.
4. **COMPLETE-SENTENCE HINDI.** No staccato fragments; kids learn syntax from the
   show. Warm colloquial, grammatically complete. Register: kids → elders आप;
   kids ↔ kids and to animals तुम/तुम्हारा (never तू/तेरा); elders → kids तुम.
5. **THE CHANT IS DEAD.** Never use «साँस… टोपी… अक्षर». For the letter-forging
   moment: the team says «कुकु, तुम कर सकते हो!» and Kuku simply breathes the
   letter into being.
6. **NO टोपी TALK.** Never mention the टोपी/hat or compare with English/other
   letters — that device is reserved ONLY for genuinely confusable pairs (क vs
   Cyrillic Ф, in its own future beat). Describe letter shapes with plain words:
   खड़ी लकीर, ऊपर की रेखा, झुकी लकीर, गोला…
7. **फ्यूरिया's catchphrase is «पहले मैं!»** (never «मैं पहले»). Use it at most 1–2
   times per episode, at earned beats; her growth arc is that she sometimes gives
   the "पहले" away.
8. **CAST NAMES (current).** The toddlers are **कैस्टर** (tiny yellow, was चीकू —
   that name is retired) and **लेडा** (tinier lavender-pink baby girl; her running
   bit: the littlest one nails the day's letter — «र! र!»). वैस्पर is spelled
   वैस्पर. मिटासुर is a reformed friend (since Ep2), lives in the valley, still
   erases-what-he-can't-make ONLY as backstory; he can now make letters, shakily.
9. **नीति IN EVERY EPISODE.** दादी closes with one spoken moral that the plot
   actually earned (Ep2: «दुश्मन को दोस्त बना लो…»; Ep3: «जो आगे जाकर ठीक जगह
   रुकता है — वही पकड़ता है»). One moral, lived not lectured.
10. **ALL ON-SCREEN TEXT IS VFX.** Video/image models NEVER render text, letters,
    names, signs, or glyphs — they are bad at it (baked Latin gibberish is a real,
    recurring failure). Every letterform the audience sees is composited typography
    (typst → gold Kohinoor overlays, door-name cards, recap cards). Prompts must
    carry "no readable text, no letters" negatives; any baked glyph that slips
    through is covered, cropped, or cut around — never re-rolled hoping.
11. **LETTER MECHANIC.** Kuku breathes glowing letters; a letter's SHAPE becomes
    the physical solution (म → cradle-boat; र → खूँटा+हुक). The recap counts ONLY
    words the story actually lived. A made letter cannot be erased.


*A single, self-contained brief for a character/visual designer. Consolidates every character
note from the series so far. The show is a Hindi-teaching kids' series (ages ~4–7): a valley of
little dragons where the smallest one breathes **letters** instead of fire, and each episode
teaches one Devanagari letter, Sesame-Street-meets-Miniforce style.*

---

## 0. World, tone & art canon (read first)

- **Setting:** **अक्षर घाटी (Akshar Ghaati / "Letter Valley")** — a soft green Himalayan valley of
  friendly little dragons. Rolling paper hills, a stream, an old stone well, a fairground, Dadi's
  stone house with a small oil lamp. Cozy, warm, never fearsome.
- **Art style (LOCKED):** **3D papercraft** — layered cut-paper illustration, soft matte
  construction-paper textures, visible paper edges/folds/creases, warm storybook palette, soft
  studio lighting, gentle depth of field. Non-photorealistic, illustrated, "made of paper."
- **The magic mechanic:** the hero breathes glowing **letters (अक्षर)** of light. When a letter
  is "made," it cannot be un-made — creation is the show's power.
- **The टोपी (hat) motif:** every Devanagari letter hangs from a top headline stroke — a "hat."
  No Latin/Cyrillic letter has it. This is a recurring visual/teaching device: letters visibly
  wear their hat; the letter often appears as a glowing golden shape with its टोपी.
- **Tone:** warm, playful, adventurous, safe. Real (kid-scale) stakes and a comic villain, but
  **nothing scary** — no menace that would frighten a small child.
- **Two of the child characters (Fyuria & Vesper) are modeled on the author's real kids.** Their
  designs should read as lovable, specific, and a little bit like real children in dragon form.
- **Existing approved reference art:** full-body character sheets already exist for the core cast
  (papercraft, plain background) at `stories/kuku/charsheets/{kuku,furia,vesper,dadi,papa,kalu,
  cheeku}.png` — treat those as the current visual canon to match/refine; **मिटासुर is not yet
  designed** (this brief is partly to design him).

## 1. कुकु — KUKU (the hero) · pronounced "KOO-koo"

- **Role:** the littlest dragon; the emotional heart AND the team's power — the **only one who can
  forge letters** (breathe them into being). Wants to be a "real" fire-dragon; his "flaw" (letters,
  not fire) is secretly his gift.
- **Physical:** a **small, round, moss-green** cut-paper baby dragon, about four years old in feel.
  Big friendly round eyes, a chubby **cream-colored belly**, **tiny** paper wings, small rounded
  horns, a short tail, a sweet eager close-mouthed smile. Huggable, toddler-proportioned.
- **Personality:** all heart, all on the surface — delight, a quick sadness, delight again. Eager,
  brave under pressure (braver than he believes), endlessly kind. Tries hard; bounces back.
- **Arc DNA:** he once couldn't make his own fire — so he has deep empathy for anyone who feels
  they "can't." That wound powers his kindness (e.g. he teaches the villain rather than gloating).
- **Voice/register:** simplest, warm, eager Hindi; complete short sentences; steadies himself when
  it matters.

## 2. फ्यूरिया — FYURIA (the spark) · "FYOO-ria" · **spelling फ्यूरिया** *(mirrors the author's daughter)*

- **Role:** the big-hearted **leader** of the kid-team; on missions she's **speed / first-to-help**.
- **Physical:** a **bright pink-red** cut-paper dragon girl, **a head taller** than a baby dragon;
  confident sparky expression, **long eyelashes**, a small **crest of paper spikes**, cream belly,
  medium paper wings, an energetic hands-on-hips stance.
- **THE PERSON (this is the point, not the catchphrase):** a leader who feels EVERYTHING at full
  volume — joy, worry, fierce love. Her competitiveness is **generosity in a hurry**: she wants to
  be first to *help*, first to *protect*. She's the group's **emotional radar** — clocks when
  someone's sad before they say it. Brave outside, and just brave enough to admit fear inside.
- **The contradiction (richness):** wants to be first — but is proudest when she helped someone
  ELSE be first (she once gave her "first" away at a rescue).
- **Real-kid grounding:** first at everything (races to be first to brush teeth, first to answer),
  kind but competitive with her younger brother, mothers the babies, dreams of buying a
  **Lamborghini** (→ in-show she dreams of a shiny red **कार**), loves **writing letters** (→ keeps
  a किताब where she writes each day's letter).
- **Signature tic (USE SPARINGLY — at most once or twice an episode):** «**मैं पहले!**» ("Me first!").
- **Voice/register:** fast, bright, warm, complete sentences; आप to elders, तुम to the other kids.

## 3. वैस्पर — VESPER (the dreamer-seer) · "VES-per" · *(mirrors the author's son)*

- **Role:** Fyuria's **younger brother**; on missions he **sees what's hidden** — spots the clue,
  the solution, the thing everyone else missed.
- **Physical:** a **soft light-blue** cut-paper dragon boy, **slightly smaller** than his sister;
  **dreamy half-lidded gentle eyes** gazing slightly upward, a relaxed sleepy smile, small paper
  wings, cream belly, a loose relaxed posture.
- **THE PERSON:** he seems the least present and is the most deeply attuned — **slow outside, vast
  inside**. His "dreaminess" is really deep **perception and imagination**: he notices beauty,
  connections, hidden things (he heard the lost puppy first; he saw the "hook" solution). He's a
  little **inventor/maker** — offers the sideways idea. Profoundly gentle and empathetic; the one
  who comforts. When something truly matters, he snaps into focus first.
- **The contradiction (richness):** the one who wanders off is the one who sees clearest.
- **Real-kid grounding:** lost in his own imagination (you must call him 4–5 times before he
  answers); **forgets and drifts into English mid-sentence** and has to be reminded to speak Hindi;
  and — his signature — **falls asleep before everyone, anywhere he happens to be.**
- **THE SHRIEK (his standout trait):** the quietest dragon has a COLOSSAL ear-splitting shriek «AAAAAAAA—!!!» when startled or when he needs to be heard — freezes the whole valley, then he carries on calmly. Comedy + genuine plot tool. This is his star trait.
- **Other signature tics (USE SPARINGLY — at most once an episode each):** the 4–5 name-calls; the
  English-drift → «**हिंदी में, वैस्पर!**» → he repeats it in Hindi (a *charming* reach for a word
  he doesn't have yet, not a running gag); falling asleep in an odd spot as the episode's closing
  button.
- **Voice/register:** soft, unhurried, but complete and quietly insightful — never vacant.

## 4. दादी माया — DADI MAYA (grandmother, mentor, host) · "DAA-dee MAA-ya"

- **Role:** the warm grandmother who hosts the letter lesson (Sesame "grown-up"), and the team's
  mentor/handler. **Quietly, she is Papa's own mother.**
- **Physical (IMPORTANT — she is a REAL DRAGON, not a human):** an **elderly grey dragon**
  grandmother with a **full dragon snout/muzzle, dragon horns, folded paper wings, a long dragon
  tail, grey scales with a silvery sheen.** Grandmotherly cues: **small round spectacles** perched
  on her dragon snout, a soft **knitted paper shawl** over her shoulders, kind crinkled smiling
  eyes, a gently stooped posture. *No human face, no human skin, no hair-bun — she is a dragon.*
  (An early draft wrongly made her a human woman with horns; that is the specific mistake to avoid.)
- **Personality:** warm, patient, playful, wise; never stern, never disappointed; leads
  call-and-response; states the day's rule plainly.

## 5. पापा — PAPA (Kuku's father) · "PAA-pa"

- **Role:** Kuku's dad; kind-but-firm; capable of being wrong and admitting it. Dadi Maya is his
  mother.
- **Physical:** a **big, broad, dark-green** cut-paper dragon father, tall and strong with wide
  shoulders; **kind tired eyes**, a warm small smile, a work-worn look; sturdy folded wings, a
  thick tail; a simple **work strap across his chest with a tiny paper radio** clipped to it.
- **Personality:** loves his son; says "no" from care, not meanness; softens visibly when moved.
  Addresses the children with **तुम** (warm), never the rough तू.

## 6. कालू — KALU (the dog) · "KAA-loo"

- **Role:** the little black puppy Kuku rescues and adopts; on missions he **tracks by scent**.
  Never speaks (barks + SFX only).
- **Physical:** a **tiny, all-black** cut-paper **puppy** (a DOG, **not** a dragon) — big soft
  **floppy ears**, big shiny adoring eyes, a little wagging tail, often head-tilted. Adorable.
- **Personality:** devoted, playful, bouncy; imprints hard on Kuku.

## 7. चीकू — CHEEKU (the toddler) · "CHEE-koo"

- **Role:** the littlest one, the **baby the big kids look after** (mirrors the author's kids
  caring for their baby siblings). Comic, giggly.
- **Physical:** a **very tiny yellow** cut-paper **toddler dragon**, the smallest of all — round
  baby proportions, huge curious eyes, stubby little wings, often sitting holding a little paper
  spoon, one tooth showing when he giggles.
- **Personality:** toddler babble (one or two repeated words), pure delight, giggles.

## 8. मिटासुर — MITASUR (villain → ally) · "mi-TAA-sur" · **DESIGN LOCKED (author, Ep2)**

- **Role:** the series' recurring **letter-thief who becomes a reformed, bumbling ally.** He can
  **only ERASE letters, never make one** — so he steals them, because his hoard is the only thing
  he's ever owned. By the end of his first episode, Kuku teaches him to *make*, and he forges a
  lopsided first letter and joins the family.
- **Personality (this drives the design):** lonely under the mischief; grabby, whiny, **comic and
  deeply sympathetic — never frightening.** A wound, not a menace: "I can never make anything, I
  can only erase; these letters are all I have." Melts into wonder when he makes his first letter.
- **Physical (LOCKED by author):** a **roly-poly, dusty grey-purple goblin**, soft and **smudgy at
  the edges**, with **big googly eyes** and **two fat pink-purple sponge-hands** (his erasers are
  part of his body). A **burlap sack** hangs off one shoulder with faint glowing stolen letters
  poking out. Bumbling, huggable, pitiable. **Zero horror** — comic, never a monster. Must support
  the "cradles a tiny lopsided glowing म and lights up" beat.

## 9. Supporting / minor

- **भोलू — BHOLU** (recurring, optional): a **big, gentle, clumsy older-brother dragon** who makes
  huge flames (contrast to Kuku); kind, never a bully, never teases Kuku about the fire.
- **मोरनी — MORNI** (episode-2 bit part): a **peahen**, the lost peachick's mother — frantic, then
  grateful. And the **peachick (मोर का बच्चा)** — tiny, cheeps, no dialogue.
- **Friends-of-the-show (referenced, not yet on screen):** **अन्ना (Anna)** and **माशा (Masha)** —
  the kids' real best friends; appear first as a postcard, guest characters later.

## 10. Cross-cutting design laws

- **Consistency:** each character has ONE locked design (colors, proportions, features) held across
  every shot/episode — use the existing character sheets as reference.
- **The kid-team ("अक्षर वीर / Letter Heroes"):** Kuku + Fyuria + Vesper + Kalu function as a little
  hero team; their power-up chant is «साँस… टोपी… अक्षर!» — design should support a light
  "suit-up / power-up" energy without literal costumes (they're dragons, not robots).
- **Names & spellings (Devanagari canon):** कुकु · **फ्यूरिया** · वैस्पर · दादी माया · पापा · कालू ·
  चीकू · मिटासुर · भोलू · मोरनी · अन्ना · माशा.
- **Never scary. Never a human-looking Dadi. Never a mean/gloating kid. Tics are seasoning.**
