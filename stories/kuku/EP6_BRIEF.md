## 1. THE TASK

Write the **complete shooting screenplay** for Episode 6 of «कुकु और अक्षर», a Hindi
letter-teaching show for children aged about 4–7. Target length ~10 minutes, 8 scenes,
roughly 80–100 dialogue lines.

**The show in one line:** कुकु is the littlest dragon — he cannot breathe fire, he breathes glowing
**letters**, and each episode the shape of that letter becomes the physical solution to the story's
problem.

---

## 2. FORMAT — follow exactly

- **The entire script is in Hindi.** Sluglines, action lines, parentheticals, dialogue. No English
  glosses, no translations, no bilingual columns.
- **Sluglines** look like: `दृश्य 3 — बाहर: सबसे पुराने पेड़ के नीचे — शाम`
- **Every single dialogue line carries a Hindi performance parenthetical.** No bare lines. Format:

```
कुकु: (ऊपर देखते हुए, उदास) तितली उड़ जाती है… मैं क्यों नहीं उड़ सकता?
```

- **Sound cues** sit on their own line in brackets: `(ध्वनि: रस्सी का चरमराना, फिर टूटन।)`
  Use them generously — they become real generated sound effects.
- Speaker names in Devanagari: कुकु · फ्यूरिया · वैस्पर · दादी · पापा · मिटासुर · कैस्टर · लेडा ·
  कालू · रीछ · सब · सब बच्चे

**Parentheticals must be drawn from this approved vocabulary** (they map to voice-engine tags at
recording; anything outside this list will not be performed):

हँसते हुए · हँसकर · हँसी दबाते · हँसी दबाकर · खिलखिलाकर · मुस्कुराकर · फुसफुसाकर · धीमे · धीरे से · धीमी आवाज़ में · बहुत धीरे, लोरी जैसे · ज़ोर से · चिल्लाकर · पूरी ताक़त से · ज़ोर से पुकारते हुए · चहकते हुए · चहककर · उछलते हुए · जोश से · ख़ुशी से · ऊँची, ख़ुश आवाज़ में · घबराकर · चिंता से · बेचैन · डरते हुए · हैरान · हैरानी से · सवालिया · उत्सुकता से · शर्म से · उदास · भावुक · फूलकर, भावुक · रोता-हँसता · गर्व से · शाबाशी से · प्यार से · नरमी से · बिना डाँटे · राहत से · गंभीर होकर · सीधे, साफ़ आवाज़ में · धीमी, सयानी आवाज़ में · सोचते हुए · लापरवाही से · खोई हुई आवाज़ में · नींद में · उनींदा · सुकून भरी साँस · जम्हाई लेते हुए · हाँफते हुए · साँस भरते हुए · साँस छोड़ते हुए · ज़ोर लगाते हुए · बंद नाक से · कमज़ोर · तुतलाते हुए · नन्ही आवाज़ में · नन्ही किलकारी से · सिखाते हुए · गिनाते हुए · गिनती शुरू करते हुए · खेल की आवाज़ में · चमकती आवाज़ में · फुसफुसाते हुए, टोकते हुए · गुनगुनाते हुए · बयान करते हुए

If a needed emotion is missing, use the closest one on the list rather than inventing a new phrase.

## 3. THE SERIES LAWS — a script violating any of these is sent back

1. **RADIO-PLAY.** The episode must be fully followable **with eyes closed**. Every story beat is
   carried in dialogue — a character says what is happening. Action lines only decorate what has
   already been said. If a beat only works visually, it is broken.
2. **Every dialogue line has a parenthetical** (see §2).
3. **Pure Hindi.** The only English permitted is वैस्पर drifting into it **exactly once per
   episode**, immediately corrected by फ्यूरिया with «हिंदी में, वैस्पर!»
4. **Complete grammatical sentences.** Warm and colloquial, never clipped or staccato — the children
   learn sentence structure from this show. Register: children → elders **आप**; children to each
   other and to animals **तुम/तुम्हारा** (never तू/तेरा); elders → children **तुम**.
5. **No power-up chant.** «साँस… टोपी… अक्षर» is retired and must never appear. Before कुकु forges a
   letter, the team says: **«कुकु, तुम कर सकते हो!»**
6. **Never mention the टोपी / headline**, and never compare a Hindi letter to English or any other
   script. Describe letter shapes in plain words only (खड़ी लकीर, लेटी हुई लकीर, मुड़ा हुआ पैर, कटोरी,
   झूला, गोला).
7. **Exactly one नीति**, spoken by दादी at the close, earned by the plot. Never lecture, never
   moralise mid-episode.
8. **All on-screen text is added later as typography.** Never write signs, labels or written words
   as things the animation must draw.
9. **Nothing frightening.** Real stakes and real feeling are wanted (the tone target is Miniforce /
   Super Kitties), but no peril shown in close-up, no violence, no child in visible danger.
10. **The closing recap counts only words the story actually lived.**
11. **Nobody is shamed.** Adults never scold a child who has confessed.

### 12. CONSONANT-PURE — this episode teaches NO मात्रा

The lesson scene teaches the **bare consonant त**: its sound, its shape, and how it differs from न.
It does **not** teach the आ-मात्रा, and दादी never breaks a word into त + ा = ता.

This is not squeamishness about difficulty — it is about the shelf life of the episode. These
episodes get rewatched. A child who comes back a year later to firm up their consonants must find a
clean consonant lesson, not a मात्रा lesson they have to sit through. Every episode stays usable as
the drill it was built to be.

Words that carry मात्राएँ are still spoken freely and still earn recap cards — **तारा**, **तितली** and
**तीन** all appear. They are simply *said and used*, never decomposed.

### 13. WRITE WELL — the real bar on figurative language

There is no rule against metaphor. Good images are welcome and wanted. The bar is that a
**five-year-old must be able to picture it instantly, and it must be true.**

What gets a script sent back is bad writing. The concrete failure to learn from: a line in Ep5 read
*«बादल तुम्हारे ऊपर था, और सच मेरे पास था»* and was cut with the note that it was nonsensical, that
even an adult reader could not decode it, and that this kind of writing has no place in a children's
show. That line failed because it was opaque, mixed two unrelated images, and demanded adult
decoding — **not** because it was a metaphor.

So: an image a child already owns (taking turns, waiting, sleeping, being carried, a flower holding a
butterfly) is good. An image that needs explaining is not. If you cannot picture it in one second,
cut it.

### 14. NOBODY CLIMBS HIGH

The whole point of this episode is that the **letter** goes where the children cannot. No child is
ever up the tree or on a branch. फ्यूरिया's attempt fails at the bottom of the trunk, comically.
See §6.

## 4. THE CAST

- **कुकु** — small green dragon, the hero. Breathes letters instead of fire. Kind, brave, aware of
  being small. *This episode his want is to get the light back before the little ones have to walk
  home in the dark, and his wound is the oldest one he has:* he cannot fly, and today something
  small and silly flies past his face without trying.
- **फ्यूरिया** — pink-red dragon girl, older, fastest in the valley. Catchphrase **«पहले मैं!»**
  — always this word order, and **only twice this episode** (§6). Her series arc: learning to give
  the "first" away. This episode she learns that fast is not the same as tall.
- **वैस्पर** — soft blue dragon boy, younger. Dreamy; notices what nobody else notices; falls asleep
  anywhere; **drifts into English once**; owns a **colossal shriek** used as a plot device, not a gag.
  *He is the one who says the true, kind thing about wings* — because he is the one who looks up.
- **दादी माया** — grandmother dragon. Teaches the letter and speaks the नीति. Warm, never preachy.
- **पापा** — big dark-green father. Introduced in Ep5, where he confessed an old mistake. **Keep him
  light here** — he carries the तेल, he is glad to be asked, and he does not solve anything. Ep5 was
  his episode; this one is not.
- **मिटासुर** — a goblin who was the villain in Episode 2 (he erased letters because he could not
  make any). Reformed. **At the end of Ep5 पापा made him keeper of the new bridge, and he promised
  «सबसे पहले मैं बताऊँगा!»** — this episode is the payoff: he is the one who notices the light is
  gone and comes running to report it. He is proud of the job. **Do not make his hands the
  instrument again** — they solved Ep4 and Ep5; here his contribution is that he *watched and told*.
- **रीछ** — a big gentle bear who **speaks only in growls** (no words at all).
- **कैस्टर** and **लेडा** — two toddlers. लेडा's running bit: the littlest one gets the day's letter
  first. Both babble rather than speak.
- **कालू** — small black puppy. Barks only.

## 5. THE STORY

**Title: «त से तारा» · Letter: त**

**Premise.** Every night the valley hangs a big paper **तारा** high on the **तना** of the oldest
tree — it is the light the little ones steer home by, and पापा keeps it filled with **तेल**. This
evening मिटासुर comes running from his bridge: the rope has frayed and the तारा has come down, and
it is snagged far up on the **तीसरी डाल**, too high for anyone to reach. Dark is coming.

While they stand under the tree, a **तितली** flutters straight up past the branch without effort,
and कुकु says the thing he has never said out loud: *तितली उड़ जाती है — मैं क्यों नहीं उड़ सकता?*
फ्यूरिया tries the trunk and slides back down, because she is fast, not tall. वैस्पर, who is the only
one looking up, tells him plainly that baby dragons' wings grow later.

**त is the shape that reaches.** Its top line hooks over the branch, its scoop cradles the तारा, its
straight leg braces against the trunk so the wind cannot swing it. The letter goes where none of them
can go yet.

**नीति (दादी, under the relit तारा, at the close):**

> «हम में से कोई वहाँ तक नहीं पहुँच पाया, कुकु। तुम्हारा अक्षर पहुँच गया।»

Said to कुकु and heard by everyone. It is earned by exactly what the audience just watched: फ्यूरिया
could not climb it, रीछ could not shake it down, nobody's reach was enough — and the smallest dragon
in the valley got something to the top of the tallest tree by making a thing.

It is **not** a consolation and must not be played as one. It names what he did.

**It must not mention पंख, उड़ना, or the तितली, and दादी must not glance at the flying question.**
That thread was closed by फ्यूरिया in scene 3 with «बड़ों का राज़ है» and stays closed for two more
episodes (see `FLYING_ARC.md`). A grandmother who brushes the secret in her closing line, having said
nothing all episode, reopens what the arc needs left shut.

**The letter mechanic — त has three named parts, and all three do a job:**
1. **ऊपर की लेटी हुई लकीर** = the bar that hooks over the branch and takes the weight
2. **बाईं तरफ़ की कटोरी** (the scoop that hangs down and curls right) = the cradle the तारा sits in —
   *this is why the letter can carry something at all*
3. **दाईं तरफ़ की खड़ी लकीर** = the straight leg that presses on the तना so the wind cannot swing it

दादी must say aloud that **the scoop is what only त has**, and distinguish it from **न**, which the
children learned two episodes ago and which is the letter they will genuinely confuse it with:
**न's left side bends up from the middle; त's left side hangs down and curls into a cradle that can
hold something.** This discrimination beat is required, not optional — न and त are the pair most
likely to blur, and Ep5 already ran the same drill for प against न.

**The letter is forged twice** — the series pattern. The first त is shaped perfectly but hooked over
the **दूसरी डाल**: the scoop sits below the तारा instead of under it, and the तारा tips out. कुकु
assumes his letter is weak again. दादी corrects the diagnosis: the letter is right, the **counting**
is wrong. Then the whole cast counts branches aloud — **एक… दो… तीन** — and the second त goes on the
तीसरी डाल. The failure is arithmetic, not character, and it is the episode's counting lesson.

**Word bank.** LIVED (these earn recap cards, so make each one happen on screen):
**तारा · तितली · तीन · तना · ताली · तकिया**.
Bench words, spoken but not carded: तेल, तीसरी, ताला, तेज़.

**Do not build anything on पानी or the पुल.** Ep5 was saturated with water and bridges. The प-पुल
appears once in scene 1 as something they simply walk across — it is furniture now, and its
permanence is a quiet payoff, not a topic.

## 6. THE THINGS THAT MUST LAND IN EXACTLY THESE PLACES

- **«पहले मैं!» twice, in opposite meanings.** Once in scene 3 to claim the glory (climbing the
  तना — and she slides straight back down), once in scene 6 to claim the **dull** job (holding the
  bottom of the letter steady while कुकु aims, the one task nobody wants). In the final scene she
  lets लेडा name the letter first *without* using the phrase.
- **कुकु asks the flying question out loud, in scene 1**, prompted by the तितली. He must actually
  say it — this is the question the audience's own children asked, and hearing कुकु ask it is the
  point. It is answered twice: once kindly and factually by वैस्पर in scene 3 (*wings grow later*),
  and once as a tease by दादी's नीति at the close.
- **वैस्पर's English drift, once, in scene 6** — he sleepily says the word for the thing that flew
  past in English, फ्यूरिया corrects him, and his Hindi word **तितली** is what gives कुकु the idea
  about the cradle. The drift must *do work*, as it did in Ep5.
- **वैस्पर's shriek, once, in scene 3** — रीछ, meaning well, leans his whole weight on the तना to
  shake the तारा loose, which would send it further out of reach. The shriek stops him. Follow it
  with a laugh within about ten seconds.
- **मिटासुर is thanked for reporting it**, explicitly, by पापा — closing the loop on the job he was
  given at the end of Ep5. Nobody suspects him of anything this episode; that story is finished and
  must not be reopened.
- **ताली is a real all-cast beat**, not a stage direction: when the light comes back everyone claps,
  दादी names the word, and the little ones do it again because clapping is its own reward.

## 7. BEAT SHEET

Follow this structure. The key lines are the intended sense — write them properly, and write
everything between them.

*Three lighting states only: **दोपहर ढलते · शाम · रात होते**. The darkening clock is carried in
dialogue, not in relights — and it is the episode's clock: the little ones must be home before dark.*

### दृश्य 1 — बाहर: प-पुल और सबसे पुराने पेड़ का रास्ता — दोपहर ढलते
**What happens:** The children cross the प-पुल from last episode (one line, no fuss — it simply
holds). दादी explains the evening tradition out loud for anyone who has not seen it: the तारा goes up
the तना every night so the little ones can find their way home. A **तितली** goes up past them, and
कुकु asks his question. Establish that the light is not up yet today.

**Heard:**
- फ्यूरिया: *(चहकते हुए)* «पुल अब भी वहीं है! कुकु का अक्षर हिला भी नहीं।»
- दादी: *(धीमी, सयानी आवाज़ में)* «रोज़ शाम हम तारा उस पुराने पेड़ के तने पर ऊपर टाँगते हैं, ताकि नन्हे बच्चे अँधेरे में घर का रास्ता देख सकें।»
- SFX: पंखों की बहुत हल्की फड़फड़ाहट।
- कुकु: *(ऊपर देखते हुए, उदास)* «तितली ऊपर तक उड़ गई… इतनी छोटी है, फिर भी उड़ लेती है। मैं क्यों नहीं उड़ सकता, दादी?»

**त words landing:** तारा · तितली · तना

---

### दृश्य 2 — बाहर: सबसे पुराने पेड़ के नीचे — शाम
**What happens:** मिटासुर comes running from the bridge with the news — the rope frayed, the तारा
fell and is caught up on the third branch. He is proud of having spotted it first; पापा thanks him
for it by name. The problem is stated plainly and the clock is started: dark is coming and the
little ones have to walk home.

**Heard:**
- मिटासुर: *(हाँफते हुए, फिर गर्व से)* «मैं दौड़कर आया हूँ! तारे की रस्सी घिस गई थी — तारा गिर गया, और ऊपर तीसरी डाल पर अटक गया है! सबसे पहले मैंने ही देखा!»
- पापा: *(शाबाशी से)* «शुक्रिया मिटासुर। तुमने अपना काम किया — और सबसे पहले बताया।»
- दादी: *(चिंता से)* «अँधेरा होने से पहले तारा ऊपर लौटना चाहिए।»

**त words landing:** तारा · तीसरी *(bench)*

---

### दृश्य 3 — बाहर: तने के नीचे — शाम · कोशिशें
**What happens:** The reaching fails, three ways, all safe and all funny. फ्यूरिया claims the climb
with «पहले मैं!» and slides straight back down the smooth तना — fast is not tall. रीछ decides to
shake the whole tree; वैस्पर's **shriek** stops him before the तारा goes further out of reach; a laugh
lands within ten seconds. Then वैस्पर, still looking up, gives कुकु the kind true answer.

**Heard:**
- फ्यूरिया: *(उछलते हुए, हक़ जताते हुए)* «पहले मैं! मैं सबसे तेज़ हूँ — मैं चढ़ जाऊँगी!» **[«पहले मैं!» use 1 of 2]**
- SFX: पंजों का रगड़ना, फिर फिसलन, फिर धम्म।
- फ्यूरिया: *(हाँफते हुए, हैरानी से)* «तना बिलकुल चिकना है! मैं तेज़ हूँ — लंबी नहीं हूँ।»
- SFX: रीछ का तने से टकराना, पत्तों की ज़ोरदार सरसराहट।
- वैस्पर: **महाचीख़।**
- दादी: *(रुककर, हँसकर)* «रीछ, रुको! पेड़ हिलाओगे तो तारा और दूर चला जाएगा।»
- कैस्टर: *(खिलखिलाकर)* «वैस्पर, एक बार और!»
- कुकु: *(ऊपर देखते हुए, सवालिया)* «तितली के पंख हैं, हमारे भी पंख हैं। हम उड़ते क्यों नहीं?»
- वैस्पर: *(उनींदा, जैसे यह सबको पता हो)* «क्योंकि हम बच्चे हैं। बच्चे नहीं उड़ते।»
- कुकु: *(सोचते हुए, फिर धीमे)* «पर मैंने पापा को भी कभी उड़ते नहीं देखा।»
- SFX: कुछ पल की चुप्पी — बस पत्ते।
- फ्यूरिया: *(धीमी आवाज़ में, कंधे उछालकर)* «वो बड़ों का राज़ है। हमें नहीं बताते।»
- फ्यूरिया: *(फिर तुरंत, जोश से)* «चलो! तारा उतारना है।»

**DIRECTION — THE FLYING THREAD. Read `FLYING_ARC.md` before writing this beat.**

This is **one short exchange and then it is gone.** It is not the plot, it is not resolved, and it
must not take a minute of screen time. It is stage 1 of a three-episode thread: asked here, deferred
in Ep7, answered by दादी's flashback in Ep8.

**Only children speak in it. No adult says a word about flying anywhere in this episode.**
- वैस्पर's «हम बच्चे हैं» is a *child's theory*, not the show's answer. That is why it is allowed to be
  said out loud and left standing.
- कुकु's «पापा को भी कभी उड़ते नहीं देखा» punctures it, and is the moment the audience understands the
  grown-ups are the mystery.
- फ्यूरिया's «बड़ों का राज़ है» closes the thread. She does not know either. Then she changes the
  subject herself, briskly, and the scene goes back to work.

**FORBIDDEN in this episode:** दादी must not answer, hint, sigh meaningfully, or be asked. पापा must
not react. Nobody says «हम उड़ना भूल गए» — that sentence does not exist in the world yet. Nobody
mentions the रथ, the रास्ता or the पुल in connection with flying; that is the Ep8 flashback's material
and spending it here kills the arc. And never «हमारे पंख छोटे हैं» or «पंख बाद में बड़े होते हैं» — age
is the answer the real children rejected, and if wings grew with age पापा could simply have flown दादी
home over the broken bridge in Ep5.

## 8. WHAT THIS EPISODE SETS UP — read `FLYING_ARC.md`

This is **episode 1 of a three-episode thread**, and its entire job is to let a child ask the question
and get no answer. Asked here → deferred in Ep7 → answered by दादी's flashback in Ep8.

The thread is **thin on purpose**. In this episode it is one exchange in scene 3, under a minute, and
then the episode forgets about it completely. A child watching only this episode still gets a whole
letter and a whole story; the thread is a bonus, never a prerequisite.

**What Ep6 does:** कुकु asks. वैस्पर guesses wrong (*we're babies*). कुकु punctures it (*I've never
seen पापा fly either*). फ्यूरिया names it a grown-ups' secret and changes the subject. Done.

**What Ep6 must NOT do:**
- **No adult speaks about flying at all** — not दादी, not पापा, not one line, not a meaningful pause.
  दादी's silence across the whole episode is what makes Ep7's «किसी और दिन बताऊँगी» land.
- Nobody says «हम उड़ना भूल गए». That sentence does not exist in the world yet.
- Nobody connects the रथ / रास्ता / पुल to flying. That is the Ep8 flashback's material.
- No child guesses correctly. Their theories are wrong and childlike.
- Nobody flies, tries to fly, or is promised they will.
- Nobody is sad about it for more than a beat. The valley does not think about flying at all.

**Where it goes:** Ep7 — कुकु begs दादी directly and she puts him off warmly. Ep8 — they really beg,
and she finally tells it as a story, in flashback: a valley that flew, then a रास्ता, then a पुल, then
a रथ that could carry more than any dragon and never got tired. Nobody decided to stop. There was
simply never a morning when anybody needed to. Then, much later, «उ से उड़ान».
