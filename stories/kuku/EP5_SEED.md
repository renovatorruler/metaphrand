## 1. Title and pitch

# «प से पुल»

On a windy kite morning, फ्यूरिया shouts «पहले मैं!» and lands both feet on the middle plank of the valley's only bridge to fetch वैस्पर's kite from the tree on the far bank. The plank says **कड़ाक** — and she tells everyone it was nothing. By afternoon दादी, रीछ, कैस्टर and लेडा have crossed to the far side, and the empty bridge falls into the stream. कुकु can forge प into a new bridge — प is a deck resting on two legs, with a hooked left foot that grips stone — but the letter's two legs need two solid stones, and under the water there is only sand. Two people in the valley know where the real stone is: फ्यूरिया, who knows what she heard, and पापा, who built this bridge when he was young and never came back to fix the pillar he set on sand. Until they both say it out loud, the letter will not stand. The truth is not a feeling in this episode — it is the load-bearing information, and the bridge is physically waiting for it.

## 2. The letter and its word bank

**Letter: प.**

**LIVED — earns a recap card (6 cards):**

| word | how it is lived |
|---|---|
| **पुल** | the thing that cracks, falls, and is rebuilt as the letter itself. Said in almost every scene. |
| **पत्थर** | the missing knowledge. रीछ slips on round ones; मिटासुर hunts for flat ones by hand; पापा names their location; the letter's foot grips one. |
| **पैर** | फ्यूरिया's two-footed jump (the crime), the letter's हुक-वाला **मुड़ा हुआ पैर**, and पापा's first step on the finished bridge. |
| **पतंग** | flies across in S1 (causes the crossing), stays stranded on the far bank all episode, and **comes home across the new bridge** in S8. |
| **पेड़** | the tree on the far bank the kite lands in, and where the पंछी nest is — the reason दादी's group crosses at all. |
| **पापा** | the second confessor; the word said aloud to him in the moment that matters («पापा, आपको कैसे मालूम?»). |

**BENCH — spoken, no recap card:** **पटरा** (the deck plank — kept in dialogue because it is the correct word for the object, but dropped from the cards: it is regionally uneven and the one word a child will never meet again; **native-speaker check before it goes in dialogue at all** — fallback «तख़्ता»), **पंछी**, **पहाड़**, **पत्ता**.

**BANNED:** **पानी** as a subject. Ep4 was saturated with water and taps. The stream here is an obstacle and a noise, never a topic.

**Sound work (this is not optional — Ep4's frame is house style):** दादी's lesson opens with the Ep4 formula — anchor word → phoneme → alliteration string → लेडा → *then* the shape. And the shape lesson is followed in S6 by a shouted **«प से...?»** call-and-response game across the water. See §5, S4 and S6.

**TTS note:** a bare «प» will be read as English "P" by the voice model, exactly as «र» was in Ep3. Every isolated letter-name line needs the phonetic-safe form, and it must be in the 5-line TAG_MAP probe before any full render.

## 3. The नीति

**दादी माया (नए पुल पर, हाथ अक्षर के मुड़े हुए पैर पर रखकर, नरमी से):**

> **«ग़लती छोटी थी, बच्चों — छुपाने ने उसे बड़ा बना दिया।»**

*English: "The mistake was small, children — hiding it is what made it big."*

**One sentence, eight words. Do not extend it.** The bridge/trust metaphor that was in the original draft moves out of दादी's mouth and into पापा's plain speech in S7, where it is a man explaining himself rather than a grandmother delivering a maxim.

**Why it is new ground:** Ep2 was about the person opposite you, Ep3 about where to stand, Ep4 about an enemy you cannot see — all outward. This is the first नीति that turns inward (what do you do when the damage is *yours*), and the first one an **adult** on screen has to obey: पापा has been hiding this exact mistake for years, and he confesses *after his daughter does*.

## 4. The letter-shape mechanic

**प is a deck standing on two legs, and its left leg ends in a hook that grips.** दादी names three parts in the Ep3 «खड़ी लकीर = खूँटा» manner, and all three do a job:

1. **ऊपर की लेटी हुई लकीर = पटरा** — the flat surface you put your feet on. The bridge deck.
2. **नीचे उतरती दो लकीरें = दो पाए** — two pillars standing in the stream. **Because प has TWO legs, it needs TWO solid places to stand.** This is the whole plot condition.
3. **बाईं तरफ़ का मुड़ा हुआ पैर** — the hooked foot that clamps onto a stone so the current cannot sweep the letter away. **This is the identity feature**: दादी says out loud «यही मुड़ा हुआ पैर सिर्फ़ प के पास है», and discriminates it from last week's न («न बीच से मुड़कर ऊपर उठता है; प का पैर नीचे जाकर पत्थर पकड़ लेता है»). Recognition must hang on this stroke, not on the top line.

**The letter is forged TWICE. That is the spine.**

- **First प (S5) — right letter, wrong place.** कुकु builds it exactly where the old bridge was. Both legs sink into sand, the current tilts it, the letter washes to the bank. कुकु's first instinct is self-blame («शायद मैं छोटा हूँ, इसलिए मेरा अक्षर भी कमज़ोर है»), and दादी corrects the diagnosis: the letter was never weak, the *place* was wrong, and nobody knows where the strong place is.
- **The missing knowledge IS the hidden truth.** Two legs need two wide flat stones. Two people know what is under the water: फ्यूरिया (she knows what cracked and why) and पापा (he built this bridge young, set the middle pillar on sand in a hurry, meant to come back and move it onto stone, and put it off every year). While either of them is silent, the letter cannot stand. Confession here is not an emotion — it is **engineering data**, which is why the mechanic never feels bolted on.
- **Second प (S8).** Left hooked foot clamps the first flat stone, right leg sits on the second, the top line pulls taut into a deck. पापा — heaviest, and the oldest offender — walks it first. Ep2's rule is spoken once: **«जो अक्षर ख़ुद बनाया जाता है, उसे कोई मिटा नहीं सकता»** — so the प-bridge is now a permanent standing set piece in the valley, free spaced repetition of this glyph in every future episode.

**Law 6 ruling (binding):** the top line may be the deck, but it is only ever «**प की** ऊपर वाली लेटी लकीर» — a part of *this letter's own body*. It is never called a टोपी, never compared to another script, and **never generalised** («हर अक्षर के ऊपर…» is forbidden). Precedent: Ep4 shipped «उसके ऊपर एक रेखा» for न after a full audit. To keep the child's recognition off the shared feature, दादी's identity line about the hooked left foot is mandatory and must land in the same breath.

### दृश्य 7 — बाहर: धारा किनारे — शाम, अँधेरा होते हुए · पापा का सच
**What happens:** **The search never stops — पापा's confession arrives as the answer to a question, not as a speech.** कुकु shouts that there must be solid stone *somewhere*; after one beat of silence पापा gives the location, मिटासुर's hands confirm it within seconds (the goblin's "flaw" is the instrument again, as in Ep4), and only *then* does पापा say how he knows. Child first, adult following the child.

**Heard:**

## 6. Cast

**कुकु** — has a *want* this episode: to get दादी home before dark. He forges twice, and between the two attempts he takes the blame on himself («शायद मेरा अक्षर ही कमज़ोर है») — which is exactly what breaks फ्यूरिया open. His change: from "I am too small" to naming the real problem out loud ("the letter isn't weak, we don't know where the stone is"). He is also the first voice to defend मिटासुर, which keeps Ep2's नीति alive without restating it.

**फ्यूरिया** — the hinge. Her greatest strength (fastest, first, hardest-landing) causes the damage. **«पहले मैं!» fires exactly twice, in opposite meanings:** S1 to claim a prize (and the plank cracks), S6 to claim the blame. **Arc advance:** Ep3 she gave away the first roti (a good thing), Ep4 she gave away the first turn (an order) — Ep5 she takes the **bad** thing first. That is «पहले मैं» converting from reward to responsibility. Her own mandated correction («हिंदी में, वैस्पर!») is what makes her brother say the word that convicts her. In S8 she quietly lets पापा go first *without* using the catchphrase — the arc paid without a third use.

**वैस्पर** — the only one who heard the crack, and he never accuses anyone; he just says what he noticed, dreamily, which is the maximum possible pressure. **English drift (once, S6):** «...and it went — crack.» → «...कड़ाक।» It is the episode's only piece of evidence. **Shriek (once, S3):** the only sound that beats the water — it stops दादी stepping onto the broken planks. He falls asleep once, on the finished bridge, as the closing button.

**पापा** — his first episode, and he is not just "kind but firm": he is a grown-up who hid a mistake for years out of shame, and who confesses *behind his own child*. He was young, he meant to come back and move the pillar onto stone, he kept putting it off. His truth is the load-bearing information. He returns responsibility physically by testing the bridge with his own weight. Ep2's «माँ तो सबकी होती है» moves one step further: बड़ों की ग़लतियाँ भी होती हैं।

**दादी माया** — teaches the entire lesson **shouted across the water**, so the pedagogy scene is pure audio. She scolds nobody and never preaches "tell the truth." She runs the «प से...?» game from the far bank. At the close she describes only what already happened.

**मिटासुर** — innocent, never accused, and the one who says the suspicion out loud himself — which is more painful and more honest than an accusation scene. His sponge-hands are the instrument that reads the riverbed («रेत... रेत... यहाँ भी रेत!») and confirms पापा's stone within seconds. He gets one प word right in the game, which starts lifting the cloud before the confession lands. He ends the episode as the valley's **पुल का पहरेदार** — the goblin who once erased letters now guards one, and whose job is to report the first कड़ाक.

**रीछ** — growls only (SFX; existing Ep3 growl library covers him). One beat, two jobs: he tries to wade across and slips on round mossy stones, which establishes the riverbed truth the entire mechanic depends on. Later he carries the toddlers home over the new bridge.

**कैस्टर और लेडा** — stranded on the far bank, **never frightened** — sleepy and bored and grizzling about supper, never crying-in-danger. **लेडा's running bit intact and better:** the littlest nails the letter first, shouted across water («प! प!»), so the letter crosses before the bridge does. She names it again mid-crossing in S8.

**कालू** — finds the break (his bark brings everyone running), barks on the wrong beat during the letter game for comedy, and is comically terrified of the new bridge until मिटासुर carries him across.

## 8. Rulings where the judges disagreed

- **Law 6 / headline-as-deck (producible wanted a ruling, pedagogy called it borderline):** **allowed**, because Ep4 already shipped «उसके ऊपर एक रेखा» for न after a full audit — but only as «प की ऊपर वाली लेटी लकीर», never generalised, and always paired with the hooked-foot identity line so recognition hangs on प's own feature.
- **पटरा as a recap card (pedagogy: drop; story: keep as a lived word):** **keep in dialogue, no card** — it is genuinely lived but dialect-uneven and unrepeatable outside this episode, and card art is a render spend.
- **The S2 crossing on screen (story liked the walk; producible wanted it gone):** **gone** — it is lossless under Law 1 and it deletes the single most fumble-prone shot plus a whole light state.
- **दादी's near-step on the broken planks (story: keep, audio-only; producible: nearly cut it):** **keep**, because it is the only real justification the shriek has ever had — but audio-only, no foot-over-gap frame, laugh within ten seconds.
- **नीति length (both story and pedagogy wanted a trim; the original had two clauses):** **one sentence**, «छुपाने ने उसे बड़ा बना दिया» — the bridge/trust image moves into पापा's plain speech in S7 so it stays an explanation, not a second maxim.
- **पापा's culpability (story judge said it was too heavy for a 5-year-old):** **softened** — he was young, meant to come back, kept putting it off; the shame of admitting it is the fault, not deliberate negligence.
- **The middle-of-episode sag (all three judges flagged it):** fixed by the pedagogy judge's insertion — the shouted «प से...?» game placed *inside* the search — which simultaneously supplies the missing phoneme practice, gives पतंग and पैर a second lived beat, and keeps the confessions inside continuous physical work.
- **कुकु as a passive tool (story judge's catch):** fixed by giving him a want (get दादी home before dark) and a wrong diagnosis (self-blame) that is the direct trigger for फ्यूरिया's confession.

| 1 | **Radio-play** | Strongest in the series: the entire lesson is shouted across water, both crossings are audio-only, कुकु names his own failure and every part of the letter as it lands, and the collapse is bark + line. **S1's crack and lie must be fully carried in dialogue, not by averted eyes** — that is the one beat to watch. |

| 6 | **No टोपी / no cross-script comparison** | Ruled in §4: «प की ऊपर वाली लेटी लकीर» only, never generalised, always paired with the hooked-foot identity line. The word टोपी is banned from script, cards and song. |
