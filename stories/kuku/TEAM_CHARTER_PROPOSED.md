# कुकु और अक्षर — team charter

**Status:** CANON from Episode 9 onward; filename retained so existing links do not break.

**Companion authority:** `AKSHAR_GURUKUL_CANON.md`

The author direction is to treat the children as capable actors. Every action sequence is a plan
with distinct jobs, and every child present makes a decision rather than waiting to be deployed by
an adult.

## The five Gurukul trainees

**कुकु — the forge.** Breathes persistent physical letters. He determines what can be built, but he
does not automatically own the whole plan. His recurring challenge is sharing control of what he
creates.

**फ्यूरिया — the first response and carry.** Fastest in the valley; first into danger and strongest
in aerial transport. Her precision test is where and when she stops, not whether she can go fast.

**वैस्पर — the route.** Perceives the hidden pattern, current, or sideways path through a problem.
He may see several possibilities; his decisive action is committing to one route the team can use.

**कैस्टर — the access.** Climbs into cracks, ledges, clasps, and enclosed spaces nobody else can
reach. In great form he can change scale to enter a seam or brace it. He chooses the risk; nobody
handles him like a tool.

**लेडा — the precision.** Finds the exact defect, target, timing point, or sound everyone else has
missed. Vesper sees the route; Leda sees the one detail that makes the route safe. Her observation
must alter action, not merely earn praise.

## Field companions and wider team

**कालू — the tracker and alarm.** Nose to the ground, finds trails, and gives the first warning. He
is Kuku's companion, not an अक्षर-कड़ा trainee.

**मिटासुर — the wall.** Strength and steadfastness; holds his post. He is a reformed valley ally,
not the continuing nemesis.

**तानसेन — the echo.** Perfect mimicry of sounds already heard. His repetition may incidentally
carry useful information, but he does not understand, intentionally relay, advise, or invent a
warning. He remains the only ordinary flyer among the valley companions.

**दादी — the tail, consent, and lore.** Rescue of last resort in the early phase, keeper of the
world's history, and the adult who makes sure training never replaces home or child agency.

**पापा — the रथ and carry.** Logistics, weight, the long road, and the practical adult point of
view.

## Action-writing rules

1. The team—not one gift—is the protagonist of an ensemble rescue.
2. Give each present trainee an independent want, initiated action, and consequence.
3. A character may be mechanically mistaken and emotionally right.
4. The child with the decisive information must cause the plan to change on screen.
5. Speed, strength, scale, sight, and letters do not replace judgment.
6. For a five-child climax, run the removal test: if any one child can vanish without changing the
   plan, rewrite the jobs.
7. A spotlight episode may use fewer than five trainees. Absence is not demotion.
8. A grown-up may hold the larger danger, provide history, or create a safe boundary. A grown-up
   does not execute the children's decisive maneuver.
9. After Episode 9, each trainee can use their own stored base transformation alone. Team joining is
   optional choreography, not a mechanical attendance requirement.

## The mythological register

From Episode 8 onward, the world uses a Puranic engine: तपस्या earns power, boons have exact words,
and exact words have consequences and loopholes. The same law can arm a nemesis and discipline a
hero. Defeats come from choices, wording, and coordinated use of established powers—not an
unannounced stronger spell.

Cheel's complete three-clause boon is quoted and interpreted in `AKSHAR_GURUKUL_CANON.md`. The
अक्षर-कड़े do not erase any clause, and landing is not an automatic loophole for a child who flies.
