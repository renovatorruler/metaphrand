# अक्षर घाटी — Episode 7: आ / ा

The first matra episode. No new consonant.

## What's already banked

| Ep | Letter |
|---|---|
| 1 | क |
| 2 | म |
| 3 | र |
| 4 | न |
| 5 | प |
| 6 | त |

Six consonants. Ep7 adds no seventh — it adds **आ** and its matra **ा**, and every
letter the kids already own doubles.

## The concept

**क has been saying *ka* since Episode 1 — not *k*.** The अ was hiding inside every
letter all along. ा just stretches it: क → का.

That is the whole episode. Once कुकु sees it, he can read twice as many words without
learning a single new letter.

Two shapes, one sound, and the difference matters:

- **आ** standing on its own — a letter in its own right (आम)
- **ा** holding on to another letter — the matra (का, ना, पा)

This is exactly step 0 → step 1 of बारहखड़ी in the app, in the same order, so the show
and the drill teach the same thing the same way.

## Shape of the episode

**Build it at night, under stars.** तारा and रात are the two words to hang it on: the
setting teaches the vocabulary, and both are readable by the end.

**आम carries the standalone आ** — it's already the आ card in the deck, so the letter
and the matra both get their moment.

**End on पापा, मामा, नाना.** Family names कुकु could not write before this episode and
can write now. That's the payoff, and it's the bit the kids will run and show someone.
