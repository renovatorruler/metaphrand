## The fused mechanic (the spine of the episode)
मिटासुर can **only erase, never create.** He hoards stolen letters because they're the only
things he's ever "had." **Kuku forges ONE new म.** A *made* letter cannot be un-made — so the very
existence of a created म **shatters मिटासुर's hoard**: every erased म snaps home *because creation
overpowers erasure.* And that SAME forged म's loop is the cradle-boat that carries the lost chick
to its माँ. **One move = save the chick + defeat मिटासुर + state the theme.** The line said aloud:
«मिटासुर सिर्फ मिटा सकता है — पर जो बनाया जाता है, उसे कोई मिटा नहीं सकता!»

## Beat sheet (8-beat adventure engine)

**1. Ordinary open (~1 min).** माशा's postcard arrives — a huge मेला in her city! Ours is TODAY.
The valley bustles: Dadi's **मिठाई** stall, the **मछली** pond (fish darting bright — *planted so it
can break*), a **मोर** family parading, chicks trailing their **माँ**. The team tumbles in happy.

**2. THE THEFT.** मिटासुर watches from a hill, sulking — no मेला, no friends, nothing of his own.
So he does the only thing he can: he **erases म.** In one visible sweep — the **मिठाई** goes
flavourless; **the मछली pond freezes, the fish going still and colourless** (मछली BREAKS); the
**मोर** can't fan; and a tiny chick cries for its "…" — the word **माँ** is GONE, mother and child
can no longer name each other. मिटासुर sweeps the glowing म's into his **sack — his hoard, the
only things he's ever had** — and waddles off cackling.

**3. आज का अक्षर (motivated) + मदद named.** The मोरनी cries across the chaos: **«मदद! मेरा बच्चा
कहाँ है?!»** (Help! Where is my child?!) — मदद spoken, in action. Dadi gathers the team: «म वापस

«हिंदी में, वैस्पर!» → M टोपी नहीं पहनता, म पहनता है. «बोलो मेरे साथ — म!» «'माया' में म है!» The
team locks in: **«हम मदद करेंगे!»**

**4. THE HUNT + the wound, out loud.** «अक्षर वीर, तैयार?!» Kalu catches मिटासुर's chalky scent;
Furia races «मैं पहले!» through the un-spelled मेला; dreamy Vesper is the one who SEES the hideout.
They corner him — he clutches the sack to his chest: **«नहीं! ये मेरे हैं! मैं कभी कुछ बना नहीं
सकता — बस मिटा सकता हूँ! ये अक्षर ही तो मेरे पास हैं!»** (No! They're mine! I can never MAKE
anything — I can only erase! These letters are all I have!) His wound is now the load-bearing hinge.

**5–6. POWER-UP = CREATION DEFEATS ERASURE (fused, one move).** Across the stream the chick teeters
at the water, crying for the माँ it cannot name; the मोरनी searches the far bank. No time to pry
the sack open. Kuku — scared but steady — forges: «अक्षर वीर, तैयार! साँस… टोपी… [टोपी SNAPS]
अक्षर — म!» A brand-new म blazes into being. Dadi calls the law aloud: **«मिटासुर सिर्फ मिटा सकता
है — पर जो बनाया जाता है, उसे कोई मिटा नहीं सकता!»** The instant the made म exists, मिटासुर's hoard
**shatters** — every stolen म snaps home: the **मछली** swim again in full colour, the **मोर** FANS
its feathers, माँ & बच्चा can say each other's names, the **मिठाई** turns sweet. And the SAME
forged म's loop unfolds into a **cradle-boat** that carries the chick across to its माँ (reunion
nuzzle). The creation that defeats मिटासुर is the very म that saves the chick.

**7. REDEMPTION THROUGH ACTION (the whole point of the villain).** मिटासुर sits in the mud, hoard
gone — and stares: the kids *made* what he could only ever steal. Kuku, who once couldn't make
fire, doesn't gloat. He kneels beside him: «तुम भी बना सकते हो. मेरे साथ बोलो — साँस… टोपी…
अक्षर!» मिटासुर, trembling, tries — and out wobbles a **lopsided little म with a crooked टोपी.**
His first created thing, ever. He gasps, cups it in both hands, and **lights up.** They pull him
into the मेला; he's handed a **मिठाई.** — Then Kuku, watching the peacock family and मिटासुर's
tiny म, asks: «दादी, क्या आपकी भी माँ थीं?» And the reveal: **दादी माया, पापा की माँ हैं.** Papa
arrives, kisses Dadi's head: «हाँ, ये मेरी माँ हैं।» Everyone is someone's child — even मिटासुर,
who just made his first म.

**8. RECAP + goodnight.** Dadi to camera counts the म-words they *lived and saved*: «म से — माँ!
