## V2 story

Krishna brings a red drawing book whose first picture was made with her mother. Furia wants her old friend to see how impressive her new large-form flight is. Rishi warns her to come inside with the first raindrop. Furia hears him, completes one safe flight, then talks Krishna into one more fast lap. Rain permanently blurs the mother-picture.

Furia cannot undo the damage with magic. She turns the same wing she used for showing off into a shelter, admits the mistake without an excuse, accepts Krishna's distance, dries the remaining pages slowly, and waits when Krishna asks for rest. Krishna later draws the rain-sheltering wing on the already stained, unfinished Furia page. The damaged mother-picture remains in the book.

The one final Nīti is:

> अगली बार कोई रोके, तो पहले उसकी बात सुनना।
