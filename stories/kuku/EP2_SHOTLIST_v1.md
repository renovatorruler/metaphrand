# EP2 «म से माँ» — SHOT LIST v1 (from screenplay v5)

**The production law this document enforces:** the video model never sees a scene. It renders
only the exact shots below. Dialogue is never given to the video model at all — every spoken
line plays over a TALKING shot (a still of the speaker, slow push-in, exact take length; optional
lip-sync later) or as VO over a specified ACTION shot. ACTION shots are 10s renders that get
trimmed DOWN to their planned window. Shot durations lock automatically once audio is recorded —
durations marked ~ are estimates until then.

**Shot types:** TALK = still + push-in carrying takes · VO = existing action clip, dialogue over it ·
ACT = wordless action clip · CARD = typst typography ($0).

**Asset status:** ✅ HAVE (already on disk, $0) · 🆕 NEED (new render, cost noted).
