## Production promise

- One main location: the Gurukul flight courtyard.
- One existing doorway composite for Dadi at the end.
- One new animal: Gauri the cow.
- One prop with three approved states: the red ball beside Gauri, far away, or resting inside the physical `ग`.
- One new magic event: Kuku forges the complete physical `ग`.
- Exact `ग` and `गाय` typography is a local compositing job. An image or video model must never be asked to spell Devanagari.
- Large and small dragon forms never share a shot. The size change is a light wipe between two locked group plates.
- Dialogue scenes use held poses, eye-lines, small reactions, and mouth motion. They do not require continuous body choreography.

## Eight hero actions

These are the only actions that need more than a held conversational image.

1. Furia flies straight through the ring; cut to her already landed.
2. Cut from the ball beside Gauri to the ball far away; rolling sound sells the missing travel.
3. Match-cut the five large dragons to the same formation in their small forms.
4. Gauri takes a few slow steps toward the seated children.
5. A rolling sound plays; cut to the ball far away, then to Furia already holding it.
6. Kuku breathes out gold light; cut through the flash to a locally composited complete `ग`.
7. A rolling-and-stop sound plays; cut to the ball already resting in the open curve of `ग`.
8. Kuku opens the home doorway; later cut to Vesper already asleep beside it.

Actions 2, 3, and 5 are deliberately state changes rather than continuous simulation. They can be made from still endpoints plus sound.

## Shot rejection rules

Reject or split any proposed shot that asks for:

- more than one independently moving subject;
- large and small versions of a dragon together;
- five simultaneous transformations rendered organically;
- precise paw-to-object contact while another character moves;
- a long continuous ball trajectory;
- a model-generated `ग`, `गाय`, or other Devanagari text;
- spoken left/right/up/down placement directions;
- a cart, rail, tower, chase, countdown, crash, or breaking structure;
- Cheel or a new campaign subplot.
