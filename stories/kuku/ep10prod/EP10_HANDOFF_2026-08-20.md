## Why V5 was rejected

V5 still treated Episode 10 as an action-rescue show. Its cart, track, countdown, aerial pursuit, precise stopping, and physical mechanism demanded expensive continuity animation. Dialogue then had to describe actions that the image track might not show clearly. That approach is abandoned, not patched.

## V6 story in plain terms

Episode 9's unfinished sentence is completed at the Gurukul flight ring. Furia flies first, but her hard landing rolls Gauri the cow's red ball away and frightens her. The five children become small, sit down, and patiently earn Gauri's trust. The repeated words गाय, गौरी, गेंद, and गुरुकुल lead Kuku to `ग`. He makes one complete physical `ग`, and Gauri's ball rests in its open curve. Dadi reviews `ग से गाय`; Vesper falls asleep beside the open home doorway.

Cheel is deliberately absent. Her campaign can continue in a later episode without making this first Gurukul lesson harder to animate.
