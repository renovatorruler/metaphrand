# EP10 «द से दीया» — the puppet-stage plan

**Status:** proposal. Nothing below is built yet. The screenplay is v5 (66 shots, 79 recorded lines, about 11 minutes) and it is not cut: every line stays. What changes is how it is made.

## The rule of the build

Everything is one stage: the courtyard plate, graded in code into its light states (dusk, lamp-night, dark, golden, and dusk again for the next evening). The characters are the puppets you have seen, moved by numbers. A "shot" in the script becomes a camera position on that stage, so a wide and its singles are the same moment seen from two distances, never two generations. The lamp, the smoke, the wind, the letter and its light are layers drawn by code on top. The video model is used for nothing in this plan; three beats are listed at the end as options with their prices, to be decided after you have watched the puppet version.

## What every scene shares

The plate never changes, only its grading. The lamp is one patch with a flame drawn in states: upright, flattened by wind, thin and blue, out. The wind is paper bits and dust drawn by code, with the same gusts timed to the flame. The letter द is the true font glyph, never generated, revealed as if breathed and lit from within; its wrong-way twin is the same glyph mirrored. Every line plays on its own take with the mouth shapes already cut, and the heads move with the rhythm of the speech as in the proof you saw.

## What is not on the puppet stage, by choice

Three beats could be handed to the video model, from a composited still of the same stage, accepting its inconsistencies there. None is in this plan. Prices are Cinema Studio's: 32.5 credits for five seconds, 65 for ten.

| Beat | Why a clip could be worth it | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| ३३-स and ३३-द, the great forms rise and are thrown back | five big bodies in real wind is the hardest thing for puppets to sell | 65 to 130 |
| २६, the flame dies | a real flame guttering out, close | 32.5 |
| ५५, the light pours down the valley | volumetric light over paper hills | 32.5 |

My recommendation is to build all 24 scenes as puppets first, watch the cut, and then decide beat by beat. The current line has 34 credits left, so any clip needs a new one.

## Order of building

Scenes 1 to 4 first, as one continuous cut with sound, so the stage, the crouch, the door and the lamp states are proven on the quiet act before the wind, the letter and the great forms. Then act 2 through scene 11, then the transformation and flight, then the letter, then the ending. Each block arrives as a clip when it is done.
