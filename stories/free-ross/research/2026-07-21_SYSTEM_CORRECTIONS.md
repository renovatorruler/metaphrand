## 2. YOU CANNOT STAKE A FACT THE OPPONENT CAN SHRUG AT

The staking system is NOT "assert a fact, opponent auto-takes the other side." A party only has to contest a fact if LOSING that fact BREAKS THEIR CASE. So the real procedure is ordinary case-building:

- A case is a CHAIN of facts plus the explanation linking them. There may be alternate routes; you pick your strongest and carry it all the way.
- Each fact in the chain can be attacked with a COUNTER-FACT. The betting happens at the CONTESTED NODES — the points where your story and theirs cannot both be true.
- You stake on a fact because the opponent MUST deny it or their theory collapses. If they can say "so what," it is not stakeable.

AUTHOR'S ILLUSTRATION (the infidelity murder): motive (found out his wife cheated) → gun bought at a named store two days prior → told the office he was driving to a client site 45 min away → took 90+ min → explained the gap with a roadside-lookout sandwich story → the missing hour is exactly enough to double back and kill her. Every node is checkable and attackable: maybe he didn't buy the gun there; maybe he really was at the lookout; maybe he was never near the house. You bet where the two stories can't coexist.

CONSEQUENCE (caught in "The Leaving Man" v1): "Otis was buying his own future the week he died" is NOT stakeable — the opponent shrugs (dog/business outlive him). A valid stake must be load-bearing against the opponent's required premise. Fixed in v2: the opponent's suicide theory REQUIRES a man in despair with no future; Ross stakes on "Otis signed a next-season crop contract and banked the advance," which they cannot concede without their theory dying, and cannot deny (signed contract + bank record).

## 3. THE KEY MOMENT IS A METAPHORICAL FLIP, NOT A TRIP-UP (governs the ability)

The signature realization the show is built to deliver (the House-episode chill) is NOT "the detective gets tricked." It is: the audience has been staring at an obvious thing the whole time, everyone reads it one way, and a METAPHORICAL reading turns it into the EXACT OPPOSITE of the consensus. Same known facts, reversed meaning.

- Author's real-world model: everyone knows Biden's frailty; read as a symptom it reveals the opposite of the assumed narrative — a party that can only field half-dead men is a dying party, not an ascendant one.
- Author's illustrative model (do not reuse): a suicide note written on the back of a lease renewal signed the day before — everyone reads "nearest scrap of paper," but a man who signs a year's lease intends to live, so the death's own paper disproves the suicide.
- The premier form of Ross's ability. It should point him toward dispositive physical/documentary evidence (compass, not proof) and it should reverse the CENTRAL, most-cited piece of the consensus.

DELIVERED EXAMPLE ("The Leaving Man" v2): the whole county reads the emptied grain elevator as a broke man cashing out before he jumps (empty elevator = empty man = suicide). Ross reads the same emptiness as its opposite: a farmer sweeps the granary EMPTY to make it ready to receive next year's harvest, and Otis poured every dollar of the sold grain straight into next spring's seed — he didn't cash out, he cleared the deck to go all in. Empty = ready, not depleted. The most-cited proof of suicide becomes proof he intended to live, which means he was killed. Same fact, opposite meaning.
