# FREE ROSS — ABILITY WORKED EXAMPLE v2: THE FEED STORE FIRE (supersedes v1)

Rebuilt after the round-five corrections to ROSS_ABILITY: the read is a COMPASS not proof; the subconscious is background explanation only; every readable act must be plausible ordinary behavior (the v1 will-change act is struck as a writer's contrivance); the case closes on physical, court-usable evidence that the read merely located.

## THE HIDDEN TRUTH (author-only)

Merrell's feed store burned the night before the bank's man was due to walk the property for the loan renewal. Cody, nineteen, set the fire with diesel from the ranch's transfer tank so his father could not chain himself to the dying store for another five years.

## THE ANOMALY THAT SUMMONS ROSS

Merrell is sabotaging his own defense. The underwriters allege owner-arson; the file favors fighting; Merrell keeps steering toward folding into the fraud finding that ruins him. The team reads a broken man giving up. The fold would also end all fact-finding — though only Ross will come to see that as the point.

## THE METAPHORICAL SIGHTINGS (the compass; rebuilt round six — cross-domain only)

STRUCK (round six): the gatepost incident (father covers son's trailer damage) — same predicate at smaller scale, a PRIOR not a metaphor; a cop could testify to it as history of similar conduct. Replaced by three sightings of one shape across three unrelated domains, none about blame or the boy:

1. THE FIREBREAK (agronomy). Grass-fire day; the neighbors disk their field edges, standard prudence. Merrell disks under his own BEST strip of standing hay — more than the risk requires, the good grass, not the edge — so nothing can cross his ground to the neighbor's wheat. Cover: fire prudence, fully intact. Signal: the excess — he sacrifices his best to contain damage away from others.
2. THE GRAMMAR (speech). "I let that baler get away from me" — the baler threw a bearing on its own. "I lost the oats" — hail lost the oats. And never the reverse: good years are "the rain made us a crop," never "I made." Cover: country modesty. Signal: the asymmetry — language that inhales fault and exhales credit.
3. THE COFFEE CAN (money). The cafe's honor-system coffee can runs short; Merrell tops it up unasked and unannounced, so nobody is ever counted short. Cover: generosity. Signal: the silence of it.

Three domains — farming, syntax, petty cash — one structure: THIS MAN CONTAINS DAMAGE BY TAKING IT INTO HIMSELF.

THE READING (hypothesis, not knowledge): the fold-pushing is not despair; it is a firebreak — he is disking under his own life. A firebreak implies two things the literal read never gives: something behind him worth the sacrifice, and A WIND DIRECTION.

THE WIND (direction derived, not psychic): check WHEN the folding started. The first fold-push came the day the underwriters noticed their intent to pull the fuel-shed logs and walk that side of the property. You cut the break on the side the wind comes from; the wind was blowing from the tank. Who uses the tank, whose path runs past it: the boy.

RULE 1 DECOY (performed rejection): Merrell visits the burned lot every evening. Ruth reads returning-to-the-scene guilt. Ross throws it away — the man's whole life is in that lot; grief visits; the plain meaning fully accounts.

## THE DIRECTED SEARCH (where the compass points)

If the boy did it, the fire's tools live on the boy's geography, not the man's. An owner-arsonist controls the property and removes evidence at leisure; a scared nineteen-year-old ditches things on his own path home. Ross walks the son's shortcut — the dirt-bike cut between the store lot and the house — and finds, in the section-line culvert: the jerry can with dye-marked diesel residue matching the ranch transfer tank, and a boot track the right size. Also on file: the loading-dock padlock, unforced, keyed — and one of its three keys hangs on the kitchen ring. Physical, courtroom-grade, and DISCRIMINATING: the can's location alone kills the owner-fraud theory, because it sits where only one person's life passes.

## THE RESOLUTION

The evidence means the underwriters would lose a showdown on "the insured set it" — and the same file points at the boy. Merrell: "Bury it." The geometry the world allows: withdraw the claim; no claim, no fraud case, no hunt; the father eats the loss he was always steering toward, now as a choice made with open eyes. The scene that pays it: the jerry can set on the kitchen table between father and son, and nobody saying the thing. Optional tag: Saturday at the cafe, two plates on the table, both eaten.

## WHAT THE READ BOUGHT

One item, the only item it is ever allowed to buy: the place to walk. No wager-as-proof, no symbol lecture, no reading of anyone's subconscious on screen — a trivial incident, a pattern hypothesis, a directed walk, and a can in a culvert that any arbitrator can weigh.
