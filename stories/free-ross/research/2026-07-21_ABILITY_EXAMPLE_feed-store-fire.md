# FREE ROSS — ABILITY WORKED EXAMPLE: THE FEED STORE FIRE (casebook #17, developed)

Kept as the record of the failure that forced the corrections.

Built per ROSS_ABILITY_v1's Doyle reverse method. This file is the construction worksheet: author-side first, surface-side after. The read obeys both PaRDeS rules and all three bans.

## STEP 1 — THE HIDDEN TRUTH (author-only)

Merrell's feed store burned the night before the bank's man was due to walk the property for the loan renewal. His son Cody, nineteen, set the fire with diesel from the ranch's own transfer tank, so his father could not chain himself to the dying store for another five years. Arson as a mercy killing of the family albatross.

## STEP 2 — THE SELF-LIE (what the read lands on)

Merrell's, not Cody's. He has assembled the truth at the second-author level (the timing, the smell in the boy's coat, the tank gauge) and consciously decided the fire was old wiring. His conscious story: "I just want this over." His subconscious's project: build a wall between the son and the fire.

## STEP 3 — THE PLANTED ACTS (each: act / innocent cover / why peshat is thin / referent)

1. THE WILL — Two weeks after the fire, Merrell pays a lawyer to take Cody off the store's succession. Cover: "no future in it for the boy." Thin: nothing is left to inherit; nobody pays a lawyer to amend a dead asset. Referent: moving the son out of the crime's blast radius — you cannot have burned what was never going to be yours.
2. THE TRUCK — He sells his good truck "for the lawyers," to the one buyer who takes it with the diesel transfer-tank still mounted, thrown in free. Cover: package deal. Thin: the same man itemized and haggled everything else he sold that month. Referent: giving away the object that measures the missing fuel.
3. THE FUEL — He converts the ranch to gasoline machines at a loss, sells the diesel drill and pump under market. Cover: "diesel's gone proud." Thin: nobody eats losses that fast on a price opinion. Referent: exorcising the fuel from his life. Diesel is his fly.
4. THE TWO PLATES — Saturdays he takes the same cafe table and still pays for his son's breakfast; Cody stopped coming a month ago and Merrell never once asks him back. Cover: habit. Thin: when the girl charges for one, he corrects the ticket. Referent: the conscious cannot sit with the boy; the subconscious will not let him leave the table.

## STEP 4 — THE DECOY (Rule 1 performed on screen)

Merrell visits the burned lot every evening. Ruth reads it cheap: returning to the scene, guilt. Ross refuses the read — the plain meaning fully accounts: the man's whole life is in that lot, and grief visits. The scene exists so the audience watches him throw a read away; the discipline is what separates him from gut detectives.

## STEP 5 — THE ANOMALY THAT SUMMONS THE READ + DISCOVERY ORDER

Ross enters because Merrell is sabotaging his own defense: the underwriters allege owner-arson, the file favors fighting, and Merrell keeps steering toward folding, toward ruin. A client trying to lose is the wrongness. Discovery order: the will (in the file) → the two plates (at the cafe) → the truck and tank (sale ledger) → the gasoline yard. Four witnesses, one referent: every act is about the diesel, and every act is about the boy. Rule 2 satisfied; the read is admissible.

## STEP 6 — THE PRICING PLAY (the terminus; off-docket, client-side)

The read cannot go to the table — Ross's job is to keep it FROM the table. He prices it in the office:

ROSS: "We fight the wiring theory, we win. I've seen their file. One thing first." He sets a single gram on the desk and slides it across. "Stake it on the wiring. One gram, on 'nobody you love touched that tank.' I'll run your case any way you say, all the way. Just put your hand on it."

Merrell looks at the coin. His hand stays where it is.

ROSS: "Yeah."

No latency, no eye-line, no tremor. The hand that declines a wager is the confession, self-administered, at market price.

## STEP 7 — THE RESOLUTION THE WORLD ALLOWS

The read changes the claim's geometry: Merrell withdraws the claim entirely — eats the loss he was always steering toward, now as a conscious choice instead of a smuggled one — and with no claim there is no fraud allegation left to prosecute. The debt died with the collateral. The boy is never named on any record. Tag: Saturday, the cafe, two plates on the table, and this time both of them eaten.

## BANS AUDIT

No gut feeling: four articulable witnesses plus one performed rejection. No lie-detection register: the only "tell" in the episode is a hand that declines a wager, which is a priced act, not a leak. No clinical vocabulary: Ross never names what anything means; he builds the coin and the quiet, and the meaning stays in the audience's lap.

## WHAT THIS TEMPLATE PROVES
