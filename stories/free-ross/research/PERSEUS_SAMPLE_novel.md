“Pronto!” Able said, “I’m running out of time and patience.”

Both Pete and Able stared at her walk away as she went up the stairs.

“What’re you looking at?” Able growled at Pete.

“I get what you see in her,” Pete said. “She ain’t bad to look at. Has a shapely behind. But she’s more trouble than she’s worth if you ask me.”

“Nobody’s asking you,” Able said. “You better focus on saving your own behind. It wasn’t Jim they were after. Now go check if someone’s coming.”
