# FREE ROSS — FULL PaRDeS PROFILE: THE FEED STORE FIRE (round seven; sits above ABILITY_EXAMPLE v2)

The round-seven law this file demonstrates: A READ'S MEANING IS NEVER IN THE ACT IN ISOLATION — it is conferred by the whole story, down to the Sod. And the case only feels AUTHORED when the crime itself is generated by the same structure the detective reads: one shape, fractal at every level (the father's containment logic → the store's failure → the son's arson → the fold → the resolution). Per project PaRDeS doctrine: Peshat shows AND withholds; the Sod is never stated on screen.

## PESHAT — the plain story (complete without the layers)

A feed store fails for years while its owner carries half the county on credit he never calls in. It burns the night before the bank's loan-renewal walk. The underwriters allege owner-arson; the owner keeps steering toward folding into the finding that would ruin him. His own agency's investigator finds a jerry can with dye-marked diesel residue and a boot track in a culvert on the son's dirt-bike shortcut. The claim is withdrawn; no one is charged; the family eats the loss. WITHHELD IN PLAIN SIGHT: the store sign reads MERRELL BROS. FEED, and there is only one Merrell; nobody remarks on it; the camera holds it once. A photograph, dug out of the ash, stands on the kitchen shelf.

## REMEZ — the hints (Ross's layer; where the ability operates and stops)

The cross-domain sightings: the FIREBREAK (disks under his own best hay, beyond prudence, to keep fire off the neighbor's wheat); the GRAMMAR (thirty years of "I let that baler get away from me" and "I lost the oats" for accidents, "the rain made us a crop" for successes — language that inhales fault and exhales credit); the COFFEE CAN (tops up the cafe's honor can, unasked, unannounced). And the store's LEDGER — the coffee can at commercial scale, page after page of carried accounts. The hint-layer truth: the business is not failing despite him; THE STORE IS THE TRAIT, INCORPORATED — a store run as penance cannot be run as commerce. Ross assembles the shape (this man contains damage by taking it into himself), derives the wind (the folding began the day the fuel-shed walk was noticed), and walks to the culvert. He goes no deeper; he never needs the why. The ability ends at Remez.

## DERASH — the story the hints tell (the interpretive layer; the writers' room's layer)

The store is an altar. Keeping it alive — the loans, the chains, the renewal about to be signed — is a service the father performs, and the family has watched the service eat him for fifteen years. THIS is what the son hates: not the store, the altar; watching his father bleed himself into a building. THE UNIFICATION: the boy burned it with the father's own move — firebreak logic, learned at the man's knee: sacrifice the good strip so the fire cannot reach what matters. He cut the break FOR his father, because the father never would. The arson is the father's metaphor, weaponized by his student. The fold, re-read: after a lifetime of absorbing stray faults, a punishment has finally arrived big enough, and he walks toward it like a man who has been waiting for it by name. The resolution's dramatic engine: withdrawing the claim converts an infinite, unpayable penance into a finite, chosen price — the first punishment he has ever been permitted to pick out himself. Tag (two plates, both eaten): the living son finally outbids the dead.

## SOD — the secret (never stated; assembled only by the audience)

Why does he believe stray punishment belongs to him? Because once it did, and nobody delivered it. The other Merrell on the sign: a brother, dead young — the grain bin, on a day the chores were swapped, and the swap was Merrell's idea. The family verdict was "nobody's fault," and the unassigned blame never landed — so he has spent his life catching every loose fault in the county because the one fault that mattered was never given to him. The store was meant to be the brother's; keeping the dead man's store alive keeps the account open. He is not a guilty man. He is an UNPUNISHED man, administering the sentence to himself in installments. Screen carriers, total: the plural on the sign; the photograph from the ash. No dialogue touches it, ever — not Ross, not Ruth, not the son.

## DOCTRINE EXTRACTED (fold into ROSS_ABILITY on approval)

1. FRACTAL LAW: the crime is generated by the same structure the detective reads — one shape at every level (trait → business → crime → fold → cure). A case assembled from unrelated parts feels written; a case grown from one structure feels found.
2. THE ABILITY'S CEILING: Ross operates at Remez and stops — pattern, wind, evidence. Derash belongs to the writers' room; Sod belongs to the audience. This keeps the anti-psychoanalysis ban structural: the detective never does etiology, and the show never speaks the root.
3. SOD CARRIERS: the secret rides in one or two Peshat-legal objects (a sign's plural, a photograph any man would save), each fully innocent under the plausibility law. The Sod is withheld DEPTH, never withheld PLOT (curiosity law intact: the audience always knows what is happening; only the root of why deepens on reflection).
