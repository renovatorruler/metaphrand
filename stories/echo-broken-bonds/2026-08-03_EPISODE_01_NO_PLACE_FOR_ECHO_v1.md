# ECHO AND THE BROKEN BONDS

## Episode 1 — NO PLACE FOR ECHO

**Authority:** Binding story brief v0.3, supplied 2026-08-03. Sections 5–8 are
immutable. This file contains story design and script only. It does not build the
game, request assets, or perform child testing.

**Project boundary:** This episode belongs to a standalone project for teaching
children lambda calculus through the series. It does not share continuity, canon,
or story dependencies with another project.

**Target runtime:** 10–12 minutes, including player-held states.

**Emotional question:** Will Echo uncamouflage and risk public rejection in time
to stop a rescue plan only he can see is wrong?

**External rescue:** A corrupted public launch drives a three-car sky-tram out of
guidance above Gate Plaza. Tavi, a young dragon whose wings cannot yet carry her,
is trapped in the swinging rear car.

**Echo's concrete want:** Be invited onto the Founding Day roster without having
to ask.

**Blaze's concrete want:** Lead a flawless public rehearsal and enter the council's
captain selection.

**Episode turn:** Echo first becomes publicly visible by contradicting Blaze. He
later ends Blaze's captain trial to save Tavi, knowing the person whose invitation
he wants may never forgive him.

## Locked story decisions

- The rescue is one continuous physical chain. Every computed result remains
  visible and makes the next action possible.
- Blaze's demonstration shares a synchronization frame with the live tram
  guidance bus. Knot's pre-deployment Bond edit therefore causes the emergency
  literally; Knot does not cast a generic failure spell.
- The first duplicated pack is a complete one-card shell blueprint. It is useful
  for a visible brace and is not a partial copy.
- The later Navigation Drone Kit is a different, larger Mission Pack. Its boundary
  visibly contains the shell blueprint, a nested navigation Power Script, and
  Tavi's beacon signal. The whole pack is copied.
- Blaze is right that the simple shell pair must deploy immediately. Echo is right
  that the shell pair cannot find Tavi. The rescue needs both judgments in order.
- The physical clock pressures the characters. The player never receives a
  failure countdown. At an interaction, the current safe state holds until the
  Linkkeeper acts.
- Knot captures Anchor Signature 1 even though Tavi is rescued. The rescue does
  not erase the campaign consequence.

## Image system

1. **The unused green formation mark:** Echo carries it unseen in Scene 1. It
   remains outside the day's four-person rehearsal boundary. The episode ends with Echo
   visible and the mark still unplaced.
2. **Smooth gold versus violet stitch:** Intact ownership is a raised, textured
   path that remains readable without color. Knot's edit leaves an angular seam
   and a physical loose tail.
3. **The persistent PAIR frame:** It remains around both shell results and both
   navigation results. The rescue never presents two loose drones as equivalent.
4. **The provisional application token:** Reya gives it only after Echo chooses
   Tavi. It creates a real route to Founding Day without pretending he already
   belongs.

# SCRIPT

## 1. EXT. GATE PLAZA - NOON

Four formation marks sit across the launch floor: falcon, wolf, duck, rabbit.
Beside them is an empty fifth socket.

A loose green chameleon mark rises from behind the control rail. No hand appears
to hold it. It drops. An unseen hand catches it before it clatters and slides it
behind a maintenance case.

BLAZE nudges his red mark half an inch with one talon.

**BLAZE**  
Red first. Blue half a step back. Gold—

**SUNNY**  
Holding center.

LULU stops bouncing. One ear turns toward the launch rail.

*Click. Click.*

**LULU**  
That clamp is loose.

Blaze looks up at the council clock.

**BLAZE**  
We have nine minutes.

From behind the maintenance case:

**ECHO (O.S.)**  
Eight and a half.

Blaze turns. The space behind the case looks empty.

A tool lifts by itself. ECHO, completely camouflaged, tightens the loose clamp.
The clicking stops.

Echo glances toward the Link Lens.

**ECHO**  
Keep the Lens low. I only need to fix this.

He takes out the green mark. Under his breath, rehearsing:

**ECHO**  
Blaze. The reserve place—

Blaze drops from the rail onto the floor. Echo vanishes into the case's plum
shadow. The green mark disappears with him.

Blaze tests the clamp. Solid.

**BLAZE**  
Good.

COUNCILOR REYA and two council observers enter the viewing rail above the floor.
Reya raises one small chime.

**COUNCILOR REYA**  
Today's rehearsal roster seals on the first chime.

Blaze takes the red mark. Bolt, Sunny, and Lulu step onto theirs.

Echo lifts the green mark toward the empty socket.

The chime sounds.

A boundary closes around the four rehearsal marks. The unused fifth socket dims
for today's launch.
Echo lowers the green mark.

**BLAZE**  
Gate Guardians ready.

Echo stays hidden beside them.

## 2. INT. GATE PLAZA - SERVICE CONDUIT - SAME TIME

The launch plan hangs inside a narrow service cavity.

A raised braided Bond runs from the lead Role Gate to the demonstration-lane Role
Spot. A fixed guidance lead connects the guidance core to the live tram bus. It
will remain there. Beside the bus is a separate emergency-priority override Role
Spot, closed by a smooth IDLE cap.

KNOT'S gloved hand enters with a violet Threader. Six small spools line its
handle.

Knot loads the first spool.

The Threader removes the IDLE cap, cuts only the braided Bond, caps the abandoned
demonstration Role Spot, and attaches the same Bond to the override Role Spot with
an angular violet stitch. The fixed guidance lead never moves. The lead Gate now
owns the tram override.

One short violet tail remains caught beneath a conduit screw.

An angular bead inside the stitch settles onto the plaza's shared deployment
telemetry. It is a passive sampler: it can record one completed rescue signature,
but it cannot edit another term.

The first spool goes dark. The Threader ejects it.

Before leaving, Knot slides the team's already prepared Bolt Strength Pack onto an
equipment shelf below the smooth Standby Gate, outside its input rail. Knot adds
no Bond and forms no Team-Up. The pack is a physical lure placed where the
frightened team will want to use it.

Knot pockets the empty spool and withdraws. The altered launch plan remains still
and complete. Nothing moves again until Blaze activates it.

## 3. EXT. GATE PLAZA / SKY-TRAM - MOMENTS LATER

A three-car sky-tram glides above the plaza toward the shared guidance curve.

In the rear observation car, TAVI, a young dragon with small folded wings, presses
her nose to the window. A little emergency bell hangs beside her seat. A separate
triangular safety beacon on the seat frame blinks steadily.

Below, Blaze places his complete Speed Pack to the right of the public launch
script. The two shapes form one ready Team-Up.

Lulu's ear turns toward the floor.

**LULU**  
There's another click.

**BLAZE**  
The tram is changing track.

From camouflage, Echo's two eyes pivot separately. The lead Gate's raised Bond
does not end in the demonstration lane. Echo illuminates its path into the live
tram bus.

**ECHO (O.S.)**  
Blaze, wait. Your route goes into the tram.

The council clock reaches its mark.

Blaze sees the chime begin to fall. He cannot see who is warning him.

**BLAZE**  
Clear the channel.

Blaze strikes DEPLOY.

The launch shell clears in one pulse.

The fixed guidance core remains connected inside the live tram bus. The
demonstration-lane Role Spot holds the smooth IDLE cap.

Blaze's entire Speed Pack appears at the bus's emergency-priority override.

The tram surges.

Its guidance lights blow dark from front car to rear. The isolation fuse snaps
open. The rear car yaws sideways as the tram enters the curve.

Tavi grabs the bell cord.

*TING-TING. Pause. TING-TING.*

On the launch floor, every alarm begins at once.

**BLAZE**  
East switch. Move!

Blaze crouches to launch. He springs.

**LULU**

**BLAZE**  
Next problem.
