# Echo and the Broken Bonds — Target-Quality Playable Slice

## The promise

In six to eight minutes, the player becomes Echo's unseen Linkkeeper, helps expose a sabotaged rescue route, makes legal transformations happen, and saves Tavi. The slice should feel like one complete dramatic rescue from a commercial game—not a tutorial dressed as a story and not a paper prototype.

## Target

- Landscape browser play at 1280×720 and 1366×768, using touch or mouse.
- Fixed 2.5D side-view civic-fantasy diorama with cinematic camera moves inside one continuous plaza-and-tram world.
- A seven-year-old desk proxy: large direct controls, one cue at a time, voice plus captions, no required reading, no punishment, and safe held states.
- Six to eight minutes for a first complete run; the player can pause indefinitely at every decision without the rescue worsening or solving itself.

## Included story spine

1. Echo brings a loose green formation mark because he wants Blaze to notice and include him.
2. Echo hides, repairs the rattling clamp unseen, and causes his own chance to close when the four-Guardian roster seals without him.
3. Knot's one-spool pre-launch edit is shown as the literal sabotage; Blaze later Deploys the corrupted complete script.
4. Tavi's tram runs away. Echo publicly unmasks to stop Blaze and exposes the violet route.
5. The player Reveals the real Bond, then legally Deploys the one-use Speed Pack; Blaze drives its transformed exoframe and physically pulls the brake.
6. The brake works, but the rear coupling begins to fail. The player repeats one complete shell blueprint into exactly two brace drones inside a persistent PAIR.
7. Echo and Blaze fight over the last reserve. Echo chooses Tavi over Blaze's trial and the invitation he hoped to earn; Blaze releases the cable.
8. The player repeats one complete Navigation Kit into exactly two whole kits. Either side may Deploy first. One clamp creates a persistent dangerous twist; only a fresh opposite Deploy creates the second clamp and enables the rescue.
9. Tavi is rescued. Only afterward, Transit Anchor 1 samples once without changing the term.
10. The demonstration remains dark; Blaze's captain bid is suspended; Reya locks the disputed tram record; Echo receives only a conditional token and no membership; his green mark remains unplaced; Bolt finds the violet trail toward Gear Forest; Knot sees Echo beside the Linkkeeper.

## First meaningful input

Within twenty seconds, after Lulu visibly locates the ordinary rattling clamp, the player presses and holds that clamp while camouflaged Echo physically tightens it. This is an ordinary world interaction: no Bond appears, no beta step occurs, and Lulu's hearing is not treated as computation. Releasing early gives immediate literal feedback and loses no story state.

## Deliberately outside this slice

- The zero-use falling-mast encounter. It remains canon and belongs in the full Episode 1 game, but is not silently compressed into this rescue slice.
- The optional extra-brace branch whose current wake-ring staging is internally contradictory. The binding rules are not changed to accommodate it.
- Exploration, collectibles, scores, fail states, combat, customization, and membership rewards.

This work must always be labelled a **playable slice**, never the complete Episode 1 game and never child-validated learning evidence.

## Non-negotiable interaction truth

- Blaze alone Deploys the sabotaged launch. The player does not cause the disaster.
- Reveal only exposes an existing Bond. It never reduces a term.
- Player rescue Deploys each commit exactly one visible beta step.
- Transformations realize the resulting pack or machine; they do not mutate its semantic identity, grant new biological powers, or act without the Guardian.
- Repeated use clears the source, creates the exact number of complete non-sentient occurrences, and retains PAIR.
- The first nested Deploy cannot buffer, skip, hide, or auto-trigger the second.
- Both left-first and right-first paths preserve the one-clamp consequence and converge on the same final term.
