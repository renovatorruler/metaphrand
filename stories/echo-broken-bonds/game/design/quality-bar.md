# Echo and the Broken Bonds — Frozen Runtime Quality Bar

**Frozen before visual generation.** This bar judges the real 1280×720 browser build. It is not evidence of child comprehension or appeal.

## One-sentence bar

Echo must match Mini Force's silent setup → activation → large-form reveal and problem → specific counteraction → held consequence, while matching PBS KIDS' age-appropriate direct manipulation with one active cue, large targets, immediate literal feedback, and a cohesive full-stage world.

## Scoring

Every numbered subitem is worth exactly two points. A critic assigns `2` only when the complete required property is plainly present, `1` when it is present but materially ambiguous or one constituent check fails, and `0` when absent, contradicted, or uninspectable. Objective thresholds are never rounded into a pass: a `1` may describe a near miss, but any hard-gated threshold still fails the candidate. Three independent fresh-context critics each score the complete evidence separately with integer arithmetic and no averaging or rounding. **Each critic must independently award at least 85/100, at least 27/30 in Story, no zero-valued Story subitem, and every hard gate must pass.** The reported round score is the minimum of the three totals, never their mean or median.

## Blind comparison rule

All five properties are judged every round; “not comparable,” abstention, and tie count as an Echo loss. Three independent fresh-context critics receive randomized unlabeled A/B evidence: stills for first-glance subject clarity and interface competition; the raw 11-second clips for scale change; and the raw 30-second clips plus before/after stills for problem-specific causality and state persistence. A property is an Echo win only when at least two of three critics choose Echo. Echo must win at least four of five properties; this is a release-blocking gate, not advisory. Each critic seals a separate verdict before aggregation. A failed property names the next round's largest gap; reference choice cannot change mid-streak.
