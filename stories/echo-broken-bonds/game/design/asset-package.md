# Asset Package Index

This is one versioned production manifest split into inspectable tables:

- `style-spec.md` defines `STYLE_FORMULA_v1`, identity locks, scale, materials, camera, motion, accessibility, and continuity.
- `canon-lock.json` freezes the exact five source-file bytes and their authority order; the four 2026-08-03 files remain binding canon.
- `audio-spec.md` separately defines `AUDIO_FORMULA_v1` and the accepted but not yet authorized `VOICE_CONFIG_v1`.
- `assets.csv` is the exhaustive asset/state inventory. External references never appear in it.
- `asset-contracts.csv` binds asset families to masters, exact runtime requirements, pivots/hitboxes/timing, status, rights, billable ceilings, and acceptance tests.
- `rights-ledger.csv` makes every provider-output class explicitly pending, defines the evidence path required to clear it, and keeps references/motion studies out of the shipping package.
- `references.csv` contains QA-only comparisons. Every row is do-not-ship and forbidden as a style-generation input.
- `reference-lock.md` freezes the exact source moments, PBS measurements, five mandatory blind-comparison properties, capture conditions, and immutable QA-evidence rule.
- `quality-bar.md` defines the runtime evidence and pass/fail rubric.
- `browser-lock.json` freezes the installed headed Chromium/Playwright bytes, launch/context settings, viewports, and exact constrained-lane throttle values.
- `test-envelope.md` freezes the named-machine, constrained-browser, load, frame-time, input, memory, audio-sync, replay, and rejoin measurements while explicitly limiting the resulting platform claim.
- `test-oracle.md` maps every gate and adverse path to an exact starting state, input, required evaluator/world result, forbidden transition, dwell, and evidence.
- `candidate-evidence.md` binds planning, runtime content, test system, precommitted session, all attempts, raw evidence, blind packets, and separately sealed critic verdicts into non-circular immutable IDs.
- `preapproval-planning-lock.json` hashes the audited paper package before reference capture. By contract it cannot authorize spend; the later `planning-lock.json` must also hash the completed reference-evidence packet.
- `lookdev-batch-01-requests.json` freezes the exact six prompts, prompt hashes, provider parameters, internal generated-reference dependencies, fail-fast checks, alpha/background limitation, and free evaluation composites.
- `lookdev-batch-01.md` is the first proposed billable batch: up to six named single-image 2K requests, a 42-credit hard cap, fail-fast dependencies, and no retries. It remains inert until its exact experimental-spend phrase is explicitly approved.

No `planned` or `blocked` row is a usable final asset. A row becomes `approved final` only after its contract passes against an unedited capture from the target build and its source/master hash, cleanup record, rights status, and actual cost are recorded. Motion-generation output is reference; it may guide a manually registered rig animation but may not be renamed or shipped as a final sprite sheet.

The manifest intentionally contains no zero-use mast assets and no optional third-brace assets. Those mechanics remain canon outside this slice.

`sequence-builder-v1.md` is preserved as failed-round evidence and is not a production candidate. `sequence-v2.md` is the current production candidate and has passed its fresh paper re-audit; runtime performance and comprehension remain unproven.

Visual direction approval and billable production approval are separate. At present, neither has been inferred.
