# Echo and the Broken Bonds — Audio Contract v1

## `AUDIO_FORMULA_v1`

Original civic-fantasy rescue audio built from tactile paper flicks, braided-cord tension, brushed-brass mechanisms, airy rail resonance, hand percussion, warm low strings, and a small bright woodwind identity for Echo. Energy rises through physical cause and consequence, never through combat aggression. Transformation sound traces the same pack opening and locking; it never implies autonomous magic. Valid Bonds sound rounded and continuous; violet sabotage sounds angular and interrupted. Clear transients, restrained low bass, no vocals, no franchise imitation, no essential information carried only by sound.

This text applies to music, ambience, and SFX prompts. The visual 80-word style formula does not.

## Story and mechanics

- The complete playthrough must remain mechanically and emotionally understandable when muted.
- Tavi's bell is the only `two rings → pause → two rings` sound. The separate triangular seat beacon makes no bell sound.
- Reveal, camouflage, hearing, cable motion, sampling, and hero movement never borrow the Deploy cadence.
- A Deploy has a three-part tactile signature: physical latch, route answer, one committed impact. Exactly one impact accompanies exactly one visible beta step.
- The first clamp ends in unresolved one-sided strain. The second clamp ends in balanced PAIR resonance and only then a door-release sound.
- In held states, danger beds loop without accelerando, countdown beeps, or escalating distress. The story stays urgent while the child remains safe to think.
- Transit Anchor 1 adds one quiet remote copy-and-cold sound after rescue; it never sounds like correctness, power-up, or a changed result.

## Mix and delivery

- Masters: 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV; web encodes derived only after final mix approval.
- True peak at or below `-1 dBTP`; integrated loudness target `-16 LUFS` for the full browser mix, checked on laptop speakers, one small mono speaker, and headphones.
- Dialogue side-chain ducking is gentle and never hides machinery causality. Captions remain available even when voice is muted.
- Every loop has declared in/out samples and passes a ten-cycle gap/click test.
- A no-recording-overhead performance run verifies audio does not cause frame hitches or desynchronization.
