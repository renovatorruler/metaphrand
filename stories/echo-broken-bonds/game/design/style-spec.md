# Echo and the Broken Bonds — Visual Production Contract v1

**Status:** proposed; no visual production until user approval.  
**Contract ID:** `STYLE_FORMULA_v1`

## Byte-identical style formula

> Premium 2.5D kinetic cut-paper action adventure, rendered as layered screen-printed card and brushed brass inside a cinematic civic-fantasy diorama. Chunky, child-readable silhouettes; crisp die-cut edges; tactile braided cords; embossed machine seams; no photorealism or toy-plastic gloss. Guardian roles use emerald, scarlet, cobalt, gold, and rose against storm-blue stone and warm cream architecture. Bonds glow white-gold; sabotage stitches glow violet. Low-angle wides, strong parallax, rim light, restrained bloom, readable faces, hard transformation silhouettes, and clear foreground/background separation at landscape gameplay distance.

The quoted 80 words must be appended byte-for-byte to every visual-generation prompt. It is a production constraint, not a claim that the style is approved.

## Originality boundary

Use only the broad action grammar measured from Mini Force: a small vulnerable hero, a player-caused activation, an unmistakably larger rescue form, and an immediate problem-specific action. Do not copy any protected character design, costume, device, animal-mech form, composition, line, shot choreography, effect, or asset. PBS KIDS governs interaction density and geometry only. ASTRO BOT is a nonbinding finish ceiling only.

## Canon identity lock

- Echo is a green chameleon and the unofficial fifth Guardian. His recognizable locks are a compact chameleon silhouette, independently directed eyes, a loose green formation mark, camouflage, and Link Vision. He never biologically shapeshifts.
- Blaze is a red falcon. His locks are a forward arrow silhouette, wings and talons, far-sight focus, and decisive launch posture. He drives transformed equipment; it never pilots him.
- Bolt is a blue wolf. His locks are grounded weight, scent/evidence posture, paper-map precision, and deliberate movement.
- Sunny is a golden duck. Her locks are a broad protective silhouette, water-rescue readiness, and hands-on care without sentimental posing.
- Lulu is a rose-pink rabbit. Her locks are high ears, spring-loaded jump silhouette, and active listening that remains distinct from Link Vision.
- Tavi is a young dragon passenger, not a mascot or prize. Her bell is physical and audible. Her seat-mounted triangular beacon is separate and non-audible.
- Reya is a civic councilor, never a Guardian-colored fifth teammate.
- Knot's species and final form remain unfixed. Episode 1 may show only obscured hands, the Threader, a silhouette, or a distant monitor image—never a completed character reveal.
- No literal ages are assigned to any character.

## Fixed scale chart

At the normal gameplay camera, one Guardian standing height is `1.0 G`.

- Echo and each Guardian: `0.9–1.1 G`; differences come from species silhouette, not adult/child coding.
- Tavi: `0.72 G` standing, readable through the tram window.
- Closed hand-carried pack: `0.35–0.48 G`.
- One shell brace drone: `0.85 G`.
- One Navigation drone unfolded: `1.8 G` from foot to opposite clamp tip.
- Blaze's Speed Pack exoframe: exactly `3.0×` Blaze's standing height and at least `2.0×` his wingspan, with Blaze visibly centered.
- One tram car: at least `4.5 G` high and `7 G` long; three cars must overwhelm Blaze in the vulnerability wide.

Master sheets include this ruler. No downstream asset may silently rescale a character or machine to solve composition.

## Color, material, and semantic grammar

- Guardian roles: emerald/green, scarlet/red, cobalt/blue, gold/yellow, and rose/pink. Every role also has a unique boundary shape, texture, position, and emblem; color is never the only carrier.
- Valid Bonds: raised tactile white-gold braid with two visibly connected endpoints.
- Knot's edit: angular violet stitch with a loose tail. It never resembles a valid raised Bond.
- Ready plan: physically raised ring and unspent closed shell.
- Evaluated plan: flat ring, cleared Power shell, result retained.
- `PAIR`: rigid crossbar connecting exactly two owned occurrences. It persists after both inner results evaluate.
- Source clearing: the source cradle remains visibly empty; it never fades into an ambiguous decorative glow.
- Same-rune lure: matching emblem but no physical braid connection; it remains empty after the valid one-use Deploy.
- Background: storm-blue stone and warm cream civic architecture. Interactive machinery gets brass edge light; noninteractive dressing stays lower contrast.

All five frozen comparison stills must remain legible muted and in grayscale.

## Master-rig and continuity rule

Generated images establish masters, not independent final frames. Production begins with one registered plaza master, one tram/coupling/brake master, one equipment scale sheet, and separate orthographic character masters. Parallax layers, poses, damaged states, transformations, and aftermath states must derive from those masters or be assembled from their rigged parts. Before/after states use the same camera, registration marks, geometry, lighting direction, and identifiers. A newly generated unrelated still cannot replace a state in a continuity chain.

Every runtime sprite or layer requires: final pixel dimensions; timing or fps; transparent alpha or declared blend mode; registration pivot; hitbox if interactive; frame padding; loop/hold rule; source/master hash; cleanup status; and an acceptance capture in the target build. Video generations are motion reference until manually cleaned and registered; extracted frames are never automatically final assets.

## Camera contract

- Runtime stage: landscape 1280×720 authored frame, safe at 1366×768.
- Core play camera: fixed side-view diorama with at most three registered parallax depth bands.
- One subject, one immediate problem, and one active cue dominate each gameplay view.
- Transformation reveal: low wide; complete form fits inside the central 70% width and 82% height; small operator remains readable.
- UI tray: no more than 25% stage width. Core targets are at least 80×80 CSS px; primary controls are at least 88×88 CSS px.
- Effects may frame the changed object but never cover its source, owned spots, persistent PAIR, or one-clamp consequence.

## Motion contract

### Transformation grammar — maximum 11 seconds

1. `0.0–1.5s`: show the closed source and specific danger together.
2. `1.5–2.0s`: player release/Deploy receives tactile response.
3. `2.0–6.5s`: the same pack visibly unfolds; no cut hides semantic continuity.
4. `6.5–9.0s`: hold the complete large form with Blaze still readable at its centre.
5. `9.0–11.0s`: Blaze drives it directly toward the east brake.

Reduced motion preserves all five states using short cross-dissolves and holds.

### Problem-specific payoff grammar — maximum 30 seconds

Constraint detail → player comprehension window → one selected Deploy → one clamp impact → at least two-second held one-clamp twist → fresh opposite Deploy → second impact → at least two-second held straightened result. Input during a transformation is acknowledged but never buffered or executed.

## Interaction and accessibility contract

- A meaningful ordinary-world input occurs within 20 seconds.
- Exactly one tutorial cue may animate at a time.
- Pointer/touch attachment is continuous; snap response is at most 150 ms; every release responds within 250 ms; visible world consequence starts within 500 ms.
- Prompts use at most 12 words across two lines. After about six idle seconds, help exposes the next relationship without moving an object or naming the answer.
- Captions, mute, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion mode are always available. Nothing essential is only audio, text, motion, or color.
