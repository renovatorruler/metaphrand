# ECHO AND THE BROKEN BONDS

## Six-episode first-season arc

**Authority:** Binding story brief v0.3, supplied 2026-08-03. Sections 5–8 are
immutable. This is a story-and-causality document, not an implementation plan.
It does not authorize game construction, assets, or child testing.

**Project boundary:** This season belongs to a standalone project for teaching
children lambda calculus through story mechanics. It has no narrative or canon
dependency on another project.

## The campaign in one line

Knot must spend six finite Thread Spools to collect six civic Anchor Signatures
before the sixth Founding Day chime; every successful sabotage opens the next
system and every rescue leaves a consequence that drives the Guardians there.

## The route through the season

```text
Launch Plaza transit
    -> Gear Forest freight
    -> Night Relay communications
    -> Civic Archive identity
    -> Tidelock emergency shelter
    -> Founding Day Heartline
```

Knot does not attack six unrelated places. Each captured signature grants access
to the next civic system. The heroes also follow the evidence or deal with damage
from the prior rescue. Plot, character consequence, campaign, and learning order
therefore travel along the same route.

## Six-spool campaign ledger

Knot owns exactly six Thread Spools and spends exactly one before one Guardian
deployment in each episode. Every replacement is a pre-evaluation story-world term
edit. It is non-computational and consumes one Thread Spool; no beta or alpha arrow
represents the edit. The spent violet stitch leaves one visible passive Signature
Sampler on the civic system's shared deployment telemetry. It cannot edit a term,
choose a result, or judge correctness. It records the literal result only after
the final relevant Deploy, then goes cold. It is not a hidden correctness switch.

## Episode 1 — NO PLACE FOR ECHO

### Personal story before the alarm

Blaze has one noon rehearsal in front of the council. A clean run places him on
the captain slate. Echo carries an unused green formation mark and wants Blaze
to ask him to stand on it. Instead of asking, Echo camouflages and repairs a loose
launch clamp. Blaze assumes the stage crew fixed it. The rehearsal floor roster
seals with four names; the separate Founding Day roster remains open.

### Knot's campaign action

The Founding Day rehearsal temporarily shares a synchronization frame with the
live sky-tram guidance bus. Before Blaze activates it, Knot spends Thread Spool 1
and moves the launch Bond from the demonstration role to a pre-existing
emergency-priority tram override. The fixed guidance lead never moves, and the
replacement remains a complete, well-formed script. Blaze's Speed Pack enters
the override rather than the demonstration role, takes priority over guidance,
makes the tram surge, and opens its isolation fuse. The spent stitch leaves a
passive Transit Signature Sampler visible on the shared deployment telemetry.
Knot cannot change anything after deployment begins, and the sampler cannot
change the rescue result.

### Rescue and conflict

A three-car sky-tram carrying a young dragon named Tavi loses guidance above Gate
Plaza. Echo sees the corrupt route, but an invisible warning sounds like stage
interference. He must uncamouflage on Blaze's public launch mark and force Blaze
to stop.

Blaze is still right about the immediate clock: the lead car has seconds before
the east switch. There is no time to inspect every plan. Echo and the Linkkeeper
trace one real Bond past a nearer matching lure. Blaze follows the resulting
one-use route and reaches the east brake.

An intentional zero-use Standby Script then releases a fixed counterweight.
Sunny resists leaving Bolt out because she sees the emotional damage of exclusion.
Echo refuses to invent a Role Spot. Bolt waits safely in the physical scene and,
because he was not consumed by the plan, retrieves the two-slot rescue harness.

Blaze next insists on immediately repeating a complete one-card shell blueprint
into both PAIR positions. Echo wants to wait for navigation. Blaze is right: the
simple pair braces the folding middle coupling before it breaks.

Tavi remains hidden beyond steam in the rear car. The larger Navigation Drone Kit
contains a shell, nested navigation plan, and Tavi's beacon. Copying and running
the whole kit requires the rehearsal scoring reserve. Echo chooses Tavi before
anyone offers him recognition. The public demonstration goes dark. Lulu calls the
load timing, Bolt closes the central harness, Sunny receives Tavi, and Blaze holds
the brake.

### Cost and irreversible turn

Tavi is safe. The demonstration is not restored. Blaze tells Echo the two truths
he is least ready to hold together: Echo saved Tavi, and Echo ended Blaze's trial.
The council cancels the event and suspends the captain bid. Knot records Transit
Anchor 1 only after the final tram Deploy settles; the visible sampler then goes
cold. Knot sees Echo standing uncamouflaged beside the Linkkeeper.

Councilor Reya locks the disputed tram record pending investigation, then gives
Echo an unstamped green application token. If he submits one Guardian-signed rescue
record before the Civic Archive's Founding roster deadline, the council will
formally consider him. It is an opportunity, not an invitation and not a promise
that he belongs.

### Direct cause of Episode 2

Bolt, available because of the zero-use action, finds the Threader's violet fiber
inside the tram conduit. The physical trail leads toward Gear Forest. He must
return with proof before the council's sunset review records the failure as
Blaze's fault.

### Story-only proof

Remove the mechanical terms: Echo wants Blaze to invite him, hides instead of
asking, appears only by publicly contradicting Blaze, and then sacrifices the
event that could have restored both their ambitions to rescue a child. The story
still functions.

## Episode 2 — THE WHOLE PACKAGE

### Personal story before the alarm

Bolt has until sunset to deliver intact physical evidence to the council. He
wants to clear Blaze and protect the team's credibility. Echo wants Bolt to treat
Link Vision as enough reason to act before conventional proof exists.

The violet trail leads to **Patch**, a city courier carrying a composite freight
pack. Bolt initiates the pursuit into Gear Forest. He does not wait for permission.

### Knot's campaign action

Transit Anchor 1 opens the city's freight handoff. Before Patch reaches it, Knot
spends Spool 2 and replaces the complete Freight Handoff Script. The spent stitch
leaves a visible passive Signature Sampler on Freight's shared deployment
telemetry. The innocent top card in Patch's composite Mission Pack is conspicuous
on purpose. Knot expects Echo to chase what looks important and Bolt to protect
the sealed package longer than he protects its frightened carrier.

### Rescue and conflict

Patch's glider enters a folding freight route and becomes pinned between two
moving sorting arms. Echo initially follows the bright top card. Bolt tracks the
full package and proves the bright card is a genuine bait. He is right against
Echo.

When the first sorting arm drops, Bolt abandons the safer evidence platform, leaps
beneath Patch's glider, and holds the arm with his own strength. He initiates the
rescue before Echo has chosen its route; the team must now save Patch before Bolt's
grip fails.

The rescue makes left/right order and grouping visible: one route can safely act
on the intact composite pack, while reversing the relationship sends the glider
toward a closed bay. The complete nested plan must travel with the courier; no
one may peel off the attractive top card and call it the whole.

Bolt can preserve the sealed cargo and its evidentiary history, or tear it open
to release Patch before the evening crushing cycle. Echo gives up the proof and
chooses Patch. Bolt makes the separate costly decision to tear the seal with his
own paws. The team deploys Knot's altered handoff to release the glider; its passive
telemetry sampler remains visible beside the resulting route.

### Cost and irreversible turn

Patch lives. After the final freight Deploy settles, the passive sampler records
the literal result as Anchor 2 and goes cold. When council guards arrive,
frightened Patch uses the torn relay route to flee before the team can establish
whether the courier helped Knot willingly. The council treats the missing intact
evidence as failure and removes Blaze from the provisional captain slate. Bolt
still wants proof, but he now acts on Echo's rescue judgment privately.

### Direct cause of Episode 3

Freight Anchor 2 opens Night Relay. A surviving sub-plan in Patch's torn courier
pack carries Lulu's call sign. That night, two distress calls begin transmitting
under the same name.

### Story-only proof

Bolt needs evidence before a deadline. Patch, the person carrying it, becomes
trapped. Bolt destroys what could vindicate his friend in order to save a courier
he still does not trust. The story remains complete without curriculum language.

## Episode 3 — WHOSE CALL SIGN?

### Personal story before the alarm

Lulu hears a weak live distress call beneath a stronger repeating signal. Night
Relay folds its access bridges and powers down at sunrise. Anyone still inside
will be sealed there through Founding Day.

Lulu wants the others to follow the faint call. Echo wants the stronger trail
because it appears to lead to Knot and the proof lost in Gear Forest. Blaze wants
the same trail because catching Knot could repair his name. Bolt suspects both.

### Knot's campaign action

Using Freight Anchor 2 to enter the relay handoff, Knot spends Spool 3 and replaces
the complete Night Relay Dispatch Script. The replacement nests two missions
bearing Lulu's call sign. The spent stitch leaves the third passive Signature
Sampler visible on Night Relay's shared deployment telemetry. The identical names
do not give the two missions identical ownership. One carries a living relay
keeper's whisper. The other carries the stronger authorization signal Knot needs.
Either path through this one altered dispatch can produce the final relevant
Deploy the sampler observes.

### Rescue and conflict

Lulu hears breath, a loose vane, and a real change between repetitions. Echo sees
the stronger route and initially discounts what Link Vision cannot prove. Lulu
breaks formation and begins crossing the folding relay bridges alone.

The two plans contain repeated call signs inside separate boundaries. The
Linkkeeper follows Bonds across and within those boundaries rather than matching
names. The weak caller is reached. Lulu was right.

The team cannot also catch the carrier of the strong signal before sunrise. Echo
follows Lulu and helps save the relay keeper. Knot escapes along the other route
while Lulu's chosen rescue finishes. The passive sampler records Communications
Anchor 3 from that literal result and goes cold.

### Cost and irreversible turn

Lulu's disobedience saves a life and costs her official emergency-channel access
pending review. The rescued keeper recognizes an obsolete Relay Steward mark on
the violet hardware and retrieves an old duty badge bearing the name **Vey**.
Nobody explains Vey's childhood or grievance.

### Direct cause of Episode 4

Vey's employment record exists only in the Civic Archive. The recovered badge is
too damaged to identify Vey by itself, but it retains one after-hours search
request. That request requires sponsorship by an active Guardian credential.
Blaze risks his still-active private credential to sponsor it. The query alerts
Knot, who uses Communications Anchor 3 to intercept Blaze's public call sign before
they arrive. Lulu's suspension has a different lasting effect: calls she would
have received will be redirected through the Archive's emergency overflow.

### Story-only proof

Lulu needs the team to believe her before the evidence becomes loud. Echo chooses
the person she can hear over the culprit he can almost catch. They save one and
lose the other.

## Episode 4 — A NAME IS NOT A BOND

### Personal story before the alarm

The Founding Day roster closes when the Archive purges compromised records at
sunset. Blaze wants the council to clear his name publicly before that happens.
Echo wants to keep the investigation private long enough to protect Blaze's
underlying credentials and identify Vey.

### Knot's campaign action

Communications Anchor 3—not Blaze's displayed name—opens the Archive control
route. Before the team arrives, Knot spends Spool 4 and replaces the complete
Archive Evacuation Script. The replacement traps workers behind a closing record
vault. The spent stitch leaves a passive Signature Sampler visible on the Archive's
shared deployment telemetry. Knot attaches Blaze's stolen call sign only to the
public incident log so the breach appears to be Blaze's action. The stolen name is
an outside signal with no ownership Bond to the real Blaze's plan; it grants no
authority by itself.

### Rescue and conflict

Blaze can chase the impostor carrying evidence or save the workers before the
vault seals. Echo initially wants the evidence. Blaze refuses to let people be
crushed under his name and starts the rescue. He is right against Echo.

The real Blaze's Role Gate and all its owned Role Spots receive one fresh call
sign together. Their Bonds and action remain unchanged. The stolen outside name
stays outside and gains no ownership. Blaze performs the rescue under the fresh
name while the false public label remains inert. Renaming prevents the collision;
it does not remove the passive sampler from the Archive's shared telemetry. Blaze
chooses to Deploy that still-altered evacuation because the workers cannot survive
the vault's purge clock. The literal route saves them. After the final evacuation
Deploy settles, the sampler records Civic Identity Anchor 4 and goes cold.

Echo possesses a public Link Vision recording that could clear Blaze and prove
his own value immediately. Blaze's fresh badge pattern makes it Guardian-signed,
so publishing it would satisfy Echo's green application token. The unedited record
also exposes that private badge pattern and shows the rescued Night Relay keeper
entering the team's temporary safe room. Knot is still watching the public feed.
Before the Archive purge, Echo can either release that full recording or submit it
under seal; there is no time to produce a safe public copy. He seals it and submits
an anonymous correction. Blaze and the witness remain protected, and the archive
workers receive public credit.

### Cost and irreversible turn

Echo's official application window closes without the proof that could have
earned him a place. Blaze sees Echo deliberately disappear from a success rather
than hide from a risk. Knot escapes after the altered evacuation records Civic
Identity Anchor 4. The recovered record reveals the Founding Day deadline and the
retired route to Tidelock Shelter.

### Direct cause of Episode 5

Civic Identity Anchor 4 authorizes Knot to reopen the retired Tidelock emergency
route. Knot sends Patch's courier tag and frightened voice through it as the bait
for Spool 5. Because Lulu's official channel is suspended, the Archive overflow
redirects the outside call to Sunny's local water-rescue receiver. Sunny hears
Patch, refuses to leave the call unanswered, and reaches the shelter before the
others. The tide doors close behind her.

### Story-only proof

Blaze wants his reputation back but abandons the only chase that could repair it
to save workers. Echo can finally receive credit and chooses to protect Blaze
instead. Both lose what they entered the Archive to obtain.

## Episode 5 — THE CAPTURED SIGNAL

### Personal story before the alarm

Tidelock's chamber will flood when the Founding Eve tide reaches its upper mark.
Sunny is trapped inside with **Patch**, a former violet-thread courier whom the
team rescued in Gear Forest but whom the others still believe may have helped Knot
willingly.

Sunny wants both of them rescued. Echo wants Sunny extracted immediately and the
fifth-signature transfer intercepted before it reaches the Heartline. Bolt does
not trust Patch. The first safe lift has room for one.

### Knot's campaign action

Using Civic Identity Anchor 4 to reach Tidelock, Knot spends Spool 5 and replaces
the complete two-person Lift Script. The replacement contains a same-name
collision. The spent stitch leaves a passive Shelter Signature Sampler on
Tidelock's shared deployment telemetry. If the incoming outside signal passes
beneath the inner gate without a visible fresh rename, it will be captured and
drive the wrong rescue position. The script is complete and well formed before
deployment; Knot cannot change it while Sunny and Patch are being lifted.

### Rescue and conflict

Sunny shields Patch before herself and gives Patch the first safe foothold. Echo
argues that a one-person extraction protects more people by freeing the team to
stop Knot. Sunny remains inside. Her action makes the disagreement physical.

Echo accepts her priority. Before the outside same-named signal enters, the
Linkkeeper visibly relabels the inner gate and every Role Spot it owns. The
outside signal keeps its open thread. The two-person rescue then deploys one
step at a time. The fresh call sign prevents capture but does not alter or remove
the passive Signature Sampler on the civic telemetry; the sampler owns no Role
Spot and changes no result.

Both people survive. After the final lift Deploy settles, the visible passive
sampler records that literal result as Shelter Anchor 5 and goes cold. Knot
advances because the altered lift ran, not because an unseen timer declared the
heroes too slow.

### Cost and irreversible turn

Sunny's shield remains intact. She commits it to the Tidelock evacuation corridor
and promises the displaced civilians that she will hold that route through
Founding Day. She will therefore remain essential to the city rescue but cannot
join the Heartline mechanism. Patch reveals a physically finished but inactive
green Role Gate made for Echo. Knot's offer is not generic power; it is the
permanent public place the team never guaranteed. Patch also reveals that Echo and
the Linkkeeper must be present to operate the central emergency plan that can
supply the sixth synchronization.

### Direct cause of Episode 6

Five Anchors let Knot seize the Founding Day assignment channel and issue
preliminary assignments to registered heroes and civic rescue posts throughout
the city. The finished but inactive green gate draws Echo toward the Heartline
chamber while the others must decide whether his private offer makes him a
liability.

### Story-only proof

Sunny refuses to buy efficiency with another person's life. Echo knows her plan
requires the altered lift that will help Knot and chooses it anyway. The rescued
apparent enemy brings the truth that makes Echo's final temptation possible.

## Episode 6 — THE DOOR THAT SAYS NO

### Personal story before the alarm

The six Founding chimes are the six stages of Heartline synchronization. Echo
wants an unequivocal public place before the ceremony ends. Knot can give it to
him immediately. The team needs his help but cannot honestly promise what the
council will decide.

Before the first chime, Echo places Knot's finished green gate where all four
Guardians can see it instead of concealing the offer. Bolt wants the gate secured
outside the Heartline chamber until they can prove what it does. Sunny, speaking
from the Tidelock evacuation route, refuses to treat Echo as guilty for having
been offered what he wanted. Lulu wants Echo to say whether he came to accept it.
Blaze wants Echo to remain with the team but cannot offer the certainty Knot can.

Echo asks Blaze directly whether he has a place. Blaze does not soften the truth.
He cannot guarantee the council's answer. He asks Echo to stand with them while
the answer is still uncertain. The disagreement is not settled before the alarm;
they enter the Heartline without knowing what Echo will choose.

Knot's offer is emotionally stronger: one signed green gate, already built, that
nobody can close.

### Knot's campaign action

Before the first chime, Knot spends Spool 6 and replaces the complete Heartline
Emergency Script. The spent stitch leaves the sixth passive Signature Sampler
visible on the Heartline's shared deployment telemetry. The five captured Anchors
issue preliminary assignments that send distant designated rescuers toward
emergencies while nearby capable people are ignored.

One such assignment closes a public evacuation route. Echo must decide whether to
leave civilians in danger or run the only ready emergency plan. He chooses the
people, illuminates Knot's waiting replacement, and asks the Linkkeeper to Deploy
it. The literal result reopens the route. When its final relevant Deploy settles,
the passive sampler records Heartline Anchor 6 and goes cold. The frame closes,
the real overwrite begins, and every spool is now spent. Knot can no longer edit
another term or improvise a new sabotage.

### Rescue and conflict

The Guardians retain every canonical power. The frozen civic network simply
refuses to route them toward needs outside their assigned posts. Blaze still uses
speed to reach people the official route ignores. Bolt uses strength and tracking
without waiting for a perfect authorization trail. Lulu hears calls that no longer
reach her suspended channel. Sunny's intact shield holds the Tidelock evacuation
corridor, so she remains part of the citywide rescue but cannot join the Heartline
mechanism. Their chosen cooperation, rather than their labels, keeps the city
moving.

At the Heartline chamber, Knot offers Echo the activation signal for the finished
green gate. That same signal is the child-facing FIRST selector—formally TRUE—
choosing the locked Heartline over the open Heartline. Echo can dock it directly
into his permanent gate, or give the complete signal to the NOT Power Script.
There is no separate speech followed by a technical solution: Echo enacts his
refusal by placing the only activation signal to the right of NOT and asking the
Linkkeeper to Deploy. The source signal clears from the green gate's activation
path, so the offered place goes dark and cannot be reclaimed afterward.

The reversal remains exact. NOT asks the current signal to choose between FALSE
and TRUE in reversed order, producing the opposite selector through separately
visible deployments. The resulting selector is offered the same ordered pair. It
chooses the open Heartline. Echo destroys the only guaranteed place he has ever
been offered in the same causal action that reopens everyone else's choices. No
"opposite beam" or correctness switch appears.

Blaze physically carries the selected open-route bar into the central gate while
Bolt braces the mechanism, Lulu calls the chime timing, Echo illuminates the
relationships, and the Linkkeeper completes the legal construction. At Tidelock,
Sunny keeps the evacuation corridor open as the released civic routes begin
answering local rescuers again.

### Cost and irreversible turn

The permanent assignments release. Echo's green gate is gone with them. Only
after the refusal does Blaze move the public formation marks apart and make room.
Sunny's gold mark remains lit from Tidelock; on the open relay, she waits for Echo's
answer rather than giving it for him. Bolt places the recovered violet thread in
Echo's hand. Lulu hears the final open relay answer from across the city.

Echo steps into the fifth place while its outline remains open. His provisional
application has expired, so this cannot be a late approval of the old token.
Councilor Reya instead asks the four registered Guardians whether they nominate
Echo. Each chooses yes after his refusal, and Reya formally records Echo as the
fifth Guardian in the current, changeable formation. The appointment is official;
the open outline means it is chosen and accountable rather than magically
permanent. No machine can guarantee that they will keep choosing one another.

One violet thread leaves the damaged chamber. It opens a future problem without
undoing this victory.

### Story-only proof

Echo is offered the certainty he has wanted all season. His friends can offer only
an honest, uncertain relationship. He rejects certainty before they choose him.
That choice, not his power, completes the change.

## Season causality check

1. Spool 1 replaces the Launch Script. Blaze's corrupted transit deployment creates
   the emergency and records Anchor 1; Bolt's zero-use availability exposes the
   Gear Forest trail.
2. Spool 2 replaces the Freight Handoff. Bolt initiates Patch's rescue; the altered
   handoff records Anchor 2, destroys Bolt's clean proof, and carries Lulu's call
   sign to Night Relay.
3. Spool 3 replaces the Night Relay Dispatch. Lulu's live-caller rescue records
   Anchor 3, exposes Vey's Archive record, and lets the stronger signal carry
   Blaze's public name away.
4. Spool 4 replaces the Archive Evacuation Script. Blaze's renamed rescue saves the
   workers and records Anchor 4; Echo seals his evidence; Anchor 4 lets Knot reopen
   the retired Tidelock route with Patch's voice.
5. Spool 5 replaces the Tidelock Lift. Capture avoidance saves Patch's outside
   signal; after the final lift Deploy, the passive telemetry sampler records
   Anchor 5 and goes cold. Sunny commits her intact shield to evacuation, and Patch
   reveals Echo's finished but inactive offer.
6. Spool 6 replaces the Heartline Emergency Script. Echo deploys it to save people,
   supplying Anchor 6 and creating the real lock. He then consumes his gate's only
   activation signal through NOT, reversing the lock and destroying the guaranteed
   role in one causal choice.

Nothing returns to its prior state between episodes. Knot's progress and the
Guardians' relationship damage are the same causal chain.
