## A. HUMAN STORY

Picture Gate Plaza at noon.

Four bright marks wait on the launch floor: red for Blaze, blue for Bolt, gold
for Sunny, pink for Lulu. Just beyond them is an empty fifth socket.

A little green chameleon mark floats toward it in an invisible hand.

Echo has brought the mark because he hopes Blaze will finally notice him and say,
"Stand with us." He has practised an answer. He has practised looking surprised.
All he has to do is stay visible long enough to be asked.

Then Lulu hears a loose clamp clicking on the launch rail.

Echo disappears into the colour of the floor. A tool rises by itself and tightens
the clamp. Blaze tests it, finds it solid, and turns back to the rehearsal. Echo
has helped him again. Blaze has no idea who did it.

The council chime rings.

Light closes around the four official marks. The empty socket goes dark. Behind a
maintenance case, Echo lowers the green mark he never got to use.

Under the plaza, someone else has been hiding.

Knot opens the launch machinery and finds a thick braided line running into
Blaze's demonstration track. With a violet tool, Knot lifts that line from its
place and stitches it onto the live tram override. The ordinary tram cable stays
where it is. Knot caps the empty place, winds the loose thread tight, and vanishes
before anyone arrives. On the way out, Knot slides Bolt's Strength Pack onto a
shelf beneath an empty standby gate.

One of six violet spools has gone dark.

Above the plaza, a three-car sky-tram glides into the curve. In the rear car,
Tavi, a young dragon whose wings cannot yet carry her, presses her nose to the
window.

Blaze sets his Speed Pack beside the launch plan. The rehearsal could put him on
the captain's slate. The council chime begins to fall.

Lulu's ear turns toward a tiny sound below.

Echo looks through the machinery and sees a line of light leading somewhere it
should never go.

"Blaze, wait. The tram."

But Echo is still invisible. To Blaze, the warning is a nervous voice from
nowhere, arriving at the one moment he cannot afford to hesitate.

Blaze strikes Deploy.

The pack follows Knot's violet stitch into the tram override. The tram jerks
forward so violently that passengers fall against their seats. Guidance lights
burst from front to rear. The last car swings out over empty air.

Tavi grabs the emergency bell.

*Ting-ting. Pause. Ting-ting.*

Blaze launches toward the controls.

Echo throws himself onto Blaze's red mark.

Green floods back over him from his toes to the top of his head. Suddenly the
whole plaza can see him: Blaze, the team, the council, the crowd, every camera.

Blaze stops with one wing brushing Echo's face.

"Move."

"Your speed went into the tram."

Blaze looks past him. Far along the rail, the east switch is almost closed.

"Seven seconds," he says. "We need that brake."

Echo turns toward the one person he has not been hiding from. The Linkkeeper is
his unseen field partner, a child trusted to handle the paths Echo can uncover.

The plaza dims. A small rescue plan glows between them. A line of light leaves
its centre, passes a bright socket marked like Blaze, and keeps going. The socket
is close. It looks inviting. But the line never touches it. Farther on, the same
line disappears into the route leading to the east brake.

The Linkkeeper lowers the two-point Lens onto the light. One point finds the
beginning. The other follows the raised braid past the flashing socket and comes
to rest at the far end.

The whole line wakes.

A fresh, closed Speed Pack waits beside the plan. The earlier one is still burning
inside the tram override. The Linkkeeper brings the new pack to the only cradle
shaped to receive it.

The line strikes the pack's latch.

One low pulse rolls through the floor.

Across the plaza, the east-brake lever answers with a second pulse.

On the third beat, the Linkkeeper strikes Deploy.

The compact pack erupts outward. Four red rails open into an exoframe three times
Blaze's height. Two spear above him and two unfold below his feet. Swept vanes
spread wider than his wings, and Blaze hangs at the centre, small and fierce. A
matching band of gold light closes around the real brake lever. The exoframe and
the brake flash back and forth, two halves of one rescue.

For one breath, the great frame hangs motionless beneath the runaway tram.

Blaze beats his wings, and the frame lunges with him. He becomes a red slash
across the plaza, driving his own speed through the path the child found. He
banks around the guide rail, drops onto the brake, and catches the lever between
both feet.

He pulls.

The front car screams around the curve instead of flying from it.

The save hits the rest of the tram like a hammer. A pipe bursts into white steam.
Two bolts shear from the middle coupling. The rear car swings even farther out,
with Tavi still inside.

Blaze cannot leave the brake.

He looks back at Echo. "Next problem."

The sideways pull bends a guide mast toward the crowd. Sunny reaches a standby
plan beside its counterweight. She snatches Bolt's Strength Pack from the shelf
and slots it beside the plan.

"Get Bolt under cover," Sunny says.

Echo looks inside the plan. Its smooth centre has no lines at all.

Sunny sees Bolt beneath the leaning mast. "Then he needs somewhere safe."

Bolt studies the sweep of the mast and the falling weight. He backs onto a clear
patch beside the rail and plants his paws. "Here."

Echo runs a glowing outline around the place Bolt has chosen.

The Linkkeeper looks again at the smooth, empty plan. Then the child strikes
Deploy without adding anything.

No armour forms around Bolt. Sunny's fixed handle lights instead. She slams it
down and throws her weight onto it. The counterweight drops. The guide mast heaves
upright, missing Bolt's marked position.

Bolt is already moving. He drags a rigid two-slot harness from beneath the rescue
forge. As he passes the open floor, he spots Knot's angular violet stitch under a
screw.

"Someone tied this."

The coupling groans, and Bolt leaves the thread where it is.

At the forge, two closed packs wait. The large Navigation Kit is already whole:
shell, pathfinder, and beacon imprint sit together behind one unbroken casing.
But a separate wake-up ring crawls around that casing, and the forge still holds
the sleeping pack in its cradle. The small pack's ring is complete. Its casing
shows a single squat brace machine folded inside.

Blaze sees the joint crumpling from the brake. "Hold the coupling!"

"Tavi's behind the steam," Echo says.

"There won't be a rear car to find if that joint folds."

The metal buckles.

The Linkkeeper studies the waiting harness. It has two empty positions facing
opposite sides of the coupling. The small closed pack fits the forge cradle now;
the larger pack is still locked.

The child chooses the small pack and strikes Deploy.

The single folded pattern stamps into both sides of the harness. Two compact
machines kick open at once. Clamp legs punch from their shells. A glowing crossbar
stays between them as Bolt hurls the harness upward.

One machine catches the left side of the coupling. The other catches the right.
Bolt cranks the middle winch. The folding metal shudders and holds.

Echo looks across the steam at Blaze. "You were right."

Blaze does not smile. "Tavi."

*Ting-ting. Pause. Ting-ting.*

Lulu closes her eyes. One ear turns left, the other right. The bell is drifting
behind the steam.

As the shell brace takes the load, the large kit's wake-up ring completes its
circle. The Navigation Kit wakes fully.

Only then does the forge ask for more power.

Only one cable still glows. It runs to the trial recorder. Four council lamps and
Blaze's captain marker shine at the other end.

Echo reaches for it.

Blaze catches the cable with one talon.

"Pull that and my trial is over."

Tavi's window dips below the steam.

Echo looks at Blaze's bright captain mark. His other eye finds the little triangle
of Tavi's safety beacon.

"How much does it buy?"

"The kit."

The bell rings again.

Echo tightens his hand on the cable. "Tavi first."

Blaze holds on for another beat. Then his talon opens.

Echo rips the cable from the recorder and drives it into the forge.

The demonstration music dies in the middle of a note. The council lamps go black.
Blaze's captain marker goes black. They do not come back.

A quick brace pack flashes beside the forge, offering more protection for the
middle car. Beyond it, Tavi's car drops lower. The Linkkeeper leaves the brace
where it is and takes the large closed kit.

It fills both arms.

The child settles it into the forge cradle and strikes Deploy.

The kit opens like a mechanical seed. Its shell, folded legs, little pathfinder,
and triangular beacon flare together. Then a second seed opens beside it. The
forge has built a pair so broad that its glowing frame spans the whole rear car.

Both inner controls rise. Both are bright and ready.

Behind the steam, Tavi rings her little bell. Lulu turns with the sound, following
it rear-left. A gap opens in the steam. Separately, the triangular beacon on
Tavi's seat flashes as the car sweeps in the same direction.

The Linkkeeper touches the left inner control.

The first machine unfolds in midair. Four long brace limbs spread from its shell.
It dives into the steam, following the triangular light, and catches the left rail
of Tavi's car.

The swing shortens.

The car begins to twist around the single clamp.

Lulu's ears snap flat. "It's twisting!"

The picture holds on the first machine straining alone against the left rail. The
rear car twists far enough for Tavi's window to tilt sideways.

Then the Link view returns. The left inner ring lies flat. The great crossbar
still reaches across to the unopened machine on the right, where the other inner
control remains raised. The Linkkeeper's hand is clear of both controls.

The child reaches in again and presses the raised right control.

The second machine bursts open, follows the same triangular beacon from the other
side, and locks onto the right rail. The two brace bodies flare together across
the full width of the car. Their crossbar holds the sides apart while Bolt closes
the centre harness by hand.

The rear car straightens.

Sunny opens the door.

Tavi steps out on shaking legs. Sunny wraps a dry cloth around her shoulders and
supports her little wings.

"I rang the bell," Tavi says.

Lulu kneels. "Twice. A pause. Twice."

Tavi nods.

Sunny looks across the platform. "Echo. Take her safety line."

Echo takes the line where everybody can see him. Tavi slips the loop over his
wrist.

Under the floor, the violet stitch gives one last pulse and goes cold. Far away,
the first empty slot on Knot's Anchor Frame fills with the tram's golden pattern.
The brace machines keep holding. Tavi remains beside Echo.

Councilor Reya looks at Blaze's dark captain marker. "End the trial. No captain
score today."

Blaze lands beside the recorder and taps his mark. Nothing happens.

"They closed my score."

"I know."

"You knew when you pulled the cable."

"Yes."

Blaze looks at Tavi. Then at Echo.

"Next time, stand where I can see you before launch."

Echo stays green. "I will."

Reya gives him a thin green token with an empty signature space. If he brings her
one rescue record signed by a Guardian before the Archive closes, she will hear
his case for Founding Day. Echo has a chance now. That is all.

Bolt pulls the violet tail from beneath the screw. It comes free with a tiny
angular stitch. The marks continue west, toward the metal canopy of Gear Forest.

"This proves the stitch," Bolt says. He looks at Echo. "Not everything."

In Knot's relay room, one of six Anchor slots is full. A small monitor shows Echo
standing in public with Tavi's safety line around his wrist. Knot watches him,
then switches the monitor off.

Later, the plaza is empty. Echo finds his loose green formation mark behind the
maintenance case. The fifth socket is still dark.

He does not hide the mark there.

He carries it openly in one hand and Reya's token in the other as he follows the
Guardians toward Gear Forest.

## B. WHAT THE CHILD DOES

and what the team is trying to save. He uncovers relationships the others cannot
see. The child determines how those relationships can legally run. The physical
rescue still belongs to Blaze, Sunny, Bolt, Lulu, and the machines they deploy.

Nobody speaks the mechanical solution before the child touches the scene.
Characters name the danger, argue about priorities, and react to consequences.
They do not announce an endpoint, pack orientation, zero-use answer, or nested
side.

The fiction has real urgency, but the interaction state holds while the child
looks. Attempts are literal and reversible. Nothing says "wrong." Nothing turns
red, laughs, removes a life, or advances the danger because the child took time.
A beta step occurs only after a complete ready Team-Up receives a real Deploy.

## C. THREE-SHOT PROOF

These are the six silent images judged against the frozen comparison bar. The
question is whether they deliver the same underlying clarity: a small hero under
an overwhelming danger, a transformation the child visibly causes, and a much
larger rescue form performing the exact action the crisis demands.

### How a visible “form” relates to the term

The word **form** adds no computation. It is the in-world physical staging of an
already-present constant or evaluated structure:

- `Const(BLAZE_SPEED_PACK)` is a complete non-sentient equipment pack. Its compact
  casing may unfold into an open exoframe three times Blaze's height and twice his
  wing span because physical size and arrangement are staging properties of that
  same constant. The four structural rails, swept vanes, and worn centre cradle
  are already parts of the pack; no new constant appears. Blaze remains the real,
  single falcon at its centre. The frame generates no movement by itself and
  grants no behaviour beyond Blaze's canonical speed.
- `Const(EAST_RAIL_BRAKE)` remains the real manual brake and the second member of
  the evaluated `TeamUp`. A gold outline and alternating pulse make that
  relationship visible without changing the brake. The exoframe moves only when
  Blaze moves, and Blaze must still land on and physically pull the ordinary
  lever. No magnetic claw, new strength, teleportation, weapon, or autonomous
  catch is added.
- `Const(DRONE_SHELL_BLUEPRINT)` realizes as one complete non-sentient shell
  drone. Two occurrences therefore produce two complete shell drones.
- `Const(PAIR)` realizes as the rigid visible frame/crossbar that maintains left
  and right structure. It never disappears merely because both positions fill.
- `Const(KIT_FRAME)`, `Const(DRONE_SHELL_BLUEPRINT)`, the nested `Power`, and
  `Const(TAVI_BEACON_SIGNAL)` remain inside each copied Navigation Kit.
- `Const(FOLLOW_BEACON)` paired with `Const(TAVI_BEACON_SIGNAL)` is the only
  navigation behaviour shown. The drones do not think, choose targets, or become
  characters.

Unfolding panels, locking plates, route light, changes of silhouette, and the
three-beat latch/brake/Deploy presentation are animations of those same
components. They do not add an AST node, beta step, or semantic ability. The
later Navigation Kit forms may spread broader than the rear car because their
visible structure comes from the two copied `KIT_FRAME` and shell components

## G. ROUND-4 SELF-DIAGNOSIS

The largest remaining weakness is that **atomic pacing can force observation
without creating interpretation**. Round 4 guarantees that the child sees one
drone transform, one clamp catch, and the car twist before a second Deploy is
possible. But both legal first choices still lead to the same mirrored problem
and the remaining raised control is then obvious.

The held intermediate may feel like a dramatic consequence, or it may feel like
an unskippable animation between two button presses. Paper cannot establish which.
If reviewers cannot explain what the first deployment changed before reaching
for the second, the climax remains causally correct but shallow as play.
